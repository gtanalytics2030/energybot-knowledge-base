DOCUMENT TYPE: Notification (Regulatory / Policy Implementation)

PROVISION: GERC Notification No. 2 of 2015, dated 1st July 2015

TITLE: Notification on Applicability of Renewable Purchase Obligation (RPO) to Captive and Open Access Users/Consumers

SUMMARY:
GERC Notification No. 2 of 2015 notifies the commencement date of sub-regulation (iv) of Regulation 1 and Regulation 8 of the GERC (Procurement of Energy from Renewable Sources) Regulations, 2010, as amended. The notification declares 1st July 2015 as the date on which these provisions come into force, making the Renewable Purchase Obligation (RPO) applicable to Captive and Open Access User(s)/Consumer(s). The notification is issued under the authority of sections 61, 86(1)(e), and 181 of the Electricity Act, 2003, and supersedes the original 2010 regulations as amended by the First Amendment Regulations 2014.

**NOTIFICATION No. 2 of 2015**

In exercise of the powers conferred under sections 61, 86(1)(e) and 181 of the Electricity Act, 2003 read with sub-regulation (iv) of Regulation 1 and Regulation 8 of the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Procurement of Energy from Renewable Sources) Regulations, 2010 (Notification No.3 of 2010) (Principal Regulations) and Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Procurement of Energy from Renewable Sources) (First Amendment) Regulations, 2014 (Notification No. 2 of 2014), and all other powers enabling it in this behalf, the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission hereby notifies the 1st day of July, 2015, as the date on which the sub-regulation (iv) of Regulations 1 and Regulation 8 of Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Procurement of Energy from Renewable Sources) Regulations, 2010 (Notification No.3 of 2010) and amendments made in it shall come into force and the Renewable Purchase Obligation shall become applicable to Captive and Open Access User(s)/Consumer(s).

## Regulatory Impact

**Effective Date**: 1st July 2015

**Affected Parties**:
- Captive Power Plant operators / Captive Users
- Open Access Consumers
- All electricity market participants in Gujarat

**Key Regulatory Change**:
The notification makes Renewable Purchase Obligation (RPO) mandatory and applicable to captive and open access consumers as of 1st July 2015. Prior to this date, RPO may have applied only to Distribution Licensees and/or certain other obligated entities. This expansion extends renewable energy procurement obligations to all specified market participants.
