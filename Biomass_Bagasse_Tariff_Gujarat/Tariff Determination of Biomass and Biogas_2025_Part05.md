## CHUNK 37: Section 2.24 – Tariff Computation Parameters for Biomass Power Projects

---

DOCUMENT: GERC Order No. 02 of 2025 – Determination of Tariff for Procurement of Power from Biomass and Bagasse based Cogeneration Projects

DOCUMENT TYPE: Tariff Order

HIERARCHY: Chapter 2 / Section 2.24

PROVISION: Section 2.24

TITLE: Computation of Tariff for Biomass Power – Parameters and Final Tariff Values

SUMMARY:
Section 2.24 presents the complete operational and financial parameters used by the Commission to compute tariffs for biomass-based power projects, along with the resulting tariff values applicable during the control period FY 2023-24 to FY 2026-27. The section provides a comprehensive parameters table covering project cost, O&M, performance metrics, and financial parameters for both water-cooled and air-cooled condenser types. Final levelized fixed costs are Rs. 2.16/kWh (without accelerated depreciation benefit) and Rs. 2.00/kWh (with AD benefit) for water-cooled systems; and Rs. 2.29/kWh and Rs. 2.12/kWh respectively for air-cooled systems. Variable tariff components escalate annually from Rs. 4.82/kWh (FY 2023-24) to Rs. 5.57/kWh (FY 2026-27) for water-cooled, and Rs. 5.01/kWh to Rs. 5.79/kWh for air-cooled projects.

KEY TOPICS: Biomass tariff, Tariff computation, Fixed cost, Variable cost, Levelized tariff, Water-cooled condenser, Air-cooled condenser, Plant load factor, Station heat rate, Fuel cost escalation, Accelerated depreciation benefit, Tariff parameters table

TEXT:

**Application and Applicability of Tariff:**

The Biomass power and Bagasse based co-generation project to be commissioned under the PPA signed during the control period of this tariff order shall be eligible to get the above fixed component of tariff over the useful life of 25 years of the plant and yearly variable component of tariff as specified in the tariff order of the Commission from time to time. The Biomass and Bagasse Cogeneration Projects commissioned during the control period of previous tariff Orders of the Commission and executed PPA with the GUVNL/DISCOM, if any, shall be eligible for yearly above variable cost component of tariff determined by the Commission in this new tariff order while they shall continue to get levelized fixed cost component of tariff as per the previous tariff orders of the Commission read with provisions of PPA executed between the parties, as applicable.

## CHUNK 38: Section 2.25 – Tariff Computation Parameters for Bagasse-based Cogeneration Projects

---

DOCUMENT: GERC Order No. 02 of 2025 – Determination of Tariff for Procurement of Power from Biomass and Bagasse based Cogeneration Projects

DOCUMENT TYPE: Tariff Order

HIERARCHY: Chapter 2 / Section 2.25

PROVISION: Section 2.25

TITLE: Computation of Tariff for Bagasse-based Cogeneration – Parameters and Final Tariff Values

SUMMARY:
Section 2.25 presents the complete operational and financial parameters used by the Commission to compute tariffs for bagasse-based cogeneration projects, along with the resulting tariff values for FY 2023-24 to FY 2026-27. A unified parameters table provides project cost, O&M, performance metrics, and financial parameters for bagasse-based systems. The levelized fixed cost component is Rs. 2.15/kWh (without accelerated depreciation benefit) and Rs. 1.99/kWh (with AD benefit) applicable over 25 years. Variable tariff components escalate annually from Rs. 4.86/kWh (FY 2023-24) to Rs. 5.63/kWh (FY 2026-27), reflecting fuel cost escalation at 5% per annum. The lower capital cost and O&M percentage for bagasse (3% vs 5% for biomass) reflect the cogeneration benefit and industrial sugar industry integration. Previous projects commissioned under prior tariff orders retain their prior fixed cost but receive the new variable cost under this order.

KEY TOPICS: Bagasse cogeneration, Tariff computation, Fixed cost, Variable cost, Levelized tariff, Cogeneration efficiency, Seasonal operation, Plant load factor, Station heat rate, Fuel cost escalation, Accelerated depreciation benefit, Sugar industry integration
