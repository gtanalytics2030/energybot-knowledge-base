PROVISION: Clause 3.6 – Banking of Surplus Energy (Sections 3.6.1 to 3.6.3)

TITLE: Banking of Surplus Energy – Provisions and Restrictions

SUMMARY:
Clause 3.6 establishes the framework for banking of surplus energy from biomass and bagasse-based cogeneration projects. Biomass and bagasse-based cogeneration projects generate power with controlled fuel supply and hence power output can be predicted and scheduled to maintain grid discipline. Projects are required to schedule power as per ABT (Available Billed Time) mechanism as amended from time to time. In its earlier Tariff Orders (Order No. 01 of 2018 and Order No. 03 of 2022), the Commission provided for allowing set-off of wheeled energy at the recipient unit(s) on the same 15-minute time block basis. The Commission proposes to continue this practice for the next control period. The Commission decides NOT to allow any banking facility to biomass-based power projects and bagasse-based cogeneration projects either selling power to third party or wheeling for self-use. The Commission received no stakeholder comments and retained this no-banking provision as proposed.

KEY TOPICS: Banking of Energy, Surplus Energy, ABT Mechanism, Set-off Provisions, 15-Minute Time Block, Grid Discipline, Intra-State ABT, Must-Run Status

TEXT:

**3.6 Clause 3.6: Banking of Surplus Energy**

**3.6.1 Proposed in Discussion Paper**

Biomass and bagasse-based co-generation projects generate power with controlled supply of fuel and hence the power generated from such projects can be predicted and scheduled to maintain grid discipline. Hence, such projects are required to schedule their power as per the provisions of ABT mechanism as amended from time to time. Accordingly, in regard to Biomass and Bagasse based Power Projects set up for captive use/ third party sale, the Commission in its earlier Tariff Order No. 01 of 2018 and Order No. 03 of 2022 provided for allowing set off wheeled energy at the recipient unit (s) in the same 15-minute time block basis. The Commission proposes to continue the same practice for the next control period. The Commission, therefore, decides not to allow any banking facility to biomass-based power projects and bagasse-based co-generation projects either selling power to third party or wheeling for self-use.

**3.6.2 Suggestions/Objections of the Stakeholders**

The Commission has not received any comments/suggestions from the stakeholders in this regard.

**3.6.3 Analysis and Commission's Ruling**

The Commission decides to keep the banking related provisions for Biomass and Bagasse based Cogeneration projects as proposed in the discussion paper either consuming power as third party consumer or wheeling for self-use for the next control period.

PROVISION: Clause 3.8 – Contract Demand for Commissioning/Start-up Power (Sections 3.8.1 to 3.8.3)

TITLE: Start-up and Standby Power Requirements and Billing

SUMMARY:
Clause 3.8 establishes the billing treatment for start-up and standby power requirements of biomass and bagasse-based cogeneration projects. For biomass power projects selling power to distribution licensees, start-up power and standby power shall be charged at par with energy charges applicable to HT industrial consumers of similar connected load/category. PROVISION: Clause 3.10 – Information and Data Sharing by Biomass and Bagasse based Cogeneration Project (Sections 3.10.1 to 3.10.2)

TITLE: Record Maintenance and Data Sharing Requirements for Biomass and Bagasse-based Cogeneration Projects

SUMMARY:
Clause 3.10 establishes comprehensive record-maintenance and data-sharing requirements for biomass and bagasse-based cogeneration project developers. Projects must maintain detailed records on daily, monthly, and annual (financial year) basis covering: (1) Opening balance of biomass/bagasse fuel; (2) Opening balance of calorific value; (3) Quantity of fuel received; (4) Calorific value of fuel received (if determined by approved laboratory); (5) Quantity of fuel utilized for electricity generation; (6) Calorific value of fuel utilized; (7) Gross electricity generation; (8) Auxiliary consumption; (9) Net electricity generation per SLDC's Settlement and Allocation (SEA); (10) Closing balance of fuel quantity; (11) Closing balance of calorific value of fuel. This information must be provided with monthly invoices to the Nodal Agency (GUVNL)/DISCOM (Procurer). Projects must also upload daily-updated data/information on their website. The Nodal Agency verifies information while processing invoices. No electricity generation from fuels other than biomass/bagasse is allowed. The Commission retained the information and data-sharing procedures as proposed and directed generators to adhere strictly without failure.

KEY TOPICS: Record Maintenance, Data Sharing, Fuel Accounting, Calorific Value, Gross Generation, Auxiliary Consumption, Net Generation, SLDC Settlement, Nodal Agency Verification, Daily Reporting, Monthly Invoices, Website Upload

TEXT:

**3.10 Clause 3.10: Information and Data Sharing by Biomass and Bagasse based Cogeneration Project**

The Commission in the discussion paper proposed that the Project Developer shall maintain the record on (a) Daily basis, (b) Monthly basis and (c) Annual basis (Financial Year) of following information:

1) Opening Balance of Biomass & Bagasse fuel, if any

2) Opening Balance of Calorific Value of Biomass & Bagasse fuel, if any

3) Quantity of Biomass & Bagasse received

4) Calorific Value of Biomass & Bagasse received, if determined by approved laboratory

5) Quantity of Biomass & Bagasse utilised for generation of electricity

6) Calorific Value of Biomass & Bagasse fuel utilised

7) Gross Electricity Generation

8) Auxiliary consumption

9) Net Electricity Generation as per SEA of SLDC

10) Closing Balance of Quantity of Biomass & Bagasse fuel

11) Closing Balance of Calorific Value of Biomass & Bagasse fuel etc.

The above information shall be provided with monthly invoices raised by the Biomass & Bagasse Developer (Supplier) to the Nodal Agency (GUVNL)/DISCOM (Procurer).

Moreover, the Biomass & Bagasse Project Developer shall also upload the above data/information/details on its website duly updated on day-to-day basis. The Nodal Agency shall verify above aspects will processing the invoice(s) raised by the Biomass & Bagasse Project Developer. It is made clear that any electricity generated through use of any other fuel other than Biomass & Bagasse fuel shall not be allowed.

PROVISION: Clause 3.11 – Applicability of the Order (Sections 3.11 to closing provision)

TITLE: Applicability and Transitional Provisions – Fixed and Variable Tariff Components

SUMMARY:
Clause 3.11 establishes the applicability framework for tariff provisions to biomass and bagasse-based cogeneration projects commissioned during and prior to the control period (01 April 2023 to 31 March 2027). Projects commissioned during this control period under PPA signed during this period are eligible for: (1) Fixed component of tariff determined in this Order over the 25-year useful life of plant, levelized across the control period; and (2) Yearly variable component as specified in this tariff order. Projects commissioned during previous control periods with existing PPAs with GUVNL/DISCOM shall receive: (1) Updated yearly variable cost component determined in this tariff order; and (2) Continued levelized fixed cost component per their previous tariff orders and PPA terms. To promote renewable energy from biomass/bagasse cogeneration, if project developers approach distribution licensees, licensees may consider proposals and enter PPAs with provision that SCOD may have up to 36 months from PPA signing date. In such cases, the fixed tariff component receivable by generators shall be the lower of: (i) tariff applicable on PPA signing date, or (ii) tariff applicable on actual SCOD date. This promotes flexibility while ensuring cost efficiency for the distribution licensee.

KEY TOPICS: Applicability of Order, Fixed Tariff Component, Variable Tariff Component, Control Period, Useful Life (25 years), Scheduled Commercial Operation Date (SCOD), PPA Terms, Transitional Provisions, Promotional Measures, Previous Control Periods

TEXT:

**3.11 Applicability of the Order**

The Biomass power and Bagasse based co-generation project to be commissioned under the PPA signed during the control period of this Order shall be eligible to get the fixed component of tariff determined in this order over the useful life of 25 years of the plant and yearly variable component of tariff as specified in the tariff order of the Commission from time to time. The Biomass and Bagasse Cogeneration Projects Commissioned during the control period of previous tariff Orders of the Commission and executed PPA with the GUVNL/ DISCOM, if any, shall be eligible for above yearly variable cost component of tariff determined by the Commission in this tariff order while they shall continue to get levelized fixed cost component of tariff as per the previous tariff orders of the Commission read with provisions of PPA executed between the parties, as applicable.

Further, we are of the view that to promote the renewable energy available from biomass/bagasse co-generation projects, if any project developers approach the distribution licensees, the distribution licensees may consider the proposal and enter into the PPA with project developer with the provisions that the SCOD of such project may have available time up to 36 months from date of signing of the PPA and the fixed component of the tariff receivable by generators should be lower of applicable tariff in two control periods, i.e., (i) date of signing of the PPA and (ii) date of actual SCOD.
