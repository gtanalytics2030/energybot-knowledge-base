PROVISION: Definition – "Capacity Utilization Factor" (CUF)

TITLE: Annual Energy Output Measurement and Performance Metric

SUMMARY:
Capacity Utilization Factor (CUF) is calculated annually based on the total energy (in MWh) injected and metered at the Delivery Point, divided by the product of the Contracted Capacity (in MW) multiplied by 8760 hours in a year, expressed as a percentage. Formula: CUF = (X MWh / (Y MW*8760)) × 100%. The CUF is declared upfront in the PPA and must be maintained within defined minimum and maximum ranges (90% to 120% of declared CUF). DISCOM/GUVNL is not obligated to purchase power beyond the CUF limit in any contract year.

KEY TOPICS: CUF, Capacity Utilization Factor, Annual Energy Output, Energy Metering, Performance Metric, 8760 Hours, Delivery Point, Minimum CUF, Maximum CUF

TEXT:

"Capacity Utilization Factor CUF" shall be calculated based on the annual energy injected and metered at the Delivery Point. In any Contract year, if 'X' Mwh of energy has been metered at the Delivery Point for 'Y' MW project capacity, CUF = (X Mwh / (Y MW*8760)) x 100%. DISCOMs/GUVNL shall not be obliged to purchase power generated from the project beyond such CUF limit as mentioned in clause No 5.1 of PPA in a contract year.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Article 5.1 (CUF provisions, minimum 19%, ranges 90%–120%, penalties for shortfall, exclusion of excess generation), Article 1.1 Definition of "Contract Year"

TITLE: Automatic Dispatch Priority – Grid Security Exception

SUMMARY:
Must Run Status means that the RE project, except for grid security reasons, shall not be directed by DISCOM/SLDC to shut down, back down, or reduce generation due to variations in generation/consumption patterns, commercial parameters, merit order dispatch, or other charges or levies related to dispatch. This provides the RE producer with priority dispatch status, guaranteeing that its power will be accepted onto the grid. However, Must Run Status is excepted during Force Majeure events and Emergencies that threaten grid reliability or public safety.

KEY TOPICS: Must Run Status, Dispatch Priority, Must-Run Generation, Grid Security, Force Majeure Exception, Emergency Exception, Automatic Dispatch

TEXT:

TITLE: Continued Non-Operation or Non-Maintenance of Project

SUMMARY:
O&M (Operations and Maintenance) Default occurs when the RE power producer fails to operate and/or maintain the Project in accordance with Prudent Utility Practices for a continuous period of 90 days. KEY TOPICS: O&M Default, Operations and Maintenance, 90 Days, Prudent Utility Practices, Project Performance, Default Event, Termination Ground

"O&M Default" shall mean any default on the part of the RE power producer for a continuous period of ninety (90) days to (i) operate and /or (ii) maintain (in accordance with Prudent Utility Practices) the Project at all times.

PROVISION: Definition – "Performance Bank Guarantee" (PBG)

TITLE: Bank Guarantee for Project Commissioning Assurance

SUMMARY:
Performance Bank Guarantee (PBG) is an irrevocable unconditional bank guarantee submitted by the RE power producer to DISCOM/GUVNL, calculated at Rs 10 lakhs per MW of Contracted Capacity, in accordance with GERC Tariff Order No. 6 of 2024. The PBG is held as security to guarantee that the project will be commissioned at full contracted capacity by the Scheduled Commencement of Supply Date (SCSD). If the RE producer fails to commission on time, the PBG may be encashed on a per-day basis at Rs 5555 per MW per day (for delays up to 180 days), or forfeited entirely (for delays beyond 180 days). The PBG is returned within 45 days of successful commissioning, less any penalties for delay.

KEY TOPICS: Performance Bank Guarantee, PBG, Bank Guarantee, Rs 10 lakhs/MW, SCSD, Encashment, Commissioning Assurance, Rs 5555/MW/day, GERC Tariff Order No. 6 of 2024

TEXT:

"Performance Bank Guarantee" shall mean the irrevocable unconditional bank guarantee submitted by the RE power producer for an amount calculated at Rs 10 lakhs / MW in accordance with the GERC tariff order No 06 of 2024.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Article 3.3 (Liquidated damages for delay – encashment of PBG), Article 4.6 (Performance Bank Guarantee amount, validity, return), Article 9.3.1 (Forfeiture of PBG as damages upon default)

PROVISION: Definition – "SEA" (State Energy Account)

TITLE: Official Energy Certification and Settlement Record

SUMMARY:
SEA (State Energy Account) is the official energy account issued by the State Load Dispatch Centre (SLDC), Gujarat, and any amendments thereto. The SEA serves as the authoritative record for certifying the quantity of energy injected by the RE producer at the Delivery Point during each billing period.
