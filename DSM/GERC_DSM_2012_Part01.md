# GERC Demand Side Management Regulations, 2012 - PART 1

---

## CHUNK 1: PREAMBLE AND NOTIFICATION HEADER

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Notification

**HIERARCHY:** Preamble / Notification Header

**PROVISION:** Notification No. 1 of 2012

**TITLE:** Preamble and Powers of Constitution

**SUMMARY:** The Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission, exercising powers under clause (zp) of sub-section (2) of section 181 of the Electricity Act, 2003, hereby constitutes the Demand Side Management Regulations, 2012. The regulations are framed to govern demand-side management initiatives by Distribution Licensees in Gujarat. The notification establishes the regulatory framework and specifies the enabling legislation under which these regulations are made.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Regulatory authority: GERC
- Enabling legislation: Electricity Act, 2003
- Enabling clause: Section 181(2)(zp)
- Jurisdiction: Distribution Licensees in Gujarat
- Regulatory scope: Demand Side Management

**TEXT:**

The Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

Notification No. 1 of 2012

Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission, in exercise of the powers conferred by clause (zp) of sub-section (2) of section 181 of the Electricity Act, 2003 (36 of 2003), hereby makes the following regulations, namely, the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Electricity Act, 2003 (36 of 2003) - Section 181, clause (zp)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Energy Conservation Act, 2001 (mentioned in Regulation 2.1.iii)

## CHUNK 2: REGULATION 1 - SHORT TITLE, EXTENT AND COMMENCEMENT

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 1: General → Regulation 1

**PROVISION:** Regulation 1 (Short Title, Extent and Commencement)

**TITLE:** Short Title, Extent and Commencement

**SUMMARY:** Regulation 1 establishes the formal nomenclature of these regulations as the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012. The regulations apply to all Distribution Licensees operating in the State of Gujarat within their respective areas of supply. The regulations come into force on the date of their notification in the Official Gazette, establishing the effective date of their legal applicability.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Formal name of regulations
- Geographical extent: State of Gujarat
- Applicability: Distribution Licensees
- Commencement: Date of notification in Official Gazette
- Spatial scope: Area of supply of each Distribution Licensee

**TEXT:**

**1. Short Title, Extent and Commencement**

1.1. These Regulations may be called the "Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012".

1.2. These Regulations shall be applicable to the Distribution Licensees in the State of Gujarat in their respective areas of supply.

1.3. These Regulations shall come into force from the date of its notification in the Official Gazette.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Official Gazette notification requirement

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- All subsequent chapters and regulations of these same regulations
- Distribution Licensees' license conditions (implied reference to distribution agreements)
