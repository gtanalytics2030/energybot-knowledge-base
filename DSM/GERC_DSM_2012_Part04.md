## CHUNK 14: REGULATION 3 - DSM OBJECTIVES

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 2: DSM Objectives, Targets and Guidelines → Regulation 3

**PROVISION:** Regulation 3 (DSM Objectives)

**TITLE:** DSM Objectives

**SUMMARY:** The Commission shall formulate DSM objectives as a guiding framework for the advancement and implementation of cost-effective DSM initiatives in Gujarat. These objectives may include power shortage mitigation, seasonal peak reduction, cost-effective energy savings, lowering electricity costs, and reducing greenhouse gas emissions. When formulating objectives, the Commission may consider National DSM Objectives and Plans developed by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency, ensuring consistency with national energy conservation plans and guidelines.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Commission's responsibility for setting objectives
- Cost effectiveness requirement
- Power shortage mitigation objective
- Seasonal peak reduction objective
- Energy savings objective
- Electricity cost reduction objective
- Emissions reduction objective
- National DSM alignment
- Bureau consultation mechanism
- Consistency with national objectives

**TEXT:**

**3. DSM Objectives**

3.1. The Commission shall formulate DSM objectives that shall be considered in the advancement and implementation of cost effective DSM initiatives in the State. The objectives may include power shortage mitigation, seasonal peak reduction, cost effective energy savings, lowering the cost of electricity, reduction in emissions of greenhouse gases etc.

3.2. While formulating the DSM Objectives, the Commission may take into consideration the National DSM Objectives / Plans formulated by Bureau, if any.

3.3. The DSM objectives may be consistent with the DSM objectives set out by Bureau as a part of Energy Conservation Plan for the country.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**

## CHUNK 15: REGULATION 4 - ASSESSMENT OF TECHNICAL POTENTIAL FOR DSM

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 2: DSM Objectives, Targets and Guidelines → Regulation 4

**PROVISION:** Regulation 4 (Assessment of Technical Potential for DSM)

**TITLE:** Assessment of Technical Potential for DSM

**SUMMARY:** Distribution Licensees in Gujarat shall conduct an assessment of the technical potential for DSM in their licensed area once every Multi-Year Tariff (MYT) Control Period, and this assessment should be initiated one year before the start of each MYT Control Period. The first technical potential assessment must be completed within twelve months of these Regulations coming into force. Distribution Licensees must be guided by the methodology developed by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency when conducting such assessments to ensure consistency and quality across the state.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Technical potential assessment requirement
- Assessment frequency: Per MYT Control Period
- Timing: One year before MYT Control Period starts
- First assessment: Within 12 months of notification
- Distribution Licensee responsibility
- Bureau methodology guidance requirement
- License area assessment scope
- Standardized methodology application

**TEXT:**

**4. Assessment of technical potential for DSM**

4.1. The Distribution Licensees in the State shall carry out assessment of potential for DSM in their license area one year before the start of every MYT Control Period. Notwithstanding the above provision, the first assessment of technical potential for DSM shall be carried out within twelve months of the notification of these Regulations.

4.2. The Distribution Licensee shall be guided by methodology developed by Bureau while assessing of technical potential for DSM.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**

## CHUNK 16: REGULATION 5 - DSM TARGETS

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 2: DSM Objectives, Targets and Guidelines → Regulation 5

**PROVISION:** Regulation 5 (DSM Targets)

**TITLE:** DSM Targets

**SUMMARY:** The Commission shall establish specific DSM targets for each Distribution Licensee operating in Gujarat. When setting these targets, the Commission will consider factors such as consumer mix composition and load profile characteristics of each licensee's area. DSM targets may be expressed in multiple formats: as percentage reductions in load growth, as absolute savings in kilowatts (kW) or kilowatt-hours (kWh), or as savings expressed as a percentage of total resources required to meet the load demand. Target establishment shall be informed by the technical potential assessments completed by each Distribution Licensee.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Commission authority to set targets
- Per-licensee target requirement
- Consumer mix consideration
- Load profile consideration
- Load growth reduction targets
- Absolute savings targets (kW, kWh)
- Percentage-based targets (% of total resources)
- Technical potential basis
- Target diversity/options

**TEXT:**

**5. DSM Targets**

5.1. The Commission shall establish DSM targets for each Distribution Licensee in the State.

5.2. While setting DSM target for the Distribution Licensee, the Commission will give due consideration to factors such as consumer mix, load profile, etc.

5.3. Examples of DSM targets may include the following:
 - Percentage reductions in load growth;
 - Savings in kW, kWh;
 - Savings as a percent of total resources to meet load;

5.4 While establishing the targets, the Commission shall consider the technical potential in the State as assessed

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Regulation 5.1, 5.2, 5.3, 5.4

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**

- Regulation 4.1 (First assessment within 12 months of notification)

## CHUNK 17: REGULATION 6 - GUIDELINES ON DSM PROCESS

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 2: DSM Objectives, Targets and Guidelines → Regulation 6

**PROVISION:** Regulation 6 (Guidelines on DSM Process)

**TITLE:** Guidelines on DSM Process

**SUMMARY:** The Commission shall issue comprehensive guidelines to provide direction and requirements for Distribution Licensees in executing key DSM activities. These guidelines shall cover: (i) Load and Market Research; (ii) Implementation of DSM programmes; (iii) Cost Effectiveness Assessment of DSM programmes; (iv) Monitoring and Reporting of DSM Plans and programmes; (v) Eligibility criteria for DSM programmes; (vi) Methodology for setting DSM targets and funding levels; and (vii) Database development framework. The Commission shall issue such guidelines from time to time, and their issuance is not a prerequisite for Distribution Licensees to prepare and submit their first DSM plan.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Commission guideline-issuing authority
- Load and market research guidelines
- Programme implementation guidelines
- Cost effectiveness assessment guidelines
- Monitoring and reporting guidelines
- Eligibility criteria guidelines
- DSM targets and funding methodology guidelines
- Database development guidelines
- Progressive guideline issuance
- First plan not conditional on guidelines

**TEXT:**

**6. Guidelines on DSM process**

6.1. The Commission shall issue guidelines to guide the Distribution Licensee in execution of the following activities

i. Load and Market Research
ii. Implementation of DSM programmes
iii. Cost Effectiveness Assessment of DSM programmes
iv. Monitoring and Reporting of DSM Plans and programmes
v. Eligibility criteria for DSM programmes
vi. Methodology for setting DSM targets and funding levels
vii. Database development framework

6.2. The Commission shall issue guidelines on these matters from time to time

6.3. Issuance of such guidelines shall not be a pre-requisite for preparation and submission of the first DSM plan by the Distribution Licensee
