## CHUNK 25: REGULATION 14 - MECHANISM FOR COST RECOVERY

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 4: DSM Process → Regulation 14

**PROVISION:** Regulation 14 (Mechanism for Cost Recovery)

**TITLE:** Mechanism for Cost Recovery

**SUMMARY:** Distribution Licensees shall identify net incremental costs associated with planning, design, and implementation of DSM programmes. Licensees may propose methodologies for recovering these net incremental costs through tariff or other mechanisms. To qualify for cost recovery, each programme must meet three conditions: (1) be approved prior to implementation, (2) be implemented in accordance with the approved programme plan, and (3) be implemented cost effectively. Distribution Licensees must provide full cooperation to the Commission or Commission-designated third parties in conducting Evaluation, Measurement and Verification (EMV) of DSM programmes. The Commission may direct Distribution Licensees to undertake DSM programmes that are not cost-effective but offer significant societal benefits; in such cases, the Commission shall make available resources to fund the projects.

**TEXT:**

**14. Mechanism for Cost Recovery**

14.1. Distribution Licensee shall identify the net incremental costs, if any, associated with planning, design and implementation of programmes

14.2. Distribution Licensee may propose methodology for recovery of net incremental costs through tariff or any other mechanism

14.3. In order to qualify for cost recovery, each program must be

i. Approved prior to implementation

ii. Implemented in accordance with the approved program plan and

iii. Implemented cost effectively

14.4. Distribution Licensee shall provide all necessary assistance to the Commission, or third party assigned by the Commission in undertaking Evaluation Measurement and Verification of DSM programmes implemented by it.

14.5. The Commission may direct the Distribution Licensee to undertake DSM programmes that may not be cost effective but is highly beneficial to the society. The Commission will make available resources for such project.

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:**
Regulation 14 establishes an important balance: (1) Cost recovery is available for approved programmes implemented as planned and cost-effectively, creating incentive for discipline and quality execution. (2) The Commission retains authority to direct programmes for social benefit even if not cost-effective and will fund them, ensuring DSM addresses broader policy objectives beyond immediate cost efficiency. This dual mechanism allows both market-driven and policy-driven DSM initiatives.

## CHUNK 26: REGULATION 15 - MONITORING AND REPORTING OF DSM PROGRAMMES

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 4: DSM Process → Regulation 15

**PROVISION:** Regulation 15 (Monitoring and Reporting of DSM Programmes)

**TITLE:** Monitoring and Reporting of DSM Programmes

**SUMMARY:** Distribution Licensees shall prepare and implement monitoring and reporting plans for DSM programmes in accordance with Commission-issued Guidelines on Monitoring and Reporting, or as approved by the Commission during the DSM programme approval stage. The monitoring and reporting framework enables tracking of programme progress, implementation status, costs, consumer participation, and achievement against targets. This ongoing visibility and accountability mechanism is essential for regulatory oversight and performance assessment of DSM initiatives throughout their implementation period.

## CHUNK 27: REGULATION 16 - EVALUATION, MEASUREMENT AND VERIFICATION (EMV) OF DSM PROGRAMME

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 4: DSM Process → Regulation 16

**PROVISION:** Regulation 16 (Evaluation, Measurement and Verification (EMV) of DSM Programme)

**TITLE:** Evaluation, Measurement and Verification (EMV) of DSM Programme

**SUMMARY:** Distribution Licensees shall prepare comprehensive evaluation, measurement and verification (EMV) plans for DSM programmes in accordance with Commission-issued Guidelines on Evaluation, Measurement and Verification, or as provided in regulations. EMV is critical for verifying actual energy/demand savings achieved and validating programme performance. Third-party EMV may be conducted directly by the Commission or through a third party designated by the Commission. When engaging third-party agencies for EMV, the Commission shall ensure the agency has no conflicting engagements that could compromise consumer interests in the State. Distribution Licensees must provide all necessary information and data to the Commission or designated third parties to enable measurement and verification of DSM programme savings.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- EMV plan preparation requirement

- Third-party designation authority
- Conflict-of-interest safeguards
- Consumer interest protection
- Data provision requirement
- Information availability requirement
- Accountability and transparency
- Savings validation

**TEXT:**

**16. Evaluation, Measurement and Verification (EMV) of DSM Programme**

16.1. Distribution Licensee shall prepare plan for evaluation, measurement and verification of savings from DSM programmes as per Guidelines on Evaluation, Measurement and Verification issued by the Commission from time to time.

16.2. Third party Evaluation, Measurement and Verification of DSM programmes may be undertaken by the Commission or a third party assigned by the Commission. While engaging the agency, the Commission shall ensure that the Agency is not undertaking any other engagement that could conflict with the interests of the consumers in the State.

16.3. Distribution Licensee shall make available necessary information/data to the Commission or a third party assigned by the Commission to measure and verify the savings from DSM programmes.

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:**
EMV is the accountability mechanism that transforms estimated savings into verified results. Regulation 16 creates a three-level verification structure: (1) Licensees self-verify through EMV plans, (2) Commission may conduct independent verification, (3) Third parties may verify with conflict-of-interest safeguards ensuring consumer protection. This multiple verification layer ensures the integrity of DSM savings reporting and builds confidence in programme outcomes.
