## CHUNK 28: REGULATION 17 - REPORTS ON PROGRESS OF DSM PLAN AND EXPENSES INCURRED

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 5: DSM Plan and Programme Completion Report → Regulation 17

**PROVISION:** Regulation 17 (Reports on Progress of DSM Plan and Expenses Incurred on Implementation of DSM Plan)

**TITLE:** Six-Monthly Progress Reports on DSM Plan Implementation

**SUMMARY:** Distribution Licensees shall submit reports to the Commission every six months detailing the progress of their DSM Plan implementation and the expenses incurred in implementing the DSM Plan. These semi-annual reports provide the Commission with ongoing visibility into programme execution, cost tracking, and achievement against planned targets. The six-monthly reporting cycle enables timely identification of implementation issues, cost overruns, and necessary corrective actions.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Six-monthly reporting requirement
- Progress reporting on DSM Plan
- Expense tracking and reporting
- Implementation status visibility
- Cost monitoring and accountability
- Commission oversight
- Semi-annual submission cycle
- Regulatory compliance

**TEXT:**

**17. Reports on progress of DSM Plan and expenses incurred on implementation of DSM Plan shall be submitted by the Distribution Licensee every six months.**

## CHUNK 29: REGULATION 18 - PROGRAMME COMPLETION REPORT

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 5: DSM Plan and Programme Completion Report → Regulation 18

**PROVISION:** Regulation 18 (Programme Completion Report)

**TITLE:** Programme Completion Report Submission

**SUMMARY:** Within three months of completing any DSM programme, the Distribution Licensee shall prepare and submit a detailed Programme Completion Report to the Commission. This report marks the formal closure of programme implementation and provides a comprehensive assessment of programme outcomes. The three-month submission deadline ensures timely reporting while allowing time for final data compilation and analysis. The Programme Completion Report serves as the final accountability document for programme execution.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Programme completion documentation requirement
- Three-month submission deadline (from completion)
- Detailed programme assessment
- Commission submission requirement
- Programme closure documentation
- Final accountability reporting
- Timing coordination

**TEXT:**

**18. The Distribution Licensee will prepare and submit a detailed Program Completion Report and submit the same to the Commission within three month of completion of such programme.**

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Regulation 18
- Three-month submission timeline

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Regulation 13 (Implementation of DSM programmes)
- Regulation 19 (Programme Completion Report content requirements)

## CHUNK 30: REGULATION 19 - CONTENT OF PROGRAMME COMPLETION REPORT

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 5: DSM Plan and Programme Completion Report → Regulation 19

**PROVISION:** Regulation 19 (Content of Programme Completion Report)

**TITLE:** Programme Completion Report Content and Requirements

**SUMMARY:** The Programme Completion Report shall comprehensively cover programme expenses, achievements, outcomes and outputs, constraints and difficulties faced during implementation, conclusions drawn from the experience, recommendations for future improvements, and lessons learned that inform subsequent programme design and implementation. This comprehensive reporting framework ensures that each DSM programme contributes not only direct energy savings but also institutional learning that improves programme effectiveness over time. The lessons-learned component supports continuous improvement in DSM programme design and execution.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Programme completion report requirements
- Expenses reporting and financial accountability
- Achievements and outcomes documentation
- Outputs reporting
- Constraints and difficulties identification
- Implementation challenges analysis
- Conclusions from programme experience
- Recommendations for improvement
- Lessons learned documentation
- Continuous improvement mechanism
- Knowledge capture and transfer
- Future programme enhancement

**TEXT:**

**19. The Report shall cover the programme expenses, achievements, outcomes and outputs, constraints and difficulties faced, conclusions, recommendations, lessons learned and way forward.**

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Regulation 19

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Regulation 17 (Six-monthly progress reports)
- Regulation 18 (Programme Completion Report submission)
- Regulation 12 (Approved programme document baseline)

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:**
Regulations 17-19 create a comprehensive reporting timeline: (1) Six-monthly progress reports track implementation in real-time, (2) Programme Completion Reports provide final accountability three months after programme closure, (3) Content requirements (Regulation 19) mandate comprehensive documentation including lessons learned. This structure ensures both accountability and institutional learning, with each programme contributing to improved future DSM implementation.
