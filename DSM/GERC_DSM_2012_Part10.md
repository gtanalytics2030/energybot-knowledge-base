## CHUNK 31: REGULATION 20 - INCENTIVES

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 6: Incentives → Regulation 20

**PROVISION:** Regulation 20 (Incentives)

**TITLE:** Commission Authority to Provide Incentives for DSM Achievement

**SUMMARY:** The Commission is authorized to provide incentives to Distribution Licensees for achieving or exceeding the DSM Objectives identified in Regulation 3. These incentives serve as a financial reward mechanism to encourage licensees to aggressively pursue DSM initiatives and exceed minimum compliance targets. The incentive framework provides positive reinforcement for DSM performance and cost-effective programme implementation. The nature, quantum, and conditions of incentives shall be determined by the Commission through its regulatory discretion and may be specified in tariff orders or DSM guidelines.

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:**
Regulation 20 establishes a performance incentive mechanism complementary to cost recovery (Regulation 14). While cost recovery reimburses reasonable programme costs, incentives reward superior performance and target surpassance. This dual mechanism creates incentives for both efficient (cost-effective) and ambitious (exceeding targets) DSM execution. Incentive details are left to Commission discretion in tariff orders, providing flexibility to adjust incentive structures based on market conditions and policy priorities.
