## PROVISION TITLE
**Objectives for Demand Side Management for the State of Gujarat**

---

## SUMMARY
The GERC established seven strategic objectives for Demand Side Management (DSM) to guide electricity demand management in Gujarat. These objectives aim to control and reduce demand through consumer behavior modification, support licensee capacity planning, lower consumer costs, reduce environmental impact, align with national DSM schemes, and induce lasting market behavioral changes toward energy efficiency adoption. The framework operates as complementary to supply-side strategies to optimize resource utilization across the electricity supply chain.

## FULL ORIGINAL TEXT

**NOTIFICATION**

Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission

**DATE:** 06.08.2012

In pursuance to the Provisions made under GERC (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012 GERC hereby formulate the Objectives for Demand Side Management for the State of Gujarat as under:

**Objectives for Demand Side Management:**

1. To control, reduce and influence electricity demand by implementation of suitable policies and measures;

2. To encourage consumers to amend their electricity consumption pattern both with respect to timing and level of electricity demand for efficient use of energy;

3. To complement supply side strategies to help the licensees to avoid or postpone costly capacity (generation, transmission & distribution network) additions by slowing demand growth;

4. To lower the overall cost of electricity to the consumers by economical and efficient use of resources;

5. To reduce the environmental damage by reducing the emission of green house gases;

6. To supplement national level efforts for implementation of various DSM schemes set out by the Bureau;

7. Strategic efforts to induce lasting structural or behavioral changes in the market that result in increased adoption of energy-efficient technologies, services, and practices.

**Signatory:**
Dr. Ketan Shukla
SECRETARY
Ahmedabad
Date: 6th August, 2012
