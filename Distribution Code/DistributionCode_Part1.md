## CHUNK 1.1: OVERVIEW AND APPLICABILITY

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Distribution Code

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulatory Code

**HIERARCHY:** Section 1 / Clause 1.1

**PROVISION:** Section 1.1 - Overview

**TITLE:** Overview and Statutory Basis of Distribution Code

**SUMMARY:** The Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission formulated the Distribution Code under Section 42(b) of the Gujarat Electricity Industry (Reorganisation and Regulation) Act 2003 and Sections 86(c), (e), and (i) of the Electricity Act 2003. The Code is applicable to the state of Gujarat and regulates the technical aspects of electricity supply, including quality, continuity, and reliability. Distribution licensees and users connected to or seeking connection to the distribution system must comply with this Code in addition to all other applicable laws, codes, standards, and regulations.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Legislative authority and basis
- Geographic applicability (Gujarat)
- Technical and regulatory scope
- Compliance obligations for licensees and users
- Quality, continuity, and reliability of supply

**TEXT:**

In order to perform the task and the requirements as stipulated in Section 42 (b) of Gujarat Act No. 24 of 2003 viz. Gujarat Electricity Industry (Reorganisation and Regulation) Act 2003, and section 86(c), (e) and (i) of the Electricity Act, 2003 (Central Act 36 of 2003), the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission has formulated this "GUJARAT ELECTRICITY DISTRIBUTION CODE" hereinafter called Distribution Code. This Distribution Code is applicable for the state of Gujarat only.

**Scope of Distribution Code:**

(a) The Distribution Code deals with technical aspects of the supply of electricity, which have impact on the quality, continuity and reliability of service by licensees and the use of the Licensee's Distribution System for the distribution of electricity. It specifies the rights and obligations of the Distribution Licensee and the Users in system planning and operation.

(b) The Distribution Code is not exhaustive as to the requirements to be complied with by the Distribution Licensee and the Users connected or seeking connection to the Licensee's Distribution System. The Distribution Licensee and all Users/Consumers must also comply with the requirement as laid down in various codes, standard and regulations under relevant laws in force.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Section 42(b), Gujarat Electricity Industry (Reorganisation and Regulation) Act 2003
- Sections 86(c), (e), (i), Electricity Act 2003
- Electricity Act 2003 (general compliance)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** None in this clause, but this clause is foundational to understanding the entire Distribution Code.

## CHUNK 1.2: STRUCTURE OF THE DISTRIBUTION CODE

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Distribution Code

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulatory Code

**HIERARCHY:** Section 1 / Clause 1.2

**PROVISION:** Section 1.2 - Structure of the Distribution Code

**TITLE:** Organizational Structure and Content of Nine Sections

**SUMMARY:** The Distribution Code comprises nine main sections, each addressing specific aspects of distribution system management: Introduction; Definitions; Management of Distribution Code; Distribution Planning Code; Connectivity Conditions; Operation Code; Metering and Protection Code; Cross Boundary Safety Code; and Incident Reporting Code. Additionally, the Code includes Attachment-1 (Distribution System Planning and Security Standards) and Annexures 1 and 2 covering load data and system data requirements respectively. Each section specifies distinct responsibilities, procedures, and technical criteria for distribution licensees and system users.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Nine-section structure of the Code
- Distribution Planning Code
- Operation Code
- Metering and Protection Code
- Connectivity Conditions
- Cross Boundary Safety Code
- Incident Reporting Code
- Attachment and Annexure provisions

**TEXT:**

This Distribution Code consists of the following 9 Sections:

1. **Section-1: Introduction** – This section outlines the broad features of the Distribution Code.

2. **Section-2: Definitions** - The various terms used in the Distribution Code are defined in this section.

3. **Section-3: Management of Distribution Code** - The Distribution Code is a live document and has to be periodically reviewed by a competent panel as and when required in the light of experience gained. This section formulates the procedures for such review.

4. **Section-4: Distribution Planning Code** – This section specifies the technical and design criteria as well as the procedures to be followed by the Distribution Licensee for planning and development of the Distribution System.

5. **Section-5: Connectivity Conditions** – This section specifies minimum technical criteria and standards to be complied with by any User connected or seeking connection with a Licensee's Distribution System. This section also specifies the minimum technical, design and operational criteria with which the Licensee concerned shall comply in relation to the part of the Distribution System at the connection site with Users.

6. **Section-6: Operation Code** – This section specifies the procedures and practices to be followed for a safe and efficient operation of the Distribution System by the Distribution Licensee of the Distribution System and by the Users of their electrical plant and lines which are connected to the Licensee's Distribution System. This shall also apply to any electrical interface between two Distribution Licensees for a safe and efficient operation of the interface.

7. **Section-7: Metering and Protection Code** – This section specifies the minimum requirements of metering for measurement of electrical energy (and related parameters) flowing in Distribution System and User's system especially across the boundary between the Distribution System and User's System. The section also specifies the minimum requirement of protection levels.

8. **Section-8: Cross Boundary Safety Code** – This section specifies safety procedures and precautions for ensuring that work in a Distribution Licensee's system or in a User's system is carried out safely in situations where it is possible to achieve safety only by operations (isolation and earthing) in other's system across the Connection Point/ Interface Point.

9. **Section-9: Incident Reporting Code** – This section specifies guidelines for incident reporting.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** All subsequent sections (Section 2 through Section 9) and Attachment-1

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** This clause provides the structural overview that anchors the entire Code.

## CHUNK 1.3: IMPLEMENTATION AND OPERATION OF THE DISTRIBUTION CODE

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Distribution Code

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulatory Code

**HIERARCHY:** Section 1 / Clause 1.3

**PROVISION:** Section 1.3 - Implementation and Operation of the Distribution Code

**TITLE:** Compliance Obligations for Licensees and Users

**SUMMARY:** The Distribution Code is applicable to all Distribution Licensees in Gujarat, who bear responsibility for implementing it within their respective areas of supply. Users must comply with all provisions. Users experiencing difficulties in compliance must promptly inform the Distribution Licensee and/or GERC. Continued non-compliance without reasonable grounds constitutes a deviation under the Electricity Act 2003 and may result in disconnection. The Distribution Licensee's non-compliance may attract consequences under the Acts or License, but the Licensee may submit a compliance plan to GERC for approval of exemptions where compliance is not feasible.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Applicability to all Distribution Licensees
- User compliance obligations
- Reporting difficulties in compliance
- Non-compliance consequences (deviation, disconnection)
- Licensee compliance plans and exemption procedures
- GERC authority to grant exemptions

**TEXT:**

**IMPLEMENTATION AND OPERATION OF THE DISTRIBUTION CODE:**

1. The Distribution Code is applicable to all the Licensees in the state of Gujarat. The Licensee's shall be responsible for its implementation within their respective Area of Supply. Users shall comply the provision of this code.

2. If any User has any difficulty in complying with any of the provisions of the Distribution Code, he shall immediately, without delay, inform to the Distribution Licensee and/ or GERC, as the case may be.

3. Any continued non compliance, without reasonable grounds shall constitute a deviation under the Act, and may lead to disconnection of the User's plant or Apparatus in line with the provisions of the Electricity Act, 2003 from the Licensee's Distribution System. The responsibility for the consequences of disconnection including payment of damages and others rests with the User who consistently violates the Distribution Code.

4. Non compliance with any provision of the Distribution Code by the Distribution Licensee shall attract the consequences as provided in the Acts or in the License. However, in the event of non-compliance with the Distribution Code, the Distribution Licensee shall prepare and submit to GERC a plan of action for compliance with the Distribution Code. GERC may exempt the Licensee from compliance of any provisions for a particular period, if it is found that the compliance is not feasible for such period, considering the resources available and the circumstances prevailing.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Electricity Act 2003 (deviation and disconnection provisions)
- Area of Supply (defined in Section 2)
- Distribution Code (entire document)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Section 1.1 (applicability)
- Section 2 (definitions of Licensee, User, Area of Supply)
- Electricity Act 2003 (legal basis for enforcement)

## CHUNK 1.4: LIMITATIONS OF THE DISTRIBUTION CODE

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Distribution Code

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulatory Code

**HIERARCHY:** Section 1 / Clause 1.4

**PROVISION:** Section 1.4 - Limitations of the Distribution Code

**TITLE:** Scope Limitations and Emergency Authority of Distribution Licensee

**SUMMARY:** The Distribution Code imposes obligations no greater than those in the Electricity Act 2003. The Code covers procedures for day-to-day technical management under normal and abnormal conditions but cannot anticipate all possible operating conditions. In unforeseen circumstances, the Distribution Licensee may act decisively and with due expedition to discharge its obligations under the License. Users must provide reasonable cooperation and assistance as required. All such emergency actions must be referred for ratification at the next Panel meeting.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Non-expansion of statutory obligations
- Unforeseen operating conditions
- Licensee emergency authority
- User cooperation obligations
- Ratification requirement for emergency actions
- Limitations and practical boundaries

**TEXT:**

**LIMITATIONS OF THE DISTRIBUTION CODE:**

Nothing contained in this Code should be interpreted as imposing obligations / duties on Consumers / Distribution Licensees greater or more onerous than those mentioned in the Electricity Act, 2003 under relevant clauses.

The Distribution Code contains procedures for the management of day to day technical situations in the Distribution System, taking into account a wide range of operational conditions likely to be encountered under both normal and abnormal conditions. The Distribution Code cannot foresee all the possible operating conditions. Users must therefore understand and accept that the Distribution Licensee, in such unforeseen circumstances, may be required to act decisively and with due expedition to discharge his obligations under the Licensee. Users shall provide such reasonable co-operation and assistance as the Distribution Licensee may require in such circumstances. The concerned Distribution Licensee shall however refer all such cases for ratification in the next meeting of the Panel.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Electricity Act 2003 (relevant clauses defining base obligations)
- Distribution Code (all sections)
- Panel (Distribution Code Review Panel per Section 3)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Section 1.1 (scope)
- Section 3 (Distribution Code Review Panel and ratification process)
- Electricity Act 2003 (statutory obligations baseline)

## CHUNK 1.5: CONFIDENTIALITY

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Distribution Code

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulatory Code

**HIERARCHY:** Section 1 / Clause 1.5

**PROVISION:** Section 1.5 - Confidentiality

**TITLE:** Confidentiality of User Information

**SUMMARY:** Under the Distribution Code, the Distribution Licensee receives business-related information from Users. The Licensee shall not disclose such information to any other person without the prior written consent of the informant, except as required by the Distribution Code or when mandated by Central/State government departments or competent authorities. This provision protects the confidentiality of commercially sensitive user data while permitting mandatory governmental disclosures.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Confidentiality of user business information
- Prohibition on unauthorized disclosure
- Written consent requirement
- Exceptions: Code requirements, government mandates
- Central and State government authority
- Competent authority disclosures

**TEXT:**

**CONFIDENTIALITY**

Under the terms of Distribution Code, the Distribution Licensee will receive information from Users relating to their business. The Distribution Licensee shall not, other than as required by Distribution Code, disclose such information to any other person without the prior written consent of such informant, unless required by Central/State government departments or any authority.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Distribution Code (entire document, where disclosure is required)
- Central and State government legislation (mandatory disclosure requirements)

## CHUNK 1.6: PROCEDURES TO SETTLE DISPUTES

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Distribution Code

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulatory Code

**HIERARCHY:** Section 1 / Clause 1.6

**PROVISION:** Section 1.6 - Procedures to Settle Disputes

**TITLE:** Dispute Resolution Mechanism

**SUMMARY:** Any dispute between a User and the Distribution Licensee regarding the interpretation of Distribution Code regulations shall be referred to the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (GERC). GERC's decision on such disputes is final and binding on both parties. This provides a clear, single-authority dispute resolution mechanism outside of contractual arbitration or litigation.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Disputes between Users and Distribution Licensees
- Code interpretation disputes
- GERC authority and jurisdiction
- Final and binding decisions
- Dispute resolution mechanism
- Regulatory adjudication

**TEXT:**

**PROCEDURES TO SETTLE DISPUTES:**

In the event of any dispute regarding interpretation of any regulations provided in the Distribution Code between any User and the Distribution Licensee, the matter shall be referred to Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission. GERC's decision shall be final and binding on both the parties.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Distribution Code (all regulatory provisions)
- GERC (powers and authority)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**

## CHUNK 1.7: DIRECTIVES

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Distribution Code

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulatory Code

**HIERARCHY:** Section 1 / Clause 1.7

**PROVISION:** Section 1.7 - Directives

**TITLE:** State Government Policy Directives and Implementation

**SUMMARY:** Under the Electricity Act 2003 and related legislation, the State Government may issue policy directives on certain matters. The Distribution Licensee must promptly inform GERC and all Users of such directives. Users, subject to the relevant sections of the Acts, shall comply with State Government directives. This provision ensures hierarchical coordination between State Government policy, the Regulatory Commission, and system participants.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- State Government policy directives
- Directive applicability under the Acts
- Notification obligations
- Distribution Licensee communication responsibility
- User compliance with directives
- Statutory limitations on directive scope

**TEXT:**

**DIRECTIVES**

Under the provisions of the Acts, the State Government may issue policy directives in certain matters. The Distribution Licensee shall promptly inform GERC and all Users of the requirement of such directives. The Users, subject to the relevant section of the Acts, shall comply with such directive.
