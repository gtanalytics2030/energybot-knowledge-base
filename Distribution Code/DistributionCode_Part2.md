## CHUNK 2.0: DEFINITIONS SECTION HEADER AND FRAMEWORK

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Distribution Code

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulatory Code

**HIERARCHY:** Section 2 / Preamble

**PROVISION:** Section 2 - Definitions (Introductory Language)

**TITLE:** Framework for Definitions and Interpretation

**SUMMARY:** Section 2 establishes the interpretive framework for all terms used in the Distribution Code. Terms are defined as they appear unless the context otherwise requires. If a term is defined in the Distribution Code, it bears that meaning throughout. If defined in the Electricity Act 2003 or related State/Central legislation applicable to electricity, the statutory meaning applies. For terms not defined in these sources but used in the electricity industry, the industry standard meaning applies. The Interpretation clause provides rules for singular/plural usage, "include/including" language, references to the Code, headings, and statutory cross-references.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Interpretive framework
- Definition sources hierarchy
- Applicability of Act definitions
- Industry standards applicability
- Rules of interpretation
- Statutory cross-references

**TEXT:**

In the Distribution Code the following words and expressions shall, unless the subject matter or context otherwise requires or is inconsistent therewith, bear the following meanings:

[Framework clause establishing that all subsequent definitions apply throughout the Code and that statutory cross-references are incorporated by reference.]

Words and expressions used and not defined in this Code but defined in the Acts shall have the meanings assigned to them in the said Acts. Expressions used herein but not specifically defined in this Code or in the said Acts but defined under any law passed by a competent legislature and applicable to the electricity industry in the state shall have the meaning assigned to them in such law. Subject to the above, expressions used herein but not specifically defined in this Code or in the Acts or any law passed by a competent legislature shall have the meaning as is generally assigned in the electricity industry.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Electricity Act 2003 (definitions and terms)
- Gujarat Electricity Industry (Reorganisation and Regulation) Act 2003
- Applicable State and Central legislation on electricity

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- All defined terms in this Section 2
- All sections using defined terms throughout the Code

## CHUNK 2.6: DEFINITION - CAPTIVE POWER PLANT

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Distribution Code

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulatory Code

**HIERARCHY:** Section 2 / Definition / Captive Power Plant

**PROVISION:** Section 2 - Definition of "Captive Power Plant"

**TITLE:** Definition: Captive Power Plant (CPP)

**SUMMARY:** "Captive Power Plant" (CPP) means a power generating station set up by any person to generate electricity for their own use, and includes power plants set up by cooperative societies or associations of persons to generate electricity primarily for the use of members of such societies or associations. CPPs are distinguished from commercial generating units by their primary purpose of self-supply.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Self-generation of electricity
- Captive generating capacity
- Cooperative generating units
- Association generating units
- Own-use generation
- Regulatory classification

**TEXT:**

**Captive Power Plant** - A Power Plant set up by any person to generate electricity for his use and includes a power plant set up by any co-operative society or association of persons for generating electricity primarily for use of members of such co-operative society or association.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**

## CHUNK 2.10: DEFINITION - CONNECTED LOAD

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Distribution Code

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulatory Code

**HIERARCHY:** Section 2 / Definition / Connected Load

**PROVISION:** Section 2 - Definition of "Connected Load"

**TITLE:** Definition: Connected Load

**SUMMARY:** "Connected Load" is the aggregate of manufacturer's ratings of all connected apparatus, including portable apparatus, in a Consumer's premises for which the Consumer has made a declaration for taking supply. Connected Load is expressed in KW or KVA, with conversions to KW from KVA (using 0.9 power factor) and from HP (multiplying by 0.746) as specified. Connected Load is a critical parameter for tariff classification and system planning.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Aggregate installed capacity
- Manufacturer ratings
- KW/KVA conversion
- HP conversion (0.746 factor)
- Power factor (0.9 standard)
- Tariff classification basis
- Consumer declaration

**TEXT:**

**Connected Load** - Aggregate of manufacturer's rating of all the connected Apparatus, including portable Apparatus, in the Consumer's Premises. It includes all Apparatus for which the Consumer has made declaration, for taking supply. This shall be expressed in KW or KVA. If rating are in KVA, the same may be converted to KW by multiplying the KVA with a Power Factor of 0.9. If the same or any other Apparatus is rated by the manufacturer in HP, the HP rating shall be converted in KW by multiplying it by 0.746.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Apparatus (definition 2.X)
- Consumer (definition 2.X)

- Power Factor (definition 2.X)
