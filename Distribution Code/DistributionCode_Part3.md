## CHUNK 2.13: DEFINITION - CONTRACT DEMAND

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Distribution Code

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulatory Code

**HIERARCHY:** Section 2 / Definition / Contract Demand

**PROVISION:** Section 2 - Definition of "Contract Demand"

**TITLE:** Definition: Contract Demand

**SUMMARY:** "Contract Demand" is the maximum KW or KVA agreed to be supplied by the Distribution Licensee and indicated in the agreement executed between the Licensee and the Consumer/User. Where the agreement stipulates supply in KVA, the equivalent in KW is calculated by multiplying by the Power Factor of 0.9. Contract Demand establishes the binding capacity commitment and is a key commercial and operational parameter.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Agreed supply capacity
- Maximum drawl limit
- KW/KVA expression
- Power factor conversion (0.9)
- Agreement basis
- Tariff and billing reference
- Operational limit

**TEXT:**

**Contract Demand** - Maximum KW or KVA agreed to be supplied by the Licensee and indicated in the agreement executed between the parties. Wherever the agreement stipulates supply in KVA, the quantum in terms of KW may be obtained by multiplying by the Power Factor of 0.9.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**

## CHUNK 2.24: INTERPRETATION CLAUSE

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Distribution Code

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulatory Code

**HIERARCHY:** Section 2 / Interpretation (Rules of Construction)

**PROVISION:** Section 2 - Interpretation (Final Clause)

**TITLE:** Rules of Interpretation for the Distribution Code

**SUMMARY:** The Interpretation clause establishes standard rules for reading and applying the Distribution Code: (1) singular and plural terms are used interchangeably; (2) the words "include" or "including" are deemed to permit expansive interpretation unless limited; (3) references to "the Code" mean the Code as amended or modified by GERC; (4) headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation; (5) references to statutes, regulations, or guidelines include all amendments, consolidations, or replacements thereof. These rules ensure consistent and broad application of the Code.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Rules of construction
- Singular/plural equivalence
- Inclusive language interpretation
- Statutory amendments and updates
- Code modifications
- Heading inapplicability
- Expansive interpretation

**TEXT:**

**Interpretation**

In the interpretation of this Code, unless the context otherwise requires:

· words in the singular or plural term, as the case may be, shall also be deemed to include the plural or the singular term, respectively;

· the terms "include" or "including" shall be deemed to be followed by "without limitation" or "but not limited to" regardless of whether such terms are followed by such phrases or words of like import;

· references herein to the "code" shall be construed as a reference to this code as amended or modified by the GERC from time to time in accordance with the applicable laws in force.

· the headings are inserted for convenience and may not be taken into account for the purpose of interpretation of this Code.

· references to any statutes, regulations or guidelines shall be construed as including all statutory provisions consolidating, amending or replacing such statutes, regulations or guidelines, as the case may be, referred to.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- GERC (authority to amend and modify)
- Electricity Act 2003 (applicable laws)
- Distribution Code (all provisions)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
