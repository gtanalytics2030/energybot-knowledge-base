DOCUMENT: GERC (Distribution Licence) Regulations, 2005 – Notification No 4 of 2005

DOCUMENT TYPE: Regulation

HIERARCHY: Chapter 4 / General Conditions of Distribution Licence

PROVISION: Regulation 27

TITLE: Acts Requiring Prior Consent

SUMMARY:
Regulation 27 requires prior written approval from the Commission for three categories of transactions: (a) acquiring, by purchase, takeover, or otherwise, the utility of any other licensee; (b) merging its utility with the utility of another licensee; (c) assigning the Distribution Licence or transferring its utility or any part thereof by sale, lease, exchange, or otherwise. Exemption applies if the utility is in a state other than Gujarat. Prior approval is not required for such utilities. Before obtaining approval, the licensee must give one month's notice to every other licensee transmitting or distributing electricity in its Area of Licence. The Commission may approve subject to conditions or modifications, or reject within 3 months, with reasons recorded in writing.

KEY TOPICS: Merger, Acquisition, Utility Transfer, Commission Approval, Interstate Transactions

TEXT:
27. Acts Requiring Prior Consent

(i) The Distribution Licensee shall not do the following acts without the prior written approval of the Commission -

(a) undertake any transaction to acquire, by purchase or takeover or otherwise, the utility of any other Licensee;

(b) merge its utility with the utility of other Licensee; or

(c) assign the Distribution Licence or transfer its utility or any part thereof, by sale, lease, exchange or otherwise

Provided that approval shall not be required if the utility referred to in sub-clause (a) or sub-clause (b) above is situated in a State other than the State of Gujarat.

(ii) Any agreement relating to any transaction specified under clause (i) above, unless made with the prior approval of the Commission, shall be void.

(iii) The Distribution Licensee shall, before obtaining the approval under clause (i) above from the Commission, give not less than one month's notice to every other Licensee who transmits or distributes electricity in its Area of Licence.

(iv) Where such prior approval is required, the Distribution Licensee shall file a suitable application with the Commission disclosing relevant facts in that behalf. The Commission may within 3 months of the application being filed, approve the arrangement subject to such terms and conditions or modifications as is considered appropriate or reject the same for reasons to be recorded in writing.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Section 32 of Electricity Act 2003, Regulation 2(i)(f) (Distribution Licence), Regulation 2(i)(g) (Licence)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Regulation 10 (Hearing and Grant), Regulation 43 (Introduction of Competition)

DOCUMENT: GERC (Distribution Licence) Regulations, 2005 – Notification No 4 of 2005

DOCUMENT TYPE: Regulation

HIERARCHY: Chapter 4 / General Conditions of Distribution Licence

PROVISION: Regulation 28

TITLE: Other Business of the Distribution Licensee

SUMMARY:
Regulation 28 permits Distribution Licensees to engage in any Other Business (business outside the core distribution activity) with prior intimation to the Commission, only so long as such activity results in optimum utilization of distribution system assets. Four conditions apply: (1) the Licensed Business must not be prejudiced or adversely affected; (2) a proportion of revenues from Other Business, as specified by the Commission, shall reduce wheeling charges; (3) the licensee must maintain separate accounts for each Other Business to ensure the Licensed Business neither subsidizes nor encumbers distribution assets for support; (4) the licensee must comply with Commission guidelines. A separate provision requires Commission approval before contracting loans or issuing guarantees beyond Companies Act permissible limits, excluding employee advances and supplier payments in ordinary course. These provisions do not apply to local authorities engaged in distribution prior to the Electricity Act's commencement.

KEY TOPICS: Other Business, Asset Utilization, Revenue Allocation, Separate Accounts, Loans and Guarantees

TEXT:
28. Other Business of the Distribution Licensee

(i) The Distribution Licensee, with prior intimation to the Commission, may engage in any Other Business, only so long as such activity is likely to result in the optimum utilisation of the assets and infrastructure comprising the distribution system and subject to the following conditions:

(a) that the Licensed Business and the conduct thereof by the Distribution Licensee is not prejudiced and/or adversely affected in any manner;

(b) that a proportion of the revenues derived from such Other Business, as may be specified by the Commission, shall be utilised for reducing the wheeling charges of the Distribution Licensee;

(c) that the Distribution Licensee shall maintain separate accounts for each of such Other Business to ensure that the Licensed Business neither subsidies such Other Business undertaking in any way nor encumbers its distribution assets in any way to support such business;

(d) the Distribution Licensee shall always comply with such guidelines as the Commission may specify in this regard.

(i) The Distribution Licensee shall seek approval of the Commission before contracting any loans to, or issuing any guarantee for any obligation of any person, where the same is either beyond the permissible limits imposed by the Companies Act, 1956, or cannot be construed as being an exception and/or exemption under the Companies Act, 1956. Loans to employees pursuant to their terms of service and advances to suppliers in the ordinary course of business are excluded from the requirement to seek such approval.

(ii) Nothing contained in this Regulation 28 shall apply to a local authority engaged, before the commencement of the Act, in the business of distribution of electricity.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Section 14 of Electricity Act 2003, Companies Act 1956, Regulation 2(i)(h) (Licensed Business), Regulation 2(i)(i) (Other Business)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Regulation 25 (Prohibition of Subsidies), Regulation 40 (Accounts of Distribution Licensee), GERC Guidelines on Other Business

DOCUMENT: GERC (Distribution Licence) Regulations, 2005 – Notification No 4 of 2005

DOCUMENT TYPE: Regulation

HIERARCHY: Chapter 4 / General Conditions of Distribution Licence

PROVISION: Regulation 29

TITLE: Franchisee

SUMMARY:
Regulation 29 permits Distribution Licensees to authorize any other person to undertake distribution of electricity for a specified area within its Area of Supply under a franchisee arrangement. Three conditions apply: (a) the franchisee operates under the overall supervision and control of the licensee and in accordance with the licence terms and conditions; (b) the licensee must inform the Commission and obtain written approval before delegating functions; (c) the licensee remains responsible for all actions of the franchisee in relation to delegated functions. This regulation allows for sub-licensing arrangements while maintaining ultimate regulatory accountability with the primary licensee.

KEY TOPICS: Franchisee, Delegation, Sub-licensing, Supervision, Accountability

TEXT:
29. Franchisee

The Distribution Licensee may authorise any other person to undertake distribution of electricity for a specified area within its Area of Supply

Provided always that:

(a) any such person shall operate under the overall supervision and control of the Distribution Licensee and in accordance with the terms and conditions of the Distribution Licence; and

(b) the Distribution Licensee shall have, prior to delegating any function, informed the Commission of such action and taken its written approval; and

(c) the Distribution Licensee shall be responsible for all actions of such person in relation to the delegated functions.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Regulation 2(i)(f) (Distribution Licence), Regulation 2(i)(g) (Licensee)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Regulation 27 (Acts Requiring Prior Consent), Regulation 31 (Compliance with Regulations)

DOCUMENT: GERC (Distribution Licence) Regulations, 2005 – Notification No 4 of 2005

DOCUMENT TYPE: Regulation

HIERARCHY: Chapter 4 / General Conditions of Distribution Licence

PROVISION: Regulation 30

TITLE: Anti-Competitive Practices

SUMMARY:
Regulation 30 authorizes the Commission to issue directions to Distribution Licensees if they enter into agreements, abuse dominant positions, or engage in combinations that are likely to cause or cause adverse effects on competition in the electricity industry. This regulation implements Section 48 and 49 of the Electricity Act 2003 regarding competition safeguards.

KEY TOPICS: Anti-competitive Conduct, Monopoly Abuse, Combination, Competition Policy

TEXT:
30. Anti-competitive practices

The Commission may issue such directions as it considers appropriate to a Distribution Licensee if such Distribution Licensee enters into any agreement or abuses its dominant position or enters into a combination which is likely to cause or causes an adverse effect on competition in electricity industry.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Section 48 and 49 of Electricity Act 2003

CROSS-REFERENCES: Regulation 26 (Prohibition of Undue Preference), Regulation 41 (Powers of Commission)

DOCUMENT: GERC (Distribution Licence) Regulations, 2005 – Notification No 4 of 2005

DOCUMENT TYPE: Regulation

HIERARCHY: Chapter 4 / General Conditions of Distribution Licence

PROVISION: Regulation 31

TITLE: Compliance with Regulations, Codes, Orders and Directions

SUMMARY:
Regulation 31 mandates that Distribution Licensees always comply with: (a) the Distribution Licence Regulations; (b) Codes including State Grid Code, Distribution Code, Supply Code, and Standards of Performance; (c) Orders issued by the Commission; (d) Guidelines and Directions issued by the Commission; (e) terms and conditions of the Distribution Licence. Licensees may only deviate from these upon obtaining Commission approval. Licensees must monitor their own compliance and notify the Commission of any material breach of the licence, applicable codes, guidelines, regulations, or agreements as soon as possible.

KEY TOPICS: Compliance, Regulatory Codes, Commission Directions, Self-Monitoring, Material Breach Reporting

TEXT:
31. Compliance with Regulations, Codes, Orders and Directions

(i) The Distribution Licensee shall always comply with the Regulations, Codes (including the State Grid Code, the Distribution Code, the Supply Code and the Standards of Performance of Licensees), Orders, Guidelines and Directions issued by the Commission from time to time as may be considered appropriate by the Commission for achieving the purposes of the Act and the State Act and shall act in accordance with the conditions of its Distribution Licence, except where the Distribution Licensee obtains the approval of the Commission for any deviation from such directions and conditions.

(ii) The Distribution Licensee shall monitor its compliance with the terms of the Distribution Licence and any order, code, guideline or regulations it is required to comply with, and if the Distribution Licensee becomes aware of a material breach, on its part, of the Distribution Licence or any order, code, guideline or regulations, the Distribution Licensee shall notify the Commission of the material breach as soon as possible.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Regulation 2(j) (Definition of Regulations), GERC State Grid Code, GERC Distribution Code, GERC Supply Code, GERC Standards of Performance

CROSS-REFERENCES: Regulation 20 (Development and Maintenance), Regulation 32 (Standards of Performance), Regulation 38 (Information to Commission)

DOCUMENT: GERC (Distribution Licence) Regulations, 2005 – Notification No 4 of 2005

DOCUMENT TYPE: Regulation

HIERARCHY: Chapter 4 / General Conditions of Distribution Licence

PROVISION: Regulation 32

TITLE: Standards of Performance

SUMMARY:
Regulation 32 establishes liability for failure to meet Commission-specified Standards of Performance applicable to Distribution Licensees. If a licensee fails to meet specified standards, without prejudice to any penalty or prosecution, the licensee is liable to pay compensation to affected persons as determined by the Commission. The licensee must receive opportunity to be heard before compensation determination. Compensation shall be paid within 90 days of determination. Licensees must furnish to the Commission: (a) performance level achieved; (b) number of compensation cases and aggregate amount paid. The Commission publishes this information at least annually in appropriate form and manner.

KEY TOPICS: Service Standards, Performance Compliance, Consumer Compensation, Reporting Obligation

TEXT:
32. Standards of Performance

(i) If a Distribution Licensee fails to meet the standards specified by the Commission in 'Standards of Performance of Licensees' as applicable to the Distribution Licensee, then, without prejudice to any penalty that may be imposed or prosecution that may be initiated, such Distribution Licensee shall be liable to pay such compensation to the person affected as may be determined by the Commission

Provided that before determination of compensation, the concerned Distribution Licensee shall be given a reasonable opportunity of being heard.

(ii) The compensation determined under clause (i) shall be paid by the concerned Distribution Licensee within ninety (90) days of such determination.

(iii) The Distribution Licensee shall, within the period specified by the Commission, furnish to the Commission the following information, namely:-

(a) the level of performance achieved under clause (i) above;

(b) the number of cases in which compensation was paid under clause (ii) above and the aggregate amount of the compensation.

(iv) The Commission shall at least once every year publish, in such form and manner as it considers appropriate, such of the information furnished to it under clause (iii) above.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: GERC Standards of Performance of Licensees, Regulation 2(n) (Definition of Year)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Regulation 20 (Development and Maintenance), Regulation 31 (Compliance), Regulation 38 (Information to Commission)
