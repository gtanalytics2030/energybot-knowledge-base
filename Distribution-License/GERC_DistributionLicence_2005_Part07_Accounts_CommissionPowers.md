DOCUMENT: GERC (Distribution Licence) Regulations, 2005 – Notification No 4 of 2005

DOCUMENT TYPE: Regulation

HIERARCHY: Chapter 4 / General Conditions of Distribution Licence

PROVISION: Regulation 40

TITLE: Accounts of the Distribution Licensee

SUMMARY:
Regulation 40 establishes comprehensive accounting requirements for Distribution Licensees. Licensees must maintain separate information and statement of accounts for Licensed Business in Commission-specified form (or Companies Act 1956 format where applicable). Accounts must keep Licensed Business separate from Other Business to prevent subsidization or asset encumbrance. Licensees must prepare consistent annual accounting statements (April 1 – March 31) comprising profit and loss account, balance sheet, and statement of source/application of funds with notes showing all revenue, costs, assets, liabilities, and reserves, and indicating charges/apportionments between business activities. Auditors must provide annual reports stating whether statements are properly prepared and present true and fair view. Licensees must submit statements and audit reports to the Commission within six months of financial year end. Commission-authorized persons may inspect and verify accounts, and licensees must provide necessary assistance.

KEY TOPICS: Financial Accounting, Separate Accounts, Audit, Financial Statements, Transparency

TEXT:
40. Accounts of the Distribution Licensee

(i) The Distribution Licensee shall:

(a) Maintain separate information and statement of accounts for the Licensed Business;

(b) Maintain the statement of accounts in such form and containing such particulars as may be specified by the Commission and till such time as these are specified by the Commission, the accounts shall be maintained in accordance with the Companies Act, 1956 (1 of 1956) as amended from time to time, where applicable, and in accordance with standard accounting practice in all other cases;

(c) Keep the accounts of the Licensed Business separate from the accounts of any Other Business carried on by the Distribution Licensee, whether licensed or otherwise;

(d) Prepare on a consistent basis from such records, accounting statements for each Year comprising a profit and loss account, a balance sheet and a statement of source and application of funds together with notes thereto and showing separately the amounts of any revenue, cost, asset, liability, reserve, or provision which has been either:

1. Charged from or to any Other Business together with a description of the basis of that charge; or

2. Determined by apportionment or allocation between the various business activities of the Distribution Licensee together with a description of the basis of such apportionment or allocation.

(e) Provide in respect of the accounting statements prepared in accordance with the foregoing clauses, a report by the auditors in respect of each Year, stating whether in their opinion the statements have been properly prepared and give a true and fair view of the revenues, costs, assets, liabilities and reserves attributable to the business to which the statements relate, and

(f) Submit to the Commission copies of the accounting statements and auditor's report not later than six months after the end of the Year to which they relate.

(ii) Any person authorised by the Commission shall be entitled to inspect and verify the accounts of the Distribution Licensee and the Distribution Licensee shall render all necessary assistance to such person.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Companies Act 1956, Regulation 2(i)(h) (Licensed Business), Regulation 2(i)(i) (Other Business), Regulation 2(n) (Definition of Year), Section 55 of Electricity Act 2003

CROSS-REFERENCES: Regulation 25 (Prohibition of Subsidies), Regulation 28 (Other Business), Regulation 44 (Expected Revenue Calculation)

DOCUMENT TYPE: Regulation

HIERARCHY: Chapter 4 / Certain Powers of the Commission

PROVISION: Regulation 41

TITLE: Regulating Supply, Distribution, Consumption and Use of Electricity

SUMMARY:
Regulation 41 establishes the Commission's authority to issue orders regulating the supply, distribution, consumption, or use of electricity if the Commission deems it necessary or expedient for maintaining efficient supply, securing equitable distribution, and promoting competition. This regulation provides the Commission with broad discretionary powers for regulatory intervention in distribution matters.

KEY TOPICS: Commission Powers, Regulatory Authority, Supply Regulation, Competition, Equitable Distribution

TEXT:
41. Regulating supply, distribution, consumption and use of electricity

If the Commission is of the opinion that it is necessary or expedient so to do for maintaining the efficient supply, securing the equitable distribution of electricity and promoting competition, it may, by order, provide for regulating the supply, distribution, consumption or use thereof.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Section 61 of Electricity Act 2003

CROSS-REFERENCES: Regulation 30 (Anti-competitive practices), Regulation 26 (Prohibition of Undue Preference), Regulation 24 (Open Access)

HIERARCHY: Chapter 4 / Certain Powers of the Commission

PROVISION: Regulation 42

TITLE: Investigation and Inspection

SUMMARY:
Regulation 42 establishes the Commission's power to direct investigation of Distribution Licensees for non-compliance with licence conditions or statutory provisions. Upon satisfaction that a licensee has failed to comply with licence conditions or the Electricity Act or State Act or rules/regulations, the Commission may direct any specified person (Investigating Authority) to investigate the licensee's affairs and report. The Investigating Authority conducts investigation per Electricity Act provisions. Upon receiving the investigation report and giving the licensee opportunity to make representations, the Commission may require corrective action or cancel the licence. The Commission specifies information to be maintained by the licensee, maintenance methods, verifications, and checks necessary for investigations. The expression "Distribution Licensee" includes subsidiaries formed for business conducted exclusively outside India and all branches inside or outside India. All investigation expenses are borne by the licensee, have priority over other debts, and are recoverable as arrears of land revenue. The Commission may issue interim or final orders, or convert interim to final orders to prevent breach of licence terms, statutory provisions, rules, regulations, directives, or orders.

KEY TOPICS: Investigation Authority, Non-Compliance, Regulatory Inquiry, Cost of Investigation

TEXT:
42. Investigation and Inspection

(i) The Commission may, on being satisfied that the Distribution Licensee has failed to comply with any of the conditions of Distribution Licence or has failed to comply with any of the provisions of the Act or the State Act or rules or regulations made thereunder, at any time, by order in writing, direct any person (hereafter in this section referred to as "Investigating Authority") specified in the order to investigate the affairs of the Distribution Licensee and to report to the Commission on any investigation made by such Investigating Authority.

(ii) The Investigating Authority shall conduct the investigation in accordance with the provisions of the Act.

(iii) On receipt of investigation report from the Investigating Authority, the Commission may, in accordance with the provisions of the Act and after giving such opportunity to the Distribution Licensee to make a representation in connection with the report as in the opinion of the Commission seems reasonable, by order in writing

a) require the Distribution Licensee to take such action in respect of any matter arising out of the report as the Commission may think fit; or

b) cancel the Distribution Licence.

The Commission may also take any other action in accordance with the provisions of the Act

(iv) The Commission may specify the minimum information to be maintained by the Distribution Licensee in its books, the manner in which such information shall be maintained, the checks and other verifications to be adopted by the Distribution Licensee in that connection and all other matters incidental thereto, as are, in its opinion, necessary to enable the Investigating Authority to discharge satisfactorily its functions under this Regulation 42.

(v) For the purposes of this Regulation 41, the expression "Distribution Licensee" shall include, in the case of a Distribution Licensee incorporated in India—

(a) the subsidiaries of such Distribution Licensee formed for the purpose of carrying on the business of distribution of electricity exclusively outside India; and

(b) all its branches whether situated in India or outside India.

(vi) All expenses of, and incidental to, any investigation made under this Regulation 42 shall be defrayed by the Distribution Licensee and shall have priority over other debts due from the Distribution Licensee and shall be recoverable as arrears of land revenue.

(vii) For the purposes of preventing the contravention of any term of the Distribution Licence or provision of the Act or the State Act or rules or regulations made thereunder, the Commission may, in accordance with Sections 34, 35, 36, 37 and 38 of the State Act, pass an interim order or a final order or convert an interim order into a final order.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Section 36 of Electricity Act 2003, Section 34-38 of State Act, Regulation 17 (Revocation)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Regulation 31 (Compliance), Regulation 38 (Information to Commission)

HIERARCHY: Chapter 4 / Certain Powers of the Commission

PROVISION: Regulation 43

TITLE: Introduction of Competition

SUMMARY:
Regulation 43 establishes two aspects of the Commission's power to introduce competition: (i) the Commission may grant distribution licences to other persons in the same Area of Licence as existing licensees; (ii) to ensure cooperation and coordination between different distribution licensees in the same Area of Licence and implement competition, the Commission may issue orders modifying or amending distribution licences as deemed fit, provided the affected licensee receives reasonable opportunity to be heard on the proposed modification or amendment.

KEY TOPICS: Competitive Distribution, Multiple Licensees, Licence Amendment, Cooperation

TEXT:
43. Introduction of Competition

(i) Subject to the provisions of the Act, the Commission may grant a Distribution Licence to any person(s) in the same Area of Licence as that of the existing Distribution Licensee,

(ii) For the purposes of introducing competition, and in order to ensure co-operation and co-ordination between the different Distribution Licensees in the same Area of Licence, the Commission may issues such appropriate orders modifying or amending the Distribution Licence as it may deem fit:

Provided that no such order shall be passed unless the Commission provides reasonable opportunity to the Distribution Licensee of being heard on the proposed modification or amendment.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Section 41 of Electricity Act 2003, Regulation 11 (Application in area of existing licensee), Regulation 16 (Amendment of licence)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Regulation 10 (Hearing and Grant of Licence), Regulation 26 (Prohibition of Undue Preference)

HIERARCHY: Chapter 4 / Certain Powers of the Commission

PROVISION: Regulation 44

TITLE: Expected Revenue Calculation and Tariffs

SUMMARY:
Regulation 44 establishes the framework for tariff determination and Annual Revenue Requirement (ARR) filings. Distribution Licensees must follow the methodology, procedures, and directions specified in the Tariff Regulations and other Commission orders when filing Annual Revenue Requirement statements and proposing or amending tariffs. The amount a licensee is permitted to recover in tariffs in any financial year is the amount determined by the Commission in accordance with Section 62 of the Electricity Act 2003.

KEY TOPICS: Tariff Determination, Annual Revenue Requirement, Cost Recovery, Regulatory Framework

TEXT:
44. Expected Revenue Calculation and Tariffs

(i) The Distribution Licensee shall follow the methodology, procedures and directions included in the Tariff Regulations and in other orders of the Commission as may be issued from time to time, while filing the statement of Annual Revenue Requirement (ARR) from charges and for proposing or amending any or all of its tariffs.

(ii) The amount that the Distribution Licensee is permitted to recover in the form of its tariffs in any financial year is the amount that the Commission determines in accordance with Section 62 of the Act.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Section 62 of Electricity Act 2003, GERC Tariff Regulations, Regulation 2(n) (Definition of Year)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Regulation 40 (Accounts), Regulation 41 (Regulating supply and distribution)

PROVISION: Regulation 45

TITLE: Provisions Applicable to Deemed Licensees

SUMMARY:
Regulation 45 establishes that the general conditions of Distribution Licence specified in Chapter 4 of these Regulations shall be applicable to deemed licensees under the first, third, and fifth provisos to Section 14 of the Electricity Act 2003. Deemed licensees are those who held distribution licenses prior to the Electricity Act 2003 and are treated as if licensed under the new Act.

KEY TOPICS: Deemed Licensee, Retroactive Application, Pre-Act Licensees

TEXT:
45. Provisions applicable to Deemed Licensees

The general conditions of Distribution Licence specified in these Regulations shall be applicable to a deemed licensee under the first, third and fifth proviso to Section 14 of the Act.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Section 14 of Electricity Act 2003 (Provisos on deemed licensees), Chapter 4 (General Conditions)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Chapter 4 (General Conditions of Distribution Licence), Regulation 20-44 (All general conditions)
