HIERARCHY: Schedule-I

PROVISION: Schedule-I

TITLE: Application Form for Grant of Distribution Licence

SUMMARY:
Schedule-I specifies the prescribed application form for distribution licence applicants. It requires detailed disclosure of applicant particulars (name, form of incorporation, address, contact details, geographic area proposed, documents to be enclosed), financial data (net worth, turnover, credit rating, RBI classification, documentation), organizational and managerial capability, business approach and methodology, and five-year business plans with cost and revenue forecasts and funding arrangements. The form requires supporting documents including certificate of registration/incorporation, commencement of business, memoranda of association, power of attorney, income tax registration, draft licence, group company details (for corporate applicants), annual accounts (3 years), details of debt financing arrangements, and organizational/management team details.
