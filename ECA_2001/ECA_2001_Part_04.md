## CHUNK 24: SECTION 15 - POWER OF STATE GOVERNMENT TO ENFORCE CERTAIN PROVISIONS FOR EFFICIENT USE OF ENERGY AND ITS CONSERVATION (PART A: CLAUSES A-D)

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Central Legislation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter VI / Section 15

**PROVISION:** Section 15, clauses (a) through (d)

**TITLE:** State Government Powers - Building Codes, Compliance, Audits, and Designated Agencies

**SUMMARY:** The State Government may, by notification in consultation with the Bureau: (a) amend energy conservation building codes for regional/local climatic conditions and notify them via state rules; (b) direct building owners/occupiers (designated consumers) to comply with building codes; (c) direct designated consumers to get energy audits by accredited auditors if necessary, in manner/intervals specified by regulations; (d) designate any agency as the designated agency to coordinate, regulate, and enforce Act provisions within the State.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Building code amendment (state authority)
- Climatic customization
- Owner/occupier compliance direction
- Discretionary audit mandate
- Designated agency appointment
- State enforcement coordinator

**TEXT:**

"15. Power of State Government to enforce certain provisions for efficient use of energy and its conservation.—The State Government may, by notification, in consultation with the Bureau—

(a) amend the energy conservation building codes to suit the regional and local climatic conditions and may, by rules made by it, specify and notify energy conservation building codes with respect to use of energy in the buildings;

(b) direct every owner or occupier of a building or building complex being a designated consumer to comply with the provisions of the energy conservation building codes;

(c) direct, if considered necessary for efficient use of energy and its conservation, any designated consumer referred to in clause (b) to get energy audit conducted by an accredited energy auditor in such manner and at such intervals of time as may be specified by regulations;

(d) designate any agency as designated agency to coordinate, regulate and enforce provisions of this Act within the State;"

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Section 14(p), (q) - Central Government building code powers
- Section 58(2)(k) - Regulations for audit manner/intervals
- Section 2(f) - Designated agency definition

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Parallel to Central Government building code authority in Section 14(p)-(s), but state-specific implementation and customization.

## CHUNK 25: SECTION 15 - POWER OF STATE GOVERNMENT TO ENFORCE CERTAIN PROVISIONS FOR EFFICIENT USE OF ENERGY AND ITS CONSERVATION (PART B: CLAUSES E-I)

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Central Legislation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter VI / Section 15

**PROVISION:** Section 15, clauses (e) through (i)

**TITLE:** State Government Powers (Continued) - Awareness, Training, Preferential Treatment, Information, Inspection

**SUMMARY:** The State Government may: (e) take measures to create awareness and disseminate conservation information; (f) arrange training for personnel and specialists in conservation techniques; (g) take steps to encourage preferential treatment for energy-efficient equipment; (h) direct designated consumers to furnish energy consumption information in form/manner/time specified by state rules; (i) specify inspection scope matters in state rules.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Awareness and dissemination
- Training programs
- Market incentives
- Information disclosure (state-prescribed)
- State rule-making authority
- Inspection scope definition (state level)

**TEXT:**

"(e) take all measures necessary to create awareness and disseminate information for efficient use of energy and its conservation;

(f) arrange and organise training of personnel and specialists in the techniques for efficient use of energy and its conservation;

(g) take steps to encourage preferential treatment for use of energy efficient equipment or appliances;

(h) direct, any designated consumer to furnish to the designated agency, in such form and manner and within such period as may be specified by rules made by it, information with regard to the energy consumed by such consumer;

(i) specify the matters to be included for the purposes of inspection under sub-section (2) of section 17."

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Section 17(2) - Inspection powers
- Section 57(2)(b), (d) - State rule-making for information/inspection

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Parallel to Central Government powers in Section 14(t)-(v); state-specific rule-making authority under Section 57.

## CHUNK 26: SECTION 16 - ESTABLISHMENT OF FUND BY STATE GOVERNMENT

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Central Legislation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter VI / Section 16

**PROVISION:** Section 16

**TITLE:** State Energy Conservation Fund - Establishment and Administration

**SUMMARY:** Each State Government must constitute a State Energy Conservation Fund for promoting efficient energy use and conservation within the State. The Fund receives all grants/loans from State Government, Central Government, or other organizations/individuals for Act purposes. Fund is applied for implementing Act provisions. The Fund's administration (persons/authority/manner) is specified in state rules.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Mandatory state fund establishment
- Multi-source funding (state, central, other)
- Fund application limitation (Act implementation only)
- Regulatory administration (state rules)
- State financial mechanism

**TEXT:**

"16. Establishment of Fund by State Government.—(1) The State Government shall constitute a Fund to be called the State Energy Conservation Fund for the purposes of promotion of efficient use of energy and its conservation within the State.

(2) To the Fund shall be credited all grants and loans that may be made by the State Government or, Central Government or any other organisation or individual for the purposes of this Act.

(3) The Fund shall be applied for meeting the expenses incurred for implementing the provisions of this Act.

(4) The Fund created under sub-section (1) shall be administered by such persons or any authority and in such manner as may be specified in the rules made by the State Government."

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Section 20 - Parallel Central Government fund
- Section 57(2)(c) - State rules for fund administration

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Mirrored structure to Section 20 (Central Energy Conservation Fund); state rule-making authority under Section 57.

## CHUNK 27: SECTION 17 - POWER OF INSPECTION (PART A: APPOINTMENT, AUTHORITY, AND INSPECTION SCOPE)

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Central Legislation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter VI / Section 17

**PROVISION:** Section 17, subsections (1)-(2)

**TITLE:** Inspection Powers - Appointment of Inspecting Officers and Inspection Authority

**SUMMARY:** The designated agency may appoint inspecting officers (after 5 years from Act commencement) to ensure compliance with: energy consumption standards under Section 14(a), equipment labeling under Section 14(b), or other assigned functions. Inspecting officers have power to: (a) inspect operations with specified equipment/appliances or consuming energy; (b) enter designated consumer premises where energy activities occur and require proprietor/employee/director/manager/secretary/other person to afford inspection facilities for equipment/appliances or production processes, make inventory of equipment, or record statements relevant to conservation; (c) [continued in next chunk].

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Inspecting officer appointment (5-year threshold)
- Appointment authority (designated agency)
- Inspection scope (standards, labeling, assigned functions)
- Premises entry authority
- Documentary examination
- Statement recording

**TEXT:**

"17. Power of inspection.—(1) The designated agency may appoint, after the expiry of five years from the date of commencement of this Act, as many inspecting officers as may be necessary for the purpose of ensuring compliance with energy consumption standards specified under clause (a) of section 14 or ensure display of particulars on label on equipment or appliance specified under clause (b) of section 14 or for the purpose of performing such other functions as may be assigned to them.

(2) Subject to any rules made under this Act, an inspecting officer shall have power to—

(a) inspect any operation carried on or in connection with the equipment or appliance specified under clause (b) of section 14 or in respect of which energy standards under clause (a) of section 14 have been specified;

(b) enter any place of designated consumer at which the energy is used for any activity and may require any proprietor, employee, director, manager or secretary or any other person who may be attending in any manner to or helping in, carrying on any activity with the help of energy—

(i) to afford him necessary facility to inspect—

(A) any equipment or appliance as he may require and which may be available at such place;

(B) any production process to ascertain the energy consumption norms and standards;

(ii) to make an inventory of stock of any equipment or appliance checked or verified by him;

(iii) to record the statement of any person which may be useful for, or relevant to, for efficient use of energy and its conservation under this Act."

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Section 14(a) - Energy consumption standards
- Section 14(b) - Equipment specification and labeling
- Section 2(f) - Designated agency definition
- Section 1(3) - Act commencement date

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Inspector appointment delayed 5 years post-commencement; authority tied to standards/labeling compliance.

## CHUNK 28: SECTION 17 - POWER OF INSPECTION (PART B: PREMISES ENTRY TIMING AND DOCUMENT PROTECTION)

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Central Legislation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter VI / Section 17

**PROVISION:** Section 17, subsections (3)-(4)

**TITLE:** Inspection Powers (Continued) - Premises Entry Restrictions and Document Protection

**SUMMARY:** Inspecting officers may enter designated consumer premises only: (a) where energy activities occur, and (b) where specified equipment/appliances are kept, during hours when the place is open for production or business. Officers acting under this section may NOT remove or cause removal of equipment, appliances, books, or other documents from inspected premises.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Temporal restriction (business hours only)
- Purpose-tied entry (equipment or production location)
- Document protection
- Equipment protection
- Non-removable seizure prohibition
- Premises access limitation

**TEXT:**

"(3) An inspecting officer may enter any place of designated consumer—

(a) where any activity with the help of energy is carried on; and

(b) where any equipment or appliance notified under clause (b) of section 14 has been kept,

during the hours at which such place is open for production or conduct of business connected therewith.

(4) An inspecting officer acting under this section shall, on no account, remove or cause to be removed from the place wherein he has entered, any equipment or appliance or books of account or other documents."

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Section 14(b) - Equipment specification
- Section 17(1)-(2) - Inspection authority

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Section 4 prohibits seizure/removal; ensures inspection without disruption to operations.

## CHUNK 29: SECTION 18 - POWER OF CENTRAL GOVERNMENT OR STATE GOVERNMENT TO ISSUE DIRECTIONS

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Central Legislation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter VI / Section 18

**PROVISION:** Section 18

**TITLE:** Directions - Central and State Authority to Issue Binding Directives

**SUMMARY:** The Central Government or State Government may, in exercise of Act powers/functions and for energy conservation, issue written directions as deemed fit to any person, officer, authority, or designated consumer. Recipients must comply with such directions. The Explanation clause declares that direction power includes authority to regulate: (a) process norms and consumption standards in any industry/building/building complex, or (b) equipment and appliance consumption standards.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Direction-issuing authority (central and state)
- Written form requirement
- Binding compliance obligation
- Regulatory scope (norms/standards)
- Industry, building, and equipment coverage
- Discretionary authority

**TEXT:**

"18. Power of Central Government or State Government to issue directions.—The Central Government or the State Government may, in the exercise of its powers and performance of its functions under this Act and for efficient use of energy and its conservation, issue such directions in writing as it deems fit for the purposes of this Act to any person, officer, authority or any designated consumer and such person, officer or authority or any designated consumer shall be bound to comply with such directions.

Explanation.—For the avoidance of doubts, it is hereby declared that the power to issue directions under this section includes the power to direct—

(a) regulation of norms for process and energy consumption standards in any industry or building or building complex; or

(b) regulation of the energy consumption standards for equipment and appliances."

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Section 14, 15 - Exercise of powers/functions
- Section 55 - Similar direction power for Central Government (miscellaneous context)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Directions are binding enforcement mechanism; "regulation" here means issuing standards/rules, not regulatory authority.
