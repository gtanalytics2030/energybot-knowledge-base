DOCUMENT: The Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2010
DOCUMENT TYPE: Amendment Act - Section
HIERARCHY: Section 11
PROVISION: Section 11
TITLE: Omission of Sections 32 to 43

SUMMARY: This section repeals and removes Sections 32 through 43 (both inclusive) from the Energy Conservation Act, 2001. This represents a substantial deletion of twelve sections from the principal legislation. The sections that are deleted relate to the original framework established in the 2001 Act, which is being restructured by the 2010 Amendment. The specific content of the deleted sections would be found in the original Energy Conservation Act, 2001, but their omission indicates a comprehensive reframing of certain regulatory provisions.

KEY TOPICS:

- Statutory repeal

TEXT:

11. Sections 32 to 43 of the principal Act shall be omitted.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS:
- Sections 32-43 of the Energy Conservation Act, 2001 (principal Act)

CROSS-REFERENCES:
- Energy Conservation Act, 2001
- Related amendments in Sections 7, 9, and 10 of this Amendment Act which establish new appellate mechanisms (replacing deleted sections relating to appellate authorities)
- Section 14A and 14B (newly inserted, replacing provisions that may have been in deleted sections)

KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:
This is a major structural change to the Energy Conservation Act, 2001. The deletion of Sections 32-43 represents removal of old regulatory provisions, replaced by the new framework established in Section 7 (Sections 14A-14B for energy savings certificates) and Sections 9-10 (new appellate mechanism). Any query about sections 32-43 of the principal Act would need to note they are no longer in force after this Amendment.
