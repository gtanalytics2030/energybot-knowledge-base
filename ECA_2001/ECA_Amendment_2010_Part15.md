TITLE: Amendment of Section 58 - Adjudicating Officer Authority

SUMMARY: This section amends Section 58 of the principal Energy Conservation Act, 2001, in sub-section (2). It replaces clause (f) with new provisions requiring the adjudicating officer to specify the qualifications, criteria, and conditions subject to which a person may be accredited as an energy auditor and the procedure for such accreditation under clause (p) of sub-section (2) of Section 13. It also amends clause (h) to expand the adjudicating officer's authority from regulating "energy managers" to include both "energy auditors and energy managers." These amendments align the adjudicating officer's powers with the expanded roles of energy auditors in the new energy savings certificate framework.

TEXT:

14. In section 58 of the principal Act, in sub-section (2),—

(a) for clause (f), the following clause shall be substituted, namely:—

"(f) the qualifications, criteria and conditions subject to which a person may be accredited as an energy auditor and the procedure for such accreditation under clause (p) of sub-section (2) of section 13;";

(b) in clause (h), for the words "energy managers", the words "energy auditors and energy managers" shall be substituted.

KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:
Section 58 defines the adjudicating officer's rule-making authority. This amendment is important because it explicitly empowers the adjudicating officer to establish energy auditor accreditation standards and procedures, and extends regulatory authority to cover both auditors and managers. Queries about energy auditor qualification standards and who sets them should reference Section 58(2)(f).
