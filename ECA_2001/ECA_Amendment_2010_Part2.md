TITLE: Short Title

SUMMARY: This section establishes the formal short title of the amending legislation. The Act shall be referred to and cited as "The Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2010." This is a standard enacting provision that provides the statutory name by which the legislation will be referenced in all legal proceedings and citations.

TEXT:

1. This Act may be called the Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2010.
