DOCUMENT: The Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2010
DOCUMENT TYPE: Amendment Act - Section
HIERARCHY: Section 4
PROVISION: Section 4
TITLE: Amendment of Section 10

SUMMARY: This section amends Section 10 of the principal Energy Conservation Act, 2001, by substituting the words "The Bureau" for "The Government" in sub-section (1). This amendment transfers or clarifies authority from "The Government" to "The Bureau" (referring to the Energy Efficiency Bureau) for a specific provision. The amendment indicates a shift in administrative responsibility from the Central Government to the designated Bureau under the Energy Conservation Act.

KEY TOPICS:
- Authority transfer
- Government to Bureau substitution
- Administrative restructuring
- Bureau of Energy Efficiency
- Section 10 modification

TEXT:

4. In section 10 of the principal Act, in sub-section (1), for the words "The Government", the words "The Bureau" shall be substituted.
