TITLE: Amendment of Section 26 - Penalties and Non-Compliance

SUMMARY: This section amends Section 26 of the principal Energy Conservation Act, 2001, regarding penalties for non-compliance. In sub-section (1), it omits grammatical elements (words, brackets, and letter "or clause (n)"), increases penalties from "ten thousand rupees" to "ten lakh rupees," and from "one thousand rupees" to "ten thousand rupees." It also inserts a new sub-section (1A) establishing a specific penalty regime for non-compliance with clause (n) of Section 14 (relating to designated consumer obligations). The new sub-section (1A) provides that a person failing to comply shall be liable to a penalty not exceeding ten lakh rupees and, in case of continuing failure, an additional penalty not less than the price of every metric ton of oil equivalent of energy prescribed under this Act that is in excess of the prescribed norms.

TEXT:

8. In section 26 of the principal Act,—

(a) in sub-section (1),—

(i) the words, brackets and letter "or clause (n)" shall be omitted;

(ii) for the words "ten thousand rupees", the words "ten lakh rupees" shall be substituted;

(iii) for the words "one thousand rupees", the words "ten thousand rupees" shall be substituted;

(b) after sub-section (1), the following sub-section shall be inserted, namely:—

"(1A) If any person fails to comply with the provisions of clause (n) of section 14, he shall be liable to a penalty which shall not exceed ten lakh rupees and, in the case of continuing failure, with an additional penalty which shall not be less than the price of every metric ton of oil equivalent of energy, prescribed under this Act, that is in excess of the prescribed norms.".

KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:
The penalty structure links non-compliance with energy consumption in excess of prescribed norms to quantifiable oil equivalent values. Queries about penalties for failing to purchase energy savings certificates or comply with energy efficiency norms should reference Section 26(1A) as amended. The "continuing failure" provision allows for escalating penalties.
