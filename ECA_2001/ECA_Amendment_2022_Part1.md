## CHUNK 1.1

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022, No. 19 of 2022

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Amendment Act to the Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**HIERARCHY:** Section 1

**PROVISION:** Section 1 - Short title and commencement

**TITLE:** Short Title and Commencement

**SUMMARY:** This section establishes the formal name of the amending legislation as the Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022, and provides that it shall come into force on a date to be notified by the Central Government in the Official Gazette.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Short title of Act
- Commencement provision
- Central Government notification
- Official Gazette publication

**TEXT:**

1. (1) This Act may be called the Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022.

(2) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Official Gazette (publication mechanism)
