# Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022 - Batch 3: Bureau Constitutional and Functional Amendments

## CHUNK 3.1

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022, No. 19 of 2022

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Amendment Act to the Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**HIERARCHY:** Section 3 - Amendment of Section 4 (Constitution of Bureau)

**PROVISION:** Section 3(a) - Amendment to sub-section (1) - Increase in Bureau Membership

**TITLE:** Expansion of Bureau Membership Strength

**SUMMARY:** The maximum and minimum membership strength of the Energy Conservation Bureau is increased from "twenty" members to "thirty-one, but not exceeding thirty-seven" members. This reflects the expansion of ex-officio representatives from multiple government ministries and the addition of domain experts.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Bureau membership strength
- Institutional capacity
- Multi-ministerial representation
- Governance structure
- Government agencies

**TEXT:**

(a) in sub-section (1), for the words "twenty, but not exceeding twenty-six", the words "thirty-one, but not exceeding thirty-seven" shall be substituted;

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Principal Act: Energy Conservation Act, 2001, Section 4, sub-section (1)
- Section 3(b) details the expanded membership composition

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Section 4(b) of the principal Act amended by Section 3(b) of this Amendment Act - details of new ex-officio members

## CHUNK 3.2

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022, No. 19 of 2022

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Amendment Act to the Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**HIERARCHY:** Section 3 - Amendment of Section 4 (Constitution of Bureau)

**PROVISION:** Section 3(b)(i) - Insertion of New Ex-Officio Members (Clauses ga-gg)

**TITLE:** Addition of Six New Ex-Officio Members from Central Government Ministries

**SUMMARY:** Six new ex-officio member positions are created, drawn from the following Central Government Ministries/Departments: Environment, Forest and Climate Change; Housing and Urban Affairs; Road Transport and Highways; Steel; Civil Aviation; and Ports, Shipping and Waterways. Additionally, a Member of the Railway Board in charge of Energy is added. These additions reflect the cross-sectoral impact of energy conservation.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Ex-officio membership
- Multi-ministerial coordination
- Government representation
- Environmental compliance
- Urban development
- Transport sector
- Industrial policy
- Logistics and shipping
- Railway energy management

**TEXT:**

(i) after clause (g), the following clauses shall be inserted, namely:—

"(ga) the Secretary to the Government of India, in charge of the Ministry or Department of the Central Government dealing with the Environment, Forest and Climate Change—ex officio member;

(gb) the Secretary to the Government of India, in charge of the Ministry or Department of the Central Government dealing with the Housing and Urban Affairs—ex officio member;

(gc) the Secretary to the Government of India, in charge of the Ministry or Department of the Central Government dealing with the Road Transport and Highways—ex officio member;

(gd) the Secretary to the Government of India, in charge of the Ministry or Department of the Central Government dealing with the Steel—ex officio member;

(ge) the Secretary to the Government of India, in charge of the Ministry or Department of the Central Government dealing with the Civil Aviation—ex officio member;

(gf) the Secretary to the Government of India, in charge of the Ministry or Department of the Central Government dealing with the Ports, Shipping and Waterways—ex officio member;

(gg) Member of the Railway Board (in charge of Energy), Ministry of Railways—ex officio member;"

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Reflects India's comprehensive energy conservation approach across all key sectors
- Environmental compliance: Climate change mitigation through energy efficiency
- Urban development: Building energy codes for housing sector
- Transport: Vehicle and fuel efficiency standards
- Industrial policy: Energy-intensive sectors (steel)
- Aviation: Fuel efficiency in air transport
- Shipping: Maritime vessel efficiency
- Railways: Rail transport energy management

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:**
The expansion demonstrates institutional recognition that energy conservation is not sectoral but cross-cutting, requiring coordinated oversight across environmental, infrastructure, transport, industrial, and logistics sectors of the economy.

**HIERARCHY:** Section 3 - Amendment of Section 4 (Constitution of Bureau)

**PROVISION:** Section 3(b)(iii) - Substitution of Clause (o) - State Officials from Power Regions

**SUMMARY:** The clause for State Government representation is substituted to specify that one official each from the energy or power department of five States—representing the five national power regions—at the rank of Principal Secretary to State Government shall be appointed by the Central Government. This ensures multi-regional State-level input into Bureau decisions.

## CHUNK 3.7

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022, No. 19 of 2022

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Amendment Act to the Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**HIERARCHY:** Section 4 - Amendment of Section 13 (Functions of Bureau)

**PROVISION:** Section 4(iii) and (iv) - Amendments to Clauses (h) and Insertion of New Clauses (ta)-(tf) of Section 13(2)

**TITLE:** Enhanced Bureau Functions for Promotion and International Collaboration

**SUMMARY:** The word "promote" in clause (h) is supplemented with "or undertake," allowing the Bureau to directly execute functions in addition to promoting them. Six new functional clauses are inserted enabling the Bureau to: (ta) collaborate with international institutions; (tb) authorize other agencies to execute Bureau functions; (tc) test samples beyond statutory requirements; (td) empanel technical experts in energy efficiency and carbon credits; (te) recommend carbon credit scheme requirements to government; and (tf) recommend minimum shares of non-fossil energy consumption.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Bureau promotional functions
- International cooperation
- Agency authorization
- Technical testing
- Expert empanelment
- Carbon credit recommendations
- Non-fossil energy targets
- Designated consumers
- Energy and feedstock consumption

**TEXT:**

(iii) in clause (h), after the word "promote", the words "or undertake" shall be inserted;

(iv) after clause (t), the following clauses shall be inserted, namely:––

"(ta) collaborate with any international institution or organisation or to obtain membership of bodies having similar objectives as that of the Bureau, in consultation with the Central Government;

(tb) authorise any agency in the country or outside the country to carry out any of the functions of the Bureau, for such purposes, and subject to such terms and conditions, as may be specified by regulations;

(tc) undertake, or authorise any other body which meets with such technical qualifications, as may be specified by regulations, to test samples for purposes other than those specified in section 14;

(td) empanel technical experts to promote energy efficiency and carbon credit trading activities undertaken to meet the objectives of the Act;

(te) recommend to the Central Government on the requirements to be specified in the carbon credit trading scheme to be notified under clause (w) of section 14;

(tf) recommend minimum share of consumption of non-fossil sources by designated consumers as energy or feedstock;"

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Principal Act: Energy Conservation Act, 2001, Section 13, sub-section (2)
- Section 14, clause (w) of principal Act - Carbon credit trading scheme
- Section 14 of principal Act - Statutory testing framework

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Carbon credit trading: Section 8 insertion of Section 14AA
- Designated consumers: Energy-intensive industries and large commercial establishments
- International standards: Alignment with global energy efficiency frameworks
- Non-fossil sources: Support for renewable energy transition
- Regulations: To be made by Central Government for implementing these functions

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:**
These expanded functions position the Bureau as an active implementer and standard-setter, not merely an advisory body, with authority to directly undertake activities and collaborate internationally. The carbon credit and non-fossil energy functions align with India's climate commitments and renewable energy targets (175 GW by 2022, 500 GW by 2030).
