# Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022 - Batch 5: Carbon Credit Mechanisms and State-Level Amendments

---

## CHUNK 5.1

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022, No. 19 of 2022

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Amendment Act to the Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**HIERARCHY:** Section 7 - Amendment of Section 14A

**PROVISION:** Section 7 - Amendment to Section 14A (Issuance of Energy Savings Certificate)

**TITLE:** Amendments to Energy Savings Certificate Issuance and Addition of Voluntary Carbon Credit Trading

**SUMMARY:** Section 14A is amended in three respects: (a) the marginal heading is changed from generic to specifically "Issuance of energy savings certificate," (b) sub-section (1) is amended to allow the Central Government "or any agency authorised by it" to issue energy savings certificates (expanding issuance authority), and (c) a new proviso to sub-section (2) allows any person (not only obligated entities) to voluntarily purchase energy savings certificates or carbon credit certificates. This democratizes carbon credit and energy savings certificate trading.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Energy savings certificate
- Issuance mechanism
- Authorized agency
- Voluntary trading
- Carbon credit certificate
- Market-based mechanism
- Central Government authority

**TEXT:**

7. In section 14A of the principal Act,––

(a) for the marginal heading, the following shall be substituted, namely:––

"Issuance of energy savings certificate.";

(b) in sub-section (1), after the words "Central Government", the words "or any agency authorised by it" shall be inserted;

(c) in sub-section (2), the following proviso shall be inserted, namely:––

"Provided that any other person may also purchase energy saving certificate or carbon credit certificate on voluntary basis."

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Principal Act: Energy Conservation Act, 2001, Section 14A
- Section 14, clause (w) - Carbon credit trading scheme specification
- Section 8 - Insertion of Section 14AA (carbon credit issuance)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Energy savings certificates: Awarded to designated consumers or industries that exceed energy conservation targets
- Voluntary trading: Allows voluntary participation in carbon markets beyond mandatory requirements
- Authorized agencies: Ministry of Power, Bureau of Energy Efficiency, or designated implementing agencies

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:**
The amendments democratize the energy savings certificate and carbon credit market by: (1) allowing authorized agencies (not just government) to issue, and (2) enabling voluntary participation by any person. This creates a broader market for emissions reductions and energy efficiency improvements.

## CHUNK 5.2

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022, No. 19 of 2022

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Amendment Act to the Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**HIERARCHY:** Section 8 - Insertion of New Section 14AA

**PROVISION:** Section 14AA - Issuance of Carbon Credit Certificate

**TITLE:** Issuance and Trading of Carbon Credit Certificates by Registered Entities

**SUMMARY:** New Section 14AA provides the statutory framework for carbon credit certificate issuance: (1) the Central Government or any authorized agency may issue carbon credit certificates to registered entities that comply with the carbon credit trading scheme requirements, and (2) registered entities are entitled to purchase or sell carbon credit certificates in accordance with the scheme. This establishes the core legal mechanism for market-based carbon emissions trading.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Carbon credit certificate issuance
- Registered entity eligibility
- Compliance requirements
- Carbon credit trading
- Purchase and sale entitlement
- Trading scheme
- Central Government authority
- Authorized agency

**TEXT:**

8. After section 14A of the principal Act, the following section shall be inserted, namely:––

"14AA. (1) The Central Government, or any agency authorised by it may issue carbon credit certificate to the registered entity which complies with the requirements of the carbon credit trading scheme.

(2) The registered entity shall be entitled to purchase or sell the carbon credit certificate in accordance with carbon credit trading scheme specified under clause (w) of section 14."

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Principal Act: Energy Conservation Act, 2001, inserted after Section 14A
- Section 14, clause (w) - Carbon credit trading scheme specification
- Section 2(da) - Definition of "carbon credit certificate"
- Section 2(db) - Definition of "carbon credit trading scheme"
- Section 2(qa) - Definition of "registered entity"

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Scheme specification: Section 14, clause (w) empowers Central Government to specify the scheme
- Registered entities: Designated consumers and other entities registered under the scheme
- Compliance framework: Established by the Central Government in the trading scheme notification

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:**
Section 14AA operationalizes the carbon credit trading mechanism. Registered entities that demonstrate compliance with the carbon credit scheme can issue and trade certificates, creating a financial incentive for emissions reductions. This is India's primary market-based climate action tool in the energy sector, complementing regulatory mandates and supporting India's NDC commitments.

## CHUNK 5.3

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022, No. 19 of 2022

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Amendment Act to the Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**HIERARCHY:** Section 9 - Amendment of Section 15 (State Government Functions)

**PROVISION:** Section 9(i) - Amendments to Clause (a) of Section 15

**TITLE:** State Government Functions: Building Code Implementation and Building Bye-Laws Integration

**SUMMARY:** Clause (a) of Section 15 is amended in two respects: (1) the reference to "energy conservation building codes" is changed to "energy conservation and sustainable building codes," and (2) a new mandate is inserted requiring States to "implement the same through building bye-laws of the State," explicitly embedding building energy efficiency standards into State building regulations. This ensures enforcement at the State and local level.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- State government function
- Building codes notification
- Sustainable building codes
- Building bye-laws
- State implementation
- Local enforcement
- Energy efficiency standards
- Regulatory integration

**TEXT:**

9. In section 15 of the principal Act,––

(i) in clause (a),––

(I) for the words "energy conservation building codes", at both the places where they occur, the words "energy conservation and sustainable building codes" shall be substituted;

(II) after the words "energy in the buildings", the words "and implement the same through building bye-laws of the State" shall be substituted;

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Principal Act: Energy Conservation Act, 2001, Section 15, clause (a)
- Section 2(c) - Definition of "building"
- Section 2(v) - Definition of "energy conservation and sustainable building code"
- Section 4(ii) - Similar terminology change in Section 13(2)(d)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Building bye-laws: State Municipal Acts, State Construction Bylaws
- Local enforcement: Municipal corporations, local planning authorities
- Integration mechanism: State building regulations must incorporate energy and sustainability standards

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:**
This amendment creates a critical enforcement bridge by requiring State integration of national energy codes into State building regulations. This ensures that Central Government standards (set under clause (p) of section 14) are enforced through existing State building approval processes, addressing a historic implementation gap.

## CHUNK 5.6

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022, No. 19 of 2022

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Amendment Act to the Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**HIERARCHY:** Section 10 - Insertion of New Section 15A

**PROVISION:** Section 15A - Budget Preparation by Designated Agency

**TITLE:** Statutory Budget and Financial Reporting Requirements for State Designated Agencies

**SUMMARY:** New Section 15A requires designated agencies to prepare budgets in prescribed form and timing (typically annual), showing estimated receipts and expenditure, and to forward these to the State Government for inclusion in the State's annual budget. This ensures financial transparency and integration of designated agency finances into State budgeting processes.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Designated agency budget
- Annual budget preparation
- Estimated receipts and expenditure
- State budget integration
- Financial reporting
- Rule-based prescription

**TEXT:**

10. After section 15 of the principal Act, the following section shall be inserted, namely:—

"15A. The designated agency shall prepare, in such form and at such time in each financial year as may be prescribed, its budget for the next financial year, showing the estimated receipts and expenditure and forward the same to the State Government, which shall include the same in the annual budget."

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Principal Act: Energy Conservation Act, 2001, inserted after Section 15
- Section 15(ha) - Fee authority
- Section 57(bb) - Rule prescription for budget form and timing

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Designated agency functions: Section 15 of the principal Act
- State budget process: Annual budgeting by State Government
- Rule prescription: Form and timing prescribed under section 57(bb)

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:**
This section creates financial accountability for State designated agencies, ensuring they are transparent fiscal entities with documented receipts and expenditures reviewed by State Governments. This supports the professionalization of State-level energy conservation administration.

## CHUNK 5.7

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022, No. 19 of 2022

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Amendment Act to the Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**HIERARCHY:** Section 11 - Substitution of New Section 16

**PROVISION:** Section 16 - Establishment and Administration of State Energy Conservation Fund

**TITLE:** Statutory State Energy Conservation Fund: Composition and Administration

**SUMMARY:** Section 16 is completely substituted with a new framework for the State Energy Conservation Fund: (1) Sub-section (1) specifies sources of fund crediting—grants/loans from State or Central Government, fees received under the Act, and other State Government-decided sources; (2) Sub-section (2) specifies fund utilization for designated agency expenses and authorized objects under the Act; and (3) Sub-section (3) provides that administration modalities be prescribed in State rules. This creates a dedicated financial mechanism for energy conservation activities at the State level.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- State Energy Conservation Fund
- Fund composition
- Grant and loan credits
- Fee receipts
- Fund utilization
- Designated agency expenses
- Fund administration
- Rule-based prescription

**TEXT:**

11. For section 16 of the principal Act, the following section shall be substituted, namely:––

"16. (1) There shall be constituted a Fund for the purposes of promotion of efficient use of energy and its conservation within the State to be called the State Energy Conservation Fund and there shall be credited thereto––

(a) all grants and loans that may be made by the State Government or the Central Government or any other organisation or individual for the purposes of this Act;

(b) all fees received by the State Government or the designated agency under this Act;

(c) all sums received by the State Government or the designated agency from such other sources as may be decided by the State Government.

(2) The Fund shall be utilised for meeting the expenses––

(a) of the designated agency in the discharge of its functions;

(b) for the objects and purposes authorised by or under this Act.

(3) The Fund created under sub-section (1) shall be administered by such person or authority and in such manner as may be prescribed by the rules made by the State Government."

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Principal Act: Energy Conservation Act, 2001, Section 16 (now completely replaced)
- Section 15 - Designated agency functions
- Section 15A - Designated agency budget
- Section 15(ha) - Fee authority

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Fund sources: Multi-source financing including government, private entities
- Fund use: Designated agency operations and authorized energy conservation activities
- State autonomy: State rules prescribe administration modalities

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:**
The State Energy Conservation Fund is the financial backbone of State-level implementation. It ensures dedicated, multi-source financing for energy conservation activities, with flexibility for States to design administration through rules while maintaining Central Government oversight. This creates a sustainable financing model beyond annual government budget allocations.
