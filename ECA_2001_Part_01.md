# ENERGY CONSERVATION ACT, 2001 - CHAPTER I (PRELIMINARY)

---

## CHUNK 1: SECTION 1 - SHORT TITLE, EXTENT AND COMMENCEMENT

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Central Legislation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter I / Section 1

**PROVISION:** Section 1

**TITLE:** Short Title, Extent and Commencement

**SUMMARY:** This section establishes the name of the Act as "Energy Conservation Act, 2001," specifies its geographical extent across India except Jammu and Kashmir, and grants the Central Government authority to appoint the commencement date(s) through notification in the Official Gazette. Different provisions may be brought into force on different dates.

**KEY TOPICS:** 
- Act title and nomenclature
- Geographical applicability
- Commencement authority
- Phased implementation

**TEXT:**

"1. Short title, extent and commencement.—(1) This Act may be called the Energy Conservation Act, 2001.

(2) It extends to the whole of India except the State of Jammu and Kashmir.

(3) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint; and different dates may be appointed for different provisions of this Act and any reference in any such provision to the commencement of this Act shall be construed as a reference to the coming into force of that provision."

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** None (foundational provision)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** All subsequent sections in the Act are contingent on commencement under this section.

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:** This section defines temporal and geographical scope. Section 2 Amendment Act 2010 provides commencement date details via footnote: 28th June, 2012 for sections 31, 33, 44, 45.

## CHUNK 2: SECTION 2 - DEFINITIONS (PART A: ENTRIES A-F)

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Central Legislation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter I / Section 2 (Definitions)

**PROVISION:** Section 2(a) through Section 2(f)

**TITLE:** Definitions - Accredited Energy Auditor, Appellate Tribunal, Building, Bureau, Chairperson, Designated Agency

**SUMMARY:** These definitions establish foundational terms for the Act. An "accredited energy auditor" is a qualified professional accredited under Section 13(2)(p). "Appellate Tribunal" refers to the Energy Conservation tribunal established under Section 30. "Building" includes structures/erections with connected load ≥100 kW or contract demand ≥120 kVA used for commercial purposes (after energy conservation building codes are notified). "Bureau" means the Bureau of Energy Efficiency established under Section 3. "Chairperson" refers to the Governing Council chair. "Designated agency" is any agency designated under Section 15(d).

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Accreditation and auditor qualification
- Appellate authority
- Building definition and threshold (100 kW / 120 kVA)
- Bureau establishment
- Governance structure
- State-level agencies

**TEXT:**

"2. Definitions.—In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

(a) "accredited energy auditor" means an energy auditor accredited in accordance with the provisions of clause (p) of sub-section (2) of section 13;

(b) "Appellate Tribunal" means the Appellate Tribunal for Energy Conservation referred to in section 30;

(c) "building" means any structure or erection or part of structure or erection after the rules relating to energy conservation building codes have been notified under clause (p) of section 14 and clause (a) of section 15 and includes any existing structure or erection or part of structure or erection, which is having a connected load of 100 Kilowatt (kW) or contract demand of 120 Kilo-volt Ampere (kVA) and above and is used or intended to be used for commercial purposes;

(d) "Bureau" means the Bureau of Energy Efficiency established under sub-section (1) of section 3;

(e) "Chairperson" means the Chairperson of the Governing Council;

(f) "designated agency" means any agency designated under clause (d) of section 15;"

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** 
- Section 13(2)(p) - Auditor accreditation
- Section 3(1) - Bureau establishment
- Section 15(d) - Agency designation
- Section 30 - Appellate Tribunal
- Section 14(p) - Building codes notification (Central Government)
- Section 15(a) - Building codes notification (State Government)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** These definitions are used throughout the Act; building threshold of 100 kW/120 kVA is critical regulatory trigger point.

## CHUNK 3: SECTION 2 - DEFINITIONS (PART B: ENTRIES G-M)

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Central Legislation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter I / Section 2 (Definitions)

**PROVISION:** Section 2(g) through Section 2(m)

**TITLE:** Definitions - Designated Consumer, Energy, Energy Audit, Energy Conservation Building Codes, Energy Consumption Standards, Energy Management Centre, Energy Manager

**SUMMARY:** "Designated consumer" means any consumer specified under Section 14(e). "Energy" includes all forms derived from fossil fuels, nuclear substances, hydro-electricity, and renewable energy/bio-mass connected to grid. "Energy audit" is verification, monitoring, and analysis of energy use with recommendations and cost-benefit analysis. "Energy conservation building codes" are norms/standards for energy consumption per square metre, including building location. "Energy consumption standards" are process and consumption norms under Section 14(a). "Energy Management Centre" is the pre-existing centre established in 1989 under Government Resolution. "Energy manager" is a qualified individual under Section 14(m).

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Consumer designation
- Energy source classification
- Audit procedures and scope
- Building code standards
- Process standards
- Pre-Act institutional continuity
- Energy professional qualification

**TEXT:**

"(g) "designated consumer" means any consumer specified under clause (e) of section 14;

(h) "energy" means any form of energy derived from fossil fuels, nuclear substances or materials, hydro-electricity and includes electrical energy or electricity generated from renewable sources of energy or bio-mass connected to the grid;

(i) "energy audit" means the verification, monitoring and analysis of use of energy including submission of technical report containing recommendations for improving energy efficiency with cost benefit analysis and an action plan to reduce energy consumption;

(j) "energy conservation building codes" means the norms and standards of energy consumption expressed in terms of per square metre of the area wherein energy is used and includes the location of the building;

(k) "energy consumption standards" means the norms for process and energy consumption standards specified under clause (a) of section 14;

(l) "Energy Management Centre" means the Energy Management Centre set up under the Resolution of the Government of India in the erstwhile Ministry of Energy, Department of Power No. 7(2)/87- EP(Vol. IV), dated the 5th July, 1989 and registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 (21 of 1860);

(m) "energy manager" means any individual possessing the qualifications prescribed under clause (m) of section 14;"

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Section 14(e) - Designated consumer specification
- Section 14(a) - Energy consumption standards
- Section 14(m) - Energy manager qualifications

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** 
- Government of India Resolution dated 5th July, 1989 (pre-Act institutional basis)
- Societies Registration Act, 1860
- Building location as component of energy codes

## CHUNK 4: SECTION 2 - DEFINITIONS (PART C: ENTRIES MA-MAA AND N-U)

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Central Legislation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter I / Section 2 (Definitions)

**PROVISION:** Section 2(ma), (maa), (n) through (u)

**TITLE:** Definitions - Energy Savings Certificate, Equipment/Appliance, Governing Council, Member, Notification, Prescribed, Regulations, Schedule, State Commission, and Incorporation by Reference

**SUMMARY:** "Energy savings certificate" means certificates issued to designated consumers under Section 14A (added 2010 Amendment). "Equipment or appliance" means any device consuming, generating, transmitting, or supplying energy, producing desired work. "Governing Council" is the Bureau's governance body under Section 4. "Member" means Governing Council member(s) including Chairperson. "Notification" means Gazette of India or State Official Gazette. "Prescribed" means prescribed by rules. "Regulations" means Bureau regulations. "Schedule" means Schedule to the Act. "State Commission" refers to State Electricity Regulatory Commission under Electricity Regulatory Commissions Act, 1998. The Act also incorporates definitions from three prior electricity Acts (1910, 1948, 1998).

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Trading mechanism for energy savings
- Energy equipment scope
- Bureau governance structure
- Regulatory instruments hierarchy
- Gazette notification requirements
- Regulatory commission integration
- Cross-Act definition incorporation

**TEXT:**

"(ma) "energy savings certificate" means any energy savings certificate issued to the designated consumers under sub-section (1) of section 14A;

(maa) "equipment or appliance" means any equipment or appliance which consumes, generates, transmits or supplies energy and includes any device that consumes any form of energy and produces a desired work;

(n) "Governing Council" means the Governing Council referred to in section 4;

(o) "member" means the member of the Governing Council and includes the Chairperson;

(p) "notification" means a notification in the Gazette of India or, as the case may be, the Official Gazette of a State;

(q) "prescribed" means prescribed by rules made under this Act;

(r) "regulations" means regulations made by the Bureau under this Act;

(s) "Schedule" means the Schedule to this Act;

(t) "State Commission" means the State Electricity Regulatory Commission established under sub-section (1) of section 17 of the Electricity Regulatory Commissions Act, 1998 (14 of 1998);

(u) words and expressions used and not defined in this Act but defined in the Indian Electricity Act, 1910 (9 of 1910) or the Electricity (Supply) Act, 1948 (54 of 1948) or the Electricity Regulatory Commissions Act, 1998 (14 of 1998) shall have the meanings respectively assigned to them in those Acts."

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Section 4 - Governing Council composition
- Section 14A - Energy savings certificate issuance
- Section 14A(1) - Certificate issuance mechanism

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Electricity Regulatory Commissions Act, 1998 (14 of 1998) - State Commission establishment
- Indian Electricity Act, 1910 (9 of 1910) - Incorporated definitions
- Electricity (Supply) Act, 1948 (54 of 1948) - Incorporated definitions
- Gazette of India and State Official Gazettes - notification medium

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:** Section 2(u) creates a three-layer definition incorporation: definitions not in this Act are inherited from three prior electricity statutes in priority order. This ensures consistency across Indian energy legislation.
