DOCUMENT: The Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2010
DOCUMENT TYPE: Amendment Act - Section
HIERARCHY: Section 9
PROVISION: Section 9
TITLE: Substitution of New Section 30 - Appellate Tribunal

SUMMARY: This section replaces Section 30 of the principal Energy Conservation Act, 2001. The new Section 30 designates the Appellate Tribunal established under Section 110 of the Electricity Act, 2003, as the appellate authority for purposes of the Energy Conservation Act, 2001. This Appellate Tribunal shall hear appeals against orders of the adjudicating officer or any other authority under the Energy Conservation Act, 2001, or the Central Government, State Government, or any other authority under the Act. This amendment represents a significant change from whatever appellate mechanism previously existed, consolidating energy-related appeals under the unified Appellate Tribunal structure of the Electricity Act framework.

TEXT:

9. For section 30 of the principal Act, the following section shall be substituted, namely:—

"30. The Appellate Tribunal established under section 110 of the Electricity Act, 2003 shall, without prejudice to the provisions of the Electricity Act, 2003, be the Appellate Tribunal for the purposes of this Act and hear appeals against the orders of the adjudicating officer or the Central Government or the State Government or any other authority under this Act.".
