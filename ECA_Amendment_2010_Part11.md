PROVISION: Section 10
TITLE: Insertion of New Section 31A - Procedure and Powers of Appellate Tribunal

SUMMARY: This section inserts a new Section 31A into the Energy Conservation Act, 2001, establishing that the provisions of Sections 120 to 123 (both inclusive) of the Electricity Act, 2003, shall apply, mutatis mutandis (with necessary changes), to the Appellate Tribunal in the discharge of its functions under the Energy Conservation Act, 2001, as they apply to it in the discharge of its function under the Electricity Act, 2003. This provision extends the procedural framework and powers of the Appellate Tribunal established under the Electricity Act to cover energy conservation appeals, ensuring procedural consistency and coherence between the two energy sector regimes.

TEXT:

10. After section 31 of the principal Act, the following section shall be inserted, namely:—

"31A. The provisions of sections 120 to 123 (both inclusive) of the Electricity Act, 2003 shall, mutatis mutandis, apply to the Appellate Tribunal in the discharge of its functions under this Act as they apply to it in the discharge of its function under the Electricity Act, 2003.".
