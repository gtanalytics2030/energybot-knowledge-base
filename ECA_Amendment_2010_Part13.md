HIERARCHY: Section 12
PROVISION: Section 12
TITLE: Amendment of Section 54 - Omission of Appellate Authority References

SUMMARY: This section amends Section 54 of the principal Energy Conservation Act, 2001, by omitting references to the "Chairperson of the Appellate Tribunal or the Members of the Appellate Tribunal or officers or employees of the Appellate Tribunal or the Members of the State Commission or the." This amendment is consequential to the restructuring of the appellate mechanism in Sections 9-10 of this Amendment Act, which transfers appellate jurisdiction from separate Energy Conservation authorities to the Appellate Tribunal established under the Electricity Act, 2003. The omission reflects the elimination of references to an independent appellate structure for the Energy Conservation Act.

KEY TOPICS:
- Appellate authority restructuring

TEXT:

12. In section 54 of the principal Act, the words "Chairperson of the Appellate Tribunal or the Members of the Appellate Tribunal or officers or employees of the Appellate Tribunal or the Members of the State Commission or the" shall be omitted.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS:
- Section 54 of the Energy Conservation Act, 2001 (principal Act)

CROSS-REFERENCES:
- Section 30 of the Energy Conservation Act, 2001 (as substituted by Section 9 of this Amendment)
- Section 31A of the Energy Conservation Act, 2001 (as inserted by Section 10 of this Amendment)
- Appellate Tribunal under the Electricity Act, 2003
- Section 110 of the Electricity Act, 2003

KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:
This is a consequential amendment that reflects the consolidation of appellate mechanisms. References to independent Energy Conservation Appellate Tribunal structures are being removed because jurisdiction now rests with the unified Electricity Act Appellate Tribunal. This change simplifies administrative references in Section 54 of the principal Act.
