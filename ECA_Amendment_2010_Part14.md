TITLE: Amendment of Section 56 - Bureau Powers Regarding Energy Auditors and Certificates

SUMMARY: This section amends Section 56 of the principal Energy Conservation Act, 2001, in sub-section (2). It modifies clause (l) to expand the Bureau's authority from "energy managers" to include both "energy auditors and energy managers." It also inserts two new clauses: (la) prescribing procedures for issuing energy savings certificates under Section 14A, and (laa) establishing the value of per metric ton of oil equivalent of energy consumed under Section 14B. These amendments empower the Bureau to develop comprehensive regulations implementing the new energy savings certificate framework and establish energy equivalent values for compliance mechanisms.

TEXT:

13. In section 56 of the principal Act, in sub-section (2),—

(i) in clause (l), for the words "energy managers", the words "energy auditors and energy managers" shall be substituted;

(ii) after sub-clause (l), the following clauses shall be inserted, namely:—

"(la) prescribing the procedure for issuing the energy savings certificate under sub-section (1) of section 14A;

(laa) the value of per metric ton of oil equivalent of energy consumed under section 14B;".

(iii) clauses (s), (t) and (u) shall be omitted.

KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:
Section 56 is the Bureau's regulatory framework provision. This amendment is critical because it empowers the Bureau to make rules for energy savings certificate issuance and to set oil equivalent energy values. Queries about certificate procedures, energy auditor qualifications, and energy equivalent valuations should reference Section 56(2)(la) and (laa). The omission of clauses (s), (t), (u) indicates deletion of superseded regulatory provisions.
