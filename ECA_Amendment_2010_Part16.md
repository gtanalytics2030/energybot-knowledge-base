DOCUMENT: The Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2010
DOCUMENT TYPE: Amendment Act - Section
HIERARCHY: Section 15
PROVISION: Section 15
TITLE: Amendment of the Schedule - Designated Consumer Classification

SUMMARY: This section amends the heading of the Schedule to the Energy Conservation Act, 2001, by omitting the words "specified as designated consumers." The Schedule contains the classification and listing of consumers who are designated consumers for purposes of the Act, particularly for energy savings certificate compliance obligations. This amendment simplifies the heading of the Schedule while the substance of the consumer classifications remains unchanged. The Schedule lists energy-intensive industries and other establishments classified as designated consumers for compliance with energy efficiency obligations.

KEY TOPICS:
- Schedule amendment
- Designated consumer classification
- Energy intensive industries
- Heading omission
- Consumer categorization

TEXT:

15. In the Schedule to the principal Act, in the heading, the words "specified as designated consumers" shall be omitted.

KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:
The Schedule lists categories of energy-intensive industries and establishments that qualify as designated consumers. This amendment simplifies administrative references in the Schedule heading. Queries about which industries are designated consumers should reference the Schedule to the Energy Conservation Act, 2001 as amended.
