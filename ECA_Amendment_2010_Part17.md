DOCUMENT: The Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2010
DOCUMENT TYPE: Amendment Act - Section
HIERARCHY: Section 16
PROVISION: Section 16
TITLE: Amendment of Certain Enactments

SUMMARY: This section provides a general provision enabling amendments to other enactments as specified in the Schedule to the Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2010. The section indicates that certain other legislative Acts may be amended in the manner and to the extent specified in the detailed Schedule appended to this Amendment Act. This is a facilitating provision that allows for coordinated amendments to related legislation (particularly the Electricity Act, 2003, as detailed in THE SCHEDULE section of this document).

KEY TOPICS:
- Amendment of other Acts
- Schedule-based amendments
- Coordinated legislative change
- Electricity Act amendments
- Consequential amendments

TEXT:

16. The enactment specified in the Schedule to this Act shall be amended in the manner specified therein.

KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:
This is a gateway provision to THE SCHEDULE. It indicates that other Acts (particularly the Electricity Act, 2003) are being amended through this legislation. The specific amendments are detailed in THE SCHEDULE section. Any query about how this Amendment impacts other legislation should reference this section and THE SCHEDULE.
