TITLE: Amendment to the Electricity Act, 2003

SUMMARY: THE SCHEDULE to the Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2010 specifies amendments to other enactments as referenced in Section 16. It contains a single amendment to the Electricity Act, 2003. The Schedule amends Section 110 of the Electricity Act, 2003, by substituting the words "under this Act" with the words "under this Act or any other law for the time being in force." This amendment expands the Appellate Tribunal's jurisdiction established under the Electricity Act to cover matters arising under any other law in force, thereby enabling the unified Appellate Tribunal to hear appeals not only under the Electricity Act but also under other related legislation such as the Energy Conservation Act, 2001 (as amended by Section 9 of this Amendment Act). This change is integral to consolidating the appellate framework across energy sector legislation.

AMENDMENT TO THE ELECTRICITY ACT, 2003
(36 of 2003)

In section 110, for the words "under this Act", the words "under this Act or any other law for the time being in force" shall be substituted.

KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:
This is a critical amendment that operationalizes the consolidated appellate framework. By expanding Section 110 of the Electricity Act to cover "any other law for the time being in force," it legally enables the Appellate Tribunal established under the Electricity Act to hear energy conservation appeals. This change complements the amendments to Sections 30 and 31A of the Energy Conservation Act (Sections 9-10 of this Amendment Act). The three provisions work together to create a unified appellate structure across electricity and energy conservation regulation.

LEGISLATIVE COORDINATION:
This amendment demonstrates coordinated legislative amendment between the Energy Conservation Act and the Electricity Act, both central pillars of India's energy regulation framework. The expansion of the Electricity Act's Appellate Tribunal jurisdiction is necessary to support the energy conservation appellate references introduced by Sections 9-10 of this Amendment Act.
