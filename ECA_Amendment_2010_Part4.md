DOCUMENT: The Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2010
DOCUMENT TYPE: Amendment Act - Section
HIERARCHY: Section 3
PROVISION: Section 3
TITLE: Amendment of Section 9

SUMMARY: This section amends Section 9 of the principal Energy Conservation Act, 2001, by substituting the words "five years" for "three years" in sub-section (3). This amendment extends the time period from three years to five years for a specified provision under Section 9 of the principal Act. The exact nature of the extended period would be evident from the context of Section 9(3) in the principal Act.

KEY TOPICS:
- Temporal amendment
- Time period extension
- Section 9 modification
- Five-year tenure
- Principal Act amendment

TEXT:

3. In section 9 of the principal Act, in sub-section (3), for the words "three years", the words "five years" shall be substituted.
