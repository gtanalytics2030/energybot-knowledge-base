# Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022 - Batch 2: Definition Amendments

---

## CHUNK 2.1

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022, No. 19 of 2022

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Amendment Act to the Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**HIERARCHY:** Section 2 - Amendment of Section 2 (Definitions)

**PROVISION:** Section 2(i) - Substitution of Clause (c) - Definition of "building"

**TITLE:** Definition of Building (Amended)

**SUMMARY:** This clause amends the definition of "building" in the principal Act to include structures constructed after energy conservation and sustainable building codes have been notified, with a minimum connected load of 100 kW or contract demand of 120 kVA, used for commercial, office, or residential purposes. State Governments may specify lower thresholds.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Building definition
- Connected load threshold
- Contract demand specification
- Commercial purpose
- Residential purpose
- Office building
- State Government discretion
- Minimum specifications

**TEXT:**

In section 2 of the Energy Conservation Act, 2001 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act),––

(i) for clause (c), the following clause shall be substituted, namely:––

'(c) "building" means any structure or erection or part of structure or erection––

(i) constructed after the rules relating to energy conservation and sustainable building codes have been notified by the Central Government under clause (p) of section 14 and by the State Government under clause (a) of section 15;

(ii) which has a minimum connected load of 100 Kilowatt (kW) or contract demand of 120 Kilovolt Ampere (kVA); and

(iii) which is used or intended to be used for commercial purpose or as an office building or for residential purpose:

Provided that the State Government may specify a lower connected load or contract demand than the load or demand specified above;'

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Section 14, clause (p) of the principal Act - Central Government notification of energy conservation and sustainable building codes
- Section 15, clause (a) of the principal Act - State Government notification
- Principal Act: Energy Conservation Act, 2001, Section 2

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Electricity Act context: Building energy efficiency tied to minimum connected load thresholds relevant to industrial and commercial consumers
- Applicable to buildings with HT (High Tension) connections or substantial power demand

## CHUNK 2.2

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022, No. 19 of 2022

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Amendment Act to the Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**HIERARCHY:** Section 2 - Amendment of Section 2 (Definitions)

**PROVISION:** Section 2(ii) - Insertion of Clauses (da) and (db) - Carbon Credit Definitions

**TITLE:** Definitions of Carbon Credit Certificate and Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (Newly Inserted)

**SUMMARY:** Two new definitions are inserted: "carbon credit certificate" is a certificate issued by the Central Government or an authorized agency under section 14AA; "carbon credit trading scheme" is a notified scheme for reduction of carbon emissions under clause (w) of section 14. These provisions establish the legal foundation for carbon credit mechanisms in the energy sector.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Carbon credit certificate
- Carbon emissions reduction
- Central Government issuance
- Agency authorization
- Carbon credit trading scheme
- Renewable energy incentivization
- Environmental compliance

**TEXT:**

(ii) after clause (d), the following clauses shall be inserted, namely:––

'(da) "carbon credit certificate" means the certificate issued by the Central Government or any agency authorised by it under section 14AA;

(db) "carbon credit trading scheme" means the scheme for reduction of carbon emissions notified by the Central Government under clause (w) of section 14;'

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Section 14AA - Issuance of carbon credit certificate (newly inserted)
- Section 14, clause (w) - Central Government power to specify carbon credit trading scheme
- Principal Act: Energy Conservation Act, 2001, Section 2

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Energy sector context: Carbon credits support India's renewable energy targets and climate commitments
- Related to: Renewable Purchase Obligation (RPO) mechanisms under electricity regulations

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:**
These definitions establish the regulatory infrastructure for carbon credit trading in energy conservation, directly supporting India's Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) and renewable energy goals.

- Non-fossil sources
- Renewable energy
- Energy conservation scope
- All energy forms

**TEXT:**

'(h) "energy" means any form of energy derived from fossil fuels or non-fossil sources or renewable sources;'

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Principal Act: Energy Conservation Act, 2001, Section 2, clause (h)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Energy sector context: Aligns with India's integrated energy policy covering thermal, hydro, nuclear, wind, solar, and other renewable sources
- Related to GERC regulations: Green Energy Open Access and Renewable Purchase Obligations

## CHUNK 2.7

**DOCUMENT:** The Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022, No. 19 of 2022

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Amendment Act to the Energy Conservation Act, 2001

**HIERARCHY:** Section 2 - Amendment of Section 2 (Definitions)

**PROVISION:** Section 2(vii) - Insertion of Clauses (ta) and (tb) - Definitions of "vehicle" and "vessel"

**TITLE:** Definitions of Vehicle and Vessel (Newly Inserted)

**SUMMARY:** Two new definitions are inserted: "vehicle" is assigned the same meaning as in clause (28) of section 2 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 (expanding energy conservation scope to transportation); "vessel" includes all water craft types used in inland or coastal waters for transportation, storage, or accommodation, including ships, boats, tugs, barges, and specialized vessels like tankers and container vessels.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Vehicle definition
- Motor Vehicles Act alignment
- Vessel definition
- Inland waters
- Coastal waters
- Water craft types
- Transportation vessels
- Fuel consumption standards
- Merchant marine

**TEXT:**

(vii) after clause (t), the following clauses shall be inserted, namely:—

'(ta) "vehicle" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in clause (28) of section 2 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988;

(tb) "vessel" includes every description of water craft used or capable of being used in inland waters or in coastal waters, including any ship, boat, sailing vessel, tug, barge or other description of vessel including non-displacement craft, amphibious craft, wing-in-ground craft, ferry, roll-on-roll-off vessel, container vessel, tanker vessel, gas carrier or floating unit or dumb vessel used for transportation, storage or accommodation within or through inland waters and coastal waters;'

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, Section 2, clause (28) - Statutory definition of vehicle
- Principal Act: Energy Conservation Act, 2001, Section 2
- Section 6(i), (ii), (iii) of this Amendment - Extension of energy standards to vehicles and vessels

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Section 12(2) - Penalty provisions for non-compliance of fuel consumption norms by vehicle manufacturers
- Section 14(c) - Prohibition on manufacture/import of non-compliant vehicles and vessels
- Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022, Section 6(i)-(iii) - Extension of energy standards to these categories

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:**
These definitions extend energy conservation requirements to the transportation sector (vehicles) and maritime sector (vessels), expanding the Act's scope from buildings and industrial units to cover fuel consumption in all transport modes. This is critical for India's transport energy efficiency and climate commitments.
