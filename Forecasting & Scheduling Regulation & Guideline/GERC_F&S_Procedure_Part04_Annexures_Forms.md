**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Form / Annexure

**HIERARCHY:** Annexures / Annexure X

**PROVISION:** Annexure X

**TITLE:** QCA Registration Form – New Registration Application

**SUMMARY:**
Annexure X is the standardized form for new QCA registration applications submitted to Chief Engineer (LD), SLDC. The form requires QCA entity details (name, legal structure, registration number, address), authorized signatory information, and declaration of compliance with all qualifying criteria (experience, net worth, CMMI certification, staffing, capabilities). Applicants attach supporting documents (experience proof per Annexure 5.1, financial statements per Annexure 5.2, CMMI certificate per Annexure 5.5, personnel details per Annexure 5.4, MOA/AOA per Annexure 5.6, board resolution per Annexure 5.7, partner/director details per Annexure 5.8, undertaking per Annexure I(a)). This form serves as the official application gateway; incomplete or unsigned forms are rejected. QCAs must maintain a completed copy for records.

**KEY TOPICS:** QCA Registration, New Registration Form, Applicant Details, Authorized Signatory, Qualifying Criteria Declaration, Supporting Documents, Signature Block, Form Submission, SLDC Application

**TEXT:**
Annexure X is the QCA New Registration Form containing fields for:

## Chunk 40: Annexure XX – QCA Registration Form (Renewal)

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Form / Annexure

**HIERARCHY:** Annexures / Annexure XX

**PROVISION:** Annexure XX

**TITLE:** QCA Registration Renewal Form (3-Year Renewal)

**SUMMARY:**
Annexure XX is the abbreviated renewal form for existing QCAs applying to renew registration before 3-year expiry. The renewal form requires: current registration details, renewal of qualifying criteria confirmation (experience updated if applicable, net worth statement as of current fiscal year, CMMI certification validity verification, staffing status), updated signatory authorization, and list of active pooling stations. Renewal is simplified compared to new registration because basic entity structure doesn't change; however, QCAs must prove continued compliance with all criteria, particularly net worth (audited balance sheet required) and CMMI certification (must be current/not expired). Renewals must be submitted 3 months before expiry per Section 6.3. Renewals processed within 30 days of complete application allowing 60-day buffer before expiration.

**KEY TOPICS:** QCA Renewal, 3-Year Renewal, Existing QCA, Updated Documentation, Net Worth Verification, CMMI Validity, Staffing Status, Current Details, Simplified Form, Renewal Application

## Chunk 45: Annexure 5.5 – CMMI Level 3 Compatibility Certificate

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Notification No.1 2019 Forecasting, Scheduling, DSM & related matters Solar and Wind generation sources regulation 2019. Implementation Procedure

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Certificate / Annexure

**HIERARCHY:** Annexures / Annexure 5.5

**PROVISION:** Annexure 5.5

**TITLE:** Software Certification – CMMI Level 3 Certificate for Forecasting/Scheduling System

**SUMMARY:**
Annexure 5.5 is the CMMI (Capability Maturity Model Integration) Level 3 certification document issued by an authorized accredited assessor body confirming that QCA's forecasting and scheduling software meets international quality standards. The certificate must show: (1) QCA organization name, (2) Software/product name, (3) Assessment date and validity period, (4) Assessor organization and lead appraiser name, (5) Statement: "This certifies that the forecasting and scheduling software of [QCA] has achieved CMMI Level 3 indicating defined processes, quality assurance, configuration management, and continuous improvement practices," (6) Scope of CMMI assessment (applicable software modules), (7) Lead Appraiser authorization. Certificate validity is typically 3 years from assessment date; QCAs must maintain current certification or lose QCA status. Renewal requires reassessment by accredited body. The certificate provides regulatory assurance of software reliability for critical grid operations.

**KEY TOPICS:** CMMI Level 3, Software Certification, Capability Maturity Model, Quality Assurance, Process Maturity, Software Reliability, Accredited Assessor, Forecast/Scheduling System, Certificate Validity, 3-Year Validity

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Legal Document / Annexure

**HIERARCHY:** Annexures / Annexure I(a)

**PROVISION:** Annexure I(a)

**TITLE:** Undertaking by QCA – Responsibility for All Listed RE Generators

**SUMMARY:**
Annexure I(a) is a notarized undertaking by QCA on Rs. 200 stamp paper binding QCA to forecasting/scheduling responsibilities for all listed RE generators at the pooling station. The undertaking confirms: (1) QCA accepts responsibility for all pooling station generators listed, (2) QCA shall ensure 100% coverage of pool members in forecasting and DSM settlement (de-pooling), (3) QCA shall notify SLDC within 7 days of any generator addition/removal/capacity change, (4) QCA shall maintain de-pooling records and settlement calculations in auditable format, (5) QCA shall respect all generator fund transfers to DSM pool account. The undertaking includes the complete list of generators (name, capacity, technology, registration) making it a historical reference. Any generator not listed is not QCA's responsibility; similarly, listed generators automatically fall under QCA scope unless formally removed via written request.

**PROVISION:** Annexure I(b)

**TITLE:** Consent and Authorization by RE Generator for QCA Appointment

**SUMMARY:**
Annexure I(b) is the individual authorization letter from each RE generator (signed by MD/CEO or authorized signatory) authorizing the QCA to represent them for forecasting, scheduling, and DSM settlement. Each generator must issue separate authorization to the designated QCA; this letter is binding proof of QCA authorization. The letter should state: (1) "We hereby authorize [QCA Name] to represent our generation facility [Generator Name, Capacity, Location] for forecasting and scheduling services," (2) QCA is authorized to submit schedules, revise forecasts, and manage DSM settlement, (3) Authorization is valid for the registration period and continues unless revoked in writing with 30 days notice, (4) Generator consents to de-pooling of DSM charges by QCA, (5) Generator acknowledges QCA's accountability to SLDC. This letter protects both QCA (proving authorization) and SLDC (ensuring genuine generator consent). QCA must retain original authorization letters and provide photocopies with registration.

**KEY TOPICS:** Generator Authorization, Consent Letter, QCA Appointment, Forecasting Authority, Scheduling Authority, DSM Settlement Authority, De-Pooling Consent, Revocation Notice Period, Binding Authorization

**TITLE:** Bank Guarantee Format – Payment Security for QCA DSM Liability

**SUMMARY:**
The Bank Guarantee (BG) is an unconditional, irrevocable financial instrument issued by a scheduled bank guaranteeing QCA's payment obligation to SLDC for DSM liabilities. The BG format is standardized: (1) Guarantor Bank letterhead with local branch details (Vadodara preferred for SBI branches), (2) Beneficiary: "State Load Despatch Center (SLDC), GETCO, Vadodara, 390021," (3) Guaranteed Amount: "110% of projected monthly DSM liability for [Pooling Station Name]" (calculated from mock trial), (4) Guarantor: QCA legal name and address, (5) Guarantee validity: "Till [expiry date] plus 60 days claim period," (6) Unconditional terms: "In case of default by [QCA], we undertake to pay to SLDC within 7 days of demand without any conditionality," (7) Jurisdiction: "Vadodara courts have exclusive jurisdiction over disputes," (8) Authorized bank signatory with seal and date. QCAs must maintain BG throughout registration period; expiry triggers automatic suspension of forecasting rights. Self/Lead RE Generators use identical BG format but guarantor is the generator instead of QCA.

**PROVISION:** Letter of Credit Format

**TITLE:** Standby Letter of Credit (SFMS/LC) – Alternative Payment Security for DSM Liability

**SUMMARY:**
The Letter of Credit (LC) is an alternative to Bank Guarantee, structured as an irrevocable revolving LC (auto-reinstating after each payment). SFMS (SWIFT standard format) LCs are transmitted internationally and carry trade bank standards. The LC is issued by a scheduled bank for the QCA or Self/Lead RE Generator's account to SLDC as beneficiary. Key LC terms: (1) Beneficiary: "State Load Despatch Center (SLDC), GETCO, Vadodara," (2) Applicant: QCA or RE Generator, (3) LC Amount: "110% of projected monthly DSM liability" (auto-revolving: after SLDC draws down on LC, LC automatically reinstates to original amount), (4) Validity: "3 years + 45 days credit period," (5) Drawn amount payment: "Immediate / Within 7 days" per stipulation, (6) Interest: "Applicant pays 1.25% per month on drawn amounts for delayed payments exceeding 30 days," (7) Through: "SBI, Old Padra Road, Shreejal Building, Vadodara – IFSC: SBIN0010687," (8) SFMS transmission ensures regulatory authenticity. LCs provide similar security to BGs but with faster international transfer capability.
