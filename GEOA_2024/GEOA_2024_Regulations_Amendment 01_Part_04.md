TITLE: Amendment in Clause 17.6(viii) of the Principal Regulations - Banking Facility and Charges with Rate Specification

SUMMARY:
Section 3 of the First Amendment Regulations amends Clause 17.6(viii) of the Principal Regulations by substituting the banking charge specification. The amended provision establishes a banking charge rate of Rs. 1.50 per unit (kWh), effective from the date of notification of these Amendment Regulations (September 23, 2024) through March 31, 2025. The provision allows for an earlier date if the Commission issues separate notifications regarding charges. After March 31, 2025 (or such earlier date), the banking charge shall be determined by separate notification issued by the Commission under the provisions of the Regulations.

TEXT:

**Section 3. Amendment in Clause 17.6 (viii) of the Principal Regulations.**

The Regulation 17.6 (viii) shall be substituted as under:

**"17.6 Banking facility and Charges**

........

**(viii) The banking charge at the rate of Rs. 1.50 per unit shall be effective from the date of this Regulations up to 31st March 2025 or earlier date on which the Commission notifies such charges. Thereafter the banking charge for the period starting from the separate notification by Regulations notified by the Commission shall be applicable".**

BANKING CHARGE RATE DETAILS:
**Rate:** Rs. 1.50 per unit
**Unit:** Per kWh (kilowatt-hour)
**Effective Date:** From the date of notification of these Amendment Regulations (September 23, 2024, being the date of publication in The Gujarat Government Gazette, Extra No. 102, Vol. LXV, dated September 23, 2024 / Asvina 1, 1946)
**Expiration Date:** March 31, 2025, or such earlier date as the Commission may notify
**Subsequent Charges:** After the expiration date, banking charges shall be determined by separate notification(s) issued by the Commission under the Regulations

NOTES:
1. This is a temporary banking charge rate valid for a period of approximately 6 months (from September 23, 2024 to March 31, 2025) or until the Commission issues an earlier notification.

2. The charge of Rs. 1.50 per unit represents the cost recovery mechanism for the banking facility provided by the distribution licensee to Green Energy Open Access consumers for injecting surplus renewable energy into the grid and drawing equivalent energy as needed.

3. The provision explicitly allows the Commission to issue a separate notification at any time before March 31, 2025 to modify the banking charges, creating regulatory flexibility for the Commission to adjust rates based on grid conditions, costs, or policy considerations.

4. Post-March 31, 2025, the banking charge shall be governed by separate notification(s) issued by the Commission, which may differ from the Rs. 1.50 per unit rate specified in this amendment.

5. This charge applies to all Green Energy Open Access consumers as defined in the Principal Regulations, including consumers, trading licensees, distribution licensees, and Generating Companies or Captive Generating Plants availing of the Open Access facility.
