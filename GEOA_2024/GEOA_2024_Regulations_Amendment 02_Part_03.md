DOCUMENT TYPE: Amendment Regulation / Notification
HIERARCHY: Section 2 / Amendment to Principal Regulations
PROVISION: Section 2 - Amendment in Clause No. 1(4) of the Principal Regulations
TITLE: Amendment to Regulation 1(4): Banking Charge Effective Date in Principal Regulations

SUMMARY:
Section 2 of the Second Amendment substitutes Regulation 1(4) of the Principal Regulations (GERC GEOA 2024, Notification No. 8 of 2024) to establish the temporal validity of Banking Charges under Regulation 17.6. The amended clause provides that the Banking Charge specified in Regulation 17.6 shall become effective from the date of notification of the amending Regulations and shall remain in force until 30th September 2025, or such earlier date as the Commission may notify through a separate notification, whichever comes first. This amendment establishes a sunset date and renewal mechanism for the Banking Charge provisions.

TEXT:

**2. Amendment in Clause No. 1 (4) of the Principal Regulations.**

The Clause 1 (4) shall be substituted as under:

"**1. Short Title, Extent and Commencement**

……

**4) The Banking Charge specified in the Regulation 17.6 shall be effective from the date of notification of these Regulations up to 30th September 2025 or earlier date on which the Commission notifies such charges through a separate notification of Regulation**."

KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:
The Banking Charge becomes operative only after the date of notification and has an automatic sunset clause on 30th September 2025 unless: (a) the Commission issues a separate notification specifying new charges, or (b) an earlier date is notified by the Commission. Consumers and DISCOMs should reference this commencement date for Banking Charge applicability in contract terms and billing systems.
