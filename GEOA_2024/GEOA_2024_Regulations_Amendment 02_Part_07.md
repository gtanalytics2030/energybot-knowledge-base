SUMMARY:
The GERC issued the Second Amendment to Green Energy Open Access Regulations on 28th March 2025 (Notification No. 06 of 2025) to operationalize Banking Charges under the Principal Regulations (GERC GEOA 2024, Notification No. 8 of 2024). The amendment makes two coordinated changes: (1) it inserts an effective date requirement for Banking Charges in Regulation 1(4), specifying they become effective from the notification date; and (2) it substitutes Regulation 17.6(viii) to fix the Banking Charge rate at Rs. 1.50 per unit (kWh). Both provisions contain an automatic sunset clause on 30th September 2025, after which the Banking Charge will be subject to Commission re-notification through separate Regulations. The amendment addresses a critical gap in the Principal Regulations by providing time-bound Banking Charge rates and mechanisms for cost recovery by distribution licensees managing Green Energy Open Access surpluses.
