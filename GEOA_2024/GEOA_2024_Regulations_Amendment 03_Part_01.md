TITLE: Preamble and Authority for Third Amendment Regulations, 2025
SUMMARY: This preamble establishes the legal authority for the third amendment to the GERC Green Energy Open Access Regulations 2024. The amendment is issued by GERC under powers conferred by Sections 39, 40, 42, 61 & 86 (read with Section 181) of the Electricity Act 2003. Published in the Gujarat Government Gazette Extraordinary (Vol. LXVI, Extra No. 273) on September 25, 2025, these amendments modify specific provisions of the Principal Regulations concerning banking charges and effective dates.
