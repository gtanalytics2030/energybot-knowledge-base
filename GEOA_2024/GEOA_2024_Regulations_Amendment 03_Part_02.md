SUMMARY: This section establishes the formal short title, territorial scope, and commencement date of the Third Amendment Regulations 2025. The amendment is named the "Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Terms & Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2025." It applies to the entire State of Gujarat and comes into force from the date of publication in the Official Gazette (September 25, 2025).

TEXT:

**SECTION 1: SHORT TITLE, SCOPE, EXTENT AND COMMENCEMENT**

**(i) Short Title:**

These Regulations shall be called:

**"Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Terms & Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2025"**

---

**(ii) Extent (Territorial Scope):**

These Regulations shall extend to the whole of the State of Gujarat.

---

**(iii) Commencement Date:**

These Regulations shall come into force from the date of their publication in the **Official Gazette.**

**Actual Date of Publication:** Thursday, September 25, 2025
**Gazette Reference:** The Gujarat Government Gazette Extraordinary, Vol. LXVI, Extra No. 273, Part IV-C, dated September 25, 2025 / Asvina 3, 1947 (Vikram Samvat)

---

---

**LEGAL SIGNIFICANCE:**

1. **Retroactive/Prospective Application:** All amendments contained in this notification become effective simultaneously upon publication in the Official Gazette, creating uniform commencement across all modified provisions.

2. **Hierarchical Authority:** As an amendment regulation, this does not replace the Principal Regulations but modifies specific clauses therein. The Principal Regulations remain in effect, as amended by this Notification and any prior amendments (including Notification No. 16 of 2024 – First Amendment).

3. **Territorial Jurisdiction:** Extension to the "whole of the State of Gujarat" means these amendments apply uniformly to all distribution licensees, open access consumers, generating companies, and transmission licensees operating within Gujarat.

---

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:** This section establishes that the Third Amendment became effective immediately upon publication on September 25, 2025. Any query about the validity date of the amended banking charges or the amended commencement framework should reference this section. The "date of publication" commencement clause is critical for determining when open access consumers and generators must comply with the new banking charge rates specified in Regulation 17.6(viii).
