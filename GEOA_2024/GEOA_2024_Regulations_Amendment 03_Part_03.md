DOCUMENT TYPE: Amendment Regulation / Notification
HIERARCHY: Notification No. 08 of 2025 / Section 2
PROVISION: Section 2 – Amendment in Regulation 1(4) of the Principal Regulations
TITLE: Amendment to Regulation 1(4) – Banking Charge Effective Period and Revision Framework
SUMMARY: Section 2 substitutes Regulation 1(4) of the Principal Regulations (2024) to specify the temporal validity of banking charges referenced in Regulation 17.6. The amended regulation establishes that banking charges specified in Regulation 17.6 are effective from the date of notification of these (Third Amendment) Regulations through March 31, 2026, or until an earlier date when the Commission notifies revised charges through separate regulation. This creates a transitional framework with a defined expiry date (March 31, 2026) for current banking charge rates.

**DETAILED PROVISION BREAKDOWN:**

**1. Banking Charge Reference:**

The regulation specifically references:
- **Source Regulation:** Regulation 17.6 of the Principal Regulations
- **Specific Sub-Clause:** Regulation 17.6(viii) (as amended by Section 3 of this Notification)
- **Subject Matter:** Banking Charge Rate and Applicability

**2. Commencement Date – Three Interpretations:**

**Interpretation A (Restrictive):** 
The phrase "date of notification of these Regulations" refers to the date of notification of the Third Amendment Regulations (September 25, 2025).

**Interpretation B (Inclusive):**
The phrase could reference the date of original publication of the Principal Regulations or subsequent amendments, but the most recent interpretation emphasizes September 25, 2025 as the operative commencement date of these banking charge validity rules.

**Interpretation C (Technical/Gazette Date):**
The "date of notification" = the official publication date in the Gujarat Government Gazette = September 25, 2025 (or the date any Government Press copy is certified).

**Operative Date:** **September 25, 2025** (Date of publication in The Gujarat Government Gazette Extraordinary, Extra No. 273)

---

**3. Terminal/Expiry Dates – Dual Framework:**

**Primary Expiry Date:**
- **Date:** March 31, 2026
- **Meaning:** Banking charges specified in Regulation 17.6(viii) automatically expire and no longer apply after this date unless earlier revoked or retained
- **Days from Commencement:** Approximately 6 months (from September 25, 2025 to March 31, 2026)

**Alternative/Earlier Expiry Date:**
- **Condition:** "Earlier date on which the Commission notifies such charges"
- **Mechanism:** "through a separate notification of Regulation"
- **Meaning:** The Commission may issue a new regulation notifying revised banking charges before March 31, 2026, superseding the rates specified in Regulation 17.6(viii) as amended
- **Example:** If on January 15, 2026 the Commission publishes Regulation 17.6 Amendment 4 with new banking charge rates, that date becomes the effective expiry date for the current rates (September 25, 2025 notification)

---

**4. Continuity Provision:**

**Implicit Framework Established:**
After the expiry date (March 31, 2026 or earlier), the banking charges regime transitions to:
- **New rates notified by separate regulation** (if Commission acts before March 31, 2026), OR
- **Default/Legacy rates** (if Commission does not issue new notification by March 31, 2026 – not specified in this document)

**Gap Risk:** The regulation does not specify what banking charges apply after March 31, 2026 if no separate notification is issued. This creates a potential gap requiring separate regulatory action.

**LEGAL IMPLICATIONS:**

**1. Temporal Limitation:**
The amended Regulation 1(4) imposes a definite time limit on the validity of banking charges specified in the amended Regulation 17.6. This is a significant change, potentially indicating:
- A review period for banking charge adequacy
- A compliance/stakeholder feedback window
- An intention to revise rates before/on March 31, 2026

**2. Regulatory Planning Requirement:**
The Commission must plan to either:
- Issue a new separate regulation before March 31, 2026 with revised banking charges, OR
- Formally extend the current rates through another amendment

**3. Consumer/Participant Expectations:**
Open access consumers and generating companies relying on current banking charges must anticipate potential changes by March 31, 2026.

**4. Compliance Framework:**
The "separate notification of Regulation" mechanism creates a formal, transparent procedure for charge revision (not informal announcements or circulars).

---

**INTERACTION WITH SECTION 3 AMENDMENT:**

Section 2 (this provision) sets the **validity period** of the rates.
Section 3 specifies the **actual rates** (Rs. 1.50 per unit).
Together, they create: **Rs. 1.50/unit banking charges effective September 25, 2025 – March 31, 2026 (or earlier revision).**

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:** 

This regulation is critical for responding to queries about when banking charges change or expire. Queries like:
- "Until when will the Rs. 1.50/unit banking charge apply?" → Answer: "Until March 31, 2026 or earlier revision date"
- "What banking charges apply after March 31, 2026?" → Answer: "Refer to Section 1(4) of the Principal Regulations as amended; charges revert to rates specified in a separate GERC regulation to be notified"
- "When does the current banking regime end?" → Answer: "March 31, 2026 (unless extended or earlier revised)"

This provision also signals a **planned regulatory review** of banking charges with a 6-month window from commencement.
