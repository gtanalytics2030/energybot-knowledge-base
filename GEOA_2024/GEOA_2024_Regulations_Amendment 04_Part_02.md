PROVISION: Section 2 - Amendment in Regulation 1 (4) of the Principal Regulations
TITLE: Amendment to Regulation 1(4) - Banking Charge Effective Date and Duration (30 June 2026 Sunset Clause)
SUMMARY: Section 2 of the Fourth Amendment modifies Regulation 1(4) of the Principal Regulations (GERC Green Energy Open Access Regulations 2024) to establish a time-bound framework for banking charges. The amended Regulation 1(4) specifies that the banking charges mentioned in Regulation 17.6 shall be effective from the date of notification of these 2026 amendments until 30 June 2026, or until an earlier date on which the Commission notifies revised charges through a separate notification. This creates an automatic sunset provision requiring the Commission to either renotify charges or allow them to expire.

**SECTION 2: AMENDMENT IN REGULATION 1 (4) OF THE PRINCIPAL REGULATIONS**

Regulation 1 (4) of the Principal Regulations shall be substituted as under:

**"1. Short Title, Extent and Commencement**

……………

**4) The Banking Charge specified in the Regulation 17.6 shall be effective from the date of notification of these Regulations up to 30th June 2026 or earlier date on which the Commission notifies such charges through a separate notification of Regulation."**

KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT: 
(1) SUNSET MECHANISM: The amendment introduces a sunset/expiry date of 30 June 2026 for the banking charges, requiring the Commission to take affirmative action (separate notification) to extend or modify charges beyond this date. This is a temporary provision mechanism.

(2) TIME-BOUND SCOPE: Banking charges are effective from the date the Fourth Amendment Regulations 2026 become effective (upon publication in Official Gazette, 30 March 2026) and expire on 30 June 2026 unless superseded earlier.

(3) PROCEDURE REQUIRED: Any extension or modification of banking charges after 30 June 2026 requires a separate Commission notification under Regulation 1(4) or through a new regulatory amendment. This prevents automatic continuation of charges.

(4) EMBEDDED COORDINATION: This amendment coordinates with Section 3's amendment to Regulation 17.6(viii), which specifies the charge quantum (Rs. 1.50 per unit) and similarly establishes the same sunset provision. Both provisions must be read together for complete legal effect.

(5) REGULATORY FLEXIBILITY: The "earlier date" alternative allows the Commission to modify banking charges before the standard 30 June 2026 expiry date through separate notification, providing regulatory flexibility to respond to market conditions or policy changes.

REGULATORY INTERACTION:
- This amendment affects participants in Green Energy Open Access schemes in Gujarat
- Banking facilities allow open access consumers to bank excess renewable energy for withdrawal in future periods
- The time-bound charge rate provides certainty for a defined period (March 30 - June 30, 2026, approximately 3 months)
- Participants must plan for potential charge modification or removal post-30 June 2026
