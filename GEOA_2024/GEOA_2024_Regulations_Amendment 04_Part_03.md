TITLE: Amendment to Regulation 17.6(viii) - Banking Facility and Charges: Rate of Rs. 1.50 per Unit (Time-Limited to 30 June 2026 with Contingent Future Applicability)
SUMMARY: Section 3 of the Fourth Amendment modifies Regulation 17.6(viii) of the Principal Regulations to prescribe a specific banking charge rate and duration. The amended provision establishes a banking charge rate of Rs. 1.50 per unit effective from the notification date (30 March 2026) until 30 June 2026, or until an earlier date on which the Commission notifies revised charges through separate notification. After 30 June 2026, any future banking charges will be determined by the Commission through separate regulatory notification, with the rate and terms to be specified in that separate order.

TEXT:

**SECTION 3: AMENDMENT IN REGULATION 17.6 (viii) OF THE PRINCIPAL REGULATIONS**

Regulation 17.6 (viii) of the Principal Regulations shall be substituted as under:

**"17.6. Banking facility and Charges**

……….

**(viii) The Banking Charge at rate of Rs. 1.50 per unit shall be effective from the date of notification of these Regulations up to 30th June 2026 or earlier date on which the Commission notifies such charges through a separate notification of Regulation. Thereafter, the banking charge for the period starting from the separate notification by Regulations notified by the Commission shall be applicable."**

KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:

(1) CHARGE QUANTUM & UNIT: Banking charge is Rs. 1.50 per unit. "Unit" in electricity regulation refers to kilowatt-hour (kWh). This is the rate payable by open access consumers for utilizing banking facilities.

(2) EFFECTIVE DATE: Charges are effective from the date these Amendment Regulations 2026 are published in the Official Gazette. Document header shows publication date as 30 March 2026. Therefore banking charges commence on 30 March 2026.

(3) TEMPORARY/TIME-LIMITED CHARGE:
   - Fixed duration: 30 March 2026 to 30 June 2026 (approximately 3 months)
   - Automatic sunset on 30 June 2026 unless earlier superseded
   - Charges do NOT automatically continue beyond 30 June 2026
   - Commission must take affirmative regulatory action for future charges

(4) EARLY TERMINATION MECHANISM: The Commission may modify, reduce, or withdraw banking charges before 30 June 2026 by issuing a separate notification through Regulation. No need to wait for 30 June deadline.

(5) FUTURE APPLICABILITY CLAUSE: The phrase "Thereafter, the banking charge for the period starting from the separate notification by Regulations notified by the Commission shall be applicable" creates a framework for post-June-30-2026 charges:
   - Future charges will be determined by separate regulatory notification
   - Rate, duration, and conditions will be specified in that future order
   - Current Rs. 1.50 per unit rate is NOT retroactively extended unless explicitly renotified

(6) COORDINATION WITH REGULATION 1(4): This amendment cannot be read in isolation. Regulation 1(4) (Section 2 of this Amendment) also establishes the same sunset provision, confirming the time-limited nature of the charges.

(7) CONSUMER IMPACT: Open access consumers availing banking facilities in the Green Energy scheme pay Rs. 1.50 per unit for the privilege of banking excess renewable generation during the March-June 2026 period.

(8) REGULATORY PRUDENCE: The time-limited charge structure allows the Commission to:
   - Assess banking facility utilization during the 3-month trial period
   - Gather data on consumer response and economic impact
   - Determine whether banking charges should be continued, modified, or withdrawn after June 2026
   - Make informed policy decision with factual basis rather than indefinite commitment

DEFINITION SUPPORT:
- **Banking**: The ability of open access consumers (particularly renewable generators) to inject excess electricity into the grid during favorable conditions and withdraw equivalent electricity during unfavorable conditions, subject to prescribed charges and losses.
