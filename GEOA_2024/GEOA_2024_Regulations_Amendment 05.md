PROVISION: GERC/2026/05/Tech/1225 (Notification No. 05 of 2026)

TITLE: GERC Fifth Amendment to Green Energy Open Access Regulations 2024 – Authority, Scope, and Commencement

SUMMARY:
The Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission, under Sections 39, 40, 42, 61, 86, and 181 of the Electricity Act 2003, issues the Fifth Amendment to the Green Energy Open Access Regulations 2024 (the "Principal Regulations"). This notification amends specific provisions of the Principal Regulations regarding banking charges effective dates and rates. The amendments extend across the State of Gujarat and come into force upon publication in the Official Gazette, dated 30th June 2026. This notification supersedes and modifies the Principal Regulations 2024 in two substantive areas: Regulation 1(4) (Banking Charge Applicability Period) and Regulation 17.6(viii) (Banking Facility Charges Rate).

KEY TOPICS: Regulatory Amendment, Banking Facility, Banking Charges, Green Energy Open Access, GERC Authority, Electricity Act 2003, Notification, Regulation, Commencement

## Chunk 2: Regulation 1(4) Amendment – Banking Charge Effective Dates

---

DOCUMENT: Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Terms & Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) (Fifth Amendment) Regulations, 2026

DOCUMENT TYPE: Regulatory Amendment Notification

HIERARCHY: Amendment Section 2 / Regulation 1(4)

PROVISION: Regulation 1(4) of the Principal Regulations (as amended by Fifth Amendment)

TITLE: Amendment to Regulation 1(4) – Banking Charge Applicability Period and Effective Date

SUMMARY:
This amendment modifies Regulation 1(4) of the Principal Regulations to specify the temporal applicability of banking charges. The amendment stipulates that banking charges specified in Regulation 17.6 shall be effective from the date of notification of the Fifth Amendment Regulations up to and including 31st August 2026, or such earlier date as the Commission may notify through a separate regulatory notification. This establishes a sunset provision for the banking charges regime, requiring the Commission to issue a new notification before 31st August 2026 to determine charges for the subsequent period.

KEY TOPICS: Banking Charge, Effective Date, Regulation Period, Temporal Applicability, Notification, Sunset Provision, 31st August 2026, Commission Authority, Separate Notification

Regulation 1 (4) of the Principal Regulations shall be substituted as under: 

"1. Short Title, Extent and Commencement
……………
4) The Banking Charge specified in the Regulation 17.6 shall be effective from the date of notification of these Regulations up to 31st August 2026 or earlier date on which the Commission notifies such charges through a separate notification of Regulation."

## Chunk 3: Regulation 17.6(viii) Amendment – Banking Facility Charges Rate and Duration

---

DOCUMENT: Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Terms & Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) (Fifth Amendment) Regulations, 2026

DOCUMENT TYPE: Regulatory Amendment Notification

HIERARCHY: Amendment Section 3 / Regulation 17.6 / Sub-clause (viii)

PROVISION: Regulation 17.6(viii) of the Principal Regulations (as amended by Fifth Amendment)

TITLE: Amendment to Regulation 17.6(viii) – Banking Facility Charge Rate (Rs. 1.50 per Unit) and Temporal Applicability

SUMMARY:
This amendment establishes the banking charge rate at Rs. 1.50 per unit, effective from the date of notification of the Fifth Amendment Regulations through 31st August 2026, or an earlier date if the Commission notifies new charges through a separate regulatory notification. After the conclusion of this period or upon the Commission's separate notification, banking charges shall be determined through a separate notification by the Commission under the regulatory framework. The amendment preserves the Commission's discretion to modify banking charges prospectively via regulatory notification, establishing a 2-month (approximately) window for consultation and determination of post-August 2026 banking charge rates.

KEY TOPICS: Banking Charge Rate, Rs. 1.50 per Unit, Banking Facility, Effective Date, Temporal Duration, 31st August 2026, Separate Notification, Regulatory Authority, Charge Determination

TEXT:

**Amendment in Regulation 17.6 (viii) of the Principal Regulations:**

Regulation 17.6 (viii) of the Principal Regulations shall be substituted as under:

………..

Thereafter, the banking charge for the period starting from the separate notification by Regulations notified by the Commission shall be applicable."
