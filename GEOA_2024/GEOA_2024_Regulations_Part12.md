QUESTION: "What if I disagree with an SNA decision?"
PRIMARY REGULATION: 19 (Dispute Resolution)

PROCESS:
1. **First-tier:** SNA dispute resolution procedure (internal)
2. **Appeal:** To GERC Commission within 30 days of SNA order
3. **Decision:** Commission disposes within 3 months of appeal receipt

=== THEME 14: EXISTING CONSUMERS (GRANDFATHERED) ===

QUESTION: "I had GEOA under 2011 Regulations. Does 2024 affect me?"
PRIMARY REGULATIONS: 11 (Existing consumers), 33 (Saving), 34 (Notwithstanding Repeal)

ANSWER: No change (unless you voluntarily opt for 2024 Regulations)
- Continue under original grant order terms
- No forced migration to 2024 Regulations
- New capacity = fresh application under 2024 Regulations
- Charges per original grant order

EXAMPLE: LTOA granted in 2020 for 5 MW, allocation period 15 years → continues under 2020 terms through 2035

=== THEME 15: INFORMATION & REPORTING ===

QUESTION: "Where can I find GEOA data and market prices?"
PRIMARY REGULATIONS: 24 (Information System), 25 (Reports)

**SLDC publishes on website (Regulation 24):**
- Monthly GEOA consumer count
- Annual GEOA report (consumers, capacity, energy wheeled)
- Floor Rates (Day-Ahead Market reference prices)
- Energy Handed Over Via (EHV) by generator/drawal point
- Wheeling losses by technology/route
- Data lag: ≤1 month

**Nodal agencies submit monthly to GERC (Regulation 25):**
- Consumer name, injection point, drawal point, capacity (MW), month
