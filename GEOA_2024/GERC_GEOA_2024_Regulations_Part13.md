TITLE: Definition of Green Energy

SUMMARY:
Green Energy is broadly defined to include electrical energy from renewable sources (hydro, solar, wind, storage using renewable energy) and any technology notified by Government of India. The definition explicitly encompasses green hydrogen and green ammonia, extending beyond traditional renewable sources. This expansive definition future-proofs the Regulations for emerging green technologies and aligns with national green energy policy objectives.

TEXT:

**Regulation 3(o): Green Energy**

"Green Energy" means the electrical energy from renewable sources of energy including hydro and storage (if the storage uses renewable energy) or any other technology as may be notified by the Government of India from time to time and shall also include any mechanism that utilizes green energy to replace fossil fuels including production of green hydrogen or green ammonia and any other sources as may be determined by the Central Government as per the provision of Clause G of sub-rule(2) of rule 4 of Electricity (Promoting Renewable Energy Through Green Energy Open Access), Rules 2022.

TECHNOLOGY SCOPE:
The definition covers:
1. Conventional renewables: Solar, Wind, Hydro, Biomass, Bagasse
2. Emerging technologies: Green Hydrogen, Green Ammonia
3. Non-fossil waste-to-energy: Municipal Solid Waste conversion
4. Storage: Energy storage using renewable energy sources
5. Future technologies: Subject to Government of India notification

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION:
This definition explicitly excludes fossil fuels (coal, lignite, gas, liquid fuel) as the definition states green energy mechanisms "utilize green energy to replace fossil fuels." The scope is intentionally technology-neutral to accommodate future renewable technologies notified by Government of India.
