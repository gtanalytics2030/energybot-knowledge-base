SUMMARY:
This chunk covers critical institutional and consumer-related definitions spanning regulatory bodies (Central Commission, Commission), nodal agencies (Central Nodal Agency), market structures (Day Ahead Market), consumer categories (Consumer, Developer, Entity, Existing Open Access Consumer), and operational concepts. These definitions establish the organizational structure governing Green Energy Open Access and identify key participant categories essential to implementation.

TEXT:

**Regulation 3: Definitions (continued)**

(f) "Central Commission" means the Central Electricity Regulatory Commission referred to in sub-section (1) of Section 76 of the Act.

(g) "Central Nodal Agency" means a Central Nodal Agency as notified by the Central Government to set up and operate a single window Green Energy Open Access system for Green Energy (Renewable Energy) as specified by Ministry of Power in the Electricity (Promoting Renewable Energy through Green Energy Open Access) Rules, 2022.

(h) "Commission" means the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission constituted under the Act.

(i) "Consumer" means any person who is supplied with electricity for his own use by a licensee or the Government or by any other person engaged in the business of supplying electricity to the public under the Electricity Act, 2003 or any other law for the time being in force and includes any person whose premises are for the time being connected for the purpose of receiving electricity with the works of a licensee, the Government or such other person, as the case may be.

(j) "Developer" means the generating company who generate electrical energy from renewable sources of energy.

(k) "Day Ahead Market (DAM)" means a market where Day Ahead Contracts are transacted on the Power Exchange(s).

(l) "Entity" means any consumer who has contracted demand or sanctioned load of Hundred (100) KW or more either through single connection or through multiple connections aggregating Hundred (100) KW or more located in the same electricity division of a distribution licensee, except for captive consumers.

(m) "Existing Open Access Consumer/Customer" means a person already availing open access for sourcing/supplying renewable (green) energy from/to the transmission system and/or distribution system of the licensees in the State under an existing agreement or Commission's Regulations/Orders on the date of coming into force of these Regulations.

INSTITUTIONAL HIERARCHY:

**Central Level:**
- Central Commission (CERC): Regulatory authority per Section 76 of Electricity Act, 2003
- Central Nodal Agency: Set up by Central Government per Ministry of Power notification
- Power Exchange(s): Where Day Ahead Contracts are transacted

**State Level (Gujarat):**
- Commission (GERC): Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission per Electricity Act, 2003
- State Nodal Agency (SNA): Comprising SLDC (Short-Term), STU (Medium/Long-Term), Distribution Licensee

**Application Flow:**
Applicant → Central Nodal Agency Portal → State Nodal Agency → Appropriate Licensee/SLDC

CRITICAL DISTINCTION: "Entity" vs "Green Energy Open Access Consumer"

**Entity (Definition 3(l)):**
- Minimum capacity: 100 kW (strict)
- Application: General open access (may or may not be green)
- Captive status: Explicitly EXCLUDED
- Purpose: Consumer or distribution licensee (non-generator)

**Green Energy Open Access Consumer (Definition 3(p)):**
- Minimum capacity: 100 kW (for non-captive) or NO LIMIT (for captive)
- Application: Specifically for green energy open access
- Captive status: Specifically INCLUDED with no capacity restriction
- Purpose: Consumer, licensee, or generating company (inclusive)

**Implication:** A consumer below 100 kW can be a Green Energy Open Access Consumer if they establish their own Captive Generating Plant, but cannot be classified as an "Entity" for general open access purposes.

DEVELOPER IDENTITY:

"Developer" specifically means "the generating company who generate electrical energy from renewable sources of energy." This excludes:
- Non-RE generators (fossil fuel plants)
- Traders who don't generate
- Conventional power generators not using RE sources
- Captive generators not classified as "generating company"

Distinction from "Generating Company": A developer is a subset of generating companies - specifically those generating from renewable sources.

DAY AHEAD MARKET (DAM) FRAMEWORK:

"Day Ahead Market (DAM)" is where Day Ahead Contracts are transacted on Power Exchange(s). This enables:
- Short-term energy trading
- Real-time scheduling
- Spot market pricing
- Daily contract settlement
- Congestion-responsive pricing (floor rate mechanism per Regulation 24)

Regulatory Reference: Per Regulation 14, DAM transactions for GEOA must be approved by State Nodal Agency before 15:00 hours on D-1 (day before).

EXISTING OPEN ACCESS CONSUMER/CUSTOMER PROTECTION:

Definition 3(m) provides critical transition provision:
- "Existing" means: Already availing GEOA on date of Regulation commencement (21 Feb 2024)
- Basis: "under an existing agreement or Commission's Regulations/Orders"
- Treatment: Per Regulation 11 - continue under prior regime until original agreement term expires
- Implication: No forced migration to new Regulation requirements mid-contract

This protects investors who had committed under prior GERC Open Access Regulations, 2011.

CONSUMER DEFINITION SCOPE:

"Consumer" (Definition 3(i)) is deliberately broad:
- Includes: Anyone receiving electricity "for his own use"
- Source: Licensee, Government, or other persons in electricity supply business
- Connection: Premises connected for receiving electricity
- Status: Whether premises currently connected or envisaged to be connected
- Scope: All persons meeting supply criteria are consumers

This broad definition encompasses:
- Domestic consumers
- Commercial/Industrial consumers
- Agricultural consumers
- Government institutions
- NGOs and charitable organizations
- Any entity receiving electricity supply

Importantly, "Consumer" is distinct from "Entity" and "Green Energy Open Access Consumer" - not all consumers are eligible for open access, only those meeting specific capacity thresholds.
