TITLE: Transmission Charges, Wheeling Charges, Cross-Subsidy Surcharge, and Additional Surcharge

SUMMARY:
This chunk covers the four primary charge categories for GEOA: (1) Transmission Charges per CERC (inter-state) or GERC MYT Regulations + Tariff Orders (intra-state, by technology: Wind, Solar, Hybrid, Biomass, Bagasse, Hydro, Mini/Small/Micro Hydro, MSW, Green H2, Green NH3); (2) Wheeling Charges determined by Commission on energy wheeled, including wheeling losses; (3) Cross-Subsidy Surcharge levied per unit on actual energy consumed via GEOA (non-applicable to Captive for self-use, MSW, frozen for 12 years from commissioning, capped at 20% of ACoS, linearly reduced to zero for <12-year LTOA); (4) Additional Surcharge (max = DL fixed cost of power purchase, linearly reduced to zero within 4 years, not applicable to Captive, MSW, offshore wind to Dec 2032). These charges collectively establish the economic framework for GEOA viability.

- Transmission charge determination (CERC inter-state, GERC intra-state)

- Wheeling charge mechanics and losses
- Cross-Subsidy Surcharge (CSS) applicability and limitations

- CSS cap (20% of Average Cost of Supply)

**Regulation 17: Charges and Tariffs**

**17.1 Transmission Charges**

The Transmission Charges shall be determined and paid as follows:

(a) **Inter-State Transmission Charges:** For power transmitted through the Inter-State Transmission System (InSTS), transmission charges shall be as determined and published by the Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (CERC) from time to time under its applicable Regulations.

(b) **Intra-State Transmission Charges:** For power transmitted within the State through the State Transmission Network, transmission charges shall be determined by the Commission in accordance with the GERC Multi-Year Tariff (MYT) Regulations and based on the Tariff Orders issued by the Commission for the respective financial year.

(c) **Technology-Specific Tariff Orders:** The Commission shall issue separate Tariff Orders for Transmission Charges applicable to Green Energy sources, categorized as follows:
   - Wind Energy
   - Solar Energy
   - Wind-Solar Hybrid Energy
   - Biomass Energy
   - Bagasse Cogeneration
   - Hydro Energy
   - Mini Hydro Energy
   - Small Hydro Energy
   - Micro Hydro Energy
   - Municipal Solid Waste (MSW) to Energy
   - Green Hydrogen Generation
   - Green Ammonia Generation
   - Any other technology as notified by the Government of India / Commission from time to time

(d) **Applicability:** These transmission charges shall apply to the quantum of power being transported through the State Transmission System on behalf of the Green Energy Open Access consumer.

**Cross-Reference:** Regulation 17.1 is distinct from Transmission Charges on inter-state routes which follow CERC guidelines.

---

**17.2 Wheeling Charges**

The Wheeling Charges shall be levied and collected as follows:

(a) **Levying Authority:** The Wheeling Charges shall be determined by the Commission under the powers vested in the Commission under Section 62 of the Electricity Act, 2003.

(b) **Basis of Calculation:** Wheeling Charges shall be calculated on a per-unit (kWh) basis on the quantum of energy that is wheeled through the distribution licensee's network for the Green Energy Open Access consumer.

(c) **Wheeling Losses:** In addition to Wheeling Charges, the Green Energy Open Access consumer shall pay wheeling losses applicable to the distribution network through which energy is being wheeled, as determined by the Commission from time to time.

(d) **Loss Percentage:** The wheeling losses shall be calculated and charged as a percentage of actual energy wheeled and shall be based on the technical losses prevalent in the respective distribution licensee's network.

(e) **Collection Mechanism:** Wheeling Charges and losses shall be collected by the respective distribution licensee and remitted to the transmission licensee/other authorized entities as per Commission's Orders.

(f) **Rate Determination:** The Wheeling Charges rate shall be specified in the respective Commission's Tariff Order and shall be reviewed annually or as per the tariff cycle specified by the Commission.

**Reference:** Section 62 of Electricity Act, 2003; Regulation 3(dd) "Wheeling"

**17.3 Cross-Subsidy Surcharge (CSS)**

The Cross-Subsidy Surcharge shall be applicable and computed as follows:

(a) **Applicability:** Cross-Subsidy Surcharge shall be levied on a per-unit (kWh) basis on the actual energy consumed by the Green Energy Open Access consumer through open access on the distribution network.

(b) **Payment to Distribution Licensee:** The CSS shall be paid to the distribution licensee in the area of supply where the Green Energy Open Access consumer is located.

(c) **Exemptions:**

   **(i) Captive Generating Plant for Self-Use:** CSS shall NOT be levied on energy generated by a Captive Generating Plant and consumed by the captive consumer for its own use, as defined in Regulation 3(e). This exemption applies only to the extent of self-consumption and does not apply to surplus energy exported to the grid.

   **(ii) Municipal Solid Waste (MSW) to Energy:** CSS shall NOT be applicable to power generated from MSW-to-Energy projects.

   **(iii) Offshore Wind Projects:** CSS shall NOT be applicable to power generated from offshore wind projects commissioned up to December 31, 2032.

(d) **CSS Freeze Period - 12 Years from Commissioning:**

   For renewable energy projects commissioned after the date of coming into force of these Regulations, the Cross-Subsidy Surcharge shall be frozen at the rate applicable on the date of grant of Green Energy Open Access and shall not be increased for a period of 12 years from the date of commissioning of the respective Green Energy Generating Plant.

   *Provided* that this freeze shall apply only to projects obtaining GEOA and Commercial Operation Date (COD) after the notification date of these Regulations (21 February 2024).

(e) **CSS Cap - 20% of Average Cost of Supply (ACoS):**

   The Cross-Subsidy Surcharge charged by a distribution licensee shall not exceed 20% of the Average Cost of Supply (ACoS) of the distribution licensee as determined by the Commission in its latest Tariff Order.

   **Formula:** CSS ≤ 20% × ACoS

(f) **Long-Term Open Access (LTOA) with Maturity < 12 Years:**

   For Long-Term Green Energy Open Access consumers whose contract period is less than 12 years (e.g., 10-year LTOA agreements), the CSS shall be linearly reduced to zero over the period of the contract.

   **Example:** For a 10-year LTOA:
   - Year 1-2: 80% of applicable CSS
   - Year 3-4: 60% of applicable CSS
   - Year 5-6: 40% of applicable CSS
   - Year 7-8: 20% of applicable CSS
   - Year 9-10: 0% CSS

(g) **No Increase Beyond 50% in First 12 Years:**

   For all Green Energy Open Access consumers, during the first 12 years from the date of commissioning of the respective renewable energy generating plant, the CSS shall not be increased by more than 50% of the CSS in force on the date of commissioning.

   **Limitation:** Even if ACoS increases significantly, CSS increase is capped at 50% over 12 years.

(h) **Review and Revision:** After the freeze period and 12-year protection period, CSS shall be subject to annual review and revision by the Commission as per its tariff determination process.

**CRITICAL DISTINCTION:** CSS differs from Additional Surcharge (Regulation 17.4):
- CSS: Per-unit charge on energy consumed via GEOA; objective = cross-subsidy recovery
- AS: Fixed cost component; objective = cost-of-supply recovery; reduced linearly to zero

**17.4 Additional Surcharge (AS)**

The Additional Surcharge shall be applicable and computed as follows:

(a) **Definition and Purpose:** The Additional Surcharge is a charge levied to recover the fixed cost component of power purchase incurred by the distribution licensee, against the capacity that would have otherwise been supplied by the licensee to the open access consumer.

(b) **Calculation Basis:**

   **Maximum AS = Per-unit fixed cost of power purchased by Distribution Licensee**

   The Additional Surcharge charged by the distribution licensee shall not exceed the per-unit fixed cost of power purchase applicable to the consumer's tariff category.

   **Formula:** AS (max) = (Total Fixed Cost of DL Power Purchase) / (Total Contracted Demand or Annual Consumption, as applicable)

(c) **Partial Exemption - Contracted Demand Maintenance:**

   The Additional Surcharge shall NOT be applicable to the extent of the contracted demand / sanctioned load that is maintained by the consumer from the distribution licensee, even after obtaining Green Energy Open Access.

   **Example:** If a consumer's contracted demand is 500 kW and they source 300 kW via GEOA and 200 kW from DL:
   - AS applies only to the 300 kW portion sourced via GEOA
   - The 200 kW maintained from DL is exempt from AS

(d) **Linear Reduction to Zero - 4-Year Period:**

   The Additional Surcharge shall be linearly reduced to zero over a period of 4 years from the date of grant of Green Energy Open Access.

   **Formula:** AS in Year N = (5 - N) / 4 × Maximum AS, where N = 1, 2, 3, 4

   **Example:** If maximum AS = Rs. 1.00/kWh:
   - Year 1 (0-1 year from GEOA): Rs. 1.00/kWh
   - Year 2 (1-2 years from GEOA): Rs. 0.75/kWh
   - Year 3 (2-3 years from GEOA): Rs. 0.50/kWh
   - Year 4 (3-4 years from GEOA): Rs. 0.25/kWh
   - Year 5+ (4+ years from GEOA): Rs. 0.00/kWh (zero)

(e) **Exemptions:**

   **(i) Captive Generating Plant:** AS shall NOT be applicable to Captive Generating Plant consumers, including single consumers and cooperative/association consumers as defined in Regulation 3(e) and Schedule I.

   **(ii) Municipal Solid Waste (MSW) to Energy:** AS shall NOT be applicable to power generated from MSW-to-Energy projects.

   **(iii) Offshore Wind Projects:** AS shall NOT be applicable to power generated from offshore wind projects commissioned up to December 31, 2032.

(f) **No Surcharge on Capacity Shortfall:**

   The Additional Surcharge shall NOT be charged on the portion of the consumer's contracted demand / sanctioned load that the consumer continues to procure from the distribution licensee. Only the portion actually sourced via open access is subject to AS.

(g) **Review After 4 Years:** After the 4-year linear reduction period, AS becomes zero and is not re-imposed unless specifically revised by the Commission through a separate Tariff Order.

**CRITICAL DISTINCTIONS BETWEEN CSS AND AS:**

| Feature | Cross-Subsidy Surcharge (CSS) | Additional Surcharge (AS) |
|---------|------------------------------|--------------------------|
| **Basis** | Per-unit charge on energy consumed | Fixed cost recovery component |
| **Calculation** | Rs./kWh on actual consumption | Rs./kWh equivalent of fixed cost |
| **Freeze Period** | 12 years from commissioning | Not applicable |
| **Cap** | 20% of ACoS | Max DL fixed cost per unit |
| **Reduction** | Only for <12-year LTOA (linear to zero) | Linear to zero over 4 years |
| **Exemptions** | Captive (self-use), MSW, offshore wind to Dec 2032 | Captive, MSW, offshore wind to Dec 2032 |
| **Purpose** | Recover cross-subsidy obligations | Recover DL fixed cost component |
| **Payee** | Distribution licensee in area of supply | Distribution licensee |

TARIFF ORDER REFERENCE FRAMEWORK:

Technology-specific Tariff Orders shall be issued annually or per tariff cycle specifying:
1. Transmission Charges (Rs./kWh) for each renewable source
2. Wheeling Charges (Rs./kWh) and loss percentages
3. Updated CSS rates and caps
4. Updated AS rates (though AS reduces linearly per Regulation 17.4)

Applicants must reference the Tariff Order **in force on the date of GEOA grant** (per Regulation 13 effective date) for charge determination.
