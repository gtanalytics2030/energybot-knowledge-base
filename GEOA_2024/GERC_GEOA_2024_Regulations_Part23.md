TITLE: Relinquishment of Green Energy Open Access - Charges, Timelines, and Penalties

SUMMARY:
Regulation 17.10 establishes the procedure and charges for relinquishing Green Energy Open Access across all three categories (Long-Term, Medium-Term, Short-Term). LTOA relinquishment attracts 1-year notice = no charge; <1 year notice = 66% of T&W charges for shortfall. LTOA <12 years: 66% NPV + 66% shortfall. MTOA: 30-day notice = minimal charges; longer waiver. STOA: 2-day minimum notice; pay T&W for 2 days or actual cancellation period. The regulation protects the licensee's revenue while providing reasonable exit pathways for consumers facing genuine force majeure.

**Regulation 17.10: Relinquishment of Green Energy Open Access and Charges for Shortfall**

The charges for relinquishment of Green Energy Open Access shall be determined as follows:

**(I) LONG-TERM GREEN ENERGY OPEN ACCESS (LTOA) - Greater Than 12 Years**

For Long-Term Green Energy Open Access with contract period of 12 years or more from the date of grant:

(a) **Relinquishment with 12 Months' Notice:**
   - **Charges:** NIL (No charges payable)
   - **Notice Period:** Minimum 12 calendar months in advance
   - **Effect:** Upon expiry of 12-month notice period, GEOA stands terminated without any financial penalty

(b) **Relinquishment with Less Than 12 Months' Notice:**
   - **Shortfall Charges:** 66% (Sixty-Six percent) of the applicable Transmission and Wheeling (T&W) Charges for the shortfall period (remaining balance of the contract or 12 months, whichever is less)
   - **Calculation:** Shortfall Charges = 66% × T&W Rate × Remaining Capacity × Shortfall Period
   - **Payment Due:** Within 30 days of relinquishment submission
   - **Example:** If annual T&W charges are Rs. 10,00,000 and consumer relinquishes with 6 months' notice, charges = 66% × Rs. 10,00,000 × 6/12 = Rs. 3,30,000

**(II) LONG-TERM GREEN ENERGY OPEN ACCESS (LTOA) - Less Than 12 Years**

For Long-Term Green Energy Open Access with contract period less than 12 years (e.g., 5-year, 10-year LTOA agreements):

(a) **Relinquishment with 12 Months' Notice:**
   - **Charges:** 66% (Sixty-Six percent) of Net Present Value (NPV) of remaining contract duration's T&W charges
   - **NPV Calculation:** NPV = Σ [Annual T&W Charges / (1 + Discount Rate)^n], where n = years from relinquishment date
   - **Discount Rate:** As specified in the respective Transmission and Wheeling Agreements or as prescribed by Commission
   - **Effect:** Consumer may exit after 12 months by paying 66% NPV of remaining term

(b) **Relinquishment with Less Than 12 Months' Notice:**
   - **Charges:** 66% (Sixty-Six percent) of NPV of remaining contract duration's T&W charges PLUS
   - **Shortfall Charges:** 66% of T&W charges for the shortfall period (period between actual relinquishment date and 12-month notice expiry)
   - **Total Payable:** 66% NPV + 66% Shortfall T&W Charges
   - **Example:** 5-year LTOA with annual T&W = Rs. 5,00,000; consumer relinquishes in Year 3 without 12-month notice:
     - NPV of Year 3-5 remaining = approx. Rs. 12,00,000 (discounted)
     - Shortfall period = 12 months
     - Total charges = (66% × Rs. 12,00,000) + (66% × Rs. 5,00,000) = Rs. 7,92,000 + Rs. 3,30,000 = Rs. 11,22,000

(c) **Special Provision - Force Majeure:**
   - In case of genuine force majeure events (natural calamities, Government orders, unforeseen circumstances causing plant closure), the consumer may approach the Commission with documentary evidence for waiver/reduction of relinquishment charges. The Commission shall examine such cases on merit within 60 days.

**(III) MEDIUM-TERM GREEN ENERGY OPEN ACCESS (MTOA)**

For Medium-Term Green Energy Open Access with contract period of 3 months to 3 years:

(a) **Relinquishment with 30 Days' Notice:**
   - **Charges:** Applicable Transmission and Wheeling charges for the period of 30 days or actual contract remainder, whichever is less
   - **Notice Period:** Minimum 30 calendar days in advance
   - **Calculation:** Charges = T&W Rate × Capacity × 30 days (or remaining contract period if <30 days)
   - **Payment Due:** Within 15 days of contract termination date

(b) **Relinquishment with Less Than 30 Days' Notice:**
   - **Charges:** Applicable Transmission and Wheeling charges for the full shortfall period (period between actual relinquishment and 30-day notice expiry)
   - **Calculation:** Shortfall Charges = T&W Rate × Capacity × Shortfall Days
   - **Payment Due:** Within 15 days of relinquishment

(c) **Waiver Provision:**
   - If consumer provides advance notice of at least 30 days before proposed relinquishment date, T&W charges for the 30-day notice period may be waived at the discretion of the State Nodal Agency, subject to grid stability and network availability.
   - If consumer requests waiver and it is granted, relinquishment is permitted at no additional charge.

**(IV) SHORT-TERM GREEN ENERGY OPEN ACCESS (STOA)**

For Short-Term Green Energy Open Access with contract period up to 1 month:

(a) **Minimum Notice Requirement:**
   - **Notice Period:** Minimum 2 calendar days in advance of intended relinquishment date
   - **Notice Submission:** To State Nodal Agency / SLDC before 17:00 hours (IST) on the day of notice

(b) **Charges for Relinquishment:**
   - **Basic Charges:** Transmission and Wheeling charges applicable for the first 2 days of contract period or actual relinquishment period, whichever is less
   - **Operating Charges:** Additional operating charges for 2 days or cancellation period from generator, whichever is applicable
   - **Total Payable:** T&W Charges (2 days) + Operating Charges (2 days)

(c) **Early Cancellation After Commencement:**
   - If STOA consumer cancels after energy injection has commenced (after Day 1):
     - **Charges:** T&W charges and operating charges for the period from commencement to actual cancellation date (minimum 2 days)
     - **No Refund:** No refund of charges paid for the days already operated

(d) **Special Case - Grid Emergency:**
   - In case of grid emergency or system security issues, the State Nodal Agency / SLDC may cancel STOA with immediate effect and without notice. In such cases, consumer shall pay T&W charges only for the period actually wheeled, without additional penalties.

**(V) GENERAL PROVISIONS FOR ALL CATEGORIES**

(a) **Transmission Charges Distinction:**
   - Relinquishment charges comprise only Transmission and Wheeling charges as defined in Regulations 17.1 and 17.2
   - Cross-Subsidy Surcharge (CSS) and Additional Surcharge (AS) are NOT included in relinquishment charges (as per Regulations 17.3 and 17.4)
   - Banking Charges (if any) shall be settled as per actual banking reconciliation for the period up to relinquishment

(b) **Payment Security Release:**
   - Upon successful relinquishment and settlement of all charges, the payment security (Bank Guarantee, LC, or advance deposits) shall be released within 30 days of final charge clearance
   - If relinquishment charges remain unpaid, payment security shall be encashed for recovery

(c) **No Continuation After Relinquishment:**
   - Upon relinquishment, GEOA agreement stands terminated
   - Consumer cannot resume open access under the same agreement; fresh application required per Regulation 13

(d) **Metering and Energy Reconciliation:**
   - Final energy reading shall be recorded at 00:00 hours (IST) on the date of relinquishment
   - Final billing including all charges shall be prepared within 10 days of relinquishment date
   - Any banking credit remaining at relinquishment shall be settled per Regulation 17.6 banking terms or forfeited if consumer does not opt for carry-forward (per regulation)

(e) **Dispute Resolution:**
   - Any dispute regarding relinquishment charges or calculation methodology shall be referred to the State Nodal Agency first
   - If unresolved, appeal may be filed with the Commission within 30 days per Regulation 19
   - Commission shall dispose of such appeal within 3 months

(f) **Licensee Obligations:**
   - Upon relinquishment submission, GETCO and distribution licensee shall:
     - Acknowledge receipt within 2 working days
     - Calculate final charges within 10 working days
     - Provide itemized bill within 10 working days
     - Release payment security within 30 days of charge clearance

KEY LEARNING POINTS FOR ENERGY BOT:

1. **LTOA ≥12 years**: Most flexible - 12-month notice = no charge exit
2. **LTOA <12 years**: Less flexible - 66% NPV charges for full remaining term
3. **MTOA**: Moderate flexibility - 30-day notice = minimal T&W charges only
4. **STOA**: Least flexible - 2-day minimum notice; minimal exit window
5. **Force Majeure**: Relief available via Commission petition with proof
6. **CSS/AS NOT Included**: Relinquishment charges are T&W only, not surcharges
7. **Payment Security**: Released after 30 days of charge settlement
