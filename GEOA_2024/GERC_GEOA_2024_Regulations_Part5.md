DOCUMENT: GERC (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations, 2024
DOCUMENT TYPE: GERC Regulation
HIERARCHY: Regulation 3 - Definitions
PROVISION: Regulation 3(p)
TITLE: Definition of Green Energy Open Access Consumer

SUMMARY:
A Green Energy Open Access Consumer is an entity with contracted demand or sanctioned load of 100 kW or more (single or aggregated connections in same electricity division) that is supplied green energy for its own use from green energy sources via licensee, captive generation, or other electricity supply businesses. Notably, captive consumers have no capacity restriction. This definition is central to determining eligibility for green energy open access and understanding the scope of beneficiaries.

- GEOA consumer eligibility
- Contracted demand threshold (100 kW)
- Single and aggregated connections
- Electricity division jurisdiction
- Captive consumption rights

TEXT:

**Regulation 3(p): Green Energy Open Access Consumer**

"Green Energy Open Access Consumer" means any person who has contract demand or sanctioned load of Hundred (100) kW or more, either through single connection or through multiple connections aggregating Hundred (100) kW or more located in same electricity division of a distribution licensee, shall be eligible to avail Green Energy through Open Access, who is supplied with electricity from green energy sources for his own use by a licensee or the Government or from its own Captive Generation Plant or by any other person engaged in the business of supplying electricity to the public under the Electricity Act, 2003 or any other law for the time being in force and includes any person whose premises are for the time being connected for the purpose of receiving green energy with the works of a licensee, the Government or such person, as the case may be.

Provided that there shall be no capacity restriction for setting up of RE projects for captive use with respect to the consumer's contract demand/sanctioned load (kW/MW, KVA/MVA) with Discoms availing power under Green Energy Open Access.

ELECTRICITY DIVISION REQUIREMENT:
Consumer connections must be "located in same electricity division of a distribution licensee" - this is a geographic constraint ensuring that multiple connections are within the same supply jurisdiction for administration and metering purposes. Different electricity divisions would require separate applications.

CAPACITY THRESHOLD EXCEPTION:
The proviso is critical: captive consumers have NO capacity restriction. This means a small consumer (even below 100 kW) can set up renewable capacity for self-consumption and obtain open access for captive use, while non-captive consumers require minimum 100 kW contracted/sanctioned load.

SUPPLY SOURCES:
A GEOA consumer can source green energy from:
1. Licensee (Distribution or Transmission Licensee)
2. Government (when Government is supplier)
3. Own Captive Generation Plant (CGP)
4. Any other person engaged in electricity supply business under Electricity Act, 2003
5. Any other lawful electricity supplier

RELATED DEFINITIONS:
- "Entity" (Regulation 3(l)) - similar but excludes captive consumers from no-capacity provision
- "Applicant" (Regulation 3(b))
- "Consumer" (Regulation 3(i)) - generic definition
- "Captive Generating Plant" (Regulation 3(e))

KEY DISTINCTION FROM "ENTITY":
Entity definition (3(l)) specifically excludes captive consumers and maintains 100 kW restriction for all consumers. GEOA Consumer definition includes captive consumers with NO capacity restriction - a significant operational distinction.
