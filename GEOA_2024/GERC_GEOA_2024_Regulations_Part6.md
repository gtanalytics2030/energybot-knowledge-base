PROVISION: Regulation 3(y)
TITLE: Definition of State Nodal Agency (SNA)

SUMMARY:
The State Nodal Agency (SNA) is the nodal agency designated for grant of open access in Gujarat, comprising multiple entities with distinct roles: Gujarat State Load Dispatch Centre (SLDC) operates as SNA for Short-Term Open Access to Intra-State Transmission System; State Transmission Utility (STU) operates as SNA for Medium-Term and Long-Term access to Intra-State Transmission Network; Distribution Licensee coordinates with SNA for Distribution Network cases. This tripartite structure ensures appropriate jurisdictional handling of different access durations and network types.

TEXT:

**Regulation 3(y): State Nodal Agency**

"State Nodal Agency" means the Nodal Agency for grant of open access in the State of Gujarat.

Gujarat State Load Dispatch Centre shall operate as the State Nodal Agency for grant of Short-Term Open Access to Intra-State Transmission System in conjunction with or without Distribution Network of Distribution Licensee of the State. The State Transmission Utility (STU) shall operate as a State Nodal Agency for grant of Medium-Term and Long-Term Open Access to the Intra-State Transmission Network in conjunction with or without Distribution Network of Distribution Licensee of the State.

The Distribution Licensee shall operate as a Nodal Agency in co-ordination with the SNA for grant of Open Access for the cases wherein the inter-se location of drawl and injection points falls on the Distribution Network of the concerned Distribution Licensee.

NODAL AGENCY FRAMEWORK:

**For Short-Term Open Access (up to 1 month):**
- Agency: Gujarat State Load Dispatch Centre (SLDC)
- Network: Intra-State Transmission System (InSTS)
- Scope: In conjunction with or without Distribution Network
- Coordination: Direct operation

**For Medium-Term Open Access (3 months to 3 years):**
- Agency: State Transmission Utility (STU)
- Network: Intra-State Transmission Network
- Scope: In conjunction with or without Distribution Network
- Coordination: Direct operation

**For Long-Term Open Access (exceeding 12 years, not exceeding 25 years):**
- Agency: State Transmission Utility (STU)
- Network: Intra-State Transmission Network
- Scope: In conjunction with or without Distribution Network
- Coordination: Direct operation

**For Distribution Network cases:**
- Agency: Distribution Licensee
- Coordination: With SNA (SLDC or STU as applicable)
- Condition: When drawl and injection points fall on Distribution Network

APPLICATION ROUTING:
Per Regulation 10, all applications are first submitted to Central Nodal Agency portal, then routed to appropriate SNA, which coordinates with other agencies as needed.

TIME SCHEDULES FOR GRANT OF GEOA:
SNA must decide applications within 15 days of receipt from Central Nodal Agency per Regulation 13, with detailed timelines for each step from T0 (application receipt) through T0+15 (final grant decision).

INFORMATION SYSTEM RESPONSIBILITIES:
SLDC shall post Green Energy Open Access Information on a separate web page per Regulation 24, including status reports, floor rates, peak loads, and transmission/distribution losses.
