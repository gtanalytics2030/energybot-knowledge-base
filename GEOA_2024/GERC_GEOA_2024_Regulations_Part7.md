TITLE: Categorization of Green Energy Open Access

SUMMARY:
Regulation 7 establishes three distinct categories of Green Energy Open Access based on duration of transmission and distribution system usage: Long-Term (exceeding 12 years, not exceeding 25 years), Medium-Term (exceeding 3 months but not exceeding 3 years), and Short-Term (up to 1 month at a time). Each category has distinct processing timelines, nodal agencies, requirements, and operational procedures. The Short-Term category includes a fresh application requirement upon expiry and renewal seeking.

**Regulation 7: Categorization of Green Energy Open Access**

The Green Energy Open Access consumers shall be classified into the following categories based on the duration of use of the Intra-State transmission and/or distribution system:

I. "Long-term Green Energy Open Access" means the right to use the intra-State transmission system and/or distribution system for a period exceeding 12 years but not exceeding 25 years.

II. "Medium-term Green Energy Open Access" means the right to use the intra-State transmission system and/or distribution system for a period exceeding three months but not exceeding three years.

III. "Short-Term Green Energy Open Access" means open access for a period up to one month at a time.

Provided that on expiry of granted Short-term Green Open Access, if such consumer/customer desires to avail the Short-Term Green Energy Open Access for further period, it shall require to file application for such period and such application shall be considered as fresh application and priority shall be fixed on the basis of date of such application.

NODAL AGENCY ASSIGNMENT BY CATEGORY:

**Short-Term (up to 1 month):**
- Nodal Agency: Gujarat State Load Dispatch Centre (SLDC)
- Decision Timeline: Within 15 days per Regulation 13
- Processing Fee: Rs. 5,000 per application (Regulation 13(III))
- Bank Guarantee: Not required (only for LTOA and MTOA)
- Application: Fresh application required for each renewal

**Medium-Term (3 months to 3 years):**
- Nodal Agency: State Transmission Utility (STU)
- Decision Timeline: Within 15 days per Regulation 13
- Processing Fee: Rs. 25,000 per application (Regulation 13(III))
- Bank Guarantee: Rs. 50,000 for <1 MW; Rs. 1,00,000 per MW for ≥1 MW (Regulation 13(V))
- Relinquishment: 30 days notice (Regulation 17.10(2))

**Long-Term (12 years < term ≤ 25 years):**
- Nodal Agency: State Transmission Utility (STU)
- Decision Timeline: Within 15 days per Regulation 13
- Processing Fee: Rs. 50,000 per application (Regulation 13(III))
- Bank Guarantee: Rs. 50,000 for <1 MW; Rs. 1,00,000 per MW for ≥1 MW (Regulation 13(V))
- Relinquishment: 1 year notice for no charges; <1 year notice attracts 66% of transmission/wheeling charges (Regulation 17.10(1))

PRIORITY HIERARCHY (Regulation 12):
Among GEOA consumers, priority for allocation follows this order:
1. Long-Term GEOA consumers (highest)
2. Medium-Term GEOA consumers
3. Short-Term GEOA consumers (lowest)

Within same category, First Come First Served (FCFS) basis applies per Regulation 12(iii).

FRESH APPLICATION REQUIREMENT FOR SHORT-TERM:
Upon expiry of granted Short-Term GEOA, if the consumer seeks further Short-Term access, they must file a fresh application. This fresh application is treated as new and priority is fixed based on the date of fresh application, not on any prior history. This ensures no entitlement carryover and maintains equal treatment among Short-Term applicants.

SYSTEM AVAILABILITY CRITERIA:
- LTOA: Allowed per transmission planning criteria in State Grid Code (Regulation 6(I))
- MTOA and STOA: Allowed if request can be accommodated using design margins, power flow variations, or spare capacity (Regulation 6(II))

CONTRACTUAL DURATION:
- LTOA: Standard form agreement for full 12-25 year term
- MTOA: Standard form agreement with quarterly/annual reviews possible
- STOA: Simple approval for monthly periods, no long-term agreement
