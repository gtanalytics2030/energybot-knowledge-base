SUMMARY:
This chunk contains the official notification framework for the First Amendment to the GERC Green Energy Open Access Regulations 2024. Published in The Gujarat Government Gazette (Extra No. 102, Vol. LXV) on September 23, 2024, the amendment is issued under Notification No. 16 of 2024 (GERC/2024/16/Legal/1807). The notification establishes the regulatory authority (GERC), the enabling legislation (Sections 39, 40, 42, 61 & 86 read with Section 181 of the Electricity Act, 2003), and the document being amended (the Principal Regulations, Notification No. 8 of 2024).

**GUJARAT ELECTRICITY REGULATORY COMMISSION (GERC)**
**GANDHINAGAR**

**Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Terms & Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) (First Amendment) Regulations, 2024**

**NOTIFICATION NO. 16 OF 2024**

**GERC/2024/16/Legal/1807:**

In exercise of Power conferred under Sections 39, 40, 42, 61 & 86 read with Section 181 of the Electricity Act, 2003 (Act 36 of 2003) and all other power enabling it in this behalf and after previous publication, the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission hereby makes the following Regulations, to amend the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations, 2024, Notification No. 8 of 2024, (hereinafter referred to as "The Principal Regulations") namely:

REFERENCED PROVISIONS:
- Section 39 of the Electricity Act, 2003
- Section 40 of the Electricity Act, 2003
- Section 42 of the Electricity Act, 2003
- Section 61 of the Electricity Act, 2003
- Section 86 of the Electricity Act, 2003
- Section 181 of the Electricity Act, 2003
- The Electricity Act, 2003 (36 of 2003)
- Principal Regulations: GERC Notification No. 8 of 2024

CROSS-REFERENCES:

- Regulation 1(4) (Amendment regarding Banking Charge)
