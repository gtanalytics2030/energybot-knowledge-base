DOCUMENT: Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Terms & Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) (First Amendment) Regulations, 2024
DOCUMENT TYPE: Amendment Regulations
HIERARCHY: Regulation 1 / Short Title, Scope, Extent and Commencement
PROVISION: Regulation 1(i), 1(ii), 1(iii)
TITLE: Short Title, Scope, Extent and Commencement of the First Amendment Regulations

SUMMARY:
Regulation 1 establishes three foundational elements of the First Amendment Regulations: (i) the official short title identifying these as the "Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Terms & Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) (First Amendment) Regulations, 2024"; (ii) territorial scope extending to the entire State of Gujarat; and (iii) the commencement date, which is the date of publication in the Official Gazette. This regulation provides the formal legal framework for the amendment's application across the state.

KEY TOPICS:
- Short Title of Regulations
- Territorial Scope
- State of Gujarat Application
- Commencement Date
- Official Gazette Publication
- First Amendment Status

TEXT:

**1. Short Title, Scope Extent and Commencement**

(i) These Regulations shall be called Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Terms & Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) (First Amendment) Regulations, 2024.

(ii) These Regulations shall extend to the whole of the State of Gujarat.

(iii) These Regulations shall come into force from the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS:
- The Electricity Act, 2003 (36 of 2003)
- Principal Regulations: GERC Notification No. 8 of 2024
- Regulation 1(4) (Banking Charge effectiveness provision - subject to amendment)
- Regulation 17.6 (Banking Facility and Charges - subject to amendments)
- Official Gazette Publication

CROSS-REFERENCES:
These foundational provisions in Regulation 1 establish the legal framework for:
- Amendment in Clause No. 1(4) of the Principal Regulations (Section 2 of this Amendment Regulation)
- Amendment in Clause 17.6(viii) of the Principal Regulations (Section 3 of this Amendment Regulation)

NOTES:
The commencement date is linked to Official Gazette publication, specifically the publication in The Gujarat Government Gazette, Extra No. 102, Vol. LXV, dated September 23, 2024 / Asvina 1, 1946.
