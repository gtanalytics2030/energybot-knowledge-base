PROVISION: Section 2 of Amendment Regulation / Substitution of Clause 1(4)
TITLE: Amendment in Clause No. 1(4) of the Principal Regulations - Banking Charge Effectiveness

SUMMARY:
Section 2 of the First Amendment Regulations amends Clause 1(4) of the Principal Regulations (GERC Notification No. 8 of 2024) by substituting the banking charge effectiveness provision. The amended Clause 1(4) specifies that the Banking Charge specified in Regulation 17.6 shall be effective from the date of notification of these Amendment Regulations up to March 31, 2025, or such earlier date as the Commission may notify through a separate notification. This amendment establishes the temporal scope and procedure for banking charge implementation.

TEXT:

**Section 2. Amendment in Clause No. 1 (4) of the Principal Regulations.**

The Clause 1 (4) shall be substituted as under:

**"1. Short Title, Extent and Commencement**

........

**4) The Banking Charge specified in the Regulation 17.6 shall be effective from the date of notification of these Regulations up to 31st March 2025 or earlier date on which the Commission notifies such charges through a separate notification of Regulation".**

HIERARCHICAL POSITION:
This amendment modifies Clause 1(4) within Regulation 1 (Short Title, Extent and Commencement) of the Principal Regulations. The amended clause now explicitly ties the banking charge effectiveness to:
1. The date of notification of these Amendment Regulations (First Amendment), AND
2. An expiration date of March 31, 2025, UNLESS
3. The Commission issues an earlier notification of charges through separate notification

NOTES:
The date of notification of these Amendment Regulations is September 23, 2024 (date of publication in The Gujarat Government Gazette, Extra No. 102, Vol. LXV). This provision creates a temporary framework for banking charges effective from September 23, 2024 through March 31, 2025, after which the Commission must issue fresh notifications regarding applicable banking charges.
