**BANKING CHARGE IMPLEMENTATION FRAMEWORK**
**First Amendment to GERC Green Energy Open Access Regulations, 2024**

---

**I. REGULATORY AMENDMENTS STRUCTURE**

The First Amendment to GERC Notification No. 8 of 2024 comprises two interconnected amendments:

**Amendment 1 (Section 2):** Modifies Clause 1(4) of the Principal Regulations to establish the temporal framework for banking charge effectiveness.

**Amendment 2 (Section 3):** Modifies Clause 17.6(viii) of the Principal Regulations to specify the banking charge rate and its applicability period.

---

**II. BANKING CHARGE RATE SPECIFICATION**

**Charge Rate:** Rs. 1.50 per unit (kWh)

**Basis:** Established under Clause 17.6(viii) of the Principal Regulations (as amended by Section 3 of the First Amendment Regulations)

**Applicability:** Applies to all Green Energy Open Access consumers as defined in the Principal Regulations, including:
- Consumers availing open access
- Trading licensees engaged in green energy trading
- Distribution licensees procuring renewable energy through open access
- Generating Companies or Captive Generating Plants utilizing open access

**Charge Category:** Administrative/Recovery charge for banking facility services

---

**III. TEMPORAL FRAMEWORK - EFFECTIVE DATES AND DURATION**

**Effective Date (Commencement):** September 23, 2024
- Date of publication in The Gujarat Government Gazette (Extra No. 102, Vol. LXV)
- Same as commencement of Amendment Regulations under Regulation 1(iii)

**Initial Expiration Date (Default):** March 31, 2025
- Specified in both amended Clause 1(4) and Clause 17.6(viii)

**Early Expiration Mechanism:** The Commission may issue a separate notification on any date before March 31, 2025 to modify banking charges, thereby superseding the March 31, 2025 default expiration

**Duration of Rs. 1.50 per unit Rate:** Approximately 6 months (September 23, 2024 to March 31, 2025), or until Commission issues modification notification, whichever is earlier

**Post-Expiration Regime:** After March 31, 2025 (or earlier Commission notification), banking charges shall be governed by separate notification(s) issued by the Commission, which may establish different rates and terms

---

**IV. REGULATORY AUTHORITY AND NOTIFICATION PROCEDURES**

**Issuing Authority:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (GERC)

**Regulatory Basis:**
- Sections 39, 40, 42, 61 & 86 read with Section 181 of the Electricity Act, 2003 (Act 36 of 2003)
- Principal Regulations: GERC Notification No. 8 of 2024
- Regulation 3(d) - Definition of "Banking"
- Regulation 17.6 - Banking Facility and Charges framework

**Modification Authority:** The Commission retains full authority to issue separate notifications at any time during the effective period (before March 31, 2025) to modify the banking charge rate and structure

**Notification Requirement:** Any modification to banking charges post-March 31, 2025 must occur through separate notification by Regulations notified by the Commission

---

**V. BANKING FACILITY DEFINITION AND SCOPE**

**Reference Provision:** Regulation 3(d) of the Principal Regulations

**Definition:** "Banking means the surplus green energy injected in the grid and credited with the distribution licensee energy by the Green Energy Open Access consumers and that shall be drawn along with charges to compensate additional costs; if any."

**Mechanism:**
1. Surplus renewable energy is injected into the distribution licensee's grid by Green Energy Open Access consumers
2. The energy is credited to the consumer's account with the distribution licensee
3. The consumer can draw an equivalent amount of energy as needed
4. A banking charge (Rs. 1.50 per unit) is levied to compensate the distribution licensee for administrative and operational costs

**VIII. QUERY RESOLUTION GUIDANCE**

**For Query: "What is the banking charge under GERC Green Energy Open Access Regulations?"**
→ Answer: Rs. 1.50 per unit (kWh), effective from September 23, 2024 through March 31, 2025 [Reference: GERC Amendment Notification No. 16 of 2024, amended Clause 17.6(viii)]

**For Query: "When is the Rs. 1.50 per unit banking charge effective?"**
→ Answer: Effective from September 23, 2024 (date of publication in Official Gazette) up to March 31, 2025, or such earlier date as the Commission may notify. [Reference: GERC Amendment Notification No. 16 of 2024, Section 3 (amended Clause 17.6(viii))]

**For Query: "Can the Commission modify banking charges before March 31, 2025?"**
→ Answer: Yes. The Commission retains authority to issue a separate notification at any time before March 31, 2025 to modify banking charges. [Reference: GERC Amendment Notification No. 16 of 2024, Sections 2 and 3 (amended Clauses 1(4) and 17.6(viii))]

**For Query: "What happens to banking charges after March 31, 2025?"**
→ Answer: After March 31, 2025, banking charges shall be determined by separate notification(s) issued by the Commission, which may establish different rates from the current Rs. 1.50 per unit. [Reference: GERC Amendment Notification No. 16 of 2024, Section 3 (amended Clause 17.6(viii))]

**For Query: "Who is liable to pay the banking charge?"**
→ Answer: All Green Energy Open Access consumers, including consumers, trading licensees, distribution licensees, and Generating Companies or Captive Generating Plants utilizing the banking facility. [Reference: Regulation 3(b) of Principal Regulations (Definition of "Applicant") and Regulation 17.6 of Principal Regulations]
