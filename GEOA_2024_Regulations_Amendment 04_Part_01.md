**NOTIFICATION**

**GUJARAT ELECTRICITY REGULATORY COMMISSION (GERC)**
**GANDHINAGAR**

Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Terms & Conditions For Green Energy Open Access) (Fourth Amendment) Regulations, 2026

**Notification No. 02 of 2026**

**GERC/2026/02/Tech/0490:** In exercise of Power conferred under Sections 39, 40, 42, 61 & 86 read with Section 181 of the Electricity Act, 2003 (Act 36 of 2003) and all other power enabling it in this behalf and after previous publication, the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission hereby amends the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations, 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the "The Principal Regulations") namely:

---

**SECTION 1: SHORT TITLE, SCOPE, EXTENT AND COMMENCEMENT**

**(i) Short Title**

These Regulations shall be called Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Terms & Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) (Fourth Amendment) Regulations, 2026.

**(ii) Extent/Scope**

These Regulations shall extend to the whole of the State of Gujarat.

**(iii) Commencement**

These Regulations shall come into force from the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT: This Fourth Amendment modifies two key regulatory provisions of the 2024 GERC Green Energy Open Access Regulations: Regulation 1(4) regarding commencement of banking charges, and Regulation 17.6(viii) regarding the quantum and duration of banking charges. The amendments introduce a time-bound banking charge rate (Rs. 1.50 per unit) with an expiry mechanism allowing the Commission to modify charges through separate notification.
