SUMMARY:

The notification establishes the legal framework for Green Energy Open Access in Gujarat, enabling non-discriminatory access to transmission and distribution systems for renewable energy consumers, generators, and trading licensees.

HIERARCHY: Regulation 1
PROVISION: Regulation 1
TITLE: Short Title, Extent and Commencement

SUMMARY:
Regulation 1 establishes the nomenclature, territorial applicability, and effective date of these Regulations. These Regulations apply to Green Energy Open Access (GEOA) customers with contracted demand or sanctioned load of 100 kW or more across Gujarat. The Regulations become effective upon publication in the Official Gazette. Banking charges specified in Regulation 17.6 follow a specific temporal implementation schedule, with a provisional rate effective until 30th September 2024, thereafter to be revised. Captive consumers are exempt from capacity restrictions for renewable energy project setup.

TEXT:

1. Short Title, Extent and Commencement

1) These Regulations shall be called the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations, 2024.

2) These Regulations shall be applicable for Green Energy Open Access (GEOA) desire to avail by the Licensee, Green Energy Generator or Consumer who have contracted demand or sanctioned load of Hundred (100) KW or more, either through single connection or through multiple connections aggregating Hundred (100) kW or more located in same electricity division of a distribution licensee.

Provided that there shall be no capacity restriction for setting up of RE projects for captive use with respect to the consumer's contract demand/sanctioned load (kW/MW, KVA/MVA) with Discoms.

3) These Regulations will come into force from the date of their notification in the Official Gazette of Gujarat.

4) The Banking Charge specified in the Regulation 17.6 shall be effective from the date of notification of these Regulations up to 30th September 2024 and thereafter the banking charges for the period starting from 1st October 2024 and onwards shall be as decided by the Commission through a separate notification of Regulation.

5) These Regulations shall extend to the whole State of the Gujarat.

PROVISION: Regulation 2
TITLE: Objective

SUMMARY:
Regulation 2 articulates the foundational objective of these Regulations: to provide non-discriminatory Open Access for Green Energy (Renewable Energy) throughout Gujarat's electricity transmission and distribution systems. The objective encompasses both intra-State transmission/distribution systems and those incidental to inter-State transmission, establishing the methodology for determining open access charges, banking charges, and related fee structures applicable to Green Energy Open Access consumers.

2. Objective

The objective of these Regulations is to provide non-discriminatory Open Access for Green Energy (Renewable Energy) for use of Intra-State Transmission System(s) (InSTS) and/or Distribution System(s) of licensee(s) in State including Intra-State Transmission or Distribution System(s), which are incidental to Inter-State Transmission of electricity, for grant of open access, methodology for the determination of Open Access Charges and Banking Charges etc. for Green Energy Open Access consumers.

HIERARCHY: Regulation 3 - Definitions
PROVISION: Regulation 3(a), 3(b), 3(c), 3(d)
TITLE: Core Definitions - Act, Applicant, Appropriate Commission, Banking

SUMMARY:
These definitions establish the foundational legal and institutional framework for the Regulations. The "Act" refers to both Central legislation (Electricity Act, 2003) and State-specific legislation (Gujarat Electricity Industry Act, 2003). "Applicant" encompasses all eligible entities seeking GEOA: consumers, trading licensees, distribution licensees, generating companies, and captive plants. "Appropriate Commission" defines the regulatory authority based on transaction scope (Central, State, or Joint Commission). "Banking" establishes the mechanism for energy credit management and drawdown through surplus green energy injection into the grid.

- Electricity Act, 2003 (36 of 2003)

- Applicant eligibility (consumers, traders, licensees, generators, captive plants)

- Central Regulatory Commission (Section 76(1) reference)

- Joint Commission (Section 83 reference)
- Banking mechanism
- Energy credit and drawdown
- Additional cost compensation

TEXT:

3. Definitions

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

(a) "Act" means the Electricity Act, 2003 (36 of 2003) and Gujarat Electricity Industry (Reorganization and Regulation) Act, 2003.

(b) "Applicant" means a consumer, trading licensee, distribution licensee or a Generating Company, Captive Generating Plant who has applied for seeking Green Energy Open Access, as the case may be.

(c) "Appropriate Commission" means the Central Regulatory Commission referred to sub-Section (1) of Section 76 or State Regulatory Commission referred to Section 82 or Joint Commission referred to Section 83 of the Act, as the case may be.

(d) "Banking" means the surplus green energy injected in the grid and credited with the distribution licensee energy by the Green Energy Open Access consumers and that shall be drawn along with charges to compensate additional costs; if any.

HIERARCHY: Regulation 3 - Definitions
PROVISION: Regulation 3(e), 3(f), 3(g), 3(h)
TITLE: Definitions - Captive Generating Plant, Central Commission, Central Nodal Agency, Commission

SUMMARY:
This section defines critical institutional entities and generation facility classifications. "Captive Generating Plant" (CGP) encompasses power plants established by persons, cooperative societies, and associations primarily for self-consumption, including co-operative and association arrangements. "Central Commission" references the Central Electricity Regulatory Commission established under Section 76 of the Electricity Act, 2003. "Central Nodal Agency" designates the entity notified by the Central Government to operate the single-window GEOA system for renewable energy under Ministry of Power rules. "Commission" refers to the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission constituted under the Act.

(e) "Captive Generating Plant" (CGP) means a power plant set up by any person to generate electricity primarily for his own use and includes a power plant set up by any co-operative society or association of persons for generating electricity primarily for use of members of such co-operative society or association.
