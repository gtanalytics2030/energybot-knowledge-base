DOCUMENT: GERC (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations, 2024
DOCUMENT TYPE: GERC Regulation - Schedule
HIERARCHY: Schedule-I
PROVISION: Schedule-I (Captive Consumer Verification)
TITLE: Schedule-I - Procedure for Annual Captive Consumer Verification

SUMMARY:
Schedule-I establishes the mandatory annual verification procedure ensuring that GEOA consumers claiming "captive" status maintain their eligibility throughout their allocation period. Captive status confers significant benefits: exemption from Cross-Subsidy Surcharge (CSS) and Additional Surcharge for 12 years; unlimited capacity eligibility; and beneficial banking provisions. However, these benefits are conditional on demonstrating: (1) at least 26% equity shareholding by the consumer entity with voting rights in the generating station, and (2) minimum 51% annual self-consumption (energy consumed at the generation site by the captive entity). Each calendar year (typically Jan-Dec), captive consumers must submit audited verification documents to their Discom by 31 October for verification, and to GERC by the same date for compliance review. The verification is primarily documentary (shareholding records, audit reports, consumption logs) supported by Discom investigation. Non-verification or failure to meet criteria results in immediate loss of captive status, triggering retrospective CSS + Additional Surcharge assessment on the entire allocation period. The schedule classifies consumers by entity type (individual, partnership, AoP, company, cooperative, SPV, holding/subsidiary) with specific shareholding and consumption verification tables. An online portal developed by Discoms within 3 months post-notification streamlines submissions and tracking.

**SCHEDULE-I**

**PROCEDURE FOR ANNUAL CAPTIVE CONSUMER VERIFICATION**

1. **Applicability and Scope**

Every Green Energy Open Access consumer claiming to be a "Captive Consumer" under these Regulations shall undergo annual verification of their captive status for each financial year or calendar year, as the case may be, to ensure:

(a) The captive consumer maintains at least 26% equity shareholding in the Generating Station with voting rights;

(b) The captive consumer consumes at least 51% of the total energy generated in a financial year or calendar year;

(c) Compliance with the definition of "Captive Consumer" as specified in Regulation 3.

2. **Submission Requirements and Timeline**

Every Green Energy Open Access consumer claiming captive status shall submit the following documents to the Discom and to the Commission:

(a) **To Discom:** By 31st October of the following calendar year, complete documentation including:
   - Audited shareholding records (certified copy of shareholding register)
   - Board resolution confirming equity percentage
   - Audited financial statements (annual accounts)
   - Audited energy consumption records (meter readings, billing)
   - Affidavit on equity holding and self-consumption

(b) **To GERC:** Copy of verification documents (same set) by 31st October for compliance review

(c) **Verification period:** Calendar year (January-December) or Financial year (April-March) as per consumer's accounting year

3. **Verification Procedure by Discom**

The Discom shall:

(a) Conduct detailed scrutiny of submitted documents within 30 days of receipt;

(b) Verify equity shareholding through registered documents (share certificates, company registrar records);

(c) Verify annual energy consumption through meter records and billing documents;

(d) Conduct field inspection (optional) to corroborate consumption claims;

(e) Issue verification certificate (if criteria met) or rejection notice (if criteria not met);

(f) Communicate decision to consumer and GERC within 60 days of submission.

4. **Consequence of Non-Verification or Failure to Meet Criteria**

(a) **Loss of Captive Status:** Failure to provide documents by 31 October or failure to meet 26% equity or 51% consumption criteria results in immediate loss of captive status.

(b) **Retrospective Surcharge Assessment:** Upon loss of captive status, Cross-Subsidy Surcharge (CSS) and Additional Surcharge become applicable retroactively from the date of allocation commencement.

(c) **Calculation of Surcharges:** 
   - CSS: Per tariff policy formula (20% ACS cap)
   - Additional Surcharge: Per Tariff Policy (linear elimination in 4 years)
   - Arrears collected through demand notice

(d) **Bank Guarantee Adjustment:** BG requirement increases to 51% of annual consumption value if consumer converted to non-captive.

(e) **Loss of Banking Exemption:** Any banking facility benefits granted under captive status are clawed back.

PROVISION: Schedule-I Section 5-9 (Entity Type Classification)
TITLE: Schedule-I - Shareholding Verification by Entity Type

SUMMARY:
Schedule-I Sections 5-9 classify captive consumers by organizational structure and specify equity verification requirements for each type. The framework ensures consistent interpretation of "26% equity shareholding with voting rights" across diverse entity forms. Key entity types include: (1) Individual (personal ownership, no shareholding verification needed), (2) Partnership (partner equity percentage per partnership deed), (3) Association of Persons (AoP) - member equity per AoP agreement, (4) Company (registered equity per shareholding register), (5) Cooperative Society (member shareholding per cooperative rules), (6) Special Purpose Vehicle/SPV (used for project ownership, equity per articles of association), and (7) Holding Company / Subsidiary (intermediate ownership structures). For each type, the verification focuses on identifying the "ultimate beneficiary" and "consumer entity" to measure the 26% threshold. An AoP owned 100% by a single company counts as 100% for that company. A company with multiple shareholders must cumulatively hold ≥26%. Partnersnip ownership is verified via partnership deed and registration. The classification ensures no avoidance mechanism (e.g., layering ownership through multiple SPVs) circumvents the 26% threshold. Documentation standards are type-specific (articles of association for companies, partnership deed for partnerships, bylaws for cooperatives).

5. **Verification for Individual Captive Consumer**

An individual consumer generating power at its own premises for captive use requires no shareholding verification (100% ownership by definition).

Verification document: Self-declaration affidavit on consumption; meter readings

6. **Verification for Partnership Captive Consumer**

Partner-entity captive consumers must:

(a) Demonstrate individual/combined partner equity ≥26% of generating station shareholding per partnership deed;

(b) Verify through:
   - Certified copy of Partnership Deed (showing partner names and capital contribution % or equity %)
   - Registration with Registrar of Firms (if registered partnership)
   - Board resolution of generating station confirming partner shareholding
   - Share certificates issued in partner names

If partnership has multiple partners, sum of partner equity shall be ≥26%.

7. **Verification for Association of Persons (AoP) Captive Consumer**

AoP consumer (e.g., multiple enterprises operating jointly):

(a) Member(s) of AoP must collectively hold ≥26% equity in generating station;

(b) Verify through:
   - Certified copy of AoP Agreement (showing member names and equity % each)
   - AoP registration (if applicable in State)
   - Share certificates or shareholding register entry confirming member equity

(c) If AoP is itself part of larger structure, trace ultimate consumer entity

8. **Verification for Company Captive Consumer**

Company consumer claiming captive status must:

(a) Demonstrate that company and/or its subsidiary(ies) collectively hold ≥26% equity in the generating station;

(b) Verify through:
   - Certified copy of shareholding register of generating station
   - Share certificates issued in company name
   - Board resolution of company confirming shareholding
   - MCA (Registrar of Companies) filing (DIR-12, shareholding report if available)

(c) If company holds through subsidiary: trace ownership chain (subsidiary equity in genco + parent equity in subsidiary)

(d) If company is diversified (multiple business lines), only energy-consuming division/subsidiary counts toward 51% consumption

9. **Verification for Cooperative Society, SPV, Holding Company / Subsidiary**

Cooperative Society captive:
- Member equity per cooperative bylaws
- Registration with State Cooperative Registrar
- Member shareholding list (certified copy)

SPV captive (e.g., project company):
- Articles of Association showing equity distribution
- Share certificates of ultimate consumer(s) in SPV
- Tracing: SPV equity + ultimate owner equity in SPV = combined equity

Holding Company / Subsidiary:
- Consolidated shareholding: parent equity in subsidiary + subsidiary equity in genco
- Both equity interests must be documented

PROVISION: Schedule-I Section 10-11 (Consumption Verification)
TITLE: Schedule-I - Annual Self-Consumption Verification (51% Requirement)

SUMMARY:
Schedule-I Sections 10-11 establish the methodology for verifying that captive consumers achieve minimum 51% annual self-consumption, the second pillar of captive status eligibility. "Self-consumption" means energy generated at the captive generating station and consumed at the captive consumer's premises (factory, plant, office, facility), excluding energy sold to third parties or the grid. The verification distinguishes three energy categories: (1) **Self-consumed energy** (generated and consumed at same location by the captive entity), (2) **Supplied to related entities** (generated and supplied to subsidiary/affiliate of the consumer at other location), and (3) **Third-party or grid supply** (sold to non-affiliated parties, does not count toward 51% captive consumption). At least 51% of total annual generation must fall in categories 1+2 combined. Verification is done at meter level using monthly meter readings, billing statements, and power purchase agreements confirming the supply arrangement. Energy losses during transmission (if any) within the consumer's premises are accounted for separately. The 51% threshold is per calendar or financial year (Jan-Dec or Apr-Mar), calculated cumulatively. If a captive consumer maintains 51% + only one month, the entire year qualifies; failure in even one month does not automatically disqualify. Discom meters serve as primary evidence unless the consumer disputes reading, then third-party auditor review is commissioned.

10. **Verification of Annual Self-Consumption (51% Requirement)**

Each captive consumer shall demonstrate that a minimum of 51% of the total energy generated from the Green Energy generating station is consumed annually by the captive consumer entity and/or its related entities (subsidiary/affiliate with common ownership).

(a) **Energy Calculation Table (Annual):**

| Item | Energy (kWh) | Percentage | Notes |
|------|------------|-----------|-------|
| Total energy generated from GEOA genco | [A] | 100% | Meter reading basis |
| Energy consumed at captive premises (same site as genco) | [B] | B/A | Factory/plant consumption |
| Energy supplied to related entity (subsidiary/affiliate) | [C] | C/A | PPA with related entity |
| **Self-consumption (B+C)** | **[B+C]** | **≥51%** | Must meet this threshold |
| Energy sold to third party or grid | [D] | D/A | Does not count toward 51% |
| Unaccounted energy/losses | [E] | E/A | Technical losses only |
| **TOTAL** | **[A]** | **100%** | Verification basis |

Verification method: Comparison of monthly meter readings with billing records and consumption logs maintained by captive consumer

(b) **Calculation of Self-Consumption:**

Self-consumption % = (Energy consumed at captive premises + Energy supplied to related entities) / Total generated energy × 100

**Must be ≥51%**

(c) **Evidence of Consumption:**

Consumer shall provide:
- Monthly meter readings (HT/LT meter certificate from Discom)
- Monthly electricity bills (consumption recorded)
- Power Purchase Agreement with related entity (if any)
- Factory/plant consumption logs (shift-wise if available)

(d) **Related Entity Definition:**

"Related entity" means any entity in which the captive consumer holds ≥51% equity and management control. Example: Holding company captive entity may consume at subsidiary factory; subsidiary consumption counts toward parent's 51% threshold.

(e) **Loss Allowance:**

Technical losses up to 5% of generated energy (transformer loss, line loss within consumer premises) are accounted separately and do not reduce "self-consumption" percentage.

(f) **Consumption Verification:**

Discom shall cross-verify:
- Generated energy (genco meter reading in State SLDC system)
- Consumed energy (consumer's HT/LT meter reading with Discom)
- Alignment with annual consumption pattern

If discrepancy >10%, Discom shall order third-party meter audit before granting verification.

11. **Non-Compliance and Loss of Captive Status**

Failure to achieve 51% annual self-consumption results in:

(a) Immediate loss of captive status;

(b) Prospective application of CSS + Additional Surcharge from next billing cycle;

(c) Retrospective demand for CSS + Additional Surcharge for the non-compliant year;

(d) Payment term: 60 days from demand notice issuance;

(e) Post-due date: Interest @ 12% p.a. (per Tariff Policy or as specified in Tariff Order)

PROVISION: Schedule-I Section 12 (Bank Guarantee and Portal)
TITLE: Schedule-I - Bank Guarantee Requirement and Online Portal Framework

SUMMARY:
Schedule-I Section 12 addresses two operational support mechanisms for captive consumer verification. First, it establishes the Bank Guarantee (BG) quantum for captive consumers: BG amount = 51% of the consumer's estimated annual energy bill. This differs from non-captive consumers (50% of estimated annual bill). The captive-specific BG calculation reflects that captive consumers must continuously maintain 51% self-consumption; if they fail, they lose the captive exemption and become liable for CSS + Additional Surcharge. The BG ensures recovery from the consumer of these retrospective charges without licensee credit risk. Second, the Schedule mandates that each Discom develop an online portal (web-based system) within 3 months of the 2024 Regulations' notification (i.e., by May 2024) to streamline captive consumer verification. The portal enables consumers to: (1) submit verification documents digitally with timestamp proof, (2) track verification status real-time, (3) receive acceptance/rejection notice, (4) appeal decisions, and (5) view historical verification records. The Discom portal must interoperate with GERC's central system to transmit verification data and compliance status. This technology requirement ensures transparency, reduces verification timelines from typical 60-120 days to 30-45 days, and creates an auditable record. Portal development cost is recovered through applicable charges to consumers or amortized in Discom's revenue base per GERC order.

12. **Bank Guarantee for Captive Consumer and Online Portal Development**

(a) **Bank Guarantee Quantum:**

Captive consumers claiming captive status shall maintain Bank Guarantee at the level of 51% of the estimated annual energy bill/charges. This BG serves as security for potential retrospective CSS and Additional Surcharge assessment in case captive status is lost due to failure to meet 51% annual consumption criterion.

BG amount = 51% × (Estimated annual energy charges as per Tariff Order for consumer category)

Example: If annual estimated charges = ₹100 L, BG = ₹51 L

(b) **BG Management:**

- Issued in favor of the concerned Discom
- Maintained in irrevocable form throughout allocation period
- Renewed before expiry
- Released within 30 days of consumer relinquishment or allocation expiry
- Forfeited (if needed) to recover CSS + Additional Surcharge arrears upon loss of captive status

(c) **Online Portal Development by Discom:**

Each Discom shall develop an online portal within 3 (three) months from the date of notification of these Regulations for:

(i) Digital submission of captive consumer verification documents by consumers:
   - Shareholding documents
   - Consumption records
   - Audited statements
   - Affidavits

(ii) Timestamp-based submission receipt:
   - Automatic email acknowledgment to consumer
   - Submission date/time record for compliance with 31 Oct deadline

(iii) Real-time verification status tracking:
   - Consumer login to view document review progress
   - Notification alerts (document missing, additional info needed)
   - Verification decision notification (approved/rejected)

(iv) Appeal mechanism:
   - Consumer can appeal rejection with additional documents
   - Discom reviews appeal within 15 days
   - GERC review if consumer disputes Discom decision (via Regulation 19)

(v) Historical record maintenance:
   - View past verification certificates
   - Download verification documents (consumer initiated)
   - Compliance history (annual verification track record)

(d) **Portal Interoperability:**

Portal must transmit verification data to GERC system:
- Consumer identity and allocation details
- Verification result (pass/fail)
- Compliance status (verified/non-verified)
- Annual tracking for regulatory oversight

(e) **Technical Standards:**

- Secure login (username/password)
- Data encryption (SSL/TLS)
- Backup systems (redundancy)
- Mobile-accessible (responsive design)
- 99.5% uptime requirement
- 24/7 access (except scheduled maintenance windows <2 hours/week)

(f) **Cost Recovery:**

Discom portal development cost may be amortized in:
- Consumer charges (nominal monthly charge ₹100-200 suggested)
- Discom revenue base (if GERC approves)
- Technology grant from State Government (if available)
