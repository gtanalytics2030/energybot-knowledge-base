TITLE: Preamble and Legal Authority for the Second Amendment Regulations

SUMMARY:
The Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (GERC) issues Notification No. 06 of 2025 under the authority of the Electricity Act, 2003, specifically Sections 39, 40, 42, 61, and 86 read with Section 181. This Second Amendment Regulation amends the Principal Regulations—the GERC (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations, 2024, Notification No. 8 of 2024—to modify provisions related to Banking Charges and their effective date. The document is issued after previous publication as required under law.
