TITLE: Short Title, Scope, Extent and Commencement of Second Amendment Regulations 2025

SUMMARY:
Section 1 establishes the formal identity, territorial applicability, and date of implementation of the Second Amendment Regulations. The Regulations are formally titled "Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Terms & Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2025" and apply throughout the State of Gujarat. They become effective from the date of publication in the Official Gazette. This section provides the foundational metadata for the amendment and clarifies that it extends GERC's regulatory authority across the entire state.

TEXT:

**1. Short Title, Scope Extent and Commencement**

(i) These Regulations shall be called Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Terms & Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2025.

(ii) These Regulations shall extend to the whole of the State of Gujarat.

(iii) These Regulations shall come into force from the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.
