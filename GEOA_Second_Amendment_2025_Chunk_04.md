PROVISION: Section 3 - Amendment in Clause 17.6(viii) of the Principal Regulations
TITLE: Amendment to Regulation 17.6(viii): Banking Charge Rate (Rs. 1.50 per Unit)

SUMMARY:
Section 3 of the Second Amendment substitutes Regulation 17.6(viii) of the Principal Regulations to establish the Banking Charge rate for Green Energy Open Access consumers. The amended clause specifies that the Banking Charge shall be levied at the rate of Rs. 1.50 per unit and shall be effective from the date of the Amendment Regulations up to 30th September 2025, or such earlier date as the Commission may notify regarding Banking Charges through a separate notification. After 30th September 2025 or the Commission's earlier notification date, the Banking Charge shall be as separately notified by the Commission through Regulations. This provision operationalizes the Banking Facility mechanism for surplus green energy management and cost compensation.

TEXT:

**3. Amendment in Clause 17.6 (viii) of the Principal Regulations.**

The Regulation 17.6 (viii) shall be substituted as under:

"**17.6 Banking facility and Charges**

……

**(viii) The banking charge at the rate of Rs. 1.50 per unit shall be effective from the date of this Regulations up to 30th September 2025 or earlier date on which the Commission notifies such charges. Thereafter the banking charge for the period starting from the separate notification by Regulations notified by the Commission shall be applicable.**"

KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:
The Banking Charge is a cost-recovery mechanism for distribution licensees under the Green Energy Open Access framework. The rate of Rs. 1.50/unit applies to energy drawn from the grid (not injected). Key dates for consumers and DISCOMs:
- Start date: Date of publication in Official Gazette
- End date: 30th September 2025 (automatic sunset)
- Post-30th September 2025: Charge subject to Commission notification through separate Regulations

Users querying banking charge rates should refer to this Rs. 1.50/unit rate with caveat of 30th September 2025 validity. After that date, users must check for subsequent Commission notifications.

TARIFF ELEMENT CLASSIFICATION:
- Rate: Rs. 1.50 per kWh
- Applicability: Green Energy Open Access consumers (all categories)
- Nature: Fixed charge per unit of energy drawn/adjusted through banking
- Justification: Compensation for additional distribution costs and grid management
- Mechanism: Applied on net surplus energy drawn from banking pool
