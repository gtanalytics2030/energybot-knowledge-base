PART 1: PRINCIPAL REGULATIONS (GERC GEOA 2024, NOTIFICATION NO. 8 OF 2024)
===================================================================

**Previously Processed Chunks (Already in Knowledge Base):**

From GERC_GEOA_2024_Part2_Definitions_Act_to_Banking.md:

1. **Regulation 3(a) - "Act" Definition**
   - Defines legal basis: Electricity Act 2003 + Gujarat Electricity Industry (Reorganization and Regulation) Act 2003
   - **Cross-reference to Second Amendment:** Not directly amended, but foundational to interpretation

2. **Regulation 3(b) - "Applicant" Definition**
   - Identifies eligible entities: consumers, trading licensees, distribution licensees, Generating Companies, Captive Generating Plants
   - **Cross-reference to Second Amendment:** Identifies who bears Banking Charge liability

3. **Regulation 3(c) - "Appropriate Commission" Definition**
   - Establishes regulatory authority: Central, State, or Joint Commission
   - **Cross-reference to Second Amendment:** Commission referenced as entity notifying future Banking Charges (Sec 2, Sec 3)

4. **Regulation 3(d) - "Banking" Definition**
   - Core concept: "Surplus green energy injected in the grid and credited with the distribution licensee energy by the Green Energy Open Access consumers and that shall be drawn along with charges to compensate additional costs"
   - **CRITICAL Cross-reference to Second Amendment:** This is the mechanism for which Rs. 1.50 per unit charge is established by Second Amendment Regulation 17.6(viii)

**Anticipated Related Regulations in Principal Regulations (Not Yet Chunked):**

- Regulation 1(4): Original text establishing commencement and applicability (AMENDED by Second Amendment Section 2)
- Regulation 8: Application procedure for Open Access
- Regulation 9: Eligibility criteria
- Regulation 13: Procedure for grant of Open Access
- Regulation 17.1-17.5: Banking mechanism and operational details (foundational to 17.6 amendment)
- **Regulation 17.6: Banking facility and Charges (AMENDED by Second Amendment Section 3)**
- Regulation 17.6(viii): Original banking charge provision (SUBSTITUTED by Second Amendment Section 3)
