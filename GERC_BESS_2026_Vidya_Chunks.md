2.       Definitions and Interpretation

2.1.     In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

          (a)   “Act” means the Electricity Act, 2003 (36 of 2003).

         (b)    "Aggregator(s)” means an entity that provides aggregation of one or more services like Demand
             Response services under the Demand Response mechanism, Distributed Generation, Energy Storage,
                  etc.

          (c)    “Ancillary Services” or “AS” means the services necessary to support the operation of the power
              system for maintaining grid frequency, voltage, reliability, and security, and includes Primary Reserve
               Ancillary Service (PRAS), Secondary Reserve Ancillary Service (SRAS), Tertiary Reserve Ancillary
               Service (TRAS), Reactive Power support, black start services, and such other services as may be
                specified by the Commission or as defined under the Ancillary Services framework as may be notified
             by the Commission from time to time.

         (d)    “Associated Generating Plant” means a Generating Plant in which a Co-located BESS is installed,
             and which shall also supply electricity for charging the Co-located BESS while it may also continue to
                 inject surplus or unutilised generation into the grid, subject to provisions of these Regulations.

          (e)    “Battery Energy Storage Systems” or “BESS” shall mean electrochemical devices connected to the
             Power System that absorb electricity from the grid or generation sources, store  it in the form of
              chemical energy, and discharge  it, in the form of electricity, when required. They typically include
                  batteries, Power Conversion System, and Battery Management System.

           (f)    “Battery Energy Storage System Developer” or “BESSD” or “Developer” shall mean the entity
              developing /owning /operating the BESS facility for supply of power under these Regulations.

         (g)    “Captive Generating Plant” or “CGP” shall have the same meaning as assigned to  it under the
               Act.

              Provided that the Captive Status of a Captive Generating Plant with a Co-located BESS shall be
              determined in accordance with the applicable captive policy framework and the relevant Regulations,
               Rules, or Orders, as amended from time to time.

         (h)    “Co-located BESS” means a BESS installed at the same location and connected to the same evacuation
                 infrastructure as a Generating Station, including Renewable or Conventional Generation, for purposes
              such as firming of power, smoothing, or enhancing dispatchability.

           (i)    “Commission” means the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (GERC).

           (j)    “Distribution Licensee” shall have the same meaning as assigned to it under the Act.

         (k)   “Generating Company” shall have the same meaning as assigned to it under the Act.

           (l)    “Grid Disturbance” shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Central Electricity Authority
               (Grid Standards) Regulations, 2010, as amended from time to time.

       (m)   “Prosumer” means a person who consumes electricity from the grid and can also inject distributed
              renewable energy generated from DRES with or without BESS, into the grid using the same network of
                 distribution licensee, in accordance with applicable Regulations/Orders of the Commission as amended
             from time to time.

         (n)   “Renewable Energy sources” includes sources defined by the Government of India from time to time,

         (o)   “Standalone BESS” means a BESS operating independently, not a part of an integrated Renewable/
              Conventional plus Storage Asset, and capable of participating in Energy Markets, Capacity Markets, or
               Ancillary Services, subject to the provisions of these Regulations.

         (p)    “State Load Despatch Centre” or “SLDC” shall have the same meaning as assigned under the Act
             and shall be responsible for integrated operation of the Power System within the State.

7.      General Principles for Planning, Procurement and Deployment of BESS

7.1.     All entities shall consider deployment of BESS as an integral component of System Planning, Resource
       Adequacy, and Grid Flexibility enhancement.

7.2.    The minimum individual BESS project size shall be above 1 MW (power rating), with a corresponding energy
         storage rating of at least two hours and shall be connected at 11 kV or above.

        Provided that this minimum size shall not apply to BESS installed at the Distribution Transformer (DTR) level,
        or Co-located with Generating Plants/Captive Generating Plants, or for BESS installed under Net Metering/
        Gross Metering/Net Billing/Group Net Metering/Virtual Net Metering arrangements by Consumers/Prosumers.

        Provided further that for BESS Co-located with existing Generating Stations, the minimum energy rating
        requirement may be reduced to less than two hours where the primary application is Ancillary Services/
        Frequency Regulation.

        Provided also that the Commission may, from time to time, review and revise the minimum project size
        through separate Order to adapt to technological advancements and market conditions.

7.3.    The planning, procurement and deployment of BESS shall be undertaken in a manner that ensures:

         a)    Optimal utilisation of storage capacity.

        b)     Efficient integration of Renewable Energy sources.

         c)    Enhancement of system reliability, resilience and security.

        d)    Economic efficiency in comparison with alternative solutions, and

         e)    Alignment with applicable Policies, including Energy Storage Obligations (ESO) and Renewable
             Energy targets.

7.4.   BESS may be deployed as:

         a)   A Generation-linked asset.

        b)   A Transmission system asset.

         c)   A Distribution system asset; or

        d)   A Standalone storage asset.

         e)    Prosumer / Consumer linked asset.

7.5.   BESS shall be procured as a service through competitive bidding under Section 63 of the Electricity Act by the
        Licensees, with tariff discovered as an Availability-based fixed charge in accordance with the prevailing

         a)     Competitive procurement; or

                carried out for the project as a whole in accordance with the provisions of the Electricity Rules, 2005 as
            amended from time to time, other applicable Regulations and Orders.

8.3.    Deployment of Generation-linked BESS

        Co-located BESS shall be operated as part of the Generating Station for the purpose of scheduling and
         despatch, unless otherwise specified.

        Provided that where a BESS is Co-located and integrated with Thermal Generating Station/Renewable Energy
        Generating Station, no separate connectivity/Open Access approval shall be required for such BESS, so long as
         the total injection at the interconnection point does not exceed the Connectivity Capacity/Open Access
       quantum granted to such Generating Station/Renewable Energy Generating Station.

        Provided also that the total injection from the Project, including injection from the Thermal Generating
        Station/Renewable Energy Generating Station and the BESS component, shall not exceed the Connectivity
       Quantum/ Sanctioned Capacity/Open Access quantum granted to such Project, as applicable. Any injection
       beyond such granted quantum shall be treated as inadvertent injection and shall not be eligible for any
        commercial settlement, apart from attracting actions in accordance with Gujarat Electricity Grid Code, as
       amended from time to time.

        Provided further that such integration shall be subject to compliance with applicable Grid Code, Connectivity,
        Metering, Protection, Scheduling and Communication requirements, Orders/Regulations of the Commission, as
