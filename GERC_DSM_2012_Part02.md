## CHUNK 4: DEFINITION - "BASELINE DATA"

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation - Definition

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 1: General → Regulation 2: Definitions → Definition (ii)

**PROVISION:** Regulation 2.1(ii)

**TITLE:** Definition of "Baseline Data"

**SUMMARY:** Baseline data refers to the initial base level of electricity consumption and/or demand established before a Demand Side Management (DSM) programme commences. It serves as the starting reference point against which programme impact is assessed and measured. Baseline data is essential for evaluating the effectiveness and energy/demand savings achieved through DSM initiatives.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Initial consumption level
- Demand reference point
- DSM programme effectiveness measurement
- Comparison baseline
- Programme impact assessment

**TEXT:**

"Baseline data" means the initial base level consumption and/or demand for electricity before a Demand Side Management (DSM) programme begins to provide a starting point for comparison for assessing programme impact;

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Regulation 2.1(ii)
- Regulation 8.3 (Development of baseline data)
- Regulation 8.4 (Initial DSM programmes before baseline data is complete)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Regulation 8 (Load and market research and development of baseline data)
- Regulation 12.2(iv) (Assessment of baseline in DSM Programme Document)
- Regulation 16 (Evaluation, Measurement and Verification of DSM programmes)

**SUMMARY:** The Bureau means the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) established under sub-section (1) of Section 2 of The Energy Conservation Act, 2001. ## CHUNK 7: DEFINITION - "COST EFFECTIVENESS INDEX"

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation - Definition

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 1: General → Regulation 2: Definitions → Definition (v)

**PROVISION:** Regulation 2.1(v)

**TITLE:** Definition of "Cost Effectiveness Index"

**SUMMARY:** The Cost Effectiveness Index is a measurable indicator used to assess the economic attractiveness of any proposed investment in a DSM programme. It compares the costs of the DSM programme with the costs of energy production and delivery that would otherwise occur in the absence of such investment. This index is a key criterion for evaluating and approving DSM programmes and for determining programme prioritization and funding allocation.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Investment attractiveness metric
- Programme evaluation criterion
- Cost comparison methodology
- Cost-benefit analysis
- Programme selection metric

**TEXT:**

"Cost Effectiveness Index" means an indicator of the attractiveness of any investment in DSM programme or when compared to the costs of energy produced and delivered in the absence of such an investment.

## CHUNK 8: DEFINITION - "DEMAND SIDE MANAGEMENT"

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation - Definition

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 1: General → Regulation 2: Definitions → Definition (vi)

**PROVISION:** Regulation 2.1(vi)

**TITLE:** Definition of "Demand Side Management" (DSM)

**SUMMARY:** Demand Side Management (DSM) means the actions undertaken by a Distribution Licensee, beyond the point of supply, with the objective of altering end-use electricity consumption. DSM objectives include increasing demand, decreasing demand, shifting demand between peak and off-peak periods, and managing intermittent load demands. The overall purpose of DSM is to reduce costs for the Distribution Licensee in their overall operations and reduce system strain. DSM is a core regulatory framework for supply-demand optimization in the electricity sector.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Electricity demand management
- Distribution Licensee responsibility
- Point of supply (beyond)
- Demand alteration objectives
- Cost reduction strategy
- Load shifting and management
- Peak and off-peak demand management
- Intermittent load management

**TEXT:**

"Demand Side Management" means the actions of a Distribution Licensee, beyond the point of supply, with the objective of altering the end-use of electricity - whether it is to increase demand, decrease it, shift it between high and low peak periods, or manage it when there are intermittent load demands - in the overall interests of reducing Distribution Licensee costs.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
