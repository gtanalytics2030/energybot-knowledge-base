## CHUNK 9: DEFINITION - "DSM RESOURCE ACQUISITION"

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation - Definition

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 1: General → Regulation 2: Definitions → Definition (vii)

**PROVISION:** Regulation 2.1(vii)

**TITLE:** Definition of "DSM Resource Acquisition"

**SUMMARY:** DSM Resource Acquisition is a mechanism for implementing DSM projects through third parties and other organizations, rather than directly by the Distribution Licensee. These parties include customers, Energy Service Companies (ESCOs), Non-Government Organisations (NGOs), manufacturers, suppliers, and other private sector organizations. The Distribution Licensee provides payment or other support to these parties in exchange for the energy and load reductions they achieve. This mechanism facilitates broader participation and expertise in DSM programme implementation.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Third-party implementation mechanism
- Energy Service Companies participation
- Private sector engagement
- Customer participation
- NGO involvement
- Equipment manufacturers and suppliers
- Payment-based incentive structure
- Energy and load reduction achievement
- Resource acquisition methodology

**TEXT:**

"DSM Resource Acquisition" means a mechanism to implement DSM projects through customers, Energy Service Companies, Non-Government Organisations, manufacturers / suppliers, or other private sector organizations, with payment or any other support made to them by the Distribution Licensee for the resultant energy and load reductions

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Regulation 2.1(vii)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Regulation 2.1(viii) (Energy Services Company definition)
- Regulation 12.2(viii) (Implementation mechanism including DSM Resource Acquisition)
- Regulation 13.4 (Implementation through appropriate independent entity)

## CHUNK 10: DEFINITION - "ENERGY SERVICES COMPANY"

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation - Definition

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 1: General → Regulation 2: Definitions → Definition (viii)

**PROVISION:** Regulation 2.1(viii)

**TITLE:** Definition of "Energy Services Company"

**SUMMARY:** An Energy Services Company (ESCO) is a company that engages in the business of providing energy-efficient and load management equipment and/or services to end-use electricity consumers. ESCOs are critical implementation partners in DSM programmes. To function as an ESCO under these regulations, a company must be approved by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE), ensuring they meet national standards and capability requirements.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Energy efficiency services provider
- Load management equipment supplier
- End-use customer service provider
- Bureau of Energy Efficiency approval requirement
- Service provider qualification
- Equipment provision capability
- DSM implementation partner

**TEXT:**

"Energy Services Company" means a company which is in the business of providing energy efficient and load management equipment and/or services to end-use customers and is approved by Bureau

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Regulation 2.1(viii)
- Bureau approval condition

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**

- Regulation 2.1(vii) (DSM Resource Acquisition - includes ESCOs)
- Regulation 12.2(viii) (Implementation mechanism - Energy Service Companies mentioned)

## CHUNK 11: DEFINITION - "EVALUATION, MEASUREMENT AND VERIFICATION"

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation - Definition

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 1: General → Regulation 2: Definitions → Definition (ix)

**PROVISION:** Regulation 2.1(ix)

**TITLE:** Definition of "Evaluation, Measurement and Verification"

**SUMMARY:** Evaluation, Measurement and Verification (EMV) encompasses all activities undertaken to evaluate, measure, and verify the performance and other relevant aspects of DSM programmes and energy efficiency initiatives. EMV also includes assessment of the market environment in which these programmes operate. EMV is essential for determining actual programme impact, validating claimed energy/demand savings, and ensuring accountability in DSM implementation.

**KEY TOPICS:**

- Savings verification
- Market assessment
- Energy efficiency programme assessment
- Performance monitoring activity
- Impact validation
- Accountability mechanism

**TEXT:**

"Evaluation, Measurement and Verification" means activities which evaluate, measure and verify performance or other aspects of DSM/energy efficiency programs or their market environment

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation - Definition

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 1: General → Regulation 2: Definitions → Definition (x)

**PROVISION:** Regulation 2.1(x)

**TITLE:** Definition of "Monitoring and Reporting"

**SUMMARY:** Monitoring and Reporting refers to the activities undertaken to monitor and evaluate the progress of DSM and energy efficiency programmes implemented by a Distribution Licensee. These activities track programme implementation status, achievement against targets, expenditure, consumer participation, and outcomes. Regular monitoring and reporting ensure accountability, facilitate course correction, and provide data for regulatory oversight of DSM activities.

## CHUNK 13: DEFINITION - "POINT OF SUPPLY"

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation - Definition

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 1: General → Regulation 2: Definitions → Definition (xi)

**PROVISION:** Regulation 2.1(xi)

**TITLE:** Definition of "Point of Supply"

**SUMMARY:** Unless otherwise agreed between the parties, the Point of Supply is defined as the incoming terminal of the cut-out (circuit breaker/isolator) installed by the consumer on the consumer's premises. For LT (Low Tension) consumers, this is the location of meters and switches installed at the consumer's premises. For HT (High Tension) and EHT (Extra High Tension) consumers, this is the location of control switchgears (if installed) on the consumer's premises, subject to the provisions of this code. The Point of Supply serves as the demarcation between Distribution Licensee responsibility and consumer responsibility for DSM actions.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Point of demarcation in electricity supply
- Circuit breaker/cut-out location
- Consumer premises boundary
- LT consumer supply point
- HT consumer supply point
- EHT consumer supply point
- Meters and switches location
- Control switchgears location
- DSM responsibility boundary

**TEXT:**

Unless otherwise agreed to, the "Point of Supply" shall be the incoming terminal of the cut-out installed by the consumer, i.e.

a) Meters/switches installed at the Consumer's Premises in case of LT Consumers;

b) Control switchgears that may be installed in the Consumer's Premises as provided subject to provision of this code in case of HT and EHT Consumers.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Regulation 2.1(xi)
- Reference to "this code" (Electricity Supply Code or similar regulations)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Regulation 2.1(vi) (DSM definition - actions "beyond the point of supply")
- Regulation 7.3(i) (DSM Cell responsibility for load research and baseline data development)
- Regulation 12.2(vii) (Marketing and delivery strategy in programme document)
