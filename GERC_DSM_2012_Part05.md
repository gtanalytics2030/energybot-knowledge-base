## CHUNK 18: REGULATION 7 - CONSTITUTION OF DSM CELL, ITS ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 3: DSM Cell → Regulation 7

**PROVISION:** Regulation 7 (Constitution of DSM Cell, its roles & responsibilities)

**TITLE:** Constitution of DSM Cell - Composition, Authority and Functions

**SUMMARY:** Every Distribution Licensee must establish a dedicated DSM Cell within one month of these Regulations taking effect. The Cell shall have a defined hierarchical composition: one Superintending Engineer (or above), two Deputy Engineers, five Junior Engineers, and three clerical staff members. The Commission may approve variations in composition and size based on licensee circumstances. The DSM Cell must be provided with necessary authority and resources to execute its assigned functions. The Cell is responsible for load research and baseline data development, formulation of DSM Plans, design and development of DSM projects (including cost-benefit analysis and implementation planning), seeking required approvals, implementing DSM programmes, and any additional functions assigned by the Commission.

**TEXT:**

**7. Constitution of DSM Cell, its roles & responsibilities**

7.1. Every Distribution Licensee shall constitute a DSM Cell within one month of notification of these regulations.

The DSM Cell shall comprise the Staff as under;

 - One officer not below the rank of Superintending Engineer
 - Two Officers not below the rank of Deputy Engineer
 - Five officers not below the rank of Junior Engineer.
 - Three members of clerical staff.

Provided that the composition and size of the cell may vary with approval of the Commission.

7.2. The DSM Cell so constituted shall be provided with necessary authority and resources so as to execute the functions assigned to it under these Regulations

7.3. The DSM Cell shall be responsible for:

i. Load Research and development of baseline data

ii. Formulation of DSM Plan

iii. Design and development of DSM projects including cost benefit analysis, plans for implementation, monitoring and reporting and for measurement and verification

iv. Seeking necessary approvals to DSM Plan and individual programmes

v. Implementation of DSM programmes

vi. Any other additional function that may be assigned by the Commission from time to time

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:**
The DSM Cell is the operational engine for all demand-side management activities at Distribution Licensees. It bridges regulatory requirements with practical implementation, combining technical expertise (engineers) with administrative support. The provision that composition can vary with Commission approval allows flexibility for smaller licensees while maintaining essential functions. The Cell's responsibilities span the entire DSM lifecycle: planning (load research, baseline development, plan formulation), design (projects, cost-benefit analysis), approval management, and execution (implementation, monitoring, verification).
