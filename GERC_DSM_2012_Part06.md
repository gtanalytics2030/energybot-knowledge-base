## CHUNK 19: REGULATION 8 - LOAD AND MARKET RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT OF BASELINE DATA

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 4: DSM Process → Regulation 8

**PROVISION:** Regulation 8 (Load and Market Research and Development of Baseline Data)

**TITLE:** Load and Market Research and Development of Baseline Data

**SUMMARY:** Distribution Licensees must conduct comprehensive load and market research as the foundation for DSM programme design. Load research identifies target consumer segments and end uses suitable for DSM programmes and builds the necessary database. Market research estimates market potential for energy efficiency technologies, establishes key performance indicators, and determines existing baseline market conditions. Based on research results, Distribution Licensees shall develop baseline data for their supply areas. Initial DSM programmes may be designed and implemented based on available data and Bureau studies before complete baseline data is established, ensuring DSM initiatives are not delayed pending data completion.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Load research requirement
- Target consumer segment identification
- End-use identification for DSM
- Database development
- Market research requirement
- Market potential estimation
- Energy efficiency technology market assessment
- Key performance indicators establishment
- Baseline market conditions determination
- Baseline data development
- Data foundation for programme design
- Bureau studies utilization
- Initial programmes without complete baseline data

**TEXT:**

**8. Load and market research and development of baseline data**

8.1. Distribution Licensee shall undertake load research to identify the target consumer segment/s and end uses for DSM programmes to build the necessary database

8.2. Distribution Licensee shall undertake market research to estimate market potential for specific energy efficiency technologies and applications, establish key performance indicators, and determine existing baseline market conditions

8.3. On the basis of the results of load and market research, the Distribution Licensee shall develop baseline data for its area of supply

8.4. Distribution Licensee shall design, develop and implement the initial DSM programmes on the basis of available data and studies completed by Bureau till the complete baseline data is available for its area of supply and establishment of base line data shall not be a pre-requisite for design of such initial DSM programmes by the Distribution Licensees.

## CHUNK 20: REGULATION 9 - FORMULATION OF DSM PLAN

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 4: DSM Process → Regulation 9

**PROVISION:** Regulation 9 (Formulation of DSM Plan)

**TITLE:** Formulation of DSM Plan

**SUMMARY:** Distribution Licensees shall formulate a perspective DSM Plan covering the duration of the MYT Control Period and submit it to the Commission within one year of notification of these Regulations. The Plan must include: an overall DSM goal, descriptions of DSM programmes to be implemented, implementation processes and schedules, a monitoring and reporting plan, and indicative cost effectiveness assessment of programmes. The first DSM Plan shall be prepared within one year of notification and shall cover until the end of the ongoing MYT Control Period. Distribution Licensees must include all relevant Bureau-developed DSM programmes in their perspective plan. Additionally, Licensees shall submit rolling annual plans (not inconsistent with the perspective plan) with their Annual Tariff Petitions. Programme selection and prioritization must be guided by Commission Cost Effectiveness Guidelines, the DSM Objectives stated in Regulation 3, alignment with national efforts by the Bureau, and programmes with high visibility for consumer awareness.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Perspective DSM Plan requirement
- MYT Control Period coverage
- Plan submission timeline
- First plan: Within one year of notification
- Plan content requirements:
  - Overall goal
  - Programme descriptions
  - Implementation process and schedule
  - Monitoring and reporting plan
  - Cost effectiveness assessment
- First plan duration: Until end of current MYT period
- Bureau programme inclusion requirement
- Rolling annual plans
- Annual Tariff Petition coordination
- Programme selection criteria
- Cost effectiveness guidance
- DSM objectives alignment
- National programme alignment
- Consumer awareness consideration

**TEXT:**

**9. Formulation of DSM Plan**

9.1. Distribution Licensee shall formulate and submit to the Commission a perspective DSM Plan covering period of the control period, within one year of notification of these regulations. The Plan shall include

i. An overall goal for DSM Plan

ii. Description of DSM programmes to form a part of DSM Plan

iii. Implementation process and schedule of each programme in the plan as a whole

iv. Plan for Monitoring and Reporting

v. Indicative cost effectiveness assessment of programmes

Notwithstanding the above, the first DSM Plan shall be prepared within one year of the date of these Regulations and shall be for the period till the end of on-going MYT Control Period.

9.2. The Distribution Licensee shall include all relevant DSM programmes (including multi-state programmes) developed by Bureau in its perspective plan as and when such programmes are announced by Bureau.

9.3. The Distribution Licensee shall submit on rolling basis, an annual plan, not inconsistent with the perspective plan, for upcoming year, along with the Annual Tariff Petitions.

9.4. Selection and prioritisation of various DSM programmes in the DSM Plan shall be guided by the following factors:

i. The Cost effectiveness guidelines issued by the Commission

ii. DSM Objectives identified in Regulation 3

iii. Whether the proposed programmes supplement National level efforts adopted by the Bureau

iv. Programmes with high visibility and therefore potential for creation of awareness within consumers

## CHUNK 21: REGULATION 10 - REVIEW AND APPROVAL OF DSM PLAN BY THE COMMISSION

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 4: DSM Process → Regulation 10

**PROVISION:** Regulation 10 (Review and Approval of DSM Plan by the Commission)

**TITLE:** Review and Approval of DSM Plan by the Commission

**SUMMARY:** Distribution Licensees must submit their DSM Plan to the Commission for approval within six months of technical potential assessment becoming available for the first time. For subsequent MYT Control Periods, the DSM Plan must be submitted at least six months before the filing of the MYT Petition. The Commission may adopt procedures as specified in its Conduct of Business Regulations for approving the DSM Plan. This provision ensures timely regulatory review and integration of DSM planning with overall tariff determination processes.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Commission approval requirement for DSM Plan
- First submission: Within 6 months of technical potential availability
- Subsequent submissions: Before 6 months of MYT Petition filing
- Conduct of Business Regulations procedure
- Timing coordination with MYT petition process
- Regulatory review process
- Plan approval authority

**TEXT:**

**10. Review and approval of DSM plan by the Commission**

10.1. Distribution Licensee shall submit the DSM Plan to the Commission for approval within 6 months of technical potential available for the first time, and afterwards before 6 months of the filing of MYT Petition

10.2. The Commission may adopt procedures as specified in the Conduct of Business Regulations for according approval to the DSM Plan

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Regulation 10.1, 10.2
- Conduct of Business Regulations (procedural reference)
- MYT Petition filing reference

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**

- Conduct of Business Regulations 2004 (procedural framework)

**REGULATORY AUTHORITY:**
