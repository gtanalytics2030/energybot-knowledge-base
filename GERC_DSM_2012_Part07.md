## CHUNK 22: REGULATION 11 - PREPARATION OF DSM PROGRAMME DOCUMENT

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 4: DSM Process → Regulation 11

**PROVISION:** Regulation 11 (Preparation of DSM Programme Document)

**TITLE:** Preparation of DSM Programme Document

**SUMMARY:** For each DSM programme included in the approved DSM Plan, a detailed Programme Document must be prepared separately. The document shall include general information, technology specifications, deployment schedule, budget, cost effectiveness assessment, detailed implementation plan, and estimation of expected savings. For each programme, cost-benefit analysis must be conducted in accordance with the Commission's Cost Effectiveness Guidelines issued from time to time. The Programme Document serves as the detailed operational blueprint for executing individual DSM initiatives within the broader plan framework.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Individual programme documentation requirement
- Separate document per programme
- General programme information
- Technology specification
- Deployment schedule
- Budget detail
- Cost effectiveness assessment
- Cost-benefit analysis
- Detailed implementation planning
- Savings estimation
- Commission guidelines compliance
- Programme-level detail requirement

**TEXT:**

**11. Preparation of DSM Programme Document**

11.1. For each DSM programme included in the DSM Plan, a detailed description shall be provided in a separate Programme Document. The description shall include general information, technology, schedule for deployment, budget, cost effectiveness assessment, detailed implementation plan, estimation of savings, etc.

11.2. For each DSM programme cost benefit analysis shall be carried out as per the guidelines (guidelines to be checked) issued by the Commission on Cost effectiveness from time to time.

## CHUNK 23: REGULATION 12 - APPROVAL OF DSM PROGRAMME DOCUMENT

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 4: DSM Process → Regulation 12

**PROVISION:** Regulation 12 (Approval of DSM Programme Document)

**TITLE:** Approval of DSM Programme Document

**SUMMARY:** Prior to implementing any DSM programme, the Distribution Licensee must obtain Commission approval of the detailed Programme Document. Each approved Programme Document shall include: programme description and objectives/rationale; consumer segments and estimated participation; baseline assessment; cost effectiveness assessment per Commission guidelines; cost recovery and performance incentive mechanisms; marketing and delivery strategy; implementation schedule; implementation mechanism (ESCOs, bidding, DSM Resource Acquisition); monitoring and evaluation plan; and consumer awareness plan including training/seminars/workshops. The Commission approves programmes that align with DSM Objectives stated in Regulation 3. The Commission may direct modifications to proposed or ongoing programmes for consistency with DSM Objectives, and must provide Distribution Licensees adequate notice to inform consumers of any modifications.

**TEXT:**

**12. Approval of DSM Programme Document**

12.1. Prior to implementing any DSM programme, the Distribution Licensee must obtain approval of the Commission.

12.2. Each Programme Document shall include the following:

i. Description of the programme;

ii. Objectives and rationale for the programme;

iii. Consumer segments and estimated level of participation;

iv. Estimate of baseline;

v. Assessment of programme in line with Cost Effectiveness Guidelines issued by the Commission

vi. Mechanism for recovery of cost and performance incentives;

vii. Marketing, delivery strategy and Implementation schedule;

viii. Implementation mechanism e.g. Energy Service Companies, Bidding for DSM Projects, DSM Resource Acquisition, etc

ix. Monitoring and evaluation plan;

x. Plan for Training/Seminars/Workshops to increase consumer awareness

12.3. The Commission shall approve a DSM program if it is in line with the Objectives set out in Section 3 of the Regulations. The Commission may direct modifications to proposed or on-going programmes to ensure consistency with the DSM Objectives. However, the Commission shall allow Distribution Licensee adequate time to notify consumers of program modification.

## CHUNK 24: REGULATION 13 - IMPLEMENTATION OF DSM PROGRAMMES

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 4: DSM Process → Regulation 13

**PROVISION:** Regulation 13 (Implementation of DSM Programmes)

**TITLE:** Implementation of DSM Programmes

**SUMMARY:** Distribution Licensees shall implement DSM programmes in accordance with guidelines issued by the Commission from time to time and in the manner approved by the Commission. Licensees must undertake implementation of "quick gain" DSM programmes that offer immediate savings and visible results. Implementation may be conducted by the Distribution Licensee directly or through appropriate independent entities (ESCOs, contractors, etc.). Regardless of implementation mechanism, the Distribution Licensee must ensure that continuity and consistency are maintained throughout programme execution and that consumer interests are not compromised. The Licensee retains ultimate responsibility for programme outcomes and consumer protection.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Commission-approved implementation requirement
- Adherence to Commission guidelines
- Approved implementation methodology
- Quick gain DSM programmes mandatory
- Direct implementation option
- Third-party/independent entity implementation
- ESCO and contractor utilization
- Continuity and consistency requirement
- Consumer interest protection
- Programme responsibility
- Implementation oversight
- Quality assurance requirement

**TEXT:**

**13. Implementation of DSM programmes**

13.1. Distribution Licensee shall undertake implementation of DSM programmes in line with the guidelines issued by the Commission from time to time

13.2. The implementation of the programme shall be undertaken in the manner as approved by the Commission

13.3. Distribution Licensee shall undertake implementation of quick gain DSM programmes

13.4. Distribution Licensee shall carry out implementation of activities assigned to it either by itself or through appropriate independent entity

While doing so, the Distribution Licensee shall ensure that continuity and consistency are maintained and interest of the consumers is not compromised

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Regulation 13.1, 13.2, 13.3, 13.4
- Commission guidelines and approval reference

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**

- Regulation 12.2(viii) (Implementation mechanism)

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:**
Regulation 13 balances flexibility with accountability. Distribution Licensees may implement programmes themselves or delegate to ESCOs and other third parties, but retain ultimate responsibility. The "quick gain" programmes requirement ensures visible early results that build consumer confidence in DSM initiatives. The consumer interest protection clause ensures that while cost efficiency is pursued, consumer service standards are maintained. All implementation must align with Commission-approved methodology and programmes.
