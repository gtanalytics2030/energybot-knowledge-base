## CHUNK 33: REGULATION 22 - DIFFICULTY PROVISION

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 7: Miscellaneous → Regulation 22

**PROVISION:** Regulation 22 (Difficulty Provision)

**TITLE:** Power to Remove Difficulties in Implementation

**SUMMARY:** If any difficulty arises in giving effect to the provisions of these Regulations, the Commission may, by general or specific order, make such provisions as may appear necessary for removing the difficulty, provided such provisions are not inconsistent with the parent Electricity Act, 2003. This is a safety-valve provision that enables the Commission to address unforeseen implementation challenges without requiring formal amendment of the regulations. The power applies through either general orders (affecting all licensees) or specific orders (addressing particular licensee situations), ensuring flexibility while maintaining consistency with the enabling legislation.

## CHUNK 36: REGULATION 25 - SAVING OF INHERENT POWER OF THE COMMISSION

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Demand Side Management) Regulations, 2012

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 7: Miscellaneous → Regulation 25

**PROVISION:** Regulation 25 (Saving of Inherent Power of the Commission)

**TITLE:** Reservation of Commission's Inherent Powers

**SUMMARY:** Regulation 25 comprehensively preserves the Commission's inherent powers and authority while these regulations are in force. Clause 25.1 provides that nothing in these Regulations limits the Commission's inherent power to make orders necessary for justice or to prevent abuse of process. Clause 25.2 states that the Commission may adopt procedures at variance with regulation provisions if special circumstances warrant, provided reasons are recorded. Clause 25.3 clarifies that the Commission's authority to deal with matters and exercise powers under the Electricity Act (for which these regulations provide no specific rules) is not curtailed; the Commission may address such matters as it deems fit. Collectively, these provisions ensure the Commission retains maximum flexibility and authority for equitable and effective regulation beyond the specific framework of these DSM Regulations.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Inherent power preservation
- Justice and fairness authority
- Abuse of process prevention
- Procedural flexibility
- Special circumstances authority
- Recording of reasons requirement
- Authority under parent Act
- Regulatory discretion preservation
- Equitable action authority
- Unforeseen circumstance handling

**TEXT:**

**25. Saving of inherent power of the Commission**

25.1 Nothing in these Regulations shall be deemed to limit or otherwise affect the inherent power of the Commission to make such orders as may be necessary for ends of justice or to prevent the abuse of the process of the Commission.

25.2 Nothing in these Regulations shall bar the Commission from adopting in conformity with the provisions of the Acts, a procedure, which is at variance with any of the provisions of these Regulations, if the Commission, in view of the special circumstances of a matter or class of matters and for reasons to be recorded in writing, deems it necessary or expedient for dealing with such a matter or class of matters.

25.3 Nothing in these Regulations shall, expressly or impliedly, bar the Commission to deal with any matter or exercise any power under the Acts for which no Regulations have been framed, and the Commission may deal with such matters, powers and functions in a manner it thinks fit.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Regulation 25.1, 25.2, 25.3
- Acts reference (Electricity Act, 2003 and related legislation)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**
- Regulation 21 (Amendment power - related authority)
- Regulation 22 (Difficulty removal - related power)
- Regulation 23 (Orders and directives - related power)
- Electricity Act, 2003 (Parent enabling legislation)

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:**
Regulation 25 is a critical safeguard that ensures these DSM Regulations, while comprehensive, do not inadvertently constrain the Commission's broader regulatory authority. The three-clause structure addresses three scenarios: (1) Justice and abuse prevention (25.1) - ensuring fairness; (2) Procedural deviation with recorded reasons (25.2) - allowing flexibility while maintaining transparency; (3) Powers not addressed by regulations (25.3) - ensuring gap coverage. Together, these clauses preserve regulatory effectiveness and equitable outcomes while maintaining the specificity of the DSM Regulations.
