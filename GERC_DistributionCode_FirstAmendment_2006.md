## CHUNK A.3: AMENDMENT 1 - SECTION 3.3.1 (DISTRIBUTION CODE REVIEW PANEL COMPOSITION)

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Distribution Code (First Amendment), 2006

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Amendment Notification / Substantive Amendment

**HIERARCHY:** Amendment Document / Clause 2 / Section 3.3.1 Amendment

**PROVISION:** Clause 2 - Amendment to Section 3.3.1 of the Principal Code

**TITLE:** Distribution Code Review Panel Chairperson and Member-Secretary Appointment - GETCO Designation Change

**SUMMARY:** Amendment 1 modifies Section 3.3.1 of the principal Distribution Code regarding the composition and appointment of the Distribution Code Review Panel. The key change is that the Chairperson of the Distribution Code Review Panel shall be from GETCO (Gujarat Energy Transmission and Despatch Company), and GERC shall nominate the Member-Secretary on a rotation basis from amongst the members of the Distribution Licensees. This replaces the original provision requiring both Chairperson and Member-Secretary to be nominated on rotation from Distribution Licensees with the Chairperson specifically coming from GETCO. The amendment specifies four categories of Panel members: (a) One senior technical officer from each Distribution Licensee, (b) One Member from State Transmission Utility (STU) or SLDC, (c) One Member from a Transmission Licensee representing all Transmission Licensees (rotating representation), (d) One Member from Captive Power Plants, co-generation units, and conventional/non-conventional generating units (rotating representation). GERC shall develop a list of potential candidates reflecting balance of various interest groups in consultation with Distribution Licensees.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- GETCO (Gujarat Energy Transmission and Despatch Company)
- Chairperson appointment authority
- Member-Secretary nomination (rotation basis)
- Distribution Licensee representation
- State Transmission Utility (STU) representation
- Transmission Licensee representation (rotating)
- Generating unit representation (rotating)
- Candidate list development
- Interest group balance requirement
- Panel composition and tenure

**TEXT:**

**2. Amendment to section 3.3.1 of the principal code:**

**Section 3.3.1 of the principal code shall be substituted as under:-**

**"3.3.1**

The chairperson and members shall be part time members of the panel. The review panel shall generally consist of the following members and having knowledge and practical experience of technical matters related to electricity supply by utilities. **The Chairperson of the Distribution Code Review Panel shall be from GETCO. GERC shall nominate the Member-secretary of the Distribution Code Review Panel on rotation basis from amongst the members of the Distribution Licensees.**

The members of the review panel shall be as follows:

(a) One senior technical officer from each Distribution Licensee.

(b) One Member from State Transmission Utility (STU) or SLDC.

(c) One Member from a Transmission Licensee to represent all Transmission Licensees. After the tenure over, such Member, the representative of a different Transmission Licensee will be a member of review panel.

(d) One Member from among Captive Power Plants, co-generation units, conventional and non-conventional generating units, to represent all such Users in Gujarat State. On completion of tenure, the Member shall be replaced by another person belonging to a different Distribution Licensee's territory.

GERC shall develop a list of potential candidates to serve on the Distribution Code Review Panel, which reflects a balance of the various interest groups in consultation with the Distribution Licensee."

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:**
The First Amendment 2006 makes a significant institutional change: appointment of the Distribution Code Review Panel Chairperson shifts from rotation among Distribution Licensees to designation from GETCO. This enhances transmission-distribution coordination by placing a transmission entity representative in a leadership position on the panel. The Member-Secretary appointment remains with GERC nominating from Distribution Licensee rotation. This reflects Gujarat's reorganization of transmission entities post-2003 legislation.

## CHUNK A.4: AMENDMENT 2 - SECTION 7.1.1 (METERING AND PROTECTION CODE - CEA REGULATIONS PROVISO)

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Distribution Code (First Amendment), 2006

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Amendment Notification / Substantive Amendment

**HIERARCHY:** Amendment Document / Clause 3 / Section 7.1.1 Amendment

**PROVISION:** Clause 3 - Amendment to Section 7.1.1 of the Principal Code

**TITLE:** Metering and Protection Code - Central Electricity Authority Regulations Override Provision

**SUMMARY:** Amendment 2 modifies Section 7.1.1 of the principal Distribution Code (Metering and Protection Code introduction) by adding a critical proviso: regulations on installation and operation of meters made by the Central Electricity Authority shall prevail over the Distribution Code provisions in Sections 7.2 to 7.4 (Operational Metering, Tariff and Commercial Metering, and Energy Import/Export Measurement). This amendment establishes a clear regulatory hierarchy placing central government (CEA) metering standards above state-level (GERC) Distribution Code metering specifications, ensuring uniformity of metering standards across India. The original Section 7.1.1 specifies that metering procedures apply with respect to operational metering, tariff metering, and commercial metering, and specifies general protection guidelines. The amendment preserves this scope but adds the CEA override provision. Section 7.1.1 introduction states: "This section specifies the procedure for metering in Distribution System with respect to operational metering as well as tariff and commercial metering. This section also specifies the general guidelines for protection of Distribution system."

**TEXT:**

**3. Amendment to section 7.1.1 of the principal code:**

**Section 7.1.1 of the principal code shall be substituted as under:-**

**"7.1.1**

This section specifies the procedure for metering in Distribution System with respect to operational metering as well as tariff and commercial metering. This section also specifies the general guidelines for protection of Distribution system.

**Provided that the regulations on installation and operation of meters made by Central Electricity Authority shall prevail over provisions 7.2 to 7.4 of Distribution Code."**

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**

**CROSS-REFERENCES:**

- Distribution Code Section 7.4 (Energy Import/Export Measurement - main/check meters, accuracy class 0.2)
- Electricity Act 2003 (CEA regulatory authority)
- Central Electricity Authority (statutory body under Ministry of Power, Government of India)

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:**
The First Amendment 2006 establishes a critical regulatory hierarchy by subordinating GERC's Distribution Code metering standards (Sections 7.2-7.4) to Central Electricity Authority (CEA) meter installation and operation regulations. This reflects the federal structure of Indian electricity regulation where central standards prevail over state-level standards to ensure consistency across states and between state and national transmission systems. The CEA meters on state transmission interfaces (Connection Points between GERC Distribution System and national/state transmission system) and large consumer metering must comply with CEA regulations rather than GERC specifications alone. This amendment appears to implement unified national metering standards following the 2003 Electricity Act restructuring.

## AMENDMENTS IMPACT ON PRINCIPAL CODE

**Amendment 1 - Section 3.3.1 Impact:**
- **Original Provision:** Chairperson and Member-Secretary nominated on rotation basis from Distribution Licensees
- **Amendment Change:** Chairperson specifically from GETCO; Member-Secretary nominated from Distribution Licensees on rotation
- **Regulatory Effect:** Establishes GETCO (transmission/despatch entity) leadership in Distribution Code review process
- **Related Sections Affected:** Section 3.1 (Panel establishment), Section 3.3 (Panel composition), Section 3.4 (Panel functions)
- **Stakeholder Impact:** Distribution Licensees, GETCO, STU/SLDC, Transmission Licensees, Generating companies

**Amendment 2 - Section 7.1.1 Impact:**
- **Original Provision:** Section specifies procedures for operational, tariff, and commercial metering; general protection guidelines
- **Amendment Change:** Adds proviso that CEA regulations on meter installation and operation prevail over Distribution Code Sections 7.2-7.4
- **Regulatory Effect:** Establishes regulatory hierarchy placing central standards above state-level standards
- **Related Sections Affected:** Section 7.2 (Operational Metering), Section 7.3 (Tariff Metering), Section 7.4 (Energy Import/Export Measurement)
- **Stakeholder Impact:** Distribution Licensees, Consumers, CEA, Generating companies, Metering equipment manufacturers
