DOCUMENT: GERC (Distribution Licence) Regulations, 2005 – Notification No 4 of 2005

DOCUMENT TYPE: Regulation

HIERARCHY: Chapter 1 / Preliminary

PROVISION: Regulation 1

TITLE: Short Title, Extent and Commencement

SUMMARY:
Regulation 1 establishes the formal identity, territorial scope, and commencement date of the GERC Distribution Licence Regulations. The regulations apply to the entire state of Gujarat and establish the procedural and substantive framework governing the grant, maintenance, and revocation of distribution licences under the Electricity Act 2003 and the Gujarat Electricity Industry (Reorganisation and Regulation) Act 2003. The commencement date is to be notified by the Commission.

KEY TOPICS: Distribution Licence, Regulations, Gujarat, Electricity Act 2003, Scope, Applicability, Commencement

TEXT:
1. Short Title, Extent and Commencement

a. These Regulations shall be called the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Distribution Licence) Regulations, 2005.

b. These Regulations shall apply to the whole of the State of Gujarat.

c. These Regulations shall come into force on such date as may be notified by the Commission.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Electricity Act 2003 (36 of 2003), Gujarat Electricity Industry (Reorganisation and Regulation) Act 2003 (Gujarat Act No. 24 of 2003)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Chapter 2 (Definitions), Chapter 3 (Procedure for Grant of Distribution Licence)

DOCUMENT TYPE: Regulation

HIERARCHY: Chapter 2 / Definitions, Interpretation and Review

PROVISION: Regulation 2 – General Interpretation Rules

TITLE: Interpretation of Undefined Terms and Expressions

SUMMARY:
Regulation 2 establishes hierarchical rules for interpreting undefined terms and expressions. Words and expressions not defined in these Regulations but defined in the Electricity Act 2003 take their meanings from that Act. Terms defined in other competent legislation applicable to the electricity industry in Gujarat take their statutory meanings. Terms defined nowhere take their meanings as generally understood in the electricity industry context.

KEY TOPICS: Interpretation, Statutory Construction, Undefined Terms, Electricity Industry Terminology

TEXT:
Words and expressions used and not defined in these Regulations but defined in the Act shall have the meanings assigned to them in the Act. Expressions used herein but not specifically defined in these Regulations or in the Act but defined under any law passed by a competent legislature and applicable to the electricity industry in the state shall have the meaning assigned to them in such law. Expressions used herein but not specifically defined in the Regulations or in the Act or any law passed by a competent legislature shall have the meaning as is generally assigned to them in the context of electricity industry.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Electricity Act 2003, Applicable State Laws

CROSS-REFERENCES: Regulation 2(ii) (Interpretation rules), Regulation 2(iii) (Review power)

PROVISION: Regulation 2(ii)

TITLE: Rules of Interpretation

SUMMARY:
Regulation 2(ii) establishes grammatical and structural rules for interpreting these Regulations. Singular and plural terms are interchangeable. The words "include" and "including" are deemed to mean "without limitation." References to "Regulations" include amendments and modifications. Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. References to statutes include all amendments and consolidations thereof.

KEY TOPICS: Interpretation, Grammatical Rules, Statutory Construction, Amendment Provisions

TEXT:
(ii) Interpretation

In the interpretation of these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

(a) words in the singular or plural term, as the case may be, shall also be deemed to include the plural or the singular term, respectively;

(b) the terms "include" or "including" shall be deemed to be followed by "without limitation" or "but not limited to" regardless of whether such terms are followed by such phrases or words of like import;

(c) references herein to the "Regulations" shall be construed as a reference to these Regulations as amended or modified by the Commission from time to time in accordance with the applicable laws in force;

(d) the headings are inserted for convenience and may not be taken into account for the purpose of interpretation of these Regulations;

(e) references to the statutes, regulations or guidelines shall be construed as including all statutory provisions consolidating, amending or replacing such statutes, regulations or guidelines, referred to.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Interpretation Act principles

CROSS-REFERENCES: All chapters and sections of these Regulations

KEY TOPICS: Regulatory Review, Amendment Authority, Open Access, Market Development, Section 66 of Electricity Act

TEXT:
(iii) Review

These Regulations may be reviewed by the Commission from time to time, inter alia for securing harmonization with the regulation of open access and/or to cater to the needs of developing power markets, in accordance with Section 66 of the Act.
