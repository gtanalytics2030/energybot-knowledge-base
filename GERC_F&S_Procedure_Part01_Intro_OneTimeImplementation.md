## Chunk 3: Applicability – Scope of the Procedure

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Notification No.1 2019 Forecasting, Scheduling, DSM & related matters Solar and Wind generation sources regulation 2019. Implementation Procedure

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation Implementation Procedure

**HIERARCHY:** Section – Applicability

**PROVISION:** Applicability

**TITLE:** Applicability and Scope of the Forecasting, Scheduling, and DSM Procedure

**SUMMARY:**
This procedure applies to all wind and solar generators with combined installed capacity exceeding 1 MW connected to the State grid/substation, including those connected via pooling stations, selling or consuming generated power within or outside the State. In cases of multiple renewable energy generators connected to a common feeder of a DISCOM or STU with aggregate capacity above 1 MW, the point of scheduling and energy accounting is at the end of the common distribution feeder. Self-consumption plants connected directly or indirectly to DISCOMs are excluded; only surplus/exportable capacity with valid PPAs exceeding 1 MW is covered.

**KEY TOPICS:** Applicability, Wind Generators, Solar Generators, 1 MW threshold, Pooling Stations, Common Feeder, Self-Consumption, PPA, Scheduling, Energy Accounting

**TEXT:**
This procedure shall apply to all wind and solar generators having combined installed capacity above 1 MW connected to the State grid/substation, including those connected via pooling stations, and selling generated power within or outside the State or consuming power generated for self-consumption.

In case of multiple RE generators connected to a common feeder of DISCOM / STU, it is clarified that aggregate RE capacity connected to a common feeder will be considered for the applicability of the said regulation. In case of aggregate capacity is more than 1 MW, point of scheduling and energy accounting would be at the end of the common distribution feeder and terminated at a sub-station of distribution company / STU / CTU. De-pooling may be done as per the guideline of GERC Notification No. 1 of 2019.

It is further clarified that RE plants installed for self-consumption connected to DISCOM/STU directly or indirectly would not be covered under above regulation. Only surplus/exportable capacity having valid PPA (more than 1 MW) would be considered under this regulation.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** GERC Notification No. 1 of 2019, Guidelines on De-pooling, Valid PPA requirements

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Section 1 (One-Time Implementation) for data submission requirements, Section 2 (Day-to-day requirements) for operational procedures applicable to covered generators

## Chunk 4: One-Time Implementation – Nameplate and Site-Related Data

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Notification No.1 2019 Forecasting, Scheduling, DSM & related matters Solar and Wind generation sources regulation 2019. Implementation Procedure

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation Implementation Procedure

**HIERARCHY:** Section 1 / Subsection 1.1

**PROVISION:** Section 1.1 – Provision of Name Plate and Site Related Data

**TITLE:** One-Time Submission of Nameplate and Site-Related Static Data

**SUMMARY:**
At the beginning of operations, QCAs and RE generators must furnish nameplate and site-related desirable data for each windmill/solar panel set and each RE pooling station as specified in Annexures 1.1, 1.2, and 1.3. This includes ABT meter details, weather sensor details, and other relevant information. Any subsequent changes in such information must be reported to SLDC within 7 working days. SLDC provides login credentials for EASS (Energy Accounting and Scheduling Software) to enable day-ahead and intraday forecasting and scheduling, with access provided to QCAs and RE generators to view relevant scheduling and energy accounting data.

**KEY TOPICS:** Nameplate Data, Site Data, ABT Meter, Weather Sensors, EASS Access, Data Submission, QCA, RE Generator, Pooling Station, One-Time Implementation, Change Notification

**TEXT:**
The SLDC shall require Name plate and site related desirable data of each wind mill/ solar panel set, that of each RE pooling station as specified and format given in Annexure 1.1, 1.2 and 1.3 for wind, solar static data and statutory compliance respectively, apart from other information like ABT meter detail, weather sensor detail etc. if any. This shall be duly compiled and furnished by QCA / RE Generator, as the case may be, at the beginning and further any change in the information, furnished earlier, shall be shared with SLDC within 7 (seven) working days from such changes.

Login Id and Password for access of EASS (Energy Accounting and Scheduling Software) will be provided by SLDC for forecasting and scheduling of RE generator for Day ahead as well as intraday revisions. The same shall be used by the QCA / RE Generator to know their relevant data of scheduling and energy accounting.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Annexure 1.1 (Wind Nameplate Data Format), Annexure 1.2 (Solar Nameplate Data Format), Annexure 1.3 (Statutory Compliance Details), EASS Software specifications

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Section 1.2 (Real-time Data Requirements), Section 2 (Day-to-day Operational Requirements), Annexures 1.1-1.3

## Chunk 5: Real-Time Data – Electrical Parameters for Wind and Solar Plants

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Notification No.1 2019 Forecasting, Scheduling, DSM & related matters Solar and Wind generation sources regulation 2019. Implementation Procedure

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation Implementation Procedure

**HIERARCHY:** Section 1 / Subsection 1.2 / 1.2.1

**PROVISION:** Section 1.2.1 – Electrical Parameters – Real-Time Data

**TITLE:** Real-Time Electrical Parameter Data Requirements at Pooling Station Level

**SUMMARY:**
SLDC requires real-time electrical parameter data at the pooling station level for all elements, including active power (MW) and reactive power (MVAR) as mandatory parameters, plus voltage (Volt), frequency (Hz), circuit breaker (CB) and isolator status, and sequence of event (SOE) status. These data must be transmitted via RTU at the pooling station over IEC-104 protocol through various communication media (GSM/GPRS, leased line, MPLS-VPN, fiber) with redundant backup communication links. RE developers bear costs for RTU installation, communication infrastructure, hardware/software modifications, database development, and annual rental charges at substations/sub-SLDCs/SLDC for space and auxiliary supply.

**KEY TOPICS:** Real-Time Data, Electrical Parameters, Active Power, Reactive Power, Voltage, Frequency, CB Status, Isolator Status, RTU, IEC-104 Protocol, Redundant Communication, GSM/GPRS, Leased Line, MPLS-VPN, Fiber

**TEXT:**
The SLDC shall require the pooling station wise real time data for all elements of pooling station:

Active Power (MW)*, 
Reactive Power (MVAR)*, 
Voltage (Volt), 
Frequency (Hz), 
Circuit Breaker (CB) and Isolator status 
Sequence of Event (SOE) status etc.

*mandatory to be complied.

The above data require for real-time monitoring of RE pooling station / generator, by providing RTU at its pooling station. The above data require at respective Sub-SLDC(s) / SLDC, over IEC - 104 protocol through GSM / GPRS, Leased line, MPLS - VPN network, fiber or by any mode of communications with redundant facility. (Main and stand by communication link for redundancy).

a) RTU Installation and communication infrastructure at both ends by RE developer. 

b) Charges for hardware / software modification and Data base development and port charges at Substation / Sub-SLDC / SLDC along with supervision charges and annual rental charges for proving space and auxiliary supply to equipment at Substation / Sub-SLDC / SLDC by RE developer.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** IEC-104 Protocol specifications, DLMS Protocol requirements (for ABT meters), Technical standards for RTU communication

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Section 1.2.1(c) (Real-time Data Telemetry Details), Section 1.2.3 (Speech and Data Connectivity Requirements), Annexure 1.1 and 1.2 (Static Data Annexures with RTU specifications)

## Chunk 6: Real-Time Data Telemetry – Wind Turbine Generating Plants

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Notification No.1 2019 Forecasting, Scheduling, DSM & related matters Solar and Wind generation sources regulation 2019. Implementation Procedure

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation Implementation Procedure

**HIERARCHY:** Section 1 / Subsection 1.2.1 / Clause (c)

**PROVISION:** Section 1.2.1(c) – Real-Time Data Telemetry for Wind Plants

**TITLE:** Real-Time Data Telemetry Requirements for Pooling Station and Wind Turbine Generating Plants

**SUMMARY:**
For wind turbine generating plants at pooling stations, real-time data telemetry must include turbine generation (MW and MVAR), voltage at WTG (volt), and generator status (on/off line) indicating availability of wind turbine generators (WTGs). Additionally, an ABT meter with DLMS protocol and automatic meter reading capability must be installed to measure MWH import/export. Control action facilities up to inverter level and bidirectional metering capabilities are required to enable real-time monitoring and operational control of wind generation facilities.

**KEY TOPICS:** Wind Turbine, Telemetry, Turbine Generation, WTG Voltage, Generator Status, Availability, ABT Meter, DLMS Protocol, Automatic Meter Reading, Control Action, MWH Import/Export

**TEXT:**
c) The Real-time Data telemetry required for plants

a. Pooling station and Wind turbine generating plant
i. Turbine Generation (MW & MVAR)
ii. Voltage at WTG (volt)
iii. Generator (WTG) Status (on / off line) – Availability of WTG

b. Pooling station and Solar generating plant
i. Solar Generation unit / Inverter wise (MW & MVAR)
ii. Voltage at interconnection point (volt)
iii. Generator / Inverter Status (on / off line)

d) Additional data:
I. Control action facility up to inverter level
II. ABT meter with DLMS protocol and compatible for automatic meter reading solutions.
III. MWH import / export

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** DLMS (Device Language Message Specification) Protocol standards, ABT Meter specifications (GJ series per Section 3.5.8), Automatic Meter Reading (AMR) solutions

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Section 1.2.2 (Weather Parameters for Wind Plants), Section 3.5.8 (ABT Meter Specifications), Related to technical requirements for grid integration

## Chunk 7: Weather Parameters for Wind Turbine Generating Plants

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Notification No.1 2019 Forecasting, Scheduling, DSM & related matters Solar and Wind generation sources regulation 2019. Implementation Procedure

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation Implementation Procedure

**HIERARCHY:** Section 1 / Subsection 1.2.2 / Clause (e) – Wind Section

**PROVISION:** Section 1.2.2(e) – Weather Parameters for Wind Plants

**TITLE:** Real-Time Weather Parameter Data Requirements for Wind Generation

**SUMMARY:**
Wind turbine generating plants must provide real-time weather parameter data including wind speed (meter/second), wind direction (degree from true north), ambient air temperature (°C), atmospheric/barometric pressure (Pascal), relative humidity (percentage), air density (kg/m³), and rainfall (mm). These weather parameters are essential for accurate generation forecasting, turbine performance monitoring, and correlation analysis between meteorological conditions and power output at wind pooling stations.

**KEY TOPICS:** Weather Parameters, Wind Speed, Wind Direction, Temperature, Atmospheric Pressure, Humidity, Air Density, Rainfall, Wind Generation, Forecasting, Meteorological Data

**TEXT:**
e) The Real-time Data telemetry required for plants 
a. Pooling station and Wind turbine generating plant
i. Wind speed (meter / second)
ii. Wind direction (degree from true north)
iii. Ambient air temperature (º C)
iv. Atmospheric / Barometric pressure (Pascal)
v. Relative humidity (in percentage)
vi. Air Density (Kg/m3)
vii. Rainfall (mm)

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Meteorological measurement standards, Wind energy technical standards

## Chunk 8: Weather Parameters for Solar Generating Plants

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Notification No.1 2019 Forecasting, Scheduling, DSM & related matters Solar and Wind generation sources regulation 2019. Implementation Procedure

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation Implementation Procedure

**HIERARCHY:** Section 1 / Subsection 1.2.2 / Clause (e) – Solar Section

**PROVISION:** Section 1.2.2(e) – Weather Parameters for Solar Plants

**TITLE:** Real-Time Weather Parameter Data Requirements for Solar Generation

**SUMMARY:**
Solar generating plants must collect real-time weather parameter data at the pooling station including global horizontal irradiance (GHI) in watts per meter square, ambient air temperature (°C), diffuse irradiance (watts per meter square), direct irradiance (watts per meter square), relative humidity (percentage), cloud cover (Okta), sunrise/sunset timings (hour:minute format), rainfall (mm), and performance ratio. These solar-specific weather parameters enable accurate power forecasting, performance monitoring against expected capacity, and real-time optimization of solar generation schedules.

**KEY TOPICS:** Solar Weather Parameters, Global Horizontal Irradiance, GHI, Diffuse Irradiance, Direct Irradiance, Cloud Cover, Ambient Temperature, Performance Ratio, Sunrise/Sunset Timings, Solar Forecasting, Irradiance Measurement

**TEXT:**
b. Pooling station and Solar generating plant
i. Global horizontal irradiance (GHI) (watt per meter square)
ii. Ambient air temperature (º C)
iii. Diffuse irradiance (watt per meter square)
iv. Direct irradiance (watt per meter square)
v. Relative humidity (in percentage)
vi. Cloud cover (Okta)
vii. Sun-rise & sunset timings (hour : minute (hh:mm))
viii. Rainfall (mm)
ix. Performance Ratio

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Solar irradiance measurement standards, Cloud cover measurement (Okta scale), Performance ratio calculation methodologies

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Section 1.2.1(c) (Electrical Data for Solar Plants), Section 1.2.2(e) – Wind weather parameters above, Annexure 1.2 (Solar Static Data), Weather sensor specifications in Annexures 1.2 and 1.3

## Chunk 9: Speech and Data Connectivity Requirements

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Notification No.1 2019 Forecasting, Scheduling, DSM & related matters Solar and Wind generation sources regulation 2019. Implementation Procedure

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation Implementation Procedure

**HIERARCHY:** Section 1 / Subsection 1.2.3

**PROVISION:** Section 1.2.3 – Speech and Data Connectivity

**TITLE:** Communication Infrastructure Requirements for SLDC Connectivity

**SUMMARY:**
RE developers and QCAs must establish speech and data connectivity from pooling stations up to sub-SLDC and SLDC with redundant communication links to ensure reliable and continuous connectivity with the State Load Dispatch Centre. This connectivity enables real-time transmission of electrical and weather parameter data, receipt of dispatch instructions, communication of outage information, and coordination of grid operations. Redundancy is mandatory to prevent loss of critical operational data and ensure uninterrupted grid management.

**KEY TOPICS:** Speech Connectivity, Data Connectivity, Sub-SLDC, SLDC, Redundant Communication, Operational Connectivity, Grid Coordination, Reliability, Communication Infrastructure

**TEXT:**
Speech and data connectivity up to sub SLDC / SLDC along with redundant communication.

Further, as per statutory requirement and / or prevailing regulation, additional Name Plate & Site Related Data and Real-time Data shall be furnished by QCA / RE Generator as per SLDC requirement.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Statutory requirements as applicable, Prevailing SLDC regulations, Communication infrastructure standards
