DOCUMENT TYPE: GERC Regulation
HIERARCHY: Regulation 9 - Eligibility Criteria
PROVISION: Regulation 9(I) through 9(III)
TITLE: Eligibility Criteria for Green Energy Open Access - General Consumers, Time-Block Stability, and System-Dependent Requirements

SUMMARY:
Regulation 9 establishes eligibility criteria for Green Energy Open Access consumers. General consumers require minimum 100 kW contracted demand or sanctioned load (single or aggregated connections within same electricity division), with no limitation for captive consumers. Consumers must commit to stable quantum of power for minimum time-blocks (not exceeding 12) to avoid disruptive demand variations. System eligibility is conditional on system availability, metering infrastructure, proper certification, Remote Terminal Unit (RTU) installation, and absence of existing PPAs for the same capacity.

**Regulation 9: Eligibility Criteria for applying Green Energy Open Access**

(I) The Consumer(s) who have contracted demand or sanctioned load of Hundred (100) KW or more, either through single connection or through multiple connections aggregating Hundred (100) kW or more located in same electricity division of a distribution licensee, shall be eligible for Open Access for sourcing Green Energy (RE) under these Regulations.

Provided that in case of captive consumers, there shall not be any load limitation in terms of contract demand/sanctioned load (kW/MW, KVA/MVA) with Discoms.

(II) The minimum number of time blocks, which shall not be more than 12 time-blocks, for which the consumer shall not change the quantum of power consumed through Green Energy Open Access so as to avoid high variations in demand to be met by the distribution licensee.

(III) Subject to the provisions of these Regulations and system availability, consumers, licensees and/or generating company shall be eligible for Green Energy Open Access to the Intra-State Transmission System of the State Transmission Utility or any Transmission Licensee(s) and/or Distribution System(s) of the Distribution Licensee within the State on payment of transmission, wheeling charges and other charges as may be determined by the Commission. The open access customer shall have to install ABT Compliant meters/Special Energy Meter at their place for energy accounting and Remote Terminal Unit (RTU) to facilitate SLDC in real time monitoring, which shall be duly certified by the licensee concerned.

Provided that Green Energy Open Access consumer seeking open access below 1 MW, shall be allowed based on installation of Special Energy Meter capable of energy recording on 15 minutes time block basis.

Provided that notwithstanding anything contained in these Regulations, any Green Energy (RE) Generating Company having subsisting Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) with the Distribution Licensee, shall not be entitled to Green Energy Open Access for the Green Energy (RE) capacity, for which PPA is entered into as well as for any capacity (quantum of power) for which Open Access is already granted, except in accordance with the terms of such PPA.

Provided further that, such Green Energy Open Access shall be available on payment of such charges as may be determined by the Commission from time to time.

CAPACITY ELIGIBILITY THRESHOLD:

**Non-Captive Consumers:**
- Single Connection: Minimum 100 kW contracted demand or sanctioned load
- Multiple Connections: Aggregate 100 kW or more, must be in SAME electricity division
- Geographic Requirement: All connections in same electricity division of a distribution licensee
- Implication: Multi-division consumers must apply separately for each division

**Captive Consumers:**
- NO CAPACITY LIMIT: Exception to 100 kW threshold
- Can be any capacity: Even < 100 kW can establish RE generation for own use
- Advantage: Small industries/commercial can opt for captive RE
- Requirements: Must meet captive status verification criteria per Schedule I

SIGNIFICANCE OF "SAME ELECTRICITY DIVISION":
This geographic constraint ensures:
- Single metering and accounting authority per application
- Unified billing and settlement within division
- Simplified load management by ALDC/SLDC
- No split authority over consumer's open access
- Multi-division operations require separate applications per division

TIME-BLOCK STABILITY REQUIREMENT (9(II)):

**Purpose:**
Prevents disruptive demand fluctuations that could destabilize the distribution licensee's demand forecasting and reserve margin calculations.

**Specification:**
- Minimum time-blocks: NOT MORE THAN 12 (Regulation 9(II))
- Each time-block: 15 minutes (per Regulation 17.6(iii) and 17.6(iv))
- Total minimum commitment: 12 × 15 minutes = 3 hours per day minimum stable consumption
- Flexibility: Consumer can choose any 12 time-blocks within 24-hour period
- Change restriction: Cannot change quantum during these 12 blocks

**Application:**
Prevents scenarios where consumer fluctuates demand every 15 minutes, which would:
- Make renewable forecasting impossible
- Require excessive standby/backup arrangements
- Create operational instability in distribution system
- Trigger frequent deviation settlement charges

SYSTEM AVAILABILITY AND METERING REQUIREMENTS (9(III)):

**System Availability:**
Eligibility is subject to "system availability" per Regulation 6:
- Long-Term: Must comply with transmission planning criteria
- Medium/Short-Term: Must fit within available margins or spare capacity

**Metering Infrastructure:**
Consumers must install at their expense:

For consumers ≥ 1 MW:
- ABT Compliant meter (4-quadrant) at generator end
- ABT Compliant meter at consumption point
- Both must conform to CEA (Installation and Operation of Meters) Regulations, 2006

For consumers < 1 MW (allowed below 1 MW):
- Special Energy Meter capable of recording energy on 15-minute time-block basis
- CEA-specified Special Energy Meter
- Must record consumption every 15 minutes for energy accounting

**Remote Terminal Unit (RTU):**
- Required for SLDC real-time monitoring
- Must be installed and certified by concerned licensee
- Enables real-time communication of consumption/generation data
- Mandatory for all GEOA consumers

**No-Dues Certificate:**
- Applicant must obtain NDC from distribution licensee
- Condition for application acceptance per Regulation 13
- Covers all outstanding dues to licensee for main supply

**Existing PPA Restriction:**
If Green Energy Generator has existing PPA with Distribution Licensee, they CANNOT:
- Seek GEOA for capacity covered under existing PPA
- Seek GEOA for capacity already granted under another GEOA
- Except: If specifically allowed under terms of that PPA
- Implication: No double-dipping of same capacity under different mechanisms

PAYMENT OBLIGATION:
GEOA eligibility is conditional on willingness and ability to pay:
- Transmission charges (per Regulation 17.1)
- Wheeling charges (per Regulation 17.2)
- Cross-subsidy surcharge (per Regulation 17.3)
- Additional surcharge (per Regulation 17.4)
- Banking charges (per Regulation 17.6)
- All other charges determined by Commission from time to time
