**Regulation 3: Definitions (continued)**

(n) "Fossil Fuel" means fuels such as coal, lignite, gas, liquid fuel, or combination of these as its primary source of energy, which are used in Thermal Generating Station for generating electricity.

(q) "Installation" means any composite portable or stationary electrical unit including electric wires, fittings, motors, and apparatus erected and wired by or on behalf of the consumer at the consumer's premises starting from the point of supply and includes apparatus that is available on his premises for being connected or envisaged to be connected to the installation but is for the time being not connected.

(r) "Month" means a calendar month as per the Gregorian calendar.

(s) "Obligated Entity" means the entities/person mandated under Clause (e) of sub-Section (1) of Section 86 of the Act to fulfill the Renewable Purchase Obligation, which includes distribution licensee, captive consumer / user, and open access consumer.

(t) "Open Access" means the non-discriminatory provisions for use of transmission lines and/or distribution system or associated facilities with such lines or system by any licensees or consumers or a person engaged in generation in accordance with the Regulations specified by the Appropriate Commission.

(u) "Person" shall include any company or body corporate or association or body of individuals, whether incorporated or not or artificial juridical person.

(v) "Premises" include any land, building or structure.

(w) "Rules" means Rules made under the Electricity Act, 2003 including Electricity (Promoting Renewable Energy Through Green Energy Open Access) Rules, 2022 and subsequent amendments.

(x) "State Transmission Utility" means Board or Government company specified as such by the State Government under sub-Section (1) of Section 39 of the Act.

(z) "State Load Dispatch Centre" means State Load Dispatch Centre established under sub-Section (1) of Section 31 of the Act.

(aa) "SERC" means the State Electricity Regulatory Commission.

(bb) "Standby Arrangement" means Standby Arrangement provided by the distribution licensee in case the Green Energy Open Access consumer is unable to procure / schedule power from the generating sources with whom they have the arrangement to avail the supply due to outages of such generators, transmission assets and the like.

(cc) "Standby Charges" means the charges applicable to green energy open access consumers against the standby arrangement provided by the distribution licensee, in case such green energy open access consumer is unable to procure/schedule power from the generating sources with whom they have the agreements to procure power due to outages of generator, transmission systems and the like.

(dd) "Wheeling" means operation whereby the distribution system and associated facilities of transmission licensee or distribution licensee as the case may be, are used by another person for the conveyance of electricity on payment of charges determined under Section 62 of the Act.

DEFINITIONS DETAIL:

**FOSSIL FUEL (3(n)) - EXCLUSION PRINCIPLE**

"Fossil Fuel" includes: Coal, Lignite, Gas, Liquid Fuel (or combination)
Definition scope: Primary energy source for Thermal Generating Stations
Implication: Explicitly excludes fossil fuel-based generation from "Green Energy"

Per Regulation 17.3, Cross-Subsidy Surcharge freeze and exemptions apply ONLY to renewable sources, not fossil fuels. This ensures clear delineation.

**INSTALLATION (3(q)) - METERING INFRASTRUCTURE**

Comprehensive definition includes:
- Electrical unit: Composite, portable or stationary
- Components: Electric wires, fittings, motors, apparatus
- Location: At consumer's premises starting from point of supply
- Inclusion: Apparatus connected or envisaged to be connected
- Exclusivity: Everything from supply point to consumer premises

Regulatory implication: Consumer bears cost of installation including ABT meters and RTU per Regulation 20.

**OBLIGATED ENTITY (3(s)) - RPO RESPONSIBILITY**

Definition source: Section 86(1)(e) of Electricity Act, 2003
Obligated entities include:
1. Distribution Licensee
2. Captive Consumer/User
3. Open Access Consumer

Implication: All three categories must fulfill Renewable Purchase Obligation per Central Government notifications. This ensures non-discriminatory green energy procurement.

**OPEN ACCESS (3(t)) - CORE CONCEPT**

"Non-discriminatory provisions" for use of:
- Transmission lines and/or
- Distribution system or
- Associated facilities

By: Licensees, consumers, or generation persons
Subject to: Regulations by Appropriate Commission

Critical word: "Non-discriminatory" - mandates equal treatment regardless of customer type (DISCOM, private, captive)

**STANDBY ARRANGEMENT (3(bb)) - BACKUP MECHANISM**

Provided by: Distribution Licensee
When triggered: Green Energy Open Access consumer unable to procure/schedule power from contracted generators
Reasons: Generator outages, transmission asset outages, etc.

Regulatory treatment: Not a failure by consumer, but force majeure event. Distribution Licensee must provide standby supply.

**STANDBY CHARGES (3(cc)) - PAYMENT MECHANISM**

Per Regulation 17.5: 10% of energy charges including FPPPA charges for relevant tariff category
Applicability: When consumer actually draws standby power
Payment to: Distribution Licensee
Avoidable: If consumer gives 1-day advance notice of standby needed (per Regulation 17.5 proviso)

Important distinction: DIFFERENT from Banking Charges (Regulation 17.6)
- Banking charges: Rs. 1.50/unit for energy banked (Feb-Sep 2024)
- Standby charges: 10% surcharge for emergency supply

**WHEELING (3(dd)) - TRANSMISSION OPERATION**

Definition source: Section 62 of Electricity Act, 2003
Mechanism: Using distribution/transmission system for conveyance of electricity
Payment: Wheeling charges per Section 62 as determined by Commission
Parties: Can be any person (licensee, trader, generator, consumer)

Application scope: Can wheel:
- Own generation to own captive location
- Purchased generation to own location
- Third-party power for trading

**STATE TRANSMISSION UTILITY (3(x)) - INSTITUTIONAL ENTITY**

Definition: Board or Government company
Designation: By State Government per Section 39(1) of Electricity Act
Role: State-level transmission operator
Functions: Long-term/Medium-term GEOA (per Definition 3(y))

**STATE LOAD DISPATCH CENTRE (3(z)) - OPERATIONAL ENTITY**

Definition: Established per Section 31(1) of Electricity Act
Role: Real-time grid operations and scheduling
Functions: Short-term GEOA grant (per Definition 3(y))
Responsibilities: System study, congestion management, scheduling approval

**SERC (3(aa)) - REGULATORY BODY**

Definition: State Electricity Regulatory Commission
Role: Complementary to GERC in certain inter-state scenarios
Reference: Used where Central Commission has jurisdiction

**PERSON (3(u)) - LEGAL ENTITY SCOPE**

Includes:
- Individual
- Company (incorporated entity)
- Body corporate
- Association
- Body of individuals (partnership, LLP, AoP)
- Incorporated or unincorporated
- Artificial juridical person (e.g., Trust, Society, Government entity)

Implication: Broadest possible legal scope ensures all entity types can participate in GEOA.

**PREMISES (3(v)) - PHYSICAL INFRASTRUCTURE**

Includes: Any land, building, or structure
Scope: Where electricity is supplied
Application: Location of consumer's installation (Definition 3(q))

**MONTH (3(r)) - BILLING PERIOD**

Definition: Calendar month per Gregorian calendar
Application: Billing cycle for energy accounting
Start: 1st of month
End: Last day of month

Critical for: Banking (per Regulation 17.6) - banked energy lapses at month-end
Implication: Monthly settlement, no carryover to next month

**RULES (3(w)) - REGULATORY FRAMEWORK**

Includes: Rules made under Electricity Act, 2003
Specifically references: Electricity (Promoting Renewable Energy Through Green Energy Open Access) Rules, 2022
Scope: All amendments and subsequent versions
