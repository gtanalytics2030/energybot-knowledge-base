TITLE: Verification of Captive Generating Plant Status - Entity Types, Shareholding, Consumption Criteria, and Annual Compliance

SUMMARY:
Schedule I establishes comprehensive verification criteria for Captive Generating Plant (CGP) status across seven entity types (single consumer, partnership, LLP, AoP, company, cooperative society, SPV) and special cases (holding/subsidiary companies). All captive consumers must file annual financial year affidavits to the distribution licensee proving: (1) minimum 26% shareholding with voting rights (equity ownership), and (2) minimum 51% self-consumption of generated energy (energy criterion). Failure to comply results in loss of captive status and recovery of CSS + AS + late payment surcharge. The schedule is the operational backbone for administering captive exemptions from surcharges.

**SCHEDULE I: VERIFICATION OF CAPTIVE GENERATING PLANT**

**BACKGROUND**

A Captive Generating Plant (CGP) is defined in Regulation 3(e) as any power plant established or proposed to be established for generating electricity wholly or substantially for its own use or for the use of any member of the cooperative consumer or an association of persons constituted for the purpose of generating and supplying electricity for members. To qualify for Captive status and claim exemptions from Cross-Subsidy Surcharge (CSS) and Additional Surcharge (AS) per Regulations 17.3 and 17.4, a plant must satisfy both ownership and consumption criteria.

**PART A: VERIFICATION CRITERIA FOR CAPTIVE STATUS**

**I. OWNERSHIP CRITERION - MINIMUM 26% SHAREHOLDING WITH VOTING RIGHTS**

For an entity establishing a Captive Generating Plant, the ownership criterion requires:

(a) **Equity Ownership:** Minimum 26% (twenty-six percent) of the equity capital of the generating company or project developer must be held by:
   - The consuming entity (in case of single consumer captive), or
   - The members of the cooperative consumer or association of persons (in case of cooperative/AoP captive)

(b) **Voting Rights:** Minimum 26% of voting rights (power to vote on board decisions, resolutions, policy matters) must be exercised by the equity holders specified above

(c) **Dual Requirement:** BOTH equity ownership (26%+) AND voting rights (26%+) must be satisfied simultaneously. Satisfaction of only one criterion is insufficient.

(d) **Transfer of Equity:** If equity holding falls below 26% or voting rights reduce due to subsequent share transfers, sale, or restructuring:
   - The entity must report to the distribution licensee within 30 days
   - Captive status is suspended immediately
   - Consumer reverts to normal open access consumer status
   - CSS and AS become applicable retrospectively from the date of transfer

**II. CONSUMPTION CRITERION - MINIMUM 51% SELF-CONSUMPTION**

For an entity operating a Captive Generating Plant, the consumption criterion requires:

(a) **Consumption Percentage:** Minimum 51% (fifty-one percent) of the total electrical energy generated by the plant during the financial year must be:
   - Consumed by the captive consumer at its own premises/facilities, or
   - Consumed by members of the cooperative consumer or association of persons at their respective facilities

(b) **Measurement Basis:** The 51% calculation shall be based on:
   - Total energy generated (in kWh) during the financial year (1 April to 31 March)
   - Total energy consumed by captive consumer/members (in kWh) during the same financial year
   - Formula: (Energy Consumed / Energy Generated) × 100 ≥ 51%

(c) **Surplus/Exported Energy:** Energy beyond the 51% self-consumption that is exported to the grid is permitted (no 100% self-consumption restriction). However:
   - Exported energy is subject to CSS and AS as if it were open access supply
   - Exported energy may be eligible for REC (Renewable Energy Certificate) trading
   - Billing treatment for exported energy differs from self-consumed energy

(d) **Multi-Location Captive:** If a captive consumer has multiple facilities (plants, factories, offices) within the same state:
   - The 51% consumption is calculated on an aggregate basis across all facilities
   - Energy transfer between facilities is treated as internal transfer (no wheeling charges if within same distribution area)
   - Energy transfer between different distribution areas attracts wheeling charges per Regulation 17.2

**PART B: ENTITY-SPECIFIC VERIFICATION REQUIREMENTS**

**SECTION 1: SINGLE CONSUMER CAPTIVE**

A single consumer (individual or business entity) establishing a Captive Generating Plant shall:

(a) **Ownership Verification:**
   - Registration document (property deed, business registration, incorporation certificate) showing the consumer as owner
   - Certificate of Share Ownership (if the CGP is structured as a separate company, minimum 26% ownership shares)
   - Board resolution or Memorandum of Association (MoA) authorizing generation and consumption

(b) **Consumption Verification - Annual Affidavit:**
   - **Affidavit Content:** Sworn statement by authorized signatory (owner, CEO, CFO) certifying:
     - Total electrical energy generated in FY (in MWh with decimal precision)
     - Total electrical energy consumed in FY (in MWh with decimal precision)
     - Consumption percentage calculation: [(Generated - Exported) / Generated] × 100
     - List of consumption locations (plant, factory, office, etc. with address and capacity)
     - Confirmation that consumption criterion (≥51%) is satisfied
     - Declaration that no material change in equity ownership has occurred since previous affidavit
   
   - **Affidavit Timing:** Submitted to distribution licensee within 90 days after end of financial year (by 30 June of following year)
   
   - **Supporting Documents:**
     - Audited financial statements showing energy costs, consumption details
     - Meter data/energy readings from consumer's load point meter (if <1 MW) or RTU data (if ≥1 MW)
     - List of all energy exported to grid with quantum and dates
     - REC sales/trading records (if applicable)

**SECTION 2: PARTNERSHIP CAPTIVE**

A partnership firm (2 or more partners) establishing a Captive Generating Plant shall:

(a) **Ownership Verification:**
   - Partnership Deed showing the firm's constitution
   - Certificate of Registration (Form A & B for Registered Firms)
   - List of all partners with their percentage share in the partnership
   - Confirmation that partners collectively hold ≥26% equity in CGP company (if CGP is separate entity)
   - Board resolution by partners authorizing establishment and operation of CGP

(b) **Consumption Verification - Annual Affidavit:**
   - **Affidavit Signatory:** Authorized partner (as per Partnership Deed) or Partner-designated CFO
   - **Affidavit Content:** Same as Section 1 (single consumer), plus:
     - Schedule of all partner consumption locations and percentage consumption by each partner
     - Confirmation that consumption is in furtherance of partnership business
     - Declaration that partnership equity holding in CGP remains ≥26%
   
   - **Affidavit Timing:** Within 90 days after FY-end (by 30 June)
   
   - **Supporting Documents:**
     - Partnership Deed certified copy
     - Audited Profit & Loss statement showing energy costs
     - Partner-wise consumption schedule with metering evidence

**SECTION 3: LIMITED LIABILITY PARTNERSHIP (LLP) CAPTIVE**

An LLP (designated partners, member partners) establishing a Captive Generating Plant shall:

(a) **Ownership Verification:**
   - LLP Agreement
   - Certificate of Incorporation issued by Ministry of Corporate Affairs
   - Schedule of members and their capital contribution percentages
   - Confirmation that LLP (through its members) holds ≥26% equity in CGP
   - Designated Partner authorized by LLP Agreement to submit affidavits

(b) **Consumption Verification - Annual Affidavit:**
   - **Affidavit Signatory:** Any Designated Partner (as per LLP Agreement) or Member-nominated officer
   - **Affidavit Content:** 
     - Total energy generated and consumed with percentage calculation
     - Member-wise consumption schedule (if applicable)
     - Confirmation of ≥26% equity holding in CGP by the LLP
     - Declaration of no change in member composition affecting equity ownership
   
   - **Affidavit Timing:** Within 90 days of FY-end
   
   - **Supporting Documents:**
     - Certified copy of LLP Agreement
     - Audited LLP financial statements
     - Member capital account statements showing equity ownership

**SECTION 4: ASSOCIATION OF PERSONS (AoP) CAPTIVE**

An AoP (group of individuals/entities with common purpose) establishing a Captive Generating Plant shall:

(a) **Ownership Verification:**
   - AoP Agreement (written contract establishing the association)
   - List of all members with equity contribution percentage by each member
   - Confirmation that members collectively hold ≥26% equity in CGP
   - Designation of Managing Member authorized to represent AoP

(b) **Consumption Verification - Annual Affidavit:**
   - **Affidavit Signatory:** Managing Member or designated officer authorized by written resolution
   - **Affidavit Content:**
     - Total energy generated in FY
     - Total energy consumed in FY by all members aggregated
     - Member-wise consumption schedule with locations
     - Percentage consumption verification: [Sum of all members' consumption / Total generation] × 100 ≥ 51%
     - Confirmation that no member's equity stake in CGP has fallen below their original contribution percentage
   
   - **Affidavit Timing:** Within 90 days of FY-end
   
   - **Supporting Documents:**
     - AoP Agreement certified copy
     - Bank statements showing energy cost reimbursement to members
     - Member-wise meter readings or consumption data

**SECTION 5: COMPANY CAPTIVE**

A registered Company (Private Ltd., Public Ltd., etc.) establishing a Captive Generating Plant shall:

(a) **Ownership Verification:**
   - Certificate of Incorporation
   - Memorandum & Articles of Association
   - List of shareholders with shareholding percentage (from latest Register of Members or Annual Report)
   - Confirmation that the Company holds ≥26% equity in CGP (or CGP is wholly-owned subsidiary)
   - Board resolution authorizing establishment and operation of CGP

(b) **Consumption Verification - Annual Affidavit:**
   - **Affidavit Signatory:** Company Secretary, CFO, or Director authorized by Board resolution
   - **Affidavit Content:**
     - Total energy generated in FY (per annual report / auditor certificate)
     - Total energy consumed in FY (per utility bills / metering records)
     - Percentage consumption with detailed calculation
     - Identification of all consumption locations (factory sites, offices, etc.)
     - Board resolution authorizing filing of the affidavit
     - Confirmation that equity ownership in CGP remains ≥26%
   
   - **Affidavit Timing:** Within 90 days of FY-end
   
   - **Supporting Documents:**
     - Board resolution (certified copy from company minutes)
     - Audited Annual Report
     - Energy bill copies (electricity utility bills for all consumption locations)
     - Shareholder register extract showing Company's holding in CGP

**SECTION 6: COOPERATIVE CONSUMER / COOPERATIVE SOCIETY CAPTIVE**

A Cooperative Consumer (group of consumers pooling resources) or registered Cooperative Society establishing a Captive Generating Plant shall:

(a) **Ownership Verification:**
   - Cooperative Memorandum & Rules
   - Certificate of Registration from Registrar of Cooperatives
   - List of all members with equity share percentage
   - Confirmation that members collectively hold ≥26% equity in CGP
   - Board resolution by Cooperative Board authorizing CGP establishment

(b) **Consumption Verification - Annual Affidavit:**
   - **Affidavit Signatory:** Cooperative Secretary, Treasurer, or Board-authorized officer
   - **Affidavit Content:**
     - Total energy generated in FY
     - Total energy consumed by all members in FY (aggregated)
     - Member-wise consumption schedule (name, location, consumption in MWh)
     - Percentage consumption verification: [Total members' consumption / Total generation] × 100 ≥ 51%
     - Confirmation that equity ownership by members has not materially changed
     - Board resolution approving the affidavit
   
   - **Affidavit Timing:** Within 90 days of FY-end
   
   - **Supporting Documents:**
     - Cooperative membership list (certified by Cooperative Secretary)
     - Audited Cooperative financial statements
     - Member consumption records (electricity bills, meter readings)
     - Board resolution minutes

**SECTION 7: SPECIAL PURPOSE VEHICLE (SPV) CAPTIVE**

A Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) established specifically to own and operate a Captive Generating Plant for a single principal or multiple principals shall:

(a) **Ownership Verification:**
   - Certificate of Incorporation
   - Memorandum & Articles of Association
   - Shareholder register showing principal(s) holding ≥26% equity with voting control
   - Board resolution designating principal as beneficial owner / consumer
   - Definition of principal(s): Individual, company, partnership, AoP, or cooperative

(b) **Consumption Verification - Annual Affidavit:**
   - **Affidavit Signatory:** SPV Director, CFO, or Company Secretary
   - **Affidavit Content:**
     - Total energy generated in FY
     - Total energy consumed by principal(s) at their premises in FY
     - Percentage consumption with detailed schedule of principal's facilities
     - Confirmation that energy is consumed by the principal and not traded to third parties
     - Declaration that SPV equity ownership by principal remains ≥26%
     - Board resolution approving filing of affidavit
   
   - **Affidavit Timing:** Within 90 days of FY-end
   
   - **Supporting Documents:**
     - Board resolution
     - Principal's energy bills and meter readings
     - Contract/MoA showing principal's ownership rights in SPV

**PART C: SPECIAL CASES - HOLDING & SUBSIDIARY COMPANIES**

**VIII. HOLDING COMPANY / SUBSIDIARY COMPANY CAPTIVE**

When a Captive Generating Plant is owned by a Holding Company but energy is consumed by one or more Subsidiary Companies:

(a) **Ownership Verification:**
   - Holding Company shareholding certificate in CGP (≥26% equity + voting rights)
   - List of all subsidiary companies
   - Board resolution from Holding Company authorizing energy supply to subsidiaries
   - Subsidiary shareholding in Holding Company (if majority/material stake)

(b) **Consumption Verification - Combined Affidavit:**
   - **Affidavit Signatories:** Holding Company CFO + each Subsidiary's authorized officer
   - **Content Requirements:**
     - Holding Company's total energy generated in FY
     - Subsidiary-wise consumption with location and quantum (in MWh)
     - Aggregated consumption percentage: [Σ Subsidiaries' consumption / Total generation] × 100 ≥ 51%
     - Confirmation that intra-group energy transfer is internal (not open access)
     - Board resolutions from both Holding and Subsidiary companies
     - Transfer Pricing documentation (if applicable) showing arm's-length energy pricing
   
   - **Affidavit Timing:** Within 90 days of FY-end (single affidavit covering all entities)
   
   - **Supporting Documents:**
     - Holding Company Board resolution
     - Subsidiaries' Board resolutions
     - Inter-company energy supply agreement (if any)
     - Each subsidiary's energy bills and consumption records
     - Transfer pricing study (if energy priced differently from open market)

**PART D: ANNUAL COMPLIANCE AND AFFIDAVIT PROCEDURE**

**IX. ANNUAL AFFIDAVIT FILING PROCESS**

(a) **Timing:**
   - **Due Date:** Within 90 days after end of financial year
   - **Financial Year in India:** 1 April to 31 March
   - **Affidavit Due:** By 30 June of following year (e.g., FY 2023-24 affidavit due by 30 June 2024)
   - **Late Filing:** If affidavit is not filed by due date, captive status is suspended, and CSS + AS become applicable

(b) **Affidavit Format:**
   - **Format:** Sworn statement in prescribed format (available from distribution licensee / State Nodal Agency website)
   - **Oath:** Affidavit must be sworn before a Notary Public or Magistrate (Class I or II)
   - **Signatory:** Authorized signatory (as per entity type specified above) with official title and designation
   - **Declarations:** Include declaration that statement is true to the best of knowledge and belief; consequences of making false statement under IPC

(c) **Submission Process:**
   - **Recipient:** Distribution Licensee in whose supply area the consumption occurs
   - **Method:** Physical submission (with seal and signature) or electronic submission (with digital signature as per IT Act, 2000)
   - **Acknowledgment:** Distribution Licensee shall issue dated receipt/acknowledgment within 5 working days
   - **Record Maintenance:** Distribution Licensee maintains affidavit copy in consumer record file

(d) **Supporting Documentation Verification:**
   - **DL Verification Timeline:** Within 30 days of affidavit receipt, distribution licensee shall verify:
     - Shareholding/equity records (check with business registry, Ministry of Corporate Affairs website, registrar)
     - Energy consumption records (verify metering data, cross-check with submitted documents)
     - Any material changes in ownership since prior affidavit
   
   - **DL Communication:** If verification raises queries, DL shall seek clarification from consumer within 15 days
   - **Approval/Rejection:** DL shall confirm captive status continuing or issue notice of status suspension within 45 days of affidavit receipt

**PART E: CONSEQUENCES OF NON-COMPLIANCE**

**X. LOSS OF CAPTIVE STATUS AND RECOVERY PROCEDURE**

(a) **Grounds for Loss of Captive Status:**
   1. Failure to file annual affidavit by due date (30 June)
   2. Affidavit filed but shareholding criterion (26% equity + voting) not satisfied
   3. Affidavit filed but consumption criterion (51% self-consumption) not satisfied
   4. Material misrepresentation in affidavit (later discovered during verification)
   5. Equity ownership falling below 26% due to share transfer/sale
   6. Equity ownership or voting rights not exercised by captive consumer for >12 months

(b) **Automatic Status Suspension:**
   - Upon failure to file affidavit by 30 June: Captive status is automatically suspended (no notice required)
   - Consumer reverts to normal open access consumer classification
   - CSS and AS become immediately applicable from 1 July
   - If affidavit is filed later (even after suspension), backdating is not permitted; status is restored only prospectively from date of belated filing

(c) **Recovery of CSS, AS, and Late Payment Surcharge:**
   - **Period of Recovery:** All months from loss of captive status until affidavit is filed and status restored (or if never filed, entire remaining contract period)
   - **Charges to be Recovered:**
     1. Cross-Subsidy Surcharge (CSS) per Regulation 17.3 (12-month freeze rate, if applicable)
     2. Additional Surcharge (AS) per Regulation 17.4 (linear reduction schedule)
     3. Late Payment Surcharge: 1.5% per month (18% per annum) on outstanding dues
   
   - **Recovery Calculation:**
     - Monthly CSS = [CSS Rate per Tariff Order] × [Total monthly energy consumption]
     - Monthly AS = [AS Rate per GEOA grant date] × [Linear reduction year factor] × [Capacity]
     - Late Surcharge = Monthly due × 1.5% per month of delay
   
   - **Example:** Consumer with 500 kW captive did not file affidavit for FY 2023-24:
     - CSS Rate for that DISCOM: Rs. 1.50/kWh (hypothetical)
     - Monthly consumption: 50 MWh
     - Monthly CSS due = Rs. 1.50 × 50,000 = Rs. 75,000
     - If affidavit filed in September (3 months late), total CSS due = Rs. 75,000 × 3 = Rs. 2,25,000
     - Plus late surcharge = Rs. 2,25,000 × 1.5% × 3 months = Rs. 10,125

(d) **Billing and Collection Procedure:**
   - **Demand Notice:** Distribution licensee issues formal demand notice specifying:
     - CSS + AS + Late Surcharge with detailed calculation
     - Period of non-compliance
     - Amount payable within 30 days
     - Consequences of non-payment (legal action, supply disconnection under Distribution Code)
   
   - **Payment Default:** If payment is not made within 30 days of demand notice:
     - Legal proceedings may be initiated (recovery suit)
     - Supply may be disconnected per Distribution Code procedures
     - Consumer record is marked as defaulter

(e) **Appeal and Relief Provisions:**
   - **Appeal to Commission:** Consumer can appeal to GERC against loss of captive status within 30 days of notice, on grounds of:
     - Bonafide error in affidavit (with corrected affidavit submitted)
     - Extraordinary circumstances (force majeure, natural calamity) causing temporary non-compliance
     - Procedural irregularity in DL's verification process
   
   - **Commission Review:** Commission shall review within 60 days and may:
     - Reinstate captive status (if consumer's appeal is justified)
     - Reduce recovery charges (if circumstances warrant)
     - Grant extended timeline for filing affidavit (in special cases)
