TITLE: Definition of Captive Generating Plant (CGP)

SUMMARY:
Captive Generating Plant (CGP) is a fundamental concept in these Regulations, enabling self-consumption of renewable energy. A CGP is established by any person to generate electricity primarily for their own use or by cooperative societies/associations for member use. This definition is critical for understanding exemptions from cross-subsidy surcharge and additional surcharge, and for determining captive consumer status requirements.

TEXT:

**Regulation 3(e): Captive Generating Plant (CGP)**

"Captive Generating Plant" (CGP) means a power plant set up by any person to generate electricity primarily for his own use and includes a power plant set up by any co-operative society or association of persons for generating electricity primarily for use of members of such co-operative society or association.

CRITICAL NOTES:
The definition explicitly includes cooperative societies and associations of persons, extending beyond individual ownership. This broadens the scope of eligible captive generators and enables collective captive consumption models. The "primarily for his own use" requirement is restrictive and verified annually as per Schedule I.
