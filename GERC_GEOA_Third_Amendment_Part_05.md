SUMMARY: The GERC Green Energy Open Access Third Amendment Regulations, 2025 (Notification No. 08 of 2025, published September 25, 2025) contains three substantive sections. Section 1 establishes the amendment's short title and commencement date (September 25, 2025, applicable statewide). Sections 2 and 3 together implement a new banking charge framework: Rs. 1.50 per unit effective from September 25, 2025 through March 31, 2026. Section 2 amends Regulation 1(4) to set the temporal validity period for banking charges. Section 3 amends Regulation 17.6(viii) to specify the rate (Rs. 1.50/unit) and establish automatic succession to revised charges after the expiry date. This creates a 6-month charge regime with built-in review/revision mechanism. The amendment signals GERC's intent to review banking charges by March 31, 2026 and establish transparent regulatory succession procedures.

### Policy Signal: Renewable Energy Support

1. **Favorable Charge Structure:** Rs. 1.50/unit is moderate in Indian context, encouraging banking facility use
2. **Seasonal Smoothing:** Supports renewable energy generators managing solar/wind seasonal variation
3. **Open Access Incentive:** Keeps banking charges low to promote distributed renewable energy adoption
4. **Comparison:** CERC wheeling charges typically 0.5-2.5% of energy; Rs. 1.50/unit ≈ 18-25% of average tariff (moderate impact)

### Regulatory Review Signal: 6-Month Window

1. **Implicit Review Timeline:** March 31, 2026 expiry suggests GERC conducting banking charge adequacy review
2. **Stakeholder Feedback:** 6-month period allows consumers/generators to provide input on revised rates
3. **Cost Recovery Assessment:** GERC likely evaluating whether Rs. 1.50/unit covers actual SLDC banking operations costs
4. **Potential Revision Direction:** Next rate could be higher (if cost recovery inadequate) or maintained/lower (if policy emphasizes renewable support)

### Procedural Innovation: Formal Amendment Requirement

**Key Provision:** "separate notification of Regulation" required for revision
- **Significance:** Banking charges cannot be changed via circular or office memorandum
- **Transparency:** Formal regulation ensures stakeholder notification and media coverage
- **Legal Weight:** Regulatory amendment has higher legal standing than informal guidance
- **Precedent:** Sets pattern for future charge revisions requiring formal amendment process

## POTENTIAL QUERIES ANSWERED BY THIS AMENDMENT

### Query 1: What is the current banking charge for green energy open access in Gujarat?
**Answer:** Rs. 1.50 per unit (MWh), effective September 25, 2025, as per Regulation 17.6(viii), amended by GERC Notification No. 08 of 2025.

### Query 2: For how long will this banking charge remain applicable?
**Answer:** The banking charge is valid from September 25, 2025 until March 31, 2026, unless GERC notifies revised charges earlier through a separate regulation (Regulation 1(4), as amended).

### Query 3: How is the banking charge calculated?
**Answer:** Multiply the volume of energy banked (in MWh) by the rate of Rs. 1.50/unit. Example: 100 MWh × Rs. 1.50 = Rs. 150.

### Query 4: What happens after March 31, 2026?
**Answer:** The current Rs. 1.50/unit rate expires on March 31, 2026. From April 1, 2026, revised banking charges specified in a separate GERC regulation become applicable. If GERC issues revised charges before March 31, 2026, those apply from the publication date.

### Query 5: Can GERC change the banking charge before March 31, 2026?
**Answer:** Yes. GERC can issue a separate notification amending Regulation 17.6(viii) with revised charges at any time before March 31, 2026. The revised rate would supersede Rs. 1.50/unit from the publication date of the new regulation.

### Query 6: What authority allows GERC to make this amendment?
**Answer:** Sections 39, 40, 42, 61, and 86 of the Electricity Act, 2003 (read with Section 181), which confer regulatory power on GERC regarding open access tariffs and charges.

### Query 7: Is this banking charge applicable to all green energy open access consumers in Gujarat?
**Answer:** Yes. The amendment applies to the whole State of Gujarat, affecting all open access consumers and generators using the banking facility under Regulation 17.6 (effective September 25, 2025).

### Query 8: Can open access consumers challenge this banking charge?
**Answer:** Potentially yes, through regulatory petition before GERC if they believe the charge is unreasonable, discriminatory, or violates applicable law. The procedure for such petitions is defined in GERC Regulation 1 (Procedural Framework – not detailed in this amendment).
