# GERC Hybrid Hearing SOP - Part 1: Framework, Access & Device Management

---

## Chunk 1.1

```
DOCUMENT: GERC SOP for Hybrid Hearing

DOCUMENT TYPE: Standard Operating Procedure / Circular

HIERARCHY: Title / Provision Cluster 1 (Clauses 1-3)

PROVISION: Clauses 1-3

TITLE: Hybrid Hearing Mode and Microsoft Teams Platform Framework

SUMMARY:
The GERC Hybrid Hearing SOP establishes a flexible hearing format wherein parties may choose to appear either in person or through virtual mode. All Virtual/Hybrid Hearings shall be conducted exclusively through the Microsoft Teams platform. GERC shall provide the Microsoft Teams meeting link to all concerned participants (petitioners, respondents, advocates, counsel) prior to the scheduled date of the hearing, ensuring accessibility for both in-person and virtual participants.

KEY TOPICS: Hybrid Hearing, Virtual Hearing, Microsoft Teams, Hearing Mode, Flexible Participation, GERC Procedure

TEXT:
1. In Hybrid Mode of hearing, parties may choose to appear either in person or through virtual mode.

2. The Virtual/Hybrid Hearing shall be conducted through the Microsoft Teams platform.

3. GERC shall provide the Microsoft Teams link to all concerned participants prior to the scheduled date of the Virtual/Hybrid Hearing.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: None (introductory procedural framework)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Related to participant identification requirements (Clause 4), technical requirements for virtual participants (Clauses 6-8), mixed-mode coordination (Clause 9)
```

## Chunk 1.2

```
DOCUMENT: GERC SOP for Hybrid Hearing

DOCUMENT TYPE: Standard Operating Procedure / Circular

HIERARCHY: Title / Provision Cluster 2 (Clause 4)

PROVISION: Clause 4

TITLE: Display Names and Participant Identification for Hybrid Hearings

SUMMARY:
All petitioners, respondents, advocates, and counsel joining the Microsoft Teams hearing session must set their display names clearly and properly. Display names must reflect the exact and full name of the individual, which shall be taken on record by the Commission. Participants must communicate their details to GERC staff in advance to enable proper identification and admission in the concerned matter.

KEY TOPICS: Display Name, Participant Identification, Advocates, Counsel, GERC Record, Proper Identification, Admission Requirements

TEXT:
4. Display names of all Petitioner(s), Respondent(s), Advocates, and Counsel joining the Microsoft Teams hearing shall be clearly and properly set by the respective participants. The display name shall reflect the exact and full name of the individual, which shall be taken on record of the Commission. Further, details of the participants shall be communicated to GERC staff in advance to enable proper identification and admission in the concerned matter.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: None (procedural requirement)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Related to hybrid hearing framework (Clauses 1-3), conduct and decorum standards (Clause 15)
```

## Chunk 1.3

DOCUMENT TYPE: Standard Operating Procedure / Circular

HIERARCHY: Title / Provision Cluster 3 (Clause 5)

PROVISION: Clause 5

TITLE: Mobile Phone Protocol During Proceedings

SUMMARY:
All participants, whether attending in person or virtually, must switch off their mobile phones or keep them on silent/airplane mode during the entirety of the proceedings. This requirement applies uniformly to all participants and is mandatory throughout the hearing session.

KEY TOPICS: Mobile Phone, Silent Mode, Airplane Mode, Hearing Conduct, Participant Discipline, Device Management

TEXT:
5. Mobile phones of all participants shall be switched off or kept on silent/airplane mode during the proceedings.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: None (operational protocol)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Related to conduct and decorum requirements (Clause 15), audio clarity requirements (Clause 7)
