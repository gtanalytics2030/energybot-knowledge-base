# GERC Hybrid Hearing SOP - Part 3: Document Management, Participation Protocol & Technical Continuity

---

## Chunk 3.1

```
DOCUMENT: GERC SOP for Hybrid Hearing

DOCUMENT TYPE: Standard Operating Procedure / Circular

HIERARCHY: Title / Provision Cluster 7 (Clause 10)

PROVISION: Clause 10

TITLE: Advance Document Submission for Hybrid Hearings

SUMMARY:
All documents intended to be relied upon during the hearing must be submitted in advance to ensure their availability to both physical and virtual participants during the proceedings. This requirement ensures that all participants, regardless of their mode of attendance, have equal access to documentary evidence and prevents delays or confusion arising from missing or late-submitted documents.

KEY TOPICS: Document Submission, Advance Submission, Hybrid Hearing Documents, Physical Participants, Virtual Participants, Equal Access, Documentation Requirements

TEXT:
10. All documents shall be submitted in advance to ensure their availability to both physical and virtual participants during the hearing.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: None (operational requirement)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Related to hybrid hearing framework (Clauses 1-3), participant identification and access (Clause 4), fairness and equal treatment principles
```

## Chunk 3.2

DOCUMENT: GERC SOP for Hybrid Hearing

DOCUMENT TYPE: Standard Operating Procedure / Circular

HIERARCHY: Title / Provision Cluster 8 (Clause 11)

PROVISION: Clause 11

TITLE: Raise Hand Feature for Seeking Permission to Make Submissions

SUMMARY:
All participants, whether attending in person or virtually, shall use the "Raise Hand" feature in Microsoft Teams to seek permission before making submissions during the hearing. This procedural requirement ensures orderly conduct of the proceedings, allows the presiding officer to manage speaking order, and prevents overlapping or disruptive speech from either physical or virtual participants.

KEY TOPICS: Raise Hand Feature, Microsoft Teams, Speaking Permission, Procedural Order, Submission Protocol, Orderly Conduct, Speaking Rights

TEXT:
11. Participants shall use the "Raise Hand" feature to seek permission before making submissions.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Clause 9 (Avoiding simultaneous speaking), Clause 5 (Device management during proceedings)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Related to conduct and decorum standards (Clause 15), camera and microphone management (Clause 8), mixed-mode coordination (Clause 9)

## Chunk 3.3

DOCUMENT TYPE: Standard Operating Procedure / Circular

HIERARCHY: Title / Provision Cluster 9 (Clause 12)

PROVISION: Clause 12

TITLE: Disconnection and Reconnection Protocol During Proceedings

SUMMARY:
In the event that a virtual participant experiences disconnection from the Microsoft Teams meeting, they shall rejoin immediately using the same meeting link provided by GERC. If immediate re-connection is not possible, the participant shall inform the Commission through email at gerc@gercin.org to notify the presiding officer of the technical issue and their efforts to reconnect. This provision ensures continuity of the proceedings and maintains the hearing record.

KEY TOPICS: Disconnection, Reconnection, Virtual Participant, Microsoft Teams, Technical Issue, Re-joining, Email Notification, GERC Contact

TEXT:
12. In case of disconnection, participants shall rejoin immediately using the same meeting link or inform the Commission through email at gerc@gercin.org.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Clause 2 (Microsoft Teams platform), Clause 14 (Technical disruptions fallback provision)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Related to technical readiness (Clause 6), technical disruptions and fallback procedures (Clause 14), participant identification (Clause 4)
