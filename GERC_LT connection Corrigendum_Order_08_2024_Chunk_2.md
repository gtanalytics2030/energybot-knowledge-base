TITLE: Rated Voltage of AC Supply – Classification by Connected Load and Contracted Demand (GERC Supply Code Amendment Notification No. 14 of 2024)
SUMMARY: The GERC notified the (Electricity Supply Code and Related Matters) (Fourth Amendment) Regulations, 2024 (Notification No. 14 of 2024), which amends Clause 3.2 of the Principal Regulations to establish a comprehensive classification system for AC supply voltage categories based on connected load and contracted demand thresholds. The classification establishes four voltage supply categories: 230V Single Phase (up to 6 kW connected load with limited motive power), 400V Three Phase (6 kW to 100 or 150 kVA/kW contracted demand), 11 kV and 22 kV Three Phase (exceeding 100/150 kVA/kW up to 4000 kVA/kW, with extended limits to 8000 kVA/kW for existing 22 kV consumers under system conversion scheme), and 33 kV and above Three Phase (exceeding 4000 kVA/kW). The amendment introduces flexibility by allowing consumers with 6–100 kVA/kW to opt for up to 150 kVA/kW on request.

**Paragraph 4. GERC Supply Code Amendment**

Subsequently, the Commission has notified the GERC (Electricity Supply Code and Related Matters) (Fourth Amendment) Regulations, 2024 as Notification No. 14 of 2024, with following amendment;

Clause 3.2 of the Principal Regulations shall be substituted as under:

**"3.2 The rated voltage of the AC supply should be as follows:**

| **Category** | **System of Supply** |
|---|---|
| For all installations up to and inclusive of 6 kW of Connected Load, subject to motive power load other than irrigation pump not exceeding 2 HP in the aggregate. | 230 V- Single Phase |
| For all installations exceeding 6 kW of Connected Load and up to 100 kVA/kW of Contracted Demand / up to 150 kVA/kW of Contracted Demand if opted by the applicant / consumer. | 400 V - Three Phase |
| For all installation with Contract Demand exceeding 100 / 150 kVA/kW and up to 4000 kVA/kW. However, for the existing 22 kV consumer, the Contact Demand limit shall be extended up to 8000 kVA/kW subject to undertaking from consumer for reverting back to 4000 kVA/kW limit in case of change of system to 11 kV under system conversion scheme. | 11 kV and 22 kV - Three Phase |
| All installation with Contract Demand exceeding 4000 kVA/kW. | At 33 kV and above- Three Phase |

CRITICAL_NOTES:
1. Motive Power Exception: For Connected Load up to 6 kW, single-phase supply is applicable only if motive power load (excluding irrigation pump) does not exceed 2 HP in aggregate. This exemption excludes irrigation pumps from the motive power restriction.

2. Optional Uprating to 150 kVA/kW: Consumers in the 100–150 kVA/kW range may opt for 150 kVA/kW contracted demand on application, expanding their eligibility for 400V three-phase supply beyond the standard 100 kVA/kW threshold.

3. Existing 22 kV Consumer Concession: Existing 22 kV consumers are permitted to extend contracted demand limit from 4000 kVA/kW to 8000 kVA/kW, provided they commit to reverting to 4000 kVA/kW if they convert their system to 11 kV under the system conversion scheme.

4. Voltage Supply Categories are hierarchical: Single Phase (LT) → 400V Three Phase (LT) → 11/22 kV Three Phase (HT) → 33 kV and above (EHT).
