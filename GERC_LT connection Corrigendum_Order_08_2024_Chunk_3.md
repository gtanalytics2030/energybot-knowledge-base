TITLE: Rationale for Corrigendum – Modification of Tariff Schedule FY 2024-25 Due to Supply Code Amendment
SUMMARY: Following the notification of the GERC (Electricity Supply Code and Related Matters) (Fourth Amendment) Regulations, 2024 (Notification No. 14 of 2024), which expanded the LT (Low Tension) consumer classification to include consumers with contracted demand up to 150 kVA/kW (on optional request, in addition to the standard 100 kVA/kW threshold), the Tariff Schedule attached to the original Tariff Orders dated 1st June 2024 requires modification. The modification is necessary to ensure correct tariff applicability to LT consumers under the expanded voltage/load classification. Accordingly, relevant paras of the Tariff Schedule for FY 2024-25 in Case Nos. 2318/2024 through 2328/2024 must be amended to reflect this revised LT Tariff Category definition.
KEY_TOPICS: Tariff Schedule Modification, LT Tariff Category Expansion, Contracted Demand Threshold, Supply Code Amendment Alignment, Tariff Applicability, Consumer Classification, Optional Demand Uprating

**Paragraph 5. Need for Tariff Schedule Modification**

With this afore-said modifications, the Tariff Schedule for FY 2024-25 of the Distribution licensees, which has been issued on Tariff Orders dtd. 1st June, 2024 needs to be modified for applicability of tariff for LT consumers.

**Paragraph 6. Amendment to Tariff Schedule – Corrigendum Direction**

Therefore, relevant Paras of the Tariff Schedule attached to the Tariff Orders dtd. 01.06.2024 in the matter of Truing up of FY 2022-23 and Determination of Tariff for FY 2024-25 in Case Nos. 2318/2024, 2319/2024, 2320/2024, 2321/2024, 2323/2024, 2324/2024, 2325/2024, 2326/2024, 2327/2024 & 2328/2024 respectively is required. Accordingly, a Corrigendum draft Order in Case Nos. 2318/2024, 2319/2024, 2320/2024, 2321/2024, 2323/2024, 2324/2024, 2325/2024, 2326/2024, 2327/2024 & 2328/2024, undergoes following amendments:
