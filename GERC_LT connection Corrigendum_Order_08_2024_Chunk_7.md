### 2. REGULATORY CHANGE LINKAGE

**TRIGGER:** GERC (Electricity Supply Code and Related Matters) (Fourth Amendment) Regulations, 2024 (Notification No. 14 of 2024)

| **Regulatory Instrument** | **Change** | **Impact on Tariff** |
|---|---|---|
| **Original Supply Code (Clause 3.2)** | Voltage supply fixed at 400V Three Phase for all installations with 6 kW to 100 kVA/kW contracted demand | LT Tariff Category limited to 100 kVA/kW |
| **Amended Supply Code (Clause 3.2, Fourth Amendment)** | Voltage supply expanded: 400V Three Phase now extends to 100–150 kVA/kW (on optional basis) for applicants/consumers | LT Tariff Category must expand to 100–150 kVA/kW (on optional basis) |
| **Result:** Regulatory Harmonization | Tariff applicability must align with permitted voltage classification to maintain legal consistency | Corrigendum Order No. 08/2024 issued to adjust LT Tariff Category definition |

---

### 3. AMENDMENT SCOPE – WHAT CHANGED AND WHAT DIDN'T

**AMENDED:**
✓ LT Tariff Category definition (Paragraph 6, Corrigendum)
  - From: "up to 100 kVA/kW"
  - To: "up to 100 kVA/kW / up to 150 kVA/kW if opted"

✓ Voltage classification applicability (Clause 3.2 Supply Code)
  - Consumers 100–150 kVA/kW now permitted 400V Three Phase (LT) on optional basis
  - (Previously mandatory shift to 11 kV HT supply)

**UNCHANGED:**
✗ All tariff rates (fixed charges, energy charges, surcharges, FAC, etc.)
✗ Demand charges and time-of-day (ToU) multipliers
✗ Voltage rebates and power factor incentives/penalties
✗ Billing methodology and payment procedures
✗ All other consumer categories' tariff definitions
✗ Compliance requirements and technical standards
✗ All other terms and conditions of original Tariff Orders (Paragraph 7)

### 4. CONSUMER ELIGIBILITY AND CHOICE MATRIX

**For LT Consumers with 100–150 kVA/kW Contracted Demand:**

| **Scenario** | **Before Corrigendum (01.06.2024)** | **After Corrigendum (Effective from Notification No. 14/2024)** |
|---|---|---|
| **New Connection (100–150 kVA/kW)** | Mandatory shift to 11 kV HT supply and HT tariffs | Choice: Opt for 400V LT supply (pay LT tariffs) OR shift to 11 kV HT supply (pay HT tariffs) |
| **Existing Connection (100 kVA/kW, seeking to upgrade to 150 kVA/kW)** | Mandatory shift to HT supply | Choice: Remain on 400V LT supply (if opted, pay LT tariffs) OR upgrade to HT supply |
| **Tariff Classification** | Automatically falls into HT category | Falls into LT category (if consumer opts for LT) or HT category (if consumer opts for upgrade) |
| **Consumer Advantage** | None – forced HT tariff payment | Opportunity to remain on lower LT tariffs despite higher contracted demand (100–150 kVA/kW range) |

### 8. CRITICAL CROSS-REFERENCE NOTES FOR RETRIEVAL

**Query: "Can a 140 kVA/kW consumer remain on LT supply in FY 2024-25?"**
- **Required chunks:** Corrigendum Chunk 4 (LT Category definition) + Supply Code Chunk 2 (Voltage classification)
- **Answer:** Yes, if the consumer opts for 400V Three Phase LT supply (under the amended Clause 3.2) and is within the expanded LT Tariff Category (100–150 kVA/kW optional).

**Query: "What tariff rates apply to a 120 kVA/kW LT consumer?"**
- **Required chunks:** Corrigendum Chunk 4 (LT Category applicability) + Original Tariff Order Chunks (LT tariff rates)
- **Answer:** The LT tariff rates from the original Tariff Orders dated 1st June 2024 apply (unchanged by Corrigendum).

**Query: "When did the 150 kVA/kW LT eligibility come into effect?"**
- **Required chunks:** Corrigendum Chunk 6 (Effective Date) + Supply Code Chunk 2 (Amendment authority)
- **Answer:** From the date of notification of Notification No. 14 of 2024 (the Supply Code Amendment notification).

**Query: "Are HT tariff rates affected by this Corrigendum?"**
- **Required chunks:** Corrigendum Chunk 5 (Unchanged Provisions)
- **Answer:** No. Only the LT Tariff Category definition was amended. All other provisions, including HT tariffs, remain unchanged.
