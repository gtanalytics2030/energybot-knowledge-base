**PROVISION:** Regulation 1 (Short Title, Extent and Commencement)

**TITLE:** Short Title, Extent and Commencement of Third Amendment Regulations 2022

**SUMMARY:**
The Third Amendment Regulations establish their short title, territorial scope, and effective date. ## CHUNK 002

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2022

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Notification

**HIERARCHY:** Notification No. 02 of 2022 / Amendment in Regulation 2.1 / Definitions

**PROVISION:** Amendment in Regulation 2.1 of Principal Regulations - Definitions (Multiple)

**TITLE:** Amendments to Regulation 2.1 - Definitions of Contracted Load, Eligible Consumer, Gross Meter, Gross Metering, Net Metering, Prosumer, and Third Party

**SUMMARY:**
The Third Amendment substantially expands the definitions section (Regulation 2.1) to support gross metering and prosumer business models. Key additions and modifications include: (1) Contracted Load/Sanctioned Load definition clarified; (2) Eligible Consumer modified to include "or Third Party Sale"; (3) New definition of "Gross Meter" as unidirectional meter measuring solar energy injection to grid; (4) New definition of "Gross Metering" accounting for total solar generation separately from consumption using feed-in-tariff and retail tariff respectively; (5) Net Metering redefined to focus on net import/export using single bidirectional meter; (6) New definition of "Prosumer" as person who both consumes from and injects into grid at same point of supply; (7) New definition of "Third Party" as developer installing and selling solar power to consumer/prosumer at same premises. These definitions establish legal framework for diverse solar business models beyond traditional net metering.

**KEY TOPICS:** Contracted Load, Eligible Consumer, Gross Meter, Gross Metering, Net Metering, Prosumer, Third Party Sale, Definitions, Feed-in Tariff, Retail Tariff, Bidirectional Meter, Unidirectional Meter

**TEXT:**
3) Amendment in Regulation 2.1 of the Principal Regulations:

(a) The definition clause Regulation 2.1 (i) of the Principal Regulations shall be substituted as under:

"Contracted load" or "Sanctioned load" or "Contracted demand" means the maximum demand in kW, kVA or HP, agreed to be supplied by the Distribution Licensee and indicated in the agreement executed between the Distribution Licensee and the Consumer and the expression "load" shall be construed accordingly;

(b) In the definition 2.1 (l) "Eligible Consumer" the words "or Third Party Sale" shall be inserted after the word "given that such system is self owned".

(c) Addition of New Definition clause "Gross Meter" and "Gross Metering" in Regulation 2.1 of the Principal Regulations:

The following new definition clauses shall stand inserted in Regulation 2.1 after existing Regulation 2.1(m) of the Principal Regulations as Regulation 2.1 (ma) and (mb):

(ma) "Gross Meter" means unidirectional energy meter installed at the point at which the electricity generated by solar energy system of the eligible consumer injects into the grid of Distribution Licensee.

(mb) "gross-metering" means a mechanism whereby the total solar energy generated from Grid Interactive Rooftop Solar Photovoltaic system of a Prosumer and the total energy consumed by the Prosumer are accounted separately through appropriate metering arrangements and for the billing purpose, the total energy consumed by the Prosumer is accounted at the applicable retail tariff and total solar power generated is accounted for at feed-in-tariff determined by the Commission;

(d) The definition of "net-metering" 2(p) provided in the principal Regulations is substituted by following definitions:

2(p) "net-metering" means a mechanism whereby solar energy exported to the Grid from Grid Interactive Rooftop Solar Photovoltaic System of a Prosumer is deducted from energy imported from the Grid in units (kWh) to arrive at the net imported or exported energy and the net energy import or export is billed or credited or carriedover by the distribution licensee on the basis of the applicable retail tariff by using a single bidirectional energy meter for net-metering at the point of supply;'.

(e) The following new definition clause shall stand inserted in Regulation 2.1 after existing Regulation 2.1(s) of the Principal Regulations as Regulation 2.1 (sa):

(sa) "prosumer" means a person who consumes electricity from the grid and can also inject electricity into the grid for Distribution Licensee, using same point of supply;

(f) The following new definition clause shall stand inserted in Regulation 2.1 after existing Regulation 2.1(x) of the Principal Regulations as Regulation 2.1 (xa):

(xa) "Third party" for this Regulations means a developer setting up solar project on the Rooftop/premises of a consumer/prosumer for generation and sale of such power to the consumer/prosumer in the same premises.

**PROVISION:** Amendment in Regulation 6.2 of Principal Regulations Read with First and Second Amendments

**TITLE:** Amendment to Regulation 6.2 - Comprehensive Capacity Framework for Net and Gross Metering, Residential, Captive, Third-Party, REC, and RPO Models

**SUMMARY:**
The Third Amendment completely rewrites Regulation 6.2 to establish unified capacity framework applicable to all solar business models. Structure: (a) Capacity limits - 1 kW to 1000 kW for net metering; above 10 kW to 1000 kW for gross metering. (b) Residential consumers - permitted irrespective of sanctioned load with incentive scheme eligibility. (c) Captive consumers - no capacity restrictions up to permissible limits (1000 kW). (d) REC mechanism - up to sanctioned load. (e) RPO compliance - regardless of sanctioned load. (f) Non-residential/non-MSME - up to 50% sanctioned load under net metering. This tiered approach recognizes diverse policy objectives: residential adoption, small business development, captive industry efficiency, and renewable energy procurement. The regulation also imposes 1 kW minimum capacity floor for all installations, preventing inefficient small-scale deployments.

**KEY TOPICS:** Rooftop Solar Capacity, Sanctioned Load, Net Metering, Gross Metering, Residential Consumers, Captive Consumers, Third Party Sale, REC Mechanism, RPO, Individual Project Capacity, Capacity Limit

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2022

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Notification

**HIERARCHY:** Notification No. 02 of 2022 / Regulation 9.1 - Residential and Government Consumers Energy Accounting

**PROVISION:** Regulation 9.1 - Energy Accounting for Residential and Government Consumers

**TITLE:** Energy Accounting and Settlement for Residential and Government Consumers

**SUMMARY:**
Regulation 9.1 establishes energy accounting for residential and government consumers. Key provisions: (1) Energy accounting on billing cycle basis; (2) If electricity supplied exceeds generation, consumer pays at prevailing tariff; (3) If injection exceeds consumption, surplus compensated at Rs. 2.25/unit for first 5 years from commissioning under self-owned systems and SURYA Gujarat scheme, then 75% of simple average of competitive bidding tariff for non-park-based solar projects in preceding six months (April-September or October-March), fixed for 25-year project term; (4) Third-party sale receives 75% rate from commencement without initial 5-year fixed rate; (5) Banking allowed within one billing cycle; (6) GUVNL declares SIC rates semi-annually and applies prospectively through interconnection agreement; (7) No banking charges for residential consumers; (8) Distribution licensee can raise invoices for fixed charges, demand charges, other permissible charges. This framework incentivizes early adopters through higher initial tariff while maintaining long-term financial sustainability through declining rates indexed to competitive procurements.

**KEY TOPICS:** Residential Consumers, Government Consumers, Energy Accounting, Billing Cycle, Surplus Injection Compensation, SIC Rate, Banking, Five-Year Fixed Rate, SURYA Gujarat, Third Party Sale, GUVNL Tariff Declaration

16.2 Addition in capacity of Solar Rooftop Plants by owner without approval of the licensee

The addition of Solar Modules after commissioning of Solar Power Plant by consumer/Solar Power Generator for which Agreement for Sale/Purchase signed between the Solar Project Developer/Distribution licensee or Consumer/Prosumer will be considered as 'Un-authorized Capacity Addition'. It shall attract penalty for utilization of generation from such plant at twice the applicable tariff for such Solar Developer/Consumer for the period of such additional capacity and equipments of Solar Power Plant to the extent of added unauthorized solar power plant capacity.

16.3 Installation and connectivity of Solar Rooftop Project with the licensee network without any approval/permission

Any installation of new Solar Rooftop Project without approval of concerned distribution licensee shall be qualified as unauthorized connection/use and it shall attract penalty for utilization of generation from such plant at twice of the applicable tariff for such Solar Developer/Consumer for the period of such capacity and equipments of Solar Power Plant to the extent of unauthorized solar power plant capacity connected without approval of licensee and such equipments of solar power plant shall liable to be removed.
