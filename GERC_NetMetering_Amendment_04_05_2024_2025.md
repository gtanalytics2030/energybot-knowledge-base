## CHUNK 002

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) (Fourth Amendment) Regulations, 2024

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Notification

**HIERARCHY:** Notification No. 13 of 2024 / Amendment in Regulation 5.1

**PROVISION:** Amendment in Regulation 5.1 of Principal Regulations - System Strengthening Charges Framework

**TITLE:** Amendment to Regulation 5.1 - Distribution Infrastructure Strengthening Charges for Rooftop Solar Above 6 kW

**SUMMARY:**
The Fourth Amendment completely rewrites Regulation 5.1 to establish system strengthening charges for rooftop solar PV installations. Framework: (1) Cost of strengthening distribution infrastructure (including distribution transformer augmentation) to facilitate installation up to 6 kW aggregate (including subsequent additions aggregating to 6 kW) shall be included in DISCOM Annual Revenue Requirement (ARR) - essentially free to consumers for installations ≤6 kW; (2) For aggregate capacity above 6 kW, system strengthening charges recovered from applicant by DISCOM based on per-kW charges for LT consumers and per-kVA for HT consumers as applicable for release of new/additional load; (3) For LT consumers above 6 kW up to 100 kW: State-owned DISCOMs and Torrent Power recover per-kW charges per their approved rate; other DISCOMs recover actual augmentation costs; (4) For HT consumers above 6 kW up to 1000 kW: State-owned DISCOMs recover per-kVA charges per approved rate; other DISCOMs recover actual augmentation costs; (5) Charges recovered only for additional capacity when existing installation expanded; (6) Minimum 1 kW capacity floor established. This framework balances consumer incentive for solar adoption (no charges ≤6 kW, free grid upgrades) with cost-recovery principle (charges >6 kW based on actual infrastructure cost or per-kW benchmark).

**KEY TOPICS:** System Strengthening, Distribution Transformer, Infrastructure Augmentation, Strengthening Charges, LT Consumer, HT Consumer, Kilo Watt, Kilo Volt Ampere, Annual Revenue Requirement, New Connection Charges, Incremental Capacity

**TEXT:**
3. Amendment in Clause 5.1 of the Principal Regulations.

The Regulation 5.1 shall be substituted as under:

5.1 The distribution licensee shall update distribution transformer capacity available for connecting Rooftop Solar PV Systems under net metering arrangement on yearly basis and shall provide the information to the Commission.

Provided that the cost of strengthening of distribution infrastructure, including distribution transformer, as necessary, to facilitate the installation of Rooftop Solar PV System up to 6 KW including subsequent addition aggregating up to 6 KW, shall be included in the Annual Revenue Requirement of Distribution Licensee.

Provided further that for aggregate Rooftop Solar capacity above 6 KW, system strengthening charges shall be recovered by the Distribution Licensee from the applicant of Rooftop Solar system as per the below stipulations:

(a) For LT consumers installing Rooftop Solar PV System above 6 kW up to 100 kW in aggregate:

(i) In respect of State owned Discoms and Torrent Power Limited:

System strengthening charges for Rooftop Solar capacity above 6 kW up to 100 kW shall be recovered from the Applicant by the Distribution Licensee based on per kW basis charges as applicable for release of new/additional load at Low Tension, being recovered from the applicant by the concerned Distribution Licensee as approved by the Commission from time to time.

Provided that in case of addition of Rooftop Solar capacity resulting into total Rooftop Solar capacity above 6 kW, the system strengthening charges shall be recovered only for additional Rooftop Solar capacity.

(ii) In respect of other Discoms:

In case there is any requirement of upgradation of distribution infrastructures like the augmentation of service line, distribution transformer capacity, and the like for installation of the required capacity of Rooftop Solar PV System, the same shall be carried out by the distribution licensee, the cost of such augmentation shall be borne by the consumer.

Provided that as and when the Commission approves recovery of charges on per kW basis for release of new connection/additional load, the same shall be applicable for recovery of charges towards system strengthening for installation of Rooftop Solar PV system.

(b) For HT consumers installing Rooftop Solar PV System above 6 kW up to 1000 kW in aggregate:

(i) In respect of State owned Discoms:

System strengthening charges for Rooftop Solar capacity above 6 kW up to 1000 kW shall be recovered from the Applicant by the Distribution Licensee based on per KVA basis charges as applicable for release of new/additional load at High Tension, being recovered from the applicant by the concerned Distribution Licensee as approved by the Commission from time to time.

Provided that in case of addition of Rooftop Solar capacity resulting into total Rooftop Solar capacity above 6 kW, the system strengthening charges shall be recovered only for additional Rooftop Solar capacity.

(ii) In respect of other licensee:

In case there is any requirement of upgradation of distribution infrastructures like the augmentation of service line, and the like for installation of the required capacity of Rooftop Solar PV System, the same shall be carried out by the distribution licensee, the cost of such augmentation shall be borne by the consumer.

Provided that as and when the Commission approves recovery of charges on per kW basis or per KVA basis for release of new connection/additional load, the same shall be applicable for recovery of charges towards system strengthening for installation of Rooftop Solar PV system.

The capacity of Rooftop Solar PV System to be installed at the premises of any consumer shall not be less than one Kilo Watt (1 kW).

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Third Amendment Regulation 5 (capacity targets); Regulation 6.2 (individual capacity limits); Regulation 7 (Technical Feasibility Report - cost estimation); Second Amendment Regulation 5 (cumulative transformer capacity); GERC Tariff Regulations 2023 (per-kW/per-kVA charges for new connections)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** System strengthening charges detailed in Technical Feasibility Report (Regulation 7, Activity 5); consumer pays estimate per Regulation 7 (Activity 6) after TFR; charges become part of Interconnection Agreement (Clause 8.1 connection costs); 6 kW threshold aligns with government PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana free installation subsidy (later added in Fifth Amendment); state-owned DISCOM rate-based recovery (LT per-kW, HT per-kVA) ensures uniformity; other DISCOM actual cost recovery allows flexibility for non-uniformly sized transformers; incremental charging for capacity additions prevents double-charging existing infrastructure

## CHUNK 003

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) (Fourth Amendment) Regulations, 2024

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Notification

**HIERARCHY:** Notification No. 13 of 2024 / Amendment in Regulation 7 - Activity Timeline Table

**PROVISION:** Amendment in Regulation 7 of Principal Regulations - Completely Rewritten Activity Timeline with Time-Bound Approvals and Detailed Procedures

**TITLE:** Amendment to Regulation 7 - Comprehensive Activity Timeline with Time-Bound Approvals, Technical Feasibility, and Commissioning Procedures

**SUMMARY:**
The Fourth Amendment completely rewrites Regulation 7, establishing a 15-activity timeline with specific time-bound durations and responsibilities for rooftop solar project approvals and commissioning. Key activities and timelines: (1) GEDA Registration - 5 days for certificate issuance; (2) Chief Electrical Inspector approval (SLD, earthing, wiring diagrams) - 10 days; (3) Application to Distribution Licensee - submitted by applicant with capacity subject to Reg 6.2; (4) Technical Feasibility Report (TFR) request - 3 days from registration; (5) TFR from field - 10 days, includes transformer details, solar capacity limits, demand recording, no-dues certificate, and system strengthening estimate; (6) Post-TFR at Head Office - 2 days, issues "In Principle" consent (if no strengthening) or issues estimate (if strengthening required, paid within 30 days); (7) Connectivity agreement signing - 5 days after consent/payment, issues letter to complete project within 6 months; (8) System strengthening by DISCOM - 15 days (if required); (9) 2-month notice to applicant on expiry of 6-month construction period; (10) Application cancellation on non-completion (forfeiting charges); (11) Applicant completion with document submission (ownership, CEI approval, meter testing, IEC certificates for all equipment, protection system/anti-islanding, second-line protection for >10 kW); (12) Head Office notification to field for meter installation; (13) Field installation of solar + bidirectional/ABT meters within 5 days; (14) Notification to GEDA/agency for commissioning certificate; (15) GEDA/agency site visit and commissioning certificate within 3 days. Special provision: Applications ≤10 kW deemed accepted without TFR if documentation complete; upgrades of sanctions load automatic at DISCOM cost. Default compensation: DISCOM liable to pay Rs. 500/day to consumer for delays beyond justified cause. This detailed timeline ensures systematic processing, consumer protection through time-binding, and clear allocation of responsibilities among GEDA, CEI, DISCOM, and applicants.

**KEY TOPICS:** Activity Timeline, Time-Bound Approvals, Technical Feasibility Report, Registration, CEI Approval, Connectivity Agreement, System Strengthening, Meter Installation, Commissioning, GEDA, Chief Electrical Inspector, Delay Compensation

## CHUNK 004

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) (Fourth Amendment) Regulations, 2024

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Notification

**HIERARCHY:** Notification No. 13 of 2024 / Amendment in Regulation 8(2)

**PROVISION:** Amendment in Regulation 8(2) - Modified Connectivity Level Reference

**TITLE:** Amendment to Regulation 8(2) - Updated Reference to Supply Voltage Level per Supply Code

**SUMMARY:**
The Fourth Amendment modifies Regulation 8(2) to reference supply voltage levels "as per the supply voltage level provided under GERC (Electricity Supply Code and Related Matters) Regulations, 2015 as amended from time to time." This clarifies that rooftop PV system connectivity levels shall be determined according to the current supply voltage classifications in the Supply Code (e.g., LT ≤440V, HT 11 kV, 22 kV, EHV etc.), ensuring alignment with evolving GERC supply standards and preventing conflicts between net metering regulations and supply code voltage classifications. The change makes the provision dynamic and responsive to future updates in supply code terminology without requiring amendments to the net metering regulations.

**KEY TOPICS:** Connectivity Level, Supply Voltage Level, GERC Supply Code 2015, Voltage Classification, Grid Connection, LT, HT, EHV

**TEXT:**
4. Amendment in Clause 8 (2) of the Principal Regulations:

The Regulation 8 (2) shall be substituted as under:

"(2) The connectivity levels at which the Rooftop PV Solar System shall be connected with the grid shall be as per the supply voltage level provided under GERC (Electricity Supply Code and Related Matters) Regulations, 2015 as amended from time to time."

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** GERC Supply Code 2015 (latest version as amended); Principal Regulation 8(1)-(2) prior connectivity level table; Regulation 6.2 (capacity limits); Regulation 7 (Technical Feasibility Report verification of connectivity level)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Dynamic reference to Supply Code allows connectivity requirements to evolve without standalone net metering amendments; ensures consistency with GERC's unified consumer interface standards; aligns with CEA Technical Standards for connectivity; Implementation in Interconnection Agreement Clause 2.5 (Interface Specification) and Technical Feasibility Report (Regulation 7 Activity 5)

## CHUNK 006

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) (Fifth Amendment) Regulations, 2025

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Notification

**HIERARCHY:** Notification No. 10 of 2025 / Amendment in Regulation 7 - PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana Provisions

**PROVISION:** Amendment in Regulation 7 of Principal Regulations - Special Provisions for PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana

**TITLE:** Amendment to Regulation 7 - Streamlined Connectivity for PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana Beneficiaries

**SUMMARY:**
The Fifth Amendment adds a proviso to Regulation 7 establishing special provisions for solar rooftop systems installed under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana (government scheme providing free/subsidized rooftop solar for residential consumers). Key provisions: (1) No requirement for execution of separate written connectivity agreement between distribution licensee and consumer - consumer deemed bound by terms of the regulations for solar rooftop PV systems; (2) Date of commencement of agreement = date of commissioning of solar rooftop PV system (not execution date); (3) In lieu of formal connectivity agreement execution, concerned DISCOM communicates to applicant under proper receipt, mentioning commencement date and applicable rate of purchase of surplus energy. This streamlines procedures for government scheme beneficiaries by eliminating separate agreement execution while maintaining regulatory compliance through deemed binding of regulation terms. The provision recognizes that scheme installers may be third-party contractors with limited flexibility for customized agreements, and government nature of scheme warrants simplified procedures.

**KEY TOPICS:** PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, Government Scheme, Connectivity Agreement, Deemed Binding, Commencement Date, Surplus Energy Rate, Simplified Procedure, Residential Consumers

**TEXT:**
3. Amendment in Clause 7 of the Principal Regulations:

Following proviso shall be incorporated after second proviso below the Activity Chart provided under Regulation 7 of the Principal Regulations read with GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) (Fourth Amendment) Regulations, 2024:

"7. …………...
……………..
Provided that there shall not be requirement for execution of separate written connectivity agreement between the Distribution Licensee and the Consumer in case of Solar Rooftop PV Systems installed under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana and the Consumer shall be deemed to be bound by terms and conditions of these Regulations in respect of Solar Rooftop PV Systems. In such case, the date of commencement of Agreement shall be the date of commissioning of Solar Rooftop PV Systems and in lieu of execution of formal connectivity agreement, the concerned Discom shall communicate to the Applicant, under proper receipt, mentioning the date of commencement of agreement along with the applicable rate of purchase of surplus energy."

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Fourth Amendment Regulation 7 (Activity Timeline); Regulation 9 (Energy Accounting and Settlement); Principal Interconnection Agreement requirements (Annexure IV); PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana guidelines (Government of India)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Deemed binding by regulation terms (rather than separate agreement) maintains legal enforceability for DISCOM rates and consumer obligations; commissioning-date commencement simplifies date tracking when scheme installer handles procedural complexity; surplus energy rate notification (in lieu of agreement) creates binding commercial terms through written communication with receipt evidence; provision applies exclusively to scheme beneficiaries, regular net metering consumers continue to execute formal interconnection agreements
