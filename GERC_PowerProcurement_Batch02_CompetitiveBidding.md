DOCUMENT: GERC Guidelines for Procurement of Power by Distribution Licensees

DOCUMENT TYPE: Notification / Guideline / Regulatory Order

HIERARCHY: Guideline Provision

PROVISION: Item 6

TITLE: Mandatory Competitive Bidding and MoU Route Approval Requirements

SUMMARY:
Item 6 establishes the default requirement that Distribution Licensees shall normally procure power through competitive bidding processes. In cases where a Distribution Licensee proposes to procure power through a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) route—which bypasses competitive bidding—the licensee must obtain prior approval from GERC before entering into such arrangement. This provision protects the interests of consumers by establishing competitive bidding as the standard procurement method, with exceptions requiring regulatory oversight.

KEY TOPICS: Competitive Bidding, Power Procurement, Distribution Licensee, MoU Route, Memorandum of Understanding, GERC Approval, Procurement Process, Transparent Procurement

TEXT:

**Item 6 – Competitive Bidding as Default Process and MoU Approval Requirement**

The Distribution Licensee shall normally endeavour to procure power through competitive bidding. In case of any proposal for procurement of power through MoU route, the Distribution Licensee shall obtain prior approval of the GERC.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Item 7 (Long-term/Medium-term Procurement through Competitive Bidding), Item 9 (GERC Determination of Tariff)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Ministry of Power Guidelines for Tariff Determination by Bidding Process (referenced in Item 7); Item 8 (CERC Tariff Determination); Item 11 (Renewable Energy Procurement); Item 14 (Short-term Competitive Bidding)

PROVISION: Item 7

TITLE: Long-Term and Medium-Term Power Procurement Through Competitive Bidding – Bid Documentation and GERC Approval Process

SUMMARY:
Item 7 outlines the detailed process for long-term and medium-term power procurement through competitive bidding, incorporating the Ministry of Power's Guidelines for Determination of Tariff by Bidding Process (notified 19/01/2005). It specifies that Distribution Licensees must: (a) prepare bid documentation in accordance with MoP guidelines and obtain GERC approval unless using Central Government standard bid documents (in which case notification to GERC is sufficient); (b) obtain GERC approval for any deviations from MoP bidding conditions; and (c) seek GERC approval prior to initiating bidding regarding quantum of capacity/energy exceeding three-year demand forecasts and fuel transfer prices under Case 2 scenarios. The provision establishes a three-tier approval framework based on document standardization and deviation severity.

KEY TOPICS: Competitive Bidding, Long-term Procurement, Medium-term Procurement, Bid Documentation, Ministry of Power Guidelines, GERC Approval, Request for Quotation, Bidding Process, Fuel Specific Procurement, Demand Forecast

TEXT:

**Item 7 – Long-Term/Medium-Term Procurement Through Competitive Bidding**

In case of procurement of power through competitive bidding, the Distribution Licensees shall initiate the process for long-term / medium-term power procurement in accordance with the Ministry of Power's 'Guidelines for Determination of Tariff by Bidding Process for Procurement of Power by Distribution Licensees' notified by the Ministry of Power on 19/01/2005 and in force from time to time. As provided in the MoP Guidelines:

a. The bid documentation shall be prepared in accordance with the MoP guidelines and the approval of the GERC shall be obtained unless the bid documents are as per the standard bid documents issued by the Central Government. In the latter case, intimation shall be sent by the Distribution Licensee to the GERC about initiation of the bidding process.

b. Approval of the GERC shall be sought in the event of any deviations from the bidding conditions contained in the MoP guidelines, and in following the process described in the MoP guidelines.

c. Approval of the GERC shall be sought prior to initiating the bidding process with regard to the following aspects:

i. For the quantum of capacity / energy to be procured, in case the same exceeds the projected additional demand forecast for the next three years following the year of expected commencement of supply proposed to be procured. Such demand forecast shall be based on the latest available (at the time of issue of RFQ) Electric Power Survey published by the Central Electricity Authority (Both for Case 1 and Case 2).

ii. For the transfer price of fuel, in case of fuel specific procurement enquiry, if such price has not been determined by government, government approved mechanism or a fuel regulator (under Case 2).

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Ministry of Power Guidelines dated 19/01/2005 for Determination of Tariff by Bidding Process for Procurement of Power by Distribution Licensees, Item 6 (Default Competitive Bidding Requirement), Item 8 (CERC Tariff Determination), Item 10 (Draft PPA Approval Process)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Electric Power Survey (CEA - Central Electricity Authority publication); Item 14 (Short-term Competitive Bidding following similar process); Item 6 (Competitive Bidding as Standard); Ministry of Power Guidelines supersede or work in conjunction with GERC authority as per this notification

PROVISION: Item 8

TITLE: Long-Term/Medium-Term Procurement from CERC Tariff-Determined Generating Sources – Prior Approval Requirement

SUMMARY:
Item 8 addresses procurement scenarios where long-term or medium-term power is sourced from generating companies where the tariff is to be determined or adopted by the Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (CERC), rather than GERC. In such cases, the Distribution Licensee must obtain prior approval from GERC before entering into any such arrangement or power purchase agreement. This provision ensures that GERC maintains regulatory oversight even for inter-state power procurement where tariff determination falls under CERC jurisdiction.

KEY TOPICS: Long-term Procurement, Medium-term Procurement, CERC Tariff, Tariff Determination, Distribution Licensee, Power Purchase Agreement, Prior Approval, Inter-state Procurement, Regulatory Approval

TEXT:

**Item 8 – CERC Tariff-Determined Procurement – Prior GERC Approval**

In case of long-term / medium-term procurement of power from generating sources, where tariff is to be determined / adopted by the Central Electricity Regulatory Commission, the Distribution Licensee shall take prior approval of the GERC before entering into any such arrangement.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Item 7 (Competitive Bidding Process for Long-term/Medium-term Procurement), Item 9 (GERC Tariff-Determined Procurement), Item 10 (Draft PPA Approval Process), Electricity Act 2003 (CERC authority for inter-state transmission)

CROSS-REFERENCES: CERC Regulations and Orders for Tariff Determination; Item 9 mirrors this provision for GERC-determined tariffs; Related to inter-state power purchase arrangements and Central Generating Stations

DOCUMENT: GERC Guidelines for Procurement of Power by Distribution Licensees

HIERARCHY: Guideline Provision

PROVISION: Item 9

TITLE: Long-Term/Medium-Term Procurement from GERC Tariff-Determined Generating Sources – Tariff Approval Requirement

SUMMARY:
Item 9 specifies that Distribution Licensees can enter into long-term or medium-term arrangements with generating sources where tariff is to be determined by GERC only after obtaining prior approval of the power purchase agreement from GERC. Unlike Item 8 (which requires prior approval before entering any arrangement), Item 9 requires prior approval of the specific PPA document that contains the negotiated terms. This provision ensures GERC oversight of intra-state power purchase agreements with tariff determination responsibility resting with GERC.

KEY TOPICS: Long-term Procurement, Medium-term Procurement, GERC Tariff Determination, Power Purchase Agreement, PPA Approval, Distribution Licensee, Tariff Approval, State-Level Procurement

TEXT:

**Item 9 – GERC Tariff-Determined Procurement – Prior PPA Approval**

In case of long-term / medium-term procurement of power from generating sources, where tariff is to be determined by the GERC, the Distribution Licensee shall enter into such arrangement or agreement only after getting prior approval of the power purchase agreement.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Item 7 (Competitive Bidding Process), Item 8 (CERC Tariff-Determined Procurement), Item 10 (Draft PPA Approval Process and Procedure), Electricity Act 2003

CROSS-REFERENCES: GERC's Tariff Determination Regulations; Item 8 addresses parallel CERC-determined tariff scenario; Item 10 provides detailed procedure for PPA approval application
