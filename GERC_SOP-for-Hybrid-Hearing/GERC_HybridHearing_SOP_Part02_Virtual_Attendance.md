# GERC Hybrid Hearing SOP - Part 2: Virtual Attendance Preparation & Participant Conduct

## Chunk 2.1

```
DOCUMENT: GERC SOP for Hybrid Hearing

DOCUMENT TYPE: Standard Operating Procedure / Circular

HIERARCHY: Title / Provision Cluster 4 (Clauses 6-7)

PROVISION: Clauses 6-7

TITLE: Virtual Participant Login Timing and Audio Equipment Requirements

SUMMARY:
Participants attending the hearing through virtual mode must log in at least 10-15 minutes prior to the scheduled hearing time to check their connectivity and ensure technical readiness. All participants, whether in-person or virtual, are advised to use earphones or headsets to ensure better audio clarity during the hearing session. This dual requirement ensures both technical preparedness and acoustic quality for all participants.

KEY TOPICS: Virtual Attendance, Login Timing, Connectivity Check, Earphones, Headsets, Audio Clarity, Technical Readiness
```

## Chunk 2.2

```
DOCUMENT: GERC SOP for Hybrid Hearing

DOCUMENT TYPE: Standard Operating Procedure / Circular

HIERARCHY: Title / Provision Cluster 5 (Clause 8)

PROVISION: Clause 8

TITLE: Camera and Microphone Management for Virtual Participants

SUMMARY:
Virtual participants must maintain strict control over their camera and microphone during the hearing. Cameras and microphones shall be switched on only at the time the participant is making submissions; they must be switched off at all other times. Virtual participants must ensure clear audio quality and participate from a quiet and well-lit environment to maintain professional hearing standards and prevent disruptions.

KEY TOPICS: Virtual Participant, Camera Control, Microphone Control, Submissions, Audio Quality, Environment Requirements, Quiet Environment, Well-lit Environment

TEXT:
8. Participants joining virtually shall keep their camera and microphone switched on only at the time of making submissions and shall keep them switched off otherwise. They shall ensure clear audio and participate from a quiet and well-lit environment.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Clause 5 (Mobile phone protocol), Clause 7 (Audio clarity equipment)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Related to mixed-mode coordination (Clause 9), conduct and decorum requirements (Clause 15), participation protocol using Raise Hand feature (Clause 11)
```

## Chunk 2.3

```
DOCUMENT TYPE: Standard Operating Procedure / Circular

HIERARCHY: Title / Provision Cluster 6 (Clause 9)

PROVISION: Clause 9

TITLE: Coordination Between Physical and Virtual Participants

SUMMARY:
Parties appearing physically in the hearing shall use the designated microphone provided by GERC and must avoid overlapping or simultaneous speaking with participants joining virtually. This requirement ensures clear audio reception for both in-person and virtual participants and prevents cross-talk that could disrupt the proceedings or make the hearing record unintelligible.

KEY TOPICS: Physical Participation, Virtual Participation, Microphone Usage, Simultaneous Speaking, Overlapping Speech, Mixed-Mode Coordination, Hearing Integrity

TEXT:
9. Parties appearing physically shall use the designated microphone and avoid overlapping or simultaneous speaking with virtual participants.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Clause 7 (Audio clarity requirements), Clause 8 (Virtual participant microphone control)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Related to conduct and decorum (Clause 15), participation protocol using Raise Hand (Clause 11), audio quality during mixed-mode proceedings
```
