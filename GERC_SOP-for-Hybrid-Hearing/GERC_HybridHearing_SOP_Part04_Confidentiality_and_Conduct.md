# GERC Hybrid Hearing SOP - Part 4: Confidentiality, Technical Fallback & Conduct Standards

---

## Chunk 4.1

```
DOCUMENT: GERC SOP for Hybrid Hearing

DOCUMENT TYPE: Standard Operating Procedure / Circular

HIERARCHY: Title / Provision Cluster 10 (Clause 13)

PROVISION: Clause 13

TITLE: Confidentiality, Recording Restrictions, and Prohibited Sharing of Proceedings

SUMMARY:
Confidentiality of the hybrid hearing proceedings shall be strictly maintained. Recording, taking screenshots, or sharing of the proceedings in any form is strictly prohibited. This requirement applies to all participants, whether attending in person or virtually, and covers all aspects of the hearing including submissions, arguments, documents, and proceedings. Breach of confidentiality may result in disciplinary action and constitutes a serious violation of hearing protocol.

KEY TOPICS: Confidentiality, Recording Prohibition, Screenshots, Sharing Prohibition, Strict Maintenance, Hearing Proceedings, Participant Obligation, Breach of Confidentiality

TEXT:
13. Confidentiality shall be strictly maintained. Recording, taking screenshots, or sharing of the proceedings in any form is strictly prohibited.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: None (administrative safeguard)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Related to conduct and decorum requirements (Clause 15), participant identification and accountability (Clause 4), participant discipline and obligation
```

## Chunk 4.2

```
DOCUMENT: GERC SOP for Hybrid Hearing

DOCUMENT TYPE: Standard Operating Procedure / Circular

HIERARCHY: Title / Provision Cluster 11 (Clause 14)

PROVISION: Clause 14

TITLE: Technical Disruptions and Fallback to Physical Mode Proceedings

SUMMARY:
In case of substantial technical disruptions affecting the conduct of a hybrid hearing, the Commission reserves the right to proceed with the hearing entirely in physical mode, at its sole discretion. This fallback provision ensures that proceedings can continue despite technological failures and prevents undue delays or adjournments. The Commission's decision to invoke this provision shall be final and binding on all participants.

KEY TOPICS: Technical Disruptions, Fallback Procedure, Physical Mode, Commission Discretion, Proceeding Continuity, Technical Failure, Hearing Integrity

TEXT:
14. In case of technical disruptions, the Commission reserves the right to proceed with the hearing in physical mode, at its discretion.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Clause 12 (Disconnection and re-connection protocol), Clause 2 (Microsoft Teams platform)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Related to virtual attendance requirements (Clauses 6-8), mixed-mode coordination (Clause 9), technical readiness and preparation (Clause 6)
```

## Chunk 4.3

```
DOCUMENT: GERC SOP for Hybrid Hearing

DOCUMENT TYPE: Standard Operating Procedure / Circular

HIERARCHY: Title / Provision Cluster 12 (Clause 15)

PROVISION: Clause 15

TITLE: Conduct, Decorum, and Discipline Standards for All Participants

SUMMARY:
All participants shall maintain proper decorum and discipline equivalent to that required in physical hearings, irrespective of whether they are appearing in person or through virtual mode. This provision extends formal hearing standards and professional conduct requirements uniformly to all participants and ensures that the hybrid format does not diminish the solemnity or procedural integrity of GERC proceedings. Violations of decorum may result in expulsion or other disciplinary action.

KEY TOPICS: Decorum, Discipline, Professional Conduct, Hearing Standards, Virtual Participants, Physical Participants, Participant Behavior, Procedural Integrity

TEXT:
15. All participants shall maintain proper decorum and discipline equivalent to that of physical hearings, irrespective of the mode of participation.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Clauses 5, 8, 9 (device and audio management relating to conduct)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Related to all procedural requirements (Clauses 1-14), participant identification (Clause 4), camera and microphone management (Clause 8), confidentiality requirements (Clause 13)
```
