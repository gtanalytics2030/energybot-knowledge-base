DOCUMENT TYPE: Regulation

PROVISION: Regulation 3

TITLE: Qualifying Requirements for Applicant

SUMMARY:
Regulation 3 specifies the mandatory technical and financial qualifying requirements that an applicant must meet to be entitled to receive a transmission license from GERC. The applicant must demonstrate technical experience (capability) in development, design, construction, operation, and maintenance of transmission lines and/or substations at the voltage level of the assets for which the license is sought. Experience in design, construction, or maintenance of transmission systems operating at 66 kV or higher is construed as relevant experience for all voltage levels at 66 kV and above. The applicant's net worth must be no less than the estimated annual transmission charges for the assets. In case of consortium applicants, affiliates may collectively meet financial requirements. Applicants must submit documentary evidence supporting their qualifying claims. These requirements ensure licensees have both operational capability and financial stability to manage transmission systems.

KEY TOPICS: Qualifying Requirements, Technical Experience, Transmission Lines, Substations, Voltage Level (66 kV), Financial Capability, Net Worth, Annual Transmission Charges, Consortium, Affiliates, Documentary Evidence

TEXT:

Chapter - 2
Qualifying Requirements for Applicant

3. Qualifying Requirements

The applicant to be entitled for grant of licence, shall fulfil the following technical and financial requirements, namely:

(1) Technical experience (capability):

The applicant shall have experience (capability) in development, design, construction, operation and maintenance of transmission line (if the assets in respect of which the application for grant of licence has been made, consists of transmission lines only) or substations (if the assets in respect of which the application for grant of licence has been made, consists of sub-stations only) or both (if the assets in respect of which the application for grant of licence has been made, includes both transmission lines and substations) at the voltage level at which the assets in respect of which the application for grant of licence has been made, shall be operated.

Provided further that experience in design, construction or maintenance, as the case may be, of transmission system operating at least at 66 kV level shall be construed as relevant experience at voltage levels of 66 kV and above.

(2) Financial capability:

Net worth of the applicant shall not be less than the estimated annual transmission charges of the assets in respect of which the application for grant of licence has been made.

Note 1

In case of consortium, affiliates may meet the qualifying requirements of the financial capability collectively.

Note 2

The applicant shall be required to submit necessary evidence in support of its claim of meeting the qualifying requirements.
