PROVISION: Schedule-1 (continued)

TITLE: Application Form for Grant of Transmission Licence (Part 3: Financing Structure)

SUMMARY:
Section 3 of Schedule-1 requires the applicant to provide details of the proposed financing structure for the transmission business. The applicant must indicate whether it will fully finance the proposed transmission activity on its own balance sheet. If financing will be obtained partially from the applicant and partially from other agencies, the applicant must provide the name, designation, address, contact details, and email of a reference person from each participating agency. For each participating agency, the applicant must specify the proposed equity contribution (both amount and percentage of total equity), the currency in which equity will be provided, and must attach the consent letter of that agency to participate in the equity. The applicant must describe the nature of the proposed tie-up arrangement with each other agency. The applicant must also provide details of debt proposed for the transmission activity, including names of lenders, amounts to be sourced from each lender, and letters from lenders confirming their commitment to provide the specified amounts. This information demonstrates the applicant's access to necessary capital and lender support for implementing transmission projects.

DOCUMENT: GERC Licensing of Transmission Regulations 2005

HIERARCHY: Schedule-2 / Transmission Licence / Preamble and General Terms

PROVISION: Schedule-2

TITLE: Transmission Licence Form (Preamble and General Terms)

SUMMARY:
Schedule-2 provides the prescribed form for issuing a Transmission Licence under the GERC Licensing of Transmission Regulations 2005. The license is issued by GERC in exercise of powers conferred under Section 14 of the Electricity Act 2003 and Section 20 of the Gujarat Electricity Industry (Reorganisation and Regulation) Act, 2003. The license grants authority to the licensee to construct, maintain, and operate specified transmission facilities (named in the license) according to the schedule attached to the license, which is read as an integral part of the license. The license is subject to the Electricity Act, the rules, and the general conditions specified in these Regulations, including any statutory amendments, alterations, modifications, and re-enactments thereof, all read as part of the license. The general license provisions include: (1) any contradiction between license terms and agreements is resolved by the license terms as amended from time to time prevailing; (2) the license is not transferable; (3) issuance to the licensee does not restrict the Commission's right to grant licenses to other persons within the same area for transmission systems other than the project described in the license schedule, and the licensee shall not claim exclusivity; (4) the license remains in force for 25 years from issue date unless revoked earlier. The license also includes a schedule specifying project-related details including transmission elements (transmission lines, substations, etc.) and their scheduled commissioning dates, signed by the Secretary and dated.

KEY TOPICS: Transmission Licence, Authority to Construct and Operate, Electricity Act 2003, Gujarat Act 2003, License Conditions, Amendments, Contradictions, Non-Transferability, Exclusivity Disclaimer, Twenty-Five Year Duration, Project Schedule, Commissioning Dates
