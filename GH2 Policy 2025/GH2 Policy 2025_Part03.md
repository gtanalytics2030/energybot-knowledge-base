HIERARCHY: Section 9 / Incentives / Electrolysis-based Green Hydrogen Projects

PROVISION: Section 9.1 – Eligibility for Electrolysis-based Projects

TITLE: Eligibility Criteria for Electrolysis-based Green Hydrogen Projects

SUMMARY:
Electrolysis-based green hydrogen projects with electrolyser capacity from 1 to 10 MW are eligible for benefits under this section, subject to an overall cap of 500 MW electrolyser capacity. The maximum benefits available to a single beneficiary is limited to 10 MW electrolyser capacity of the project. This tiered approach encourages distributed development while capping maximum individual project size.

KEY TOPICS: Electrolysis-based Projects, Electrolyser Capacity, 1–10 MW Range, 500 MW Cap, Beneficiary Limitation, Project Eligibility, Maximum Benefits, Capacity Ceiling, Individual Project Limit

TEXT:
Projects with an electrolyser capacity of 1 to 10 MW shall be eligible for benefits under this section, subject to an overall cap of 500 MW electrolyser capacity.

Provided, the maximum benefits, under this section, which can be availed by a single beneficiary will be limited to 10 MW electrolyser capacity of the project.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Section 9 (Incentives), Section 8.2 (Project Eligibility), Section 8.2.5 (Single Incentive Claim)

PROVISION: Section 9.1.2 – Grid Charges Reimbursement

TITLE: Grid Charges Reimbursement for Green Hydrogen Production

SUMMARY:
50% reimbursement of transmission and wheeling charges is provided for renewable energy used in green hydrogen production for an initial period of five years from the date of project commissioning. Transmission capacity charges reimbursement is capped at maximum 4 MW for 1 MW electrolyser capacity. This mechanism reduces the operational cost of green hydrogen production.

KEY TOPICS: Grid Charges, Transmission Charges, Wheeling Charges, 50% Reimbursement, Five-Year Period, Renewable Energy, Production Incentive, Operational Cost Reduction, Transmission Capacity Cap

TEXT:
50% reimbursement of transmission and wheeling charges shall be provided for renewable energy used in green hydrogen production, for an initial period of five years from the date of commissioning of project. Provided that, in case of transmission charges reimbursement, maximum 4 MW transmission capacity charges would be reimbursed for 1 MW electrolyser capacity.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Section 9.1.2 (Grid Charges), Section 8.2.3 (Renewable Energy Sources)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Section 11.2 (Grid Connectivity and Charges), CERC/GERC Regulations

PROVISION: Section 9.4 – Refuelling Station Capital Subsidy

TITLE: Capital Subsidy for Hydrogen Refuelling Stations

c) Single Beneficiary can claim such subsidy up to 3 green hydrogen refueling stations.

Provided, overall incentive shall be capped up to INR 250 crore overall under this section and up to INR 50 crore per CGD operator during the Policy operative period.

Such blending of Green Hydrogen in CGD Network is subject to prevailing GoI norms.
