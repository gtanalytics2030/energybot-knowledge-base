PROVISION: Section 12.2.1 – Nodal Agency Water Infrastructure Coordination

TITLE: Nodal Agency Coordination for Water Supply to Green Hydrogen Projects

SUMMARY:
The State Nodal Agency shall coordinate with relevant state authorities in matters related to supply of water to green hydrogen projects covering: (a) facilitation of water infrastructure development; (b) mapping of water resources near potential project sites; (c) formulation of guidelines for water allocation; and (d) facilitation to obtain approval from WRD/SSNNL and relevant authorities. This centralizes water management coordination.

KEY TOPICS: Water Supply, Nodal Agency, Water Infrastructure, Water Resource Mapping, Allocation Guidelines, WRD Approval, SSNNL Coordination, Project Support, Facilitation

TEXT:
12.2.1 The State Nodal Agency shall coordinate with relevant state authorities in matters related to supply of water to green hydrogen projects covering:

a) Facilitate the development of water infrastructure.

b) Facilitate mapping of water resources near potential project sites.

c) Formulate guidelines for allocation of water from water resources.

d) Facilitate to obtain approval from WRD/SSNNL and/or relevant concerned authority.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Section 12.2 (Water for Electrolysis and Biomass), Section 15.3 (Nodal Agency)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Section 9.9 (Water Desalination Subsidy)
