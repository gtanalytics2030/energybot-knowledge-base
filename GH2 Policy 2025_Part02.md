PROVISION: Section 8.2.3 – Renewable Energy Source Options

TITLE: Renewable Energy Sourcing Options for Green Hydrogen

SUMMARY:
Projects should source renewable energy from the following five pathways: (a) captive route from co-located or differently located renewable energy projects within the state; (b) third-party mode under open access route; (c) distribution licensee; (d) power exchange; or (e) a mix of any of these options including energy storage facilities charged from the above sources. This provides flexibility in renewable energy procurement.

KEY TOPICS: Renewable Energy Source, Captive Route, Open Access, Third-Party Supply, Distribution Licensee, Power Exchange, Energy Storage, Energy Mix, Procurement Options, Flexibility

TEXT:
8.2.3 Projects should source renewable energy from following sources:

a) A captive route, from co-located and/or differently located renewable energy projects within the State

b) Third-party mode under an open access route

c) Distribution licensee

d) Power exchange

e) A mix of any of the above including energy storage facilities charged from the above sources
