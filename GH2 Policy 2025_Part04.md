HIERARCHY: Section 9 / Incentives / Water Desalination

PROVISION: Section 9.9 – Water Desalination Plant Capital Subsidy

TITLE: Capital Subsidy for Water Desalination Plants in Green Hydrogen Hubs

SUMMARY:
20% capital expenditure subsidy on eligible components is provided for the first 5 desalination plants supporting green hydrogen producers, subject to a maximum of INR 2 crore per Million Liter per Day (MLD) for plants with capacity ranging from 1 to 5 MLD. If the desalination plant is set up for multiple uses beyond green hydrogen production, the subsidy is limited to the proportion of water capacity contracted by green hydrogen producers. This incentivizes water management infrastructure in hydrogen production.

KEY TOPICS: Water Desalination, Capital Subsidy, 20% Subsidy, INR 2 Crore per MLD, 5 Desalination Plants, 1–5 MLD Capacity, Multi-Use Facility, Water Allocation, Green Hydrogen Hub, Water Infrastructure

TEXT:
20% capital expenditure subsidy (on eligible components) for first 5 desalination plants shall be provided to green hydrogen producers, subject to a maximum of INR 2 crore/MLD for plants with a capacity ranging from 1 to 5 MLD.

Provided, if the desalination plant is set up for multiple uses other than for green hydrogen production, the maximum capital expenditure considered will be limited to the portion of water capacity contracted by the green hydrogen producers. In such cases, the 20% capital expenditure subsidy will apply only to the capital cost corresponding to the contracted water capacity of the green hydrogen producers within the Green Hydrogen Hub.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Section 9.9 (Water Desalination), Section 9.3 (Green Hydrogen Hub)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Section 12.2 (Water for Electrolysis and Biomass)

PROVISION: Section 10.2 – Single Policy Benefit Limitation

TITLE: Limitation on Benefit Claim from Multiple State Policies

PROVISION: Section 10.3 – Exclusion for Waste Land Policy Beneficiaries

TITLE: Exclusion of Developers Availing Waste Land Allocation Policy Benefits

SUMMARY:
If a developer has availed or chooses to avail benefits under the Gujarat Waste Land Allocation Policy (for Green Hydrogen Production) 2023, such developer will not be eligible for benefits and incentives under this policy. KEY TOPICS: Waste Land Allocation Policy, Policy Exclusivity, Land Acquisition Route, Benefit Ineligibility, Terms Precedence, Policy Hierarchy, Mutually Exclusive Options, Developer Choice

Further, the terms and conditions of that policy shall take precedence over the Gujarat Green Hydrogen Policy-2025.

TITLE: Electricity Duty Exemption for Renewable Energy Consumption
