PROVISION: Section 15.4.3 – Capital Subsidy Disbursement Milestones

TITLE: Phased Capital Subsidy Disbursement Based on Project Milestones

SUMMARY:
Capital subsidies shall be disbursed in four equal installments (25% each) based on: (1) component installation completion; (2) final commissioning and production commencement; (3) achievement of 80% annual contracted production in year one; and (4) achievement of 90% annual contracted production in year two. Additional guidelines govern disbursement for sections 9.5, 9.6, 9.7, and 9.8 incentives.

KEY TOPICS: Capital Subsidy Disbursement, Phased Payment, Performance Milestones, Installation, Commissioning, Production Achievement, Performance-Linked, Financial Incentive, Disbursement Condition

TEXT:
15.4.3 All financial incentives in the form of capital subsidy, as specified in this policy, shall be disbursed upon achieving the following milestones, subject to compliance with the terms and conditions for project allocation under this policy:

First 25% (twenty-five percent): Upon installation of components eligible for capital subsidy.

Second 25% (twenty-five percent): Upon final commissioning of the GH plant and commencement of commercial production.

Third 25% (twenty-five percent): Upon achievement of 80% (eighty percent) of the annual contracted GH production during the first year of commercial operations.

Final 25% (twenty-five percent): Upon achieving 90% (ninety percent) of the annual contracted GH production during the second year of commercial operations.

Guidelines shall be issued for the disbursement of financial incentives under clauses 9.5, 9.6, 9.7, and 9.8 of this Policy.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Section 15.4 (Operational Guidelines), Sections 9.1–9.8 (Incentive Framework)
