DOCUMENT: GERC Guidelines for Procurement of Power by Distribution Licensees

DOCUMENT TYPE: Notification / Guideline / Regulatory Order

HIERARCHY: Guideline Provision

PROVISION: Item 10

TITLE: Draft PPA Approval Procedure – Public Notice, Objections, and Commission Decision Process

SUMMARY:
Item 10 establishes a comprehensive procedure for obtaining GERC approval of draft Power Purchase Agreements. The Distribution Licensee must submit an application with quantum of power, justification for source/technology selection, and the draft PPA document. Within 7 days of application registration, the licensee must publish a notice in at least two daily newspapers (one English, one vernacular) with wide local circulation. The public may file suggestions and objections within 30 days of publication. GERC shall issue an approval or rejection order within 90 days of receiving a complete application, after considering all public submissions, with such modifications or conditions as it deems appropriate. This provision ensures transparent and participatory PPA approval.

KEY TOPICS: Power Purchase Agreement, PPA Approval, Draft PPA, Public Notice, Objections, Public Participation, GERC Approval, Suggestions, Distribution Licensee Application, Justification, Technology Selection

TEXT:

**Item 10 – Draft PPA Approval Process**

For getting approval of draft PPA, the Distribution Licensee shall submit an application along with the details of quantum of power, justification for selecting a particular source/technology and the draft PPA document.

a. The Distribution Licensee shall, within 7 days after registration of the application, publish a notice of his application in at least two daily newspapers, one in English language and one in vernacular language, having wide circulation in the relevant area.

b. The suggestions and objections, if any, on the proposal for procurement of power and draft PPA, may be filed before Secretary, GERC, by any person within 30 days of publication of this notice with a copy to the applicant.

c. The GERC shall, within ninety (90) days from the date of registration of a complete application and after considering all suggestions and objections received from the public, issue Order approving/rejecting the proposal and draft PPA with such modifications or such conditions as may be specified in that Order.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Item 9 (Prior PPA Approval Requirement for GERC Tariff-Determined Procurement), Item 8 (Prior Approval for CERC Tariff-Determined Procurement), Item 12 (PPA Submission within 30 Days of Execution)

DOCUMENT: GERC Guidelines for Procurement of Power by Distribution Licensees

DOCUMENT TYPE: Notification / Guideline / Regulatory Order

HIERARCHY: Guideline Provision

PROVISION: Item 11

TITLE: Renewable Energy Procurement Using Model PPA and Deviation Approval

SUMMARY:
Item 11 mandates that Distribution Licensees must procure power from renewable sources of energy using long-term agreements that conform to a Model PPA to be prescribed separately by GERC. If the Distribution Licensee proposes any deviations from the Model PPA terms, prior approval from GERC must be obtained. This provision ensures standardization of renewable energy procurement agreements while maintaining flexibility for site-specific or technology-specific variations when justified and approved.

KEY TOPICS: Renewable Energy, Renewable Sources, Power Procurement, Model PPA, Long-term Agreement, Renewable Purchase Obligation, REC, Deviations, GERC Model Template

TEXT:

**Item 11 – Renewable Energy Procurement with Model PPA**

For procurement of power from renewable sources of energy, the Distribution Licensee shall enter into long-term agreement in accordance with the Model PPA to be prescribed by the GERC separately. Approval of the GERC shall be sought in the event of any deviations from the Model PPA.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Item 6 (Competitive Bidding Standard), Item 9 (GERC PPA Approval Requirement), Item 10 (Draft PPA Approval Procedure), Electricity Act 2003 (Renewable Purchase Obligation framework)

CROSS-REFERENCES: GERC Model PPA for Renewable Energy (to be prescribed separately); Related to Renewable Purchase Obligation (RPO) requirements; Related to Wind and Solar energy procurement guidelines; Item 6 (Competitive Bidding) may apply to renewable procurement depending on GERC directives

PROVISION: Item 12

TITLE: Executed PPA Submission Requirement to GERC

SUMMARY:
Item 12 establishes a mandatory post-execution compliance requirement. Within 30 days after a Distribution Licensee enters into a long-term or medium-term power purchase agreement, the licensee must submit a copy of the executed PPA to GERC. This provision ensures that GERC maintains a current record of all power purchase commitments undertaken by Distribution Licensees and can monitor compliance with regulatory requirements and tariff determinations.

KEY TOPICS: Power Purchase Agreement, PPA Submission, Executed Agreement, Distribution Licensee, Compliance, Long-term Agreement, Medium-term Agreement, GERC Filing Requirement

TEXT:

**Item 12 – PPA Submission to GERC After Execution**

The Distribution Licensee shall submit a copy of the 'power purchase agreement' within 30 days of entering into an agreement for long-term / medium-term procurement of power.
