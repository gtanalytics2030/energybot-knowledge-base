PROVISION: Item 17

TITLE: Exemption from Competitive Process in Unforeseen and Exceptional Situations – Justification and Economic Proof Requirement

SUMMARY:
Item 17 empowers GERC to permit Distribution Licensees to procure power without requiring that the purchase be subject to competitive or open processes when unforeseen and exceptional situations arise. However, this exemption comes with significant procedural safeguards: the Distribution Licensee cannot automatically benefit from exemption but must affirmatively demonstrate (a) the need and reason for departure from competitive process, (b) economic justification for the purchase, (c) the means by which, absent competition, the licensee proposes to secure the best possible terms, and (d) such other information as GERC may require. This provision balances regulatory flexibility with consumer protection by requiring robust justification for non-competitive procurement.

KEY TOPICS: Exemption, Competitive Process, Unforeseen Situation, Exceptional Situation, Non-competitive Procurement, Economic Justification, GERC Discretion, Best Terms, Burden of Proof, Regulatory Safeguards

TEXT:

**Item 17 – Exemption from Competitive Process in Unforeseen/Exceptional Situations**

The GERC may permit any Distribution Licensee to make purchase of power without requiring that such purchase be subject to Competitive/Open Process in the event of an unforeseen and an exceptional situation. However, the Distribution Licensee shall not, thereby, be exempted from demonstrating the need and the reason for departure from a competitive process together with the economic justification for the purchase, the means, whereby, in the absence of competition, the Distribution Licensee proposes to secure the best possible terms and such other information as the GERC may require.
