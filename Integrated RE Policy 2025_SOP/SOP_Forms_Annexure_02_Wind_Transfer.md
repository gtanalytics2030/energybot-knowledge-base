TITLE: Annexure-2: Required Details for Wind Power Project Transfer Permission Application
SUMMARY:
This form is for transferring wind power project ownership from an existing developer (Transferor) to a new developer (Transferee) during development phase, before commissioning. It requires details of both parties, project information, transfer agreement, financial capability assessment, and board authorizations. Essential for project sale, merger, or ownership changes.
KEY TOPICS: Transfer Permission, Wind Power, Project Ownership Change, Transferor, Transferee, Financial Assessment, Development Phase
TEXT:
ANNEXURE-2: WIND POWER PROJECT - TRANSFER PERMISSION APPLICATION
REQUIRED DETAILS & INFORMATION CHECKLIST

SECTION A: TRANSFEROR DETAILS (Original Developer/Current Owner)

Information About the Original Project Developer:

1. Transferor Identity
   - Legal name of existing project owner
   - Company registration number (if company)
   - GST registration number
   - PAN number
   - Contact person name and designation
   - Phone, email, mobile

2. Existing Project Details
   - Provisional Registration Certificate Number (from GEDA)
   - Date of Provisional Registration (DD/MM/YYYY)
   - Current approved project capacity (in MW)
   - Project location: Village, Taluka, District
   - Status of project: 
     * Development Permission - Approved
     * Development Permission - Under Processing
     * Transfer Permission - Already in process
   - Attach copy of Provisional Registration Certificate

3. Development Permission Details (if already issued)
   - Development Permission Certificate Number
   - Date of approval
   - Copy of Development Permission letter
   - Validity period of development permission

4. Transferor Authorization
   - Board Resolution from Transferor entity authorizing transfer
   - Must be certified/notarized copy
   - Date of Board meeting/resolution
   - Resolution number or reference

5. Transferor Financial Status (Provide)
   - Company balance sheet (last 2 years)
   - Any debt obligations related to the project
   - Details of project financing (if any)
   - Bank account details for refund of fees (if applicable)

SECTION B: TRANSFEREE DETAILS (New Developer/Purchaser)

Information About the New Project Developer:

1. Transferee Identity
   - Legal name of new project owner
   - Type of entity: Individual, Partnership, Private Ltd., Public Ltd., MSME, Other
   - Company registration certificate (certified copy)
   - Company incorporation date
   - GST registration number
   - PAN number
   - Contact person name and designation
   - Phone, email, mobile

2. Transferee Eligibility Verification
   - MUST meet all same eligibility criteria as original developer
   - Experience in renewable energy projects (list previous projects if any)
   - No adverse compliance record
   - No pending regulatory penalties

3. Transferee Financial Capability Assessment (CRITICAL - Must Provide All)
   - Audited Balance Sheet (last 3 years)
   - Profit & Loss Statement (last 3 years)
   - Cash flow statement (last 2 years)
   - Latest bank account statements (3-6 months recent)
   - Net worth certificate from Chartered Accountant
   - Must show positive net worth
   - Unencumbered assets certificate

4. Bank Financing Approval (if applicable)
   - Letter from lending bank/financial institution
   - Indicating willingness to finance the project
   - Approved loan amount (in Rs. Crores/Lakhs)
   - Loan tenure and repayment schedule
   - Interest rate
   - Loan conditions

5. Previous Project Experience
   - If applicable, provide details of:
     * Previous renewable energy projects developed/owned
     * Total capacity commissioned (MW)
     * Operational track record
     * Compliance record with regulatory authorities
     * Performance against committed timelines
   - Attach performance certificates/completion letters

6. Transferee Authorization
   - Board Resolution from Transferee entity accepting project transfer
   - Must be certified/notarized
   - Date of Board meeting
   - Resolution number
   - Authorization to manage/commission the project

SECTION C: TRANSFER AGREEMENT & TRANSACTION DETAILS

Transfer Agreement Documentation (Must Provide):

1. Executed Transfer Agreement
   - Complete copy of transfer/purchase agreement
   - Signed by both Transferor and Transferee
   - Include all terms and conditions:
     * Purchase price/consideration
     * Payment terms and milestone
     * Delivery/handover conditions
     * Warranty and indemnification clauses
     * Liability allocation (environmental, regulatory, financial)
     * Non-compete clauses (if any)
     * Dispute resolution mechanism
     * Termination conditions

2. Reason for Transfer (Select & Explain)
   - Company merger or acquisition
   - Business restructuring
   - Change of ownership / investor exit
   - Financial constraints
   - Technology upgrade / capacity increase
   - Strategic reasons
   - Other (specify details)

3. Project Continuity Commitment
   - Transferee confirmation that project will proceed as planned
   - Same or revised commissioning date (in DD/MM/YYYY)
   - Any changes to technical specifications (describe)
   - Confirmation of no change in PPA/wheeling terms (if applicable)
   - Timeline for project completion (milestone schedule)

SECTION D: LAND & LEGAL POSSESSION

Land Transfer Documentation:

1. Land Possession Status
   - Transferee MUST have legal possession of project land
   - Proof of legal possession in Transferee's name OR
   - Copy of executed purchase agreement for land acquisition OR
   - Copy of valid lease agreement (minimum 25-year lease period)

2. Land Lease Agreement (if applicable)
   - Copy of lease deed in Transferee's name
   - Or evidence of assignment of lease to Transferee
   - Lease period: Minimum 25 years recommended
   - Lease renewal terms
   - Lessor consent for transfer to Transferee

3. Non-Agricultural Permission (if on agricultural land)
   - NA (Non-Agricultural use) permission in Transferee's name
   - OR Deemed NA order
   - OR Bonafide Industrial Use permission
   - Approval authority and date

4. Land Documents Update
   - If land was leased to Transferor, new lease execution to Transferee
   - Lessor consent letter for transfer
   - Survey number / plot details confirmation
   - Location verification

SECTION E: PPA & GRID CONNECTIVITY AGREEMENT

Existing Agreements Handover:

1. Power Purchase Agreement (PPA)
   - Copy of existing PPA (if signed with GUVNL/DISCOM)
   - Copy of LOI (Letter of Intent) if PPA not yet signed
   - Confirmation that PPA terms remain valid post-transfer
   - Any amendments required (provide details)

2. Wheeling & Transmission Agreement
   - Copy of wheeling agreement (if applicable)
   - Copy of transmission access agreement
   - Confirmation of terms remaining valid
   - Amendments needed (if any)

3. Grid Connectivity Approval
   - GETCO/DISCOM connectivity approval (copy)
   - Technical Feasibility Report (if issued)
   - Impact assessment on grid post-transfer
   - Any new approvals required from GETCO/DISCOM/SLDC

4. DISCOM/GETCO Consent for Transfer
   - Written approval from DISCOM/GETCO
   - Confirmation that transfer does not affect connectivity approval
   - Any changed conditions post-transfer
   - Updated coordination contact details

SECTION F: EQUIPMENT & ENVIRONMENTAL COMPLIANCE

Equipment & Specifications Handover:

1. Equipment List & Status
   - Same WTG make/model/capacity as approved (unless modification)
   - ALMM/RLMM approval status of equipment
   - If equipment change proposed: Requires fresh ALMM approval

2. Environmental Compliance
   - Copy of environmental clearance (if applicable)
   - Environmental management plan
   - Any ongoing environmental obligations transfer to Transferee
   - Transferee acceptance of environmental responsibilities

3. Social & Community Engagement
   - Community engagement plan (if any)
   - Pending community obligations
   - Transferee commitment to existing agreements
   - Any social liability transfer

SECTION G: PROCESSING FEES & CHARGES

Transfer Permission Application Fee Calculation:

1. Fee Structure
   - 50% of applicable Development Permission Processing Fee
   - Development Permission Fee ranges (Section 4.4):
     * <1 MW: Rs. 10,000 → Transfer fee = Rs. 5,000
     * 1-10 MW: Rs. 15,000/MW → Transfer fee = Rs. 7,500/MW
     * >10 MW: Rs. 20,000/MW → Transfer fee = Rs. 10,000/MW

2. Example Fee Calculations
   - 5 MW project:
     * Dev Permission = 5 × Rs. 15,000 = Rs. 75,000
     * Transfer fee = 50% = Rs. 37,500
     * GST 18% = Rs. 6,750
     * Total = Rs. 44,250
   
   - 15 MW project:
     * Dev Permission = 15 × Rs. 20,000 = Rs. 3,00,000
     * Transfer fee = 50% = Rs. 1,50,000
     * GST 18% = Rs. 27,000
     * Total = Rs. 1,77,000

3. Payment Method
   - Online through RE Portal
   - Non-refundable
   - Payment receipt mandatory

SECTION H: REGULATORY & COMPLIANCE UNDERTAKINGS

Transferee Must Certify & Undertake:

1. Regulatory Compliance Undertaking
   - Adherence to all GIREP-2025 provisions
   - Compliance with all GERC regulations
   - Compliance with Grid Code requirements
   - All safety and technical standards
   - Adherence to forecasting and scheduling regulations

2. Financial Obligations Undertaking
   - Timely payment of processing fees
   - Payment of security deposits/PBG as required
   - Compliance with tariff and payment obligations

3. Project Execution Commitment
   - Commissioning within approved timeline
   - Adherence to project specifications
   - Acceptance of penalty clauses for delay
   - Quality assurance commitment

4. Equipment & Technology Undertaking
   - All equipment from ALMM/RLMM approved lists
   - Use of certified manufacturers only
   - Acceptance of performance warranties
   - Commitment to use genuine spare parts

PROCESSING TIMELINE:

- Application submission with all documents: Day 1
- Preliminary scrutiny: 5-7 days
- Financial capability review: 10-15 days
- DISCOM/GETCO coordination: 5-10 days
- Total approval time: 30-45 working days
- Transfer Permission validity: 24 months
- Extension: Possible for 12 additional months
- Commissioning must occur before validity expires

IMPORTANT RESTRICTIONS & NOTES:

⚠️ Transferee must meet ALL original eligibility criteria
⚠️ Multiple transfers may face additional scrutiny
⚠️ Financial capability assessment is MANDATORY
⚠️ Transferee with poor regulatory record may be rejected
⚠️ PPA terms must remain valid (cannot renegotiate major terms)
⚠️ DISCOM/GETCO consent is mandatory
⚠️ Land transfer documentation is mandatory
⚠️ Any equipment change requires fresh ALMM approval and new fees
