TITLE: Detailed Requirements for Solar Power Project Development Permission Application (Ground-Mounted)
SUMMARY:
This form is required for development permission of ground-mounted solar PV projects. It collects comprehensive technical specifications, site assessment data, equipment details, land information, BESS (if applicable), performance estimates, work progress tracking framework, and commissioning timeline. Development permission is mandatory before starting civil work.
KEY TOPICS: Solar Power, Development Permission, Ground-Mounted, PV Modules, Inverters, Site Assessment, Metering, Grid Connectivity, Processing Fees

SECTION I: PROCESSING FEES & CHARGES

Development Permission Application Fee:

1. Fee Structure (Exclusive of 18% GST)

   | Project Capacity | Processing Fee |
   |---|---|
   | < 1 MW | Rs. 8,000 |
   | 1-10 MW | Rs. 12,000 per MW |
   | > 10 MW | Rs. 18,000 per MW |

2. Fee Calculation Example
   - 5 MW project: 5 MW × Rs. 12,000/MW = Rs. 60,000
   - GST 18%: Rs. 60,000 × 18% = Rs. 10,800
   - Total Payable: Rs. 70,800

3. Payment Details
   - Payment mode: Online through RE Portal
   - Non-refundable
   - Payment confirmation receipt: Keep for application

PROCESSING TIMELINE & VALIDITY:

- Complete application to approval: 30-45 working days
- Incomplete applications: 60 days maximum
- Development permission validity: 24 months from issue
- Extension possibility: 12 additional months on request
- Must commission before validity expiry or reapply

KEY REQUIREMENTS:

✓ All measurements in meters, capacities in kW/MW, voltages in V
✓ All rupee amounts in Indian currency
✓ All dates in DD/MM/YYYY format
✓ Site coordinates in decimal degrees (latitude, longitude)
✓ Equipment must be from ALMM-approved lists (where applicable)
✓ All calculations backed by technical reports
✓ Photos must be geotagged with date/time
✓ Structural stability certified by licensed engineer
✓ Electrical design per IS standards & GERC guidelines
