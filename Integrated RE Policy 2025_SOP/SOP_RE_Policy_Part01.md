PROVISION: Section 1

TITLE: Preamble - Executive Procedures for RE Project Development

SUMMARY:
The preamble establishes the scope and purpose of the operational guidelines for renewable energy project development in Gujarat under GIREP-2025. It covers the development of grid-connected and off-grid RE projects including wind, solar, hybrid, and storage systems, and establishes the framework for transparent application processing while ensuring compliance with technical and grid standards.

KEY TOPICS: Renewable Energy, Project Development, BESS, Grid-connected, Off-grid, Wind, Solar, Hybrid, Compliance, Technical Standards

TEXT:
The Guidelines for Renewable Energy Project Development cover the development of Renewable Energy Projects with or without Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) in Gujarat, following the operational guidelines under the Gujarat Integrated Renewable Energy Policy - 2025.

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) establishes executive procedures for setting up grid-connected and off-grid renewable energy projects including wind power projects, solar projects, wind-solar hybrid projects, and standalone BESS projects within the state of Gujarat.

The procedures are designed to facilitate smooth and transparent processing of applications for renewable energy project development while ensuring compliance with technical standards, grid connectivity requirements, and safety regulations.

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy / Standard Operating Procedure

HIERARCHY: Section 2

PROVISION: Section 2

TITLE: Objective of the SOP

SUMMARY:
This section outlines the primary objectives of the Standard Operating Procedure, which include establishing a transparent registration and approval process, ensuring grid connectivity compliance, facilitating RE project development with or without BESS, establishing clear responsibilities and timelines, ensuring safety and environmental compliance, promoting RE investment, and enabling efficient resource utilization. The SOP aims to support Gujarat's renewable energy targets through streamlined procedures.

KEY TOPICS: SOP Objective, Project Registration, Grid Connectivity, BESS, Transparency, Compliance, RE Targets, Investment Promotion

TEXT:
The objective of this Standard Operating Procedure is to:

(a) Provide a transparent and streamlined process for registration and approval of renewable energy projects in Gujarat;

(b) Ensure grid connectivity of renewable energy projects in accordance with the provisions of the Gujarat Integrated Renewable Energy Policy-2025 and applicable technical standards;

(c) Facilitate the development of renewable energy projects with or without Battery Energy Storage System (BESS);

(d) Establish clear responsibilities and timelines for project developers and government agencies;

(e) Ensure compliance with safety standards, environmental regulations, and technical specifications;

(f) Promote investment in renewable energy and support the state's renewable energy targets;

(g) Enable efficient utilization of renewable resources and grid infrastructure in the state of Gujarat.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: GIREP-2025, Technical Standards, Safety Standards, Environmental Regulations

CROSS-REFERENCES: Definitions (Section 3), General Provisions (Section 4), All Procedural Sections
