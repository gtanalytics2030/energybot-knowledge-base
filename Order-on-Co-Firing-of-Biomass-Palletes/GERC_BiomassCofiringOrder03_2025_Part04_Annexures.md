## CHUNK 1: Annexure I - Stakeholders Who Communicated Views on Approach Paper

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Order No. 03 of 2025 - Methodology for Co-firing of Biomass Pellets and Recovery of Its Costs

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulatory Order - Administrative Reference Material

**HIERARCHY:** Annexure I

**PROVISION:** Annexure I - List of Stakeholders Communicated Views on Approach Paper

**TITLE:** Complete List of Stakeholders Providing Comments on Approach Paper

**SUMMARY:**
Annexure I provides the official list of stakeholders who communicated their views and comments on the Approach Paper for Methodology for Co-firing of Biomass Pellets and Recovery of Its Costs in the Fixed and Variable Charges / ECR in Biomass Co-fired Thermal Power Plants. Five major stakeholders participated in the consultation process: Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Limited (GUVNL), a state-owned entity responsible for procurement and distribution of power in Gujarat; Gujarat State Electricity Corporation Limited (GSECL), the state's thermal and hydro power generator; Reliance Industries Limited (RIL), a major private industrial conglomerate with power generation interests; Abellon Clean Energy Limited, a renewable and biomass energy company; and DCM Shriram Limited, an industrial group with biomass-related operations. The presence of both utility companies (GUVNL, GSECL), private generators (RIL), renewable energy developers (Abellon), and industrial entities reflects the diverse stakeholder spectrum engaged in the regulatory consultation process.

## CHUNK 2: Annexure II - Stakeholders Who Attended Public Hearing

**DOCUMENT:** GERC Order No. 03 of 2025 - Methodology for Co-firing of Biomass Pellets and Recovery of Its Costs

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulatory Order - Administrative Reference Material

**HIERARCHY:** Annexure II

**PROVISION:** Annexure II - List of Stakeholders Who Attended Public Hearing and Submitted Views

**TITLE:** Complete List of Stakeholders Presenting at Public Hearing on 25 October 2024

**SUMMARY:**
Annexure II provides the official list of stakeholders who attended the public hearing held on 25 October 2024 at the Commission's office in Gandhinagar and presented their objections, suggestions, and views on the Approach Paper for Methodology for Co-firing of Biomass Pellets and Recovery of Its Costs. All five stakeholders who had previously communicated written views also participated in the oral hearing process, ensuring comprehensive stakeholder engagement. The stakeholders are: Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Limited (GUVNL), as the state's power procurement and distribution entity representing consumer/beneficiary interests; Gujarat State Electricity Corporation Limited (GSECL), representing state-owned generation capacity; Reliance Industries Limited (RIL), representing private sector thermal and power generation interests; Abellon Clean Energy Limited, representing renewable and biomass energy sector interests; and DCM Shriram Limited, representing industrial stakeholders. The participation of all identified stakeholders in the public hearing demonstrates their active engagement in the regulatory consultation process and provides the Commission with comprehensive stakeholder feedback for formulating the final methodology.
