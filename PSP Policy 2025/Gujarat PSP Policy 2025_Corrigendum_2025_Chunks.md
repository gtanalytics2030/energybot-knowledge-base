SUMMARY:
This corrigendum, issued on 30 January 2026, corrects Clause 2.2 of the Gujarat Pump Storage Policy 2025 (notified vide Government Resolution dated 24.12.2025). The amendment modifies the reference point for the policy's ten-year operational period from "the date of notification in the State Gazette" to "the date of issuance of this policy". Additionally, the replacement text changes "until a new Policy is notified" to "until a new policy is published". This correction clarifies the policy's commencement and termination provisions.

KEY TOPICS: Pump Storage Policy, Policy Validity Period, Implementation Period, Clause 2.2, Policy Issuance, State Gazette Notification, Policy Termination, Corrigendum Amendment

TEXT:
**CORRIGENDUM**

In Department's resolution of the even number dated 24.12.2025 referred above, "This Policy shall remain in operation for a period of ten years from the date of notification in the State Gazette or until a new Policy is notified" in the clause 2.2 shall be replaced by "This Policy shall remain in operation for a period of ten years from the date of issuance of this policy or until a new policy is published".
