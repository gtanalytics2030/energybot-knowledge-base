## CHUNK 11: Policy Title and Validity

---
DOCUMENT: Gujarat Pumped Storage Project Policy 2025

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 2 / Title and Validity

PROVISION: Sections 2.1 to 2.3

TITLE: Policy Title, Duration, and Applicability

SUMMARY:
The policy is formally titled "Gujarat Pumped Storage Project Policy-2025". It shall remain in operation for a period of ten years from the date of notification in the State Gazette, or until a new policy is notified, whichever occurs first. The policy applies to all pumped storage projects (PSPs) installed and commissioned within the state during its operational period. Benefits and incentives declared under the policy are available to PSPs for a period of 40 years from their commissioning date or for the lifespan of the project, whichever is earlier.

KEY TOPICS: Policy Title, Operational Period, Ten Years, State Gazette Notification, Policy Applicability, Benefit Duration, 40-Year Incentive Period, Project Lifespan

TEXT:
2.1 This Policy shall be known as "Gujarat Pumped Storage Project Policy-2025"

2.2 The Policy shall remain in operation for a period of ten years from the date of notification in the State Gazette or until a new Policy is notified.

2.3 This Policy shall be applicable to all the PSPs in the State.

2.4 The PSPs installed and commissioned during the Policy's operating period shall become eligible for the benefits and incentives declared under this Policy, for a period of 40 years from their date of commissioning or the life span of PSPs, whichever is earlier.

2.5 Energy & Petrochemicals Department (EPD), GoG may amend / modify / extend / review this Policy as and when required.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: State Gazette notification procedures; benefit eligibility framework

## CHUNK 12: Objectives - PSP Development and Grid Integration

---
DOCUMENT: Gujarat Pumped Storage Project Policy 2025

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 5 / Objectives, Goals and Strategy / Subsection 5.1

PROVISION: Section 5.1(a) - Objectives for PSP Development and Grid Integration

TITLE: Objectives for Pumped Storage Development and Grid Integration

SUMMARY:
The first major objective category establishes goals related to PSP infrastructure development and grid integration. These include: harnessing available PSP potential; developing adequate storage capacity aligned with the Resource Adequacy Plan; facilitating fulfillment of Energy Storage Obligation (ESO) and Renewable Purchase Obligation (RPO) targets; and mitigating grid integration challenges arising from large-scale renewable energy penetration. These objectives frame PSP development as both a resource management and grid stability imperative.

KEY TOPICS: PSP Development, Grid Integration, Resource Adequacy, Storage Capacity, Energy Storage Obligation, Renewable Purchase Obligation, Grid Challenges, Renewable Penetration, Potential Harnessing, Variability Mitigation

TEXT:
5.1 Objectives

a) PSP Development & Grid Integration
• To harness the PSP potential available.
• To develop adequate storage capacity to align with the Resource Adequacy Plan.
• To facilitate the fulfillment of Energy Storage Obligation and Renewable Purchase Obligation targets.
• To mitigate the grid integration challenges arising out of large-scale RE penetration.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Resource Adequacy Plan framework; ESO and RPO regulations by GERC/CERC

CROSS-REFERENCES: Connected to grid code requirements; relates to SLDC operational mandates; aligns with renewable energy policy targets

## CHUNK 16: Goals - Capacity and Investment Targets

---
DOCUMENT: Gujarat Pumped Storage Project Policy 2025

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 5 / Objectives, Goals and Strategy / Subsection 5.2

PROVISION: Section 5.2 - Goals

TITLE: Quantified Goals for PSP Capacity and Investment

SUMMARY:
The policy establishes two time-bound, quantified goals: First, establish 75 pumped storage project units with attraction of ₹75,000 crore investment by 2035, coinciding with the state's 75th year of statehood (symbolic alignment). Second, establish 100 PSPs by 2047, aligned with the Viksit Bharat @2047 vision. These goals operationalize the mission statement with specific capacity and financial targets, providing measurable progress benchmarks.

## CHUNK 18: Administrative Mechanism - Nodal Agency Role

---
DOCUMENT: Gujarat Pumped Storage Project Policy 2025

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 6 / Administrative Mechanism

PROVISION: Section 6.1 - Nodal Agency

TITLE: Nodal Agency Designation and Responsibilities

SUMMARY:
The policy designates two entities as Nodal Agencies responsible for implementation. Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Limited (GUVNL) serves as the Nodal Agency for Category I and II pumped storage project sites. Gujarat Power Corporation Limited (GPCL) is designated as the Nodal Agency for Category III and IV projects. The roles and responsibilities of Nodal Agencies are outlined in separate Implementation Guidelines to be issued by EPD. The Nodal Agencies function as the primary administrative interface between project developers, the state government, and other regulatory bodies.

KEY TOPICS: Nodal Agency, GUVNL, GPCL, Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Limited, Gujarat Power Corporation Limited, Category I, Category II, Category III, Category IV, Implementation Responsibility, Administrative Role

TEXT:
6.1 Nodal Agency

a) Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Limited (GUVNL) shall be the Nodal Agency for the implementation of this Policy in respect of Category I and II PSP sites, as defined in Clause-7 of this Policy. Gujarat Power Corporation Limited (GPCL) is designated as the nodal agency for Category III and IV projects.

b) Roles and responsibilities of the Nodal Agency shall be outlined in the Implementation Guidelines.

PROVISION: Section 6.2(a) - Apex Committee

TITLE: Apex Committee for PSP Site Allocation and Approval

SUMMARY:
The administrative structure includes a three-level committee system. The Apex Committee holds the highest authority and serves to review and approve proposals submitted by the Nodal Agency. The Committee reviews proposals after due scrutiny and recommendation by the State Level Executive Committee. The Apex Committee then makes direct recommendations to EPD and the Government of Gujarat for final allocation decision. The Apex Committee composition is detailed in Annexure-II. This committee structure ensures high-level government scrutiny and approval for PSP site allocation decisions.

## CHUNK 20: Administrative Mechanism - State Level Executive Committee

---
DOCUMENT: Gujarat Pumped Storage Project Policy 2025

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 6 / Administrative Mechanism / Subsection 6.2

PROVISION: Section 6.2(b) - State Level Executive Committee

TITLE: State Level Executive Committee for PSP Site Review and Recommendation

SUMMARY:
The State Level Executive Committee serves as the intermediate review authority between Nodal Agencies and the Apex Committee. This committee reviews proposals submitted by the Nodal Agency for PSP site allotment and recommends them to the Apex Committee for final allocation. The committee also has purview over periodic review of project issues, extensions of project completion timelines, project cancellation decisions, and other matters assigned by the Apex Committee. The committee may appoint sub-committees for technical assistance or assign specific tasks as necessary. The State Level Executive Committee composition is specified in Annexure-II, including representatives from multiple government departments, agencies, and industry bodies.

KEY TOPICS: State Level Executive Committee, Proposal Review, Nodal Agency Coordination, Apex Committee Recommendation, Periodic Review, Project Extension, Project Cancellation, Sub-committee Authority, Technical Assistance, Committee Composition

TEXT:
b) State Level Executive Committee will review proposals submitted by the Nodal Agency for allotment of PSP sites and recommend Apex Committee for allocation. Periodic review of the project, issues related to extension of the project completion timeline, cancellation of the allocated PSP site and such other matters as assigned by the Apex Committee will be in the purview of this committee. It may appoint or constitute a sub-committee for technical assistance or to perform any of the tasks assigned to it, if deemed necessary.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Annexure-II (Committee Members); Section 7 (Categorization and Allotment procedures)

---

HIERARCHY: Section 6 / Administrative Mechanism / Subsection 6.2

PROVISION: Section 6.2(c) - District Level Implementation Committee

TITLE: District Level Implementation Committee for PSP Project Facilitation

SUMMARY:
The District Level Implementation Committee operates at the local/district level and facilitates operational aspects of PSP implementation. Responsibilities include facilitating land acquisition processes, water allotment coordination, Research and Development (R&R) activities of project proponents, and cooperation with project developers to accelerate project implementation. The committee comprises district-level officials representing multiple departments (collector, development officer, forest officer, water resources) as well as nominated members from line departments and designated Nodal Agency representatives. This committee structure ensures localized coordination and support for project development activities.
