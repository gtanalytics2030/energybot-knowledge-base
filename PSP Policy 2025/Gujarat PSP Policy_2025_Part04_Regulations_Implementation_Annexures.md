## CHUNK 37: Regulatory Coordination Between Policy and GERC

---
DOCUMENT: Gujarat Pumped Storage Project Policy 2025

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 9 / Regulations

PROVISION: Section 9 - Regulations

TITLE: Coordination Between PSP Policy and GERC Regulatory Framework

SUMMARY:
The policy establishes that the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (GERC) may be guided by this policy and/or implementation guidelines when framing its rules, regulations, and orders related to PSPs. However, the provision maintains GERC's regulatory primacy by establishing a conflict-resolution hierarchy: unless the Government specifies otherwise, in case of conflict or mismatch between GERC regulations and this policy/implementation guidelines, GERC regulatory provisions shall prevail. This ensures that PSP policy operates within the established regulatory framework while preventing policy provisions from inadvertently constraining regulatory authority.

KEY TOPICS: GERC, Regulatory Coordination, Policy Guidance, Regulatory Authority, Conflict Resolution, Regulatory Primacy, GERC Regulations, Grid Code, Tariff Framework

TEXT:
9. Regulations

The Hon'ble Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (GERC) may be guided by this Policy and/or Implementation Guidelines while framing its rules, regulations and orders. However, unless specifically provided otherwise by the Government, in case of any conflict or mismatch between GERC regulations framed by GERC and provisions of this Policy and/or Implementation Guidelines, it is unambiguously stated that regulatory provisions framed by GERC shall prevail.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: GERC Act; GERC Regulations (Supply Code, Grid Code, Tariff Regulations, Scheduling and DSM Regulations)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Related to all provisions requiring regulatory compliance; connected to implementation through GERC-issued procedures; ties to tariff framework determination

## CHUNK 38: Removal of Difficulties and Clarification Authority

---
DOCUMENT: Gujarat Pumped Storage Project Policy 2025

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 10 / Removal of Difficulties

PROVISION: Section 10.1 and 10.2 - Removal of Difficulties

TITLE: Government Authority to Resolve Implementation Difficulties and Issue Clarifications

SUMMARY:
The Energy and Petrochemicals Department (EPD) of the Government of Gujarat is vested with authority to remove any difficulties arising in giving effect to this policy or implementation guidelines. EPD may issue clarifications or interpretations to resolve such difficulties. Additionally, EPD may amend, review, relax, or interpret any provision of the policy or implementation guidelines as and when required. If any difficulty arises in implementing a provision, EPD shall issue clarifications and interpretations as necessary and expedient. This provision provides administrative flexibility to address unforeseen implementation challenges while maintaining centralized government control over policy interpretation.

KEY TOPICS: Removal of Difficulties, Clarification Authority, Amendment Power, Interpretation Power, Implementation Flexibility, Government Discretion, Administrative Authority, Policy Adaptation

TEXT:
10. Removal of Difficulties

10.1 The EPD, GoG shall have the right to remove any difficulties arising in giving effect to this Policy and/or Implementation Guidelines and may issue clarification or interpretation to remove such difficulties.

10.2 The EPD, GoG shall amend, review, relax or interpret any of the provisions under this Policy and/or Implementation Guidelines as and when required. If any difficulty arises in giving effect to any provision of this Policy and/or Implementation Guidelines, the EPD, GoG shall issue clarifications and interpretations to such provisions of the Policy and/or Implementation Guidelines as may appear to be necessary and expedient for removing the difficulties.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Implementation Guidelines framework

CROSS-REFERENCES: Related to Right to Interpret in Section 11; connected to Interim Review in Section 12; ties to all substantive policy provisions

## CHUNK 39: Right to Interpret - Final Interpretive Authority

---
DOCUMENT: Gujarat Pumped Storage Project Policy 2025

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 11 / Right to Interpret

PROVISION: Section 11.1 to 11.3 - Right to Interpret

TITLE: Government's Final Interpretive Authority and Policy Synchronization Requirements

SUMMARY:
If any ambiguity or dispute arises regarding the meaning, intent, or purpose of any provision in this policy or implementation guidelines, the interpretations issued by EPD, Government of Gujarat, are final and binding on all parties. All provisions of the policy must be read, interpreted, construed, and implemented in harmony and synchronization with relevant guidelines issued pursuant to the policy. Detailed implementation guidelines may be issued separately by EPD/Government. This provision concentrates interpretive authority with government while requiring that all provisions work in coordinated fashion, preventing fragmented or contradictory application.

KEY TOPICS: Right to Interpret, Final Authority, Government Interpretation, Binding Interpretation, Policy Synchronization, Guidelines Coordination, Dispute Resolution, Interpretive Primacy

TEXT:
11. Right to Interpret

11.1 If there is any ambiguity or dispute about the meaning, intent or purpose of any provision of this Policy and/or Implementation Guidelines, the interpretations of the EPD, GoG shall be final and binding.

11.2 Provisions of this Policy shall be read, interpreted, construed and implemented in harmony and in synchronization with the provisions detailed in relevant and necessary guidelines issued pursuant to this Policy, if any.

11.3 Detailed guidelines for implementing this Policy may be issued separately by the EPD, GoG.

## CHUNK 40: Interim Review and Policy Evolution

---
DOCUMENT: Gujarat Pumped Storage Project Policy 2025

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 12 / Interim Review

PROVISION: Section 12 - Interim Review

TITLE: Interim Review Mechanism for Policy Assessment and Adaptation

SUMMARY:
The Energy and Petrochemicals Department may undertake an interim review of this policy and/or implementation guidelines as and when required, either in view of any technological breakthroughs in PSP technology or to remove any inconsistency with the Electricity Act 2003, associated rules, regulations, or other Government of India policies. This provision recognizes that energy technology and regulatory frameworks evolve, and creates a mechanism for the policy to adapt without requiring a complete repeal. The interim review option preserves policy flexibility while maintaining continuity of strategic direction.

KEY TOPICS: Interim Review, Policy Assessment, Technological Breakthrough, Regulatory Alignment, Electricity Act Consistency, Government of India Policy Coordination, Policy Evolution, Adaptive Governance

TEXT:
12. Interim Review

EPD, GoG may undertake an interim review of this Policy and/or Implementation Guidelines as and when required, either in view of any technological breakthroughs or to remove any inconsistency with the Electricity Act 2003 or rules and regulations made thereunder or any other Government of India Policy.

## CHUNK 41: Repeal of Previous PSP Policies

---
DOCUMENT: Gujarat Pumped Storage Project Policy 2025

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 13 / Repeal and Savings

PROVISION: Section 13.1 - Repeal

TITLE: Repeal of Previous PSP Policies and Related Provisions

SUMMARY:
All provisions pertaining to pumped storage project development in Gujarat under any previous State Policy and amendments thereto are hereby repealed, except for PSP-related provisions in other Gujarat policies that remain in effect. This repeal ensures a single, unified PSP policy framework without conflicting or overlapping requirements. The selective repeal allows other state policies (e.g., renewable energy policy) to continue operating where they do not directly conflict with PSP-specific provisions. This provision creates a clean regulatory slate for PSP development under this new 2025 policy.

KEY TOPICS: Repeal, Previous Policies, PSP-Specific Provisions, Policy Unification, Regulatory Clarity, Related Provisions, Amended Policies

TEXT:
13. Repeal and Savings

13.1 Save as otherwise provided in this Policy, all provisions pertaining to the development of PSPs in Gujarat under any State Policy and all amendments thereunder (if applicable) are hereby repealed. For clarification, only the PSP related provisions of any Policy of Gujarat State shall be repealed, while the other provisions of such Policy shall remain with full effect.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Previous PSP policy provisions (repealed); continuing provisions of other state policies

CROSS-REFERENCES: Related to policy validity in Section 2; connected to transitional arrangements in Section 13.2; ties to policy framework unification

## CHUNK 42: Savings Clause for Pre-Existing Actions and Continuity

---
DOCUMENT: Gujarat Pumped Storage Project Policy 2025

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 13 / Repeal and Savings

PROVISION: Section 13.2 - Savings

TITLE: Savings Clause for Continuity of Pre-Policy Actions and Transitional Arrangements

SUMMARY:
Notwithstanding the repeal of previous PSP provisions, any action taken or purported to be taken under previous policies or provisions is deemed to have been done under the corresponding provisions of this new policy to facilitate smooth and justifiable transition. This savings clause prevents disruption to projects that may have been initiated under previous policy frameworks. All such actions are treated as if executed under this policy's provisions, ensuring legal continuity and preventing technical invalidity of prior administrative decisions. The provision applies with approval of the State Government and concurrence of the Revenue and Finance Departments, ensuring proper coordination across government entities.

KEY TOPICS: Savings Clause, Continuity, Transitional Arrangements, Pre-Policy Actions, Legal Continuity, Action Validation, Administrative Coordination, Smooth Transition

TEXT:
13.2 Notwithstanding such repeal, anything done, or any action taken or purported to have been done or taken under the Policies or provisions repealed above shall, insofar as it is not inconsistent with the provisions of this Policy, be deemed to have been done or taken under the relevant/ corresponding provisions of this Policy to facilitate smooth and justifiable transition.

This issues with the approval of the State Government and the concurrence of the Revenue Department and Finance Department on the Department's file of even number.
