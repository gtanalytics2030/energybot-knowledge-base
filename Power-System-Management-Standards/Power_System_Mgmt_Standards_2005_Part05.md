## CHUNK 1: SECTION-3.9 - ISLANDING SCHEME - SYSTEM SEPARATION FOR EMERGENCY RESTORATION

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Gujarat Power System Management Standards) 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Standard

**HIERARCHY:** Document → Section-3 Transmission System Management Standards → Subsection 3.9 Islanding Scheme

**PROVISION:** Section 3.9

**TITLE:** Islanding Scheme – Development of System Separation Plans for Emergency Recovery

**SUMMARY:** To avoid total grid blackout during system disturbances and enable early normalization, islanding schemes for major generating stations and portions of the transmission system shall be developed by SLDC in consultation with WRLDC, STU, generating stations, and distribution licensees. Islanding schemes create operational flexibility to compartmentalize the grid during severe disturbances, preserve generation capacity in separated zones, and facilitate staged system restoration without cascading failures.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- System Disturbances; severe operational events
- Islanding Objectives; prevent total blackout, enable rapid restoration
- Major Generating Stations; isolation capability
- Transmission System Sections; compartmentalization planning
- SLDC Coordination; scheme development and management
- WRLDC Consultation; inter-regional coordination
- STU Participation; transmission system technical input
- Generating Station Input; capacity and capability assessment
- Distribution Licensee Input; load restoration sequence planning

**TEXT:**

**3.9 ISLANDING SCHEME**

3.9.1 To avoid total blacking out of the grid during system disturbances and for early normalisation, islanding schemes for major Generating Stations and part/parts of the Transmission System shall be developed by SLDC in consultation with WRLDC, STU, Generating Stations and Distribution Licensees.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Section 2.4.4 (Frequency Management – high-frequency islanding)
- Section 2.4.7 (Frequency Management – low-frequency islanding)
- Section 3.3 (SLDC load despatch functions)
- Section 3.10 (Post Disturbance Analysis)
- Section 4.7.1 (Distribution Licensee restoration procedures)
- Grid Code (system restoration procedures)

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:** Islanding is a planned separation strategy, distinct from emergency shutdown. Well-designed islanding schemes can preserve generation and enable faster restoration than full blackout scenarios.

## CHUNK 2: SECTION-3.10 - POST DISTURBANCE ANALYSIS - MAJOR EVENT INVESTIGATION AND CORRECTIVE RECOMMENDATIONS

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Gujarat Power System Management Standards) 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Standard

**HIERARCHY:** Document → Section-3 Transmission System Management Standards → Subsection 3.10 Post Disturbance Analysis

**PROVISION:** Section 3.10

**TITLE:** Post Disturbance Analysis – Investigation of Major Grid Disturbances and Implementation of Corrective Measures

**SUMMARY:** All major grid disturbances causing (1) tripping of generating units 110 MW and above, (2) tripping of transmission lines 220 kV and above causing full/partial system blackout, (3) breakdown of transmission lines causing prolonged interruption, or (4) load restrictions shall be promptly discussed in the Grid Code Review Panel. If required, a sub-committee may be formed immediately after occurrence for further investigation. Disturbance reports and recommendations compiled by such committees shall be circulated to all Grid Code Review Panel members for implementation. This process ensures systematic analysis, lesson-learning, and operational improvement across the transmission system.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Major Disturbances; triggering thresholds
- Generating Unit Tripping; 110 MW threshold
- Transmission Line Tripping; 220 kV and above, blackout-causing
- System Blackout; full or partial failure
- Prolonged Interruption; line breakdown impacts
- Load Restrictions; supply curtailment events
- Grid Code Review Panel; multi-stakeholder forum
- Sub-Committee Investigation; ad-hoc analysis
- Report and Recommendations; formal documentation
- Implementation; panel-wide corrective action

**TEXT:**

**3.10 POST DISTURBANCE ANALYSIS**

3.10.1 All major grid disturbances causing tripping of generating units (110 MW and above), tripping of Transmission lines (220KV and above) causing full/partial system blackout, breakdown of Transmission lines causing prolonged interruption and load restrictions may be promptly discussed in the Grid Code Review Panel. Additionally a sub-committee that may be formed if required by the Panel for further investigation , immediately after the occurrence. Disturbance reports and recommendations made in such meetings shall be compiled and circulated to all members of the Grid Code review Panel for implementation.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Section 3.11 (Event Reporting)
- Section 3.3 (SLDC load despatch functions – adequacy studies)
- Grid Code (Grid Code Review Panel procedures)

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:** The 110 MW and 220 kV thresholds define "major disturbances" triggering mandatory panel analysis. Systematic post-disturbance review is essential for continuous operational improvement.

## CHUNK 3: SECTION-3.11 - EVENT REPORTING - ABNORMAL OCCURRENCE MONITORING AND REPORTING PROCEDURES

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Gujarat Power System Management Standards) 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Standard

**HIERARCHY:** Document → Section-3 Transmission System Management Standards → Subsection 3.11 Event Reporting

**PROVISION:** Section 3.11

**TITLE:** Event Reporting – Monitoring, Verbal Notification, Preliminary and Detailed Reporting Procedures

**SUMMARY:** STU/Transmission Licensee/SLDC shall monitor all abnormal occurrences or events affecting power system operation as outlined in Grid Code section "Operational event and Incident/Accident reporting." Within 10 minutes of occurrence, transmission licensee shall make verbal/telephonic communication to SLDC's shift officer, who has authority to direct follow-up action as deemed appropriate. The shift officer must prioritize system safeguarding before initiating reporting procedures. Within one hour of restoration of normalcy, a preliminary report shall be prepared in standardized form by the transmission licensee's designated officer in consultation with relevant agencies and communicated to designated officers of concerned agencies as decided by SLDC. Within 48 hours, a detailed report in standardized form shall be prepared and communicated to designated officers of concerned agencies.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Abnormal Occurrences; operational events monitoring
- Incident Reporting; structured communication procedures
- Immediate Notification; 10-minute verbal reporting
- Shift Officer; SLDC authority and responsibility
- System Safeguarding; priority over reporting
- Preliminary Report; 1-hour post-restoration deadline
- Detailed Report; 48-hour deadline
- Standardized Form; consistent documentation
- Multi-Agency Coordination; stakeholder notification

**TEXT:**

**3.11 EVENT REPORTING**

3.11.1 The STU/Transmission Licensee/SLDC shall monitor all abnormal occurrences or events affecting the operation of the system as outlined in the Grid Code in section "Operational event and Incident/Accident reporting".

3.11.2 The Transmission Licensee shall ensure that within 10 minutes of an occurrence, verbal/telephonic communication shall be made by its to a designated officer to the officer in charge of the shift of SLDC who shall have authority to ask for follow up action as deemed fit. The shift officer at SLDC must, however, give utmost priority in safeguarding the system before initiating the reporting procedure.

3.11.3 Within one hour of restoration of normalcy, a preliminary report shall be prepared in a form (to be standardised by the STU) by the designated officer of the Transmission Licensee in consultation with various agencies and communicated to the designated officers of the concerned agencies. (as may be decided by the SLDC)

3.11.4 Within 48 hours, a detailed report shall be prepared in a form (to be standardised by the STU) and communicated to the designated officer/officers of concerned agencies. (as may be decided by the SLDC)

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Grid Code (Operational event and Incident/Accident reporting section)
- Section 3.10 (Post Disturbance Analysis)
- Section 3.3 (SLDC load despatch functions)

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:** Event reporting has strict timelines: 10-minute verbal, 1-hour preliminary, 48-hour detailed. System safeguarding takes priority over reporting bureaucracy.

## CHUNK 8: SECTION-3.16 - SAFETY COORDINATION - CONSTRUCTION, OPERATION, AND MAINTENANCE SAFETY STANDARDS

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Gujarat Power System Management Standards) 2005

**PROVISION:** Section 3.16

**TITLE:** Safety Coordination – IE Rules Compliance and Control Person Designation for Safety Procedures

**SUMMARY:** STU/Transmission Licensee shall observe general safety requirements for construction, installation, protection, operation, and maintenance of electricity supply lines and apparatus as laid down in IE Rules 1956 (in force at the time) or subsequently replaced by CEA technical standards for construction of electrical plants, electric lines, and grid connectivity per Section 73(b) of the Electricity Act, 2003. Transmission Licensee shall designate suitable Control Persons per Grid Code specifications to coordinate safety procedures before, during, and after work is undertaken, both within transmission system boundaries and across working areas between transmission systems and user systems. Transmission Licensee shall develop its own safety manual for safety coordination purposes.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Safety Compliance; IE Rules 1956 and CEA standards
- Construction Safety; line and plant installation requirements
- Operation Safety; live system operation procedures
- Maintenance Safety; work authorization and protection
- Control Persons; designated safety coordinators

- System Reenergization; system restoration after work
- Inter-System Coordination; user area safety coordination
- Safety Manual; organizational safety documentation

**TEXT:**

**3.16 SAFETY CO-ORDINATION**

3.16.1 The STU/ Transmission Licensee shall observe the general safety requirements for construction; installation, protection, operation and maintenance of electricity supply lines and apparatus as laid down in IE Rules 1956, which are in force for time being and as may be subsequently replaced by technical standards for construction of electrical plants, electrical lines and connectivity to the grid formulated by CEA as specified under Section 73(b) of Electricity Act, 2003.

3.16.2 The STU/ Transmission Licensee shall designate suitable Control Persons as specified in Grid Code for co-ordination of safety procedures before work is taken up, during work and after work is completed till the concerned system component is energised, both inside its own Transmission System and across working area between its Transmission System and that of any User.

3.16.3 The STU/ Transmission Licensee shall develop its own safety manual for the purpose of safety co-ordination.

- Grid Code (Control Person specifications and procedures)
- Section 4.9 (Distribution Licensee safety coordination)

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:** Safety coordination is dual-level: IE Rules provide baseline; CEA technical standards provide current requirements. Control Persons are key to operational safety discipline.

**3.23 INSPECTION OF LINES AND SUBSTATIONS**

3.23.1 The STU/ Transmission Licensee shall carry out periodical inspection of all lines and sub-stations of its transmission system, to ensure that preventive maintenance of lines and substations is carried out according to preventive maintenance schedules and maintain appropriate records.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
