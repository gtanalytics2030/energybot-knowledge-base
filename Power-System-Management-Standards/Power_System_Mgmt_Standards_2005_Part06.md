## CHUNK 1: ANNEXURE-1 - INTRODUCTION AND SCOPE - DATA SPECIFICATIONS FOR TRANSMISSION SYSTEM OPERATIONS

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Gujarat Power System Management Standards) 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Standard / Technical Appendix

**HIERARCHY:** Document → Section-3 Transmission System Management Standards → Section 3.24.2 ANNEXURE-1

**PROVISION:** Section 3.24.2 (Annexure-1)

**TITLE:** Annexure-1 Introduction – Data for Operation and Studies with Reference to Clause 3.2

**SUMMARY:** Annexure-1 specifies comprehensive data requirements for transmission system operation and studies referenced in Clause 3.2 (Data Management and System Studies). All data shall be expressed on 100 MVA base for standardization. The data inventory covers line parameters, transformer specifications, bus data, generating unit characteristics, demand patterns, and real-time operational data necessary for load flow studies, stability analysis, and real-time monitoring. This technical appendix operationalizes the data management requirements of Section 3.2.

- Generator Data; machine parameters and control systems

- SCADA Integration; system data acquisition

## CHUNK 2: ANNEXURE-1 - LINE DATA SPECIFICATIONS (132 KV AND ABOVE)

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Gujarat Power System Management Standards) 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Standard / Technical Appendix

**HIERARCHY:** Document → Section-3 Annexure-1 → Subsection 1 Line Data

**PROVISION:** Annexure-1, Line Data Specifications (Items 1a through 1z)

**TITLE:** Annexure-1 Line Data – Transmission Line Parameters (132 kV and Above)

**SUMMARY:** Complete line data for all transmission lines at 132 kV and above shall include: bus identification numbers and names (from and to), voltage level and circuit count, line length in kilometers, conductor specification (size and number per phase), positive and zero sequence electrical parameters (resistance, reactance, susceptance per km), line current capacity under specified ambient and temperature-rise conditions, any line reactors with MVAR capacity at both ends, year of commissioning, number of suspension/tension locations, joint locations and counts, and all crossings (railway, road, express/highway, canal, river, EHT line) with location identifiers. This data enables system studies for load flow, stability, and fault analysis.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Bus Identification; From/To Bus numbers and names
- Voltage Class; 132 kV and above lines
- Circuit Configuration; single or multiple circuits
- Line Length; kilometers per section
- Conductor Specification; size and number of conductors
- Electrical Parameters; R, X, B (positive and zero sequence)
- Current Capacity; thermal rating under specified conditions
- Line Reactors; MVAR capacity at line terminals
- Commissioning Date; year of operation commencement
- Physical Crossings; railway, road, highway, canal, river, EHT
- Geographic Details; crossing locations and suspension points
- Physical Integrity; joint locations and count

## CHUNK 3: ANNEXURE-1 - TRANSFORMER DATA SPECIFICATIONS (400/220 KV AND 220/132 KV AUTO-TRANSFORMERS AND GENERATOR TRANSFORMERS)

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Gujarat Power System Management Standards) 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Standard / Technical Appendix

**HIERARCHY:** Document → Section-3 Annexure-1 → Subsection 2 Transformer Data

**PROVISION:** Annexure-1, Transformer Data Specifications (Items 2a through 2.1h)

**TITLE:** Annexure-1 Transformer Data – Power Transformer Parameters and Three-Winding Transformer Details

**SUMMARY:** Complete transformer data for all 400/220 kV and 220/132 kV auto-transformers and generator transformers shall include: from/to bus identification and voltage levels, number of transformers, impedance (resistance and reactance) in per-unit, tap settings (nominal and range with step size), transformer type and vector group, MVA rating, and commissioning year. For three-winding transformers, additional data shall include: impedance between each winding pair (HV-MV, MV-LV, HV-LV), voltage levels on all three windings, tertiary loading capacity (if applicable), grounding impedance (if neutral grounded), tan delta (loss angle), and FRA (Frequency Response Analysis) data. This data enables accurate power flow modeling and stability studies.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Transformer Identification; bus numbers and names
- Voltage Levels; HV, MV, LV winding voltages
- Transformer Count; number of units at each location
- Electrical Parameters; R, X (impedance per phase)
- Tap Settings; nominal and adjustable range
- Transformer Rating; MVA capacity
- Transformer Type; 2-winding or 3-winding, auto or conventional
- Vector Group; phase displacement angle
- Grounding Impedance; neutral grounding characteristics
- Tan Delta; insulation quality indicator
- FRA; transformer winding condition
- Tertiary Capacity; three-winding loading capability

## CHUNK 5: ANNEXURE-1 - GENERATOR DATA (SYNCHRONOUS MACHINE, TURBINE, EXCITER, GOVERNOR)

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Gujarat Power System Management Standards) 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Standard / Technical Appendix

**HIERARCHY:** Document → Section-3 Annexure-1 → Subsection 4 Machine Data

**PROVISION:** Annexure-1, Machine Data – Generator, Turbine, Exciter, and Governor Specifications (Items 4A through 4D)

**TITLE:** Annexure-1 Machine Data – Generator, Turbine, Exciter, and Governor Control System Parameters

**SUMMARY:** Generator data shall include: capacity (MVA, MW, MVAR min/max), voltage (nominal, min, max), sequence impedances and grounding impedance, synchronous and transient reactance (direct and quadrature axes), transient and sub-transient time constants, inertia and damping constants, and armature parameters. Turbine data shall include: type (hydro/steam/Francis/Kaplan), block diagram of turbine model with gain constants and time constants, damping constant, nominal and operating speed, and other model parameters. Exciter data shall include: block diagram of exciter model with voltage regulator, main exciter, and power system stabilizer gains and time constants. Governor data shall include: block diagram with gain, time constant, max/min valve position limits, speed governor system gain, and valve servo time constant. This data enables dynamic stability analysis and control system simulation.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Generator Capacity; MVA, MW, MVAR range
- Voltage Operating Range; min and max bus voltage
- Impedance Data; sequence and grounding impedance
- Synchronous Reactance; Xd, Xq (direct and quadrature axes)
- Transient Reactance; X'd, X'q transient reactance
- Sub-transient Reactance; X''d, X''q (subtransient)
- Time Constants; Td', Td'', Tq', Tq'' (transient time constants)
- Inertia Constant; H (MW-sec/MVA)
- Damping Constant; damping coefficient
- Turbine Type; hydraulic, steam, or other prime mover
- Turbine Model; block diagram representation
- Governor Characteristics; speed regulation and valve dynamics
- Exciter Model; voltage regulation and control
- Power System Stabilizer; oscillation damping control

## CHUNK 6: ANNEXURE-1 - DEMAND DATA AND REAL-TIME DATA SPECIFICATIONS

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Gujarat Power System Management Standards) 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Standard / Technical Appendix

**HIERARCHY:** Document → Section-3 Annexure-1 → Subsections 5 and 6 (Demand Data and Real-Time Data)

**PROVISION:** Annexure-1, Demand Data and Real-Time Data (Items 5 and 6)

**TITLE:** Annexure-1 Demand Data and Real-Time Data – Load Forecasting and Operational Monitoring Parameters

**SUMMARY:** Demand data shall be collected for all 220 kV and 110 kV substations and shall include: weekly MW/MVAR load details with breakdown by consumer category (agricultural, industrial, domestic, commercial), restrictions imposed with estimated demand reduction, and unrestricted demand. Substation name, typical daily load curve for weekdays, and seasonal variations shall be recorded. Real-time data shall be collected continuously for: generation from each unit (MW), all 400/220 kV line flows (MW and MVAR), all 400/220 kV transformer flows (MW and MVAR), all 220/132 kV transformer flows (MW and MVAR), voltages at all 400 kV and 220 kV buses, transformer tap positions (400/220 kV and 220/132 kV), and circuit breaker status for all 400/220/132 kV equipment. This real-time data feeds SCADA systems for operational monitoring and post-event analysis.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Demand Data Collection; load pattern documentation
- Load Categorization; agricultural, industrial, domestic, commercial
- Load Restrictions; scheduled curtailment documentation
- Unrestricted Demand; theoretical maximum load
- Daily Load Curves; weekday and weekend patterns
- Seasonal Variations; yearly load profile
- Real-Time Generation; unit output monitoring
- Line Flows; power transmission path monitoring
- Transformer Flows; voltage transformation monitoring
- Bus Voltages; nodal voltage tracking
- Tap Positions; transformer tap changer status
- Circuit Breaker Status; equipment availability
- 400/220/132 kV Equipment; monitoring across voltage levels
