## CHUNK 1: SHORT TITLE, EXTENT AND COMMENCEMENT

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Gujarat Power System Management Standards) 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Standard

**HIERARCHY:** Document → Short Title, Extent and Commencement

**PROVISION:** Notification No. 7 of 2005

**TITLE:** Short Title, Extent and Commencement

**SUMMARY:** These Standards establish the regulatory framework for power system management across Gujarat's electricity infrastructure. They apply statewide and became effective upon publication in the Gujarat Government Gazette (March 30, 2005). The Standards provide operational guidelines for generation, transmission, and distribution systems, supplementing the Grid Code.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Regulatory Authority; Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission
- Statutory Powers; Section 42(b) of Gujarat Electricity Industry (Reorganisation and Regulation) Act 2003
- Applicability; Entire State of Gujarat
- Commencement; Date of publication in Gazette
- Supplement to Grid Code

**TEXT:**

In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 42 (b) of the Gujarat Electricity Industry (Reorganisation and Regulation) Act, 2003 (Gujarat Act 24 of 2003), and all powers enabling it in that behalf, the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission hereby makes following standards, namely:-

**Short Title, Extent and Commencement:**

a. These Standards may be called the Gujarat Electricity regulatory Commission (Gujarat Power System Management Standards) 2005.

b. These Regulations extend to the whole of the State of Gujarat.

c. These Regulations shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Gazette.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Section 42(b) of Gujarat Electricity Industry (Reorganisation and Regulation) Act, 2003 (Gujarat Act 24 of 2003)
- Grid Code (referenced as supplementary framework)

## CHUNK 2: SECTION-1 INTRODUCTION - OVERVIEW AND STRUCTURE

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Gujarat Power System Management Standards) 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Standard

**HIERARCHY:** Document → Section-1 Introduction → Subsection 1.1 Overview & 1.2 Structure

**PROVISION:** Section-1, Subsections 1.1 and 1.2

**TITLE:** Section-1 Introduction – Overview and Structure

**SUMMARY:** Section-1 outlines the foundational framework of the Power System Management Standards. The Standards provide guidelines for operation and management of power generation, transmission, and distribution systems as supplementary to the Grid Code. The Standards are organized into four Sections addressing introduction, generation management, transmission system management, and distribution system management.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Power System Management; operational and management guidelines
- Scope; generation, transmission, distribution systems
- Hierarchical Structure; four-section organization
- Supplemental Function; extends Grid Code

**TEXT:**

**1.1 OVERVIEW:**

1.1.1 Scope of Gujarat Power System Management Standards:

The Gujarat Power System Management Standards, herein after called Power System Management Standards, provide guidelines for the operation and management of power system including power generation, power transmission and power distribution and will be supplemental to Grid Code.

**1.2 STRUCTURE OF THE POWER SYSTEM MANAGEMENT STANDARDS:**

Power System Management Standards consist of 4 Sections as follows:

1.2.1 Section-1: Introduction. This section outlines the broad features of the Power System Management Standards.

1.2.2 Section-2: Power Generation Management Standards. This section provides guidelines for operation of Generating Stations.

1.2.3 Section-3: Transmission System Management Standards. This section provides guidelines for operation of Transmission System.

1.2.4 Section-4: Distribution System Management Standards. This section outlines the guidelines for operation of Distribution System.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Grid Code (primary regulatory framework)

## CHUNK 4: SECTION-1 - RESPONSIBILITY OF SLDC FOR SYSTEM SECURITY

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Gujarat Power System Management Standards) 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Standard

**HIERARCHY:** Document → Section-1 Introduction → Subsection 1.4 Responsibility

**PROVISION:** Section 1.4

**TITLE:** Responsibility – SLDC Control and Authority for Real-Time Operations

**SUMMARY:** The State Load Despatch Centre (SLDC) holds full control over real-time power system operations in the interest of system security. The SLDC is authorized to issue operational instructions to Generating Companies, Stations, Licensees, and Users to ensure safe grid operation. The SLDC is notified by the Government of Gujarat under Section 31 of the Electricity Act, 2003, and operates according to Grid Code provisions.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- State Load Despatch Centre (SLDC); operational control
- System Security; primary objective
- Operational Authority; issuance of instructions
- Regulatory Basis; Section 31 Electricity Act 2003
- Compliance Scope; Generating Companies, Stations, Licensees, Users

**TEXT:**

**1.4 RESPONSIBILITY**

1.4.1 In the interest of system security, SLDC shall have full control for real time operations of power system. SLDC shall issue suitable instructions to any Generating Company / Station or Licensee or User for safe grid operation, according to the provisions in Grid Code.

1.4.2 The Load Despatch Centre as may be notified by the Government of Gujarat under section 31 of the Electricity Act, 2003 (36 of 2003), shall be the State Load Despatch Centre (SLDC) for the purpose of Grid code.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Section 31 of Electricity Act, 2003 (36 of 2003)
- Grid Code (operational framework)

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:** SLDC has exclusive real-time operational authority. Any query about who controls grid operations should reference this provision.

## CHUNK 5: SECTION-1 - NON-COMPLIANCE WITH SLDC INSTRUCTIONS

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Gujarat Power System Management Standards) 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Standard

**HIERARCHY:** Document → Section-1 Introduction → Subsection 1.5 Non Compliance

**PROVISION:** Section 1.5

**TITLE:** Non-Compliance – Reporting and Remedial Action for Operational Difficulties

**SUMMARY:** Any User experiencing difficulty in complying with SLDC-issued operational instructions must promptly report the difficulty and take remedial action as directed by SLDC. Repeated non-compliance with SLDC instructions triggers appropriate enforcement action under the Grid Code to ensure system security. This provision establishes accountability for operational compliance and system reliability.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Compliance Obligation; SLDC instructions
- Reporting Requirement; prompt notification of difficulties
- Remedial Action; SLDC-directed corrections
- Enforcement; repeated non-compliance penalties
- System Security; enforcement objective

**TEXT:**

**1.5 NON COMPLIANCE**

1.5.1 Any User experiencing difficulty in complying with the instructions issued by SLDC, shall promptly report such difficulty to SLDC and take remedial action as directed by SLDC.

1.5.2 In case of repeated non compliance with the instructions of SLDC, SLDC will take appropriate action according to Grid Code for ensuring the security of the system.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Section 1.4 (SLDC instructions)
- Grid Code (enforcement mechanisms)

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:** Non-compliance is not excused; Users must report difficulties promptly and implement SLDC-directed remedies.

## CHUNK 6: SECTION-1 - STANDARDS AND REQUIREMENTS SPECIFIED BY CENTRAL ELECTRICITY AUTHORITY

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Gujarat Power System Management Standards) 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Standard

**HIERARCHY:** Document → Section-1 Introduction → Subsection 1.6 Standards and Requirements by Central Electricity Authority

**PROVISION:** Section 1.6

**TITLE:** Standards and Requirements Specified by Central Electricity Authority – Technical, Safety, Grid, and Metering Standards

**SUMMARY:** Licensees must comply with Standards and requirements specified by the Central Electricity Authority (CEA) as prescribed under Section 73 of the Electricity Act, 2003, for effective power system management. These standards cover technical requirements for electrical plant and line construction, safety requirements for construction and operation, Grid Standards for transmission operation, and metering requirements for transmission and supply of electricity. CEA-mandated standards are binding across all licensee operations.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Central Electricity Authority (CEA); standard-setting authority
- Technical Standards; electrical plants, electric lines, grid connectivity
- Safety Requirements; construction, operation, maintenance
- Grid Standards; transmission line operation and maintenance
- Metering Requirements; transmission and supply meters
- Compliance Obligation; all licensees

**TEXT:**

**1.6 STANDARDS AND REQUIREMENTS SPECIFIED BY CENTRAL ELECTRICITY AUTHORITY**

1.6.1 For effective power system management and operation, the licensees shall follow the Standards and requirements as specified by the Authority according to Section 73 of Electricity Act, 2003 covering matters such as:

(a) the technical standards for construction of electrical plants, electric lines and connectivity to the grid;

(b) the safety requirements for construction, operation and maintenance of electrical plants and electric lines;

(c) the Grid Standards for operation and maintenance of transmission lines;

(d) the conditions for installation of meters for transmission and supply of electricity.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:**
- Section 73 of Electricity Act, 2003
- Central Electricity Authority Standards (binding)

**KEY INSIGHT FOR ENERGY BOT:** CEA standards are mandatory baseline requirements. Any compliance query should reference CEA standards as primary obligation.
