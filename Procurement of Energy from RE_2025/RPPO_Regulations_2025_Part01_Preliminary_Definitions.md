## Chunk 1

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Procurement of Energy from Renewable Sources) Regulations, 2025

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 1 / Regulation 1

**PROVISION:** Regulation 1 - Short Title, Extent and Commencement

**TITLE:** Short Title, Extent and Commencement

**SUMMARY:**
The GERC Procurement of Energy from Renewable Sources Regulations, 2025, are a set of rules governing the procurement and consumption of energy from renewable sources in Gujarat. The regulations come into force from the date of their publication in the Official Gazette of the State of Gujarat and apply to the entire state of Gujarat. These regulations replace and repeal the earlier 2005 and 2010 regulations on renewable energy procurement.

**KEY TOPICS:** Short Title, Commencement Date, Geographical Extent, State of Gujarat, Renewable Energy Procurement, Regulation Framework, Effective Date

**TEXT:**

1. Short Title, Extent and Commencement: -
(1) These Regulations may be called the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Procurement of Energy from Renewable Sources) Regulations, 2025.
(2) These Regulations shall come into force from the date of their publication in the Official Gazettes of State of Gujarat.
(3) These Regulations shall extend to the whole of the State of Gujarat.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Electricity Act 2003 (Section 181, Sections 61 & 86); Energy Conservation Act 2001 & Amendments (2007, 2022); Ministry of Power Notification No. 4617(E) dated 28.10.2023

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Regulatory Authority: GERC under Electricity Act 2003; Predecessor Regulations: GERC RPPO Regulations 2005 and 2010

**PROVISION:** Regulation 2 - Definitions - Commission, Captive Generating Plant, Designated Consumers

**TITLE:** Definitions: Commission, Captive Generating Plant, Designated Consumers

**SUMMARY:**
"Commission" refers to the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (GERC). "Captive Generating Plant" (CGP) means a power plant set up by any person to generate electricity primarily for their own use, and includes plants set up by cooperative societies or associations of persons for member use. "Designated Consumers" are consumers specified as such under Section 14 of the Energy Conservation Act 2001 and its amendments, and are treated as obligated entities under these regulations.

**KEY TOPICS:** Commission, GERC, Captive Generating Plant, CGP, Designated Consumers, Energy Conservation Act, Self-Generation, Obligated Entity

**TEXT:**

(g) "Commission" means the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission;

(h) "Captive Generating Plant" (CGP) means a power plant set up by any person to generate electricity primarily for his own use and includes a power plant set up by any co-operative society or association of persons for generating electricity primarily for use of members of such co-operative society or association.

(h) "Designated Consumers" means the consumers who are specified as 'Designated Consumer' in the Schedule under Clause (e) of Section 14 of 'The Energy Conservation Act, 2001' and subsequent amendments in it from time to time.

## Chunk 5

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Procurement of Energy from Renewable Sources) Regulations, 2025

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 1 / Regulation 2(1)(i)

**PROVISION:** Regulation 2 - Definitions - Distributed Renewable Energy Component

**TITLE:** Definition: Distributed Renewable Energy Component

**SUMMARY:**
"Distributed Renewable Energy Component" (DRE) refers to energy generated from renewable energy projects smaller than 10 MW, including solar installations in all configurations notified by the Central Government. Compliance is measured in energy (kilowatt-hour) units. If generation data is unavailable, reported capacity is converted to energy using a multiplier of 3.5 kWh/kW/day.

**KEY TOPICS:** Distributed Renewable Energy, DRE, Solar PV, Small Solar, Rooftop Solar, Net Metering, Energy Generation Estimate, Capacity Multiplier, Kilowatt-hour Units, Central Government Notification

**TEXT:**

(i) "Distributed Renewable Energy Component" means the energy generated from renewable energy projects that are less than 10 MW in size and shall include solar installations under all configurations notified by the Central Government:

Provided that the compliance against distributed renewable energy shall ordinarily be considered in terms of energy (Kilowatt hour units):

Provided further that in case the obligated entity is unable to provide generation data against distributed renewable energy installations, the reported capacity shall be transformed into distributed renewable energy generation in terms of energy by a multiplier of 3.5 units per kilowatt per day (kWh/kW/day).

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Central Government Notifications on solar installations; Regulation 4 (RPPO Targets); Table-1 (DRE targets); Energy Storage Obligation provisions

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** DRE compliance reporting in Appendix-I (DISCOM formats); DRE compliance statements for various obligated entities; Energy conversion methodology

## Chunk 7

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Procurement of Energy from Renewable Sources) Regulations, 2025

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 1 / Regulation 2(1)(k)

**PROVISION:** Regulation 2 - Definitions - Hydro Renewable Obligation (HPO)

**TITLE:** Definition: Hydro Renewable Obligation (HPO)

**SUMMARY:**
"Hydro Renewable Obligation" (HPO) is the portion of renewable energy that must be met exclusively from hydro power projects, including pump storage projects (PSPs) and small hydro projects (SHPs), commissioned after March 31, 2024. HPO can also be met from free power provided to the state from qualified hydro projects or from hydro projects outside India approved by the Central Government on a case-by-case basis.

**KEY TOPICS:** Hydro Renewable Obligation, HPO, Hydro Power Projects, Pump Storage Projects, PSPs, Small Hydro Projects, SHPs, Free Power, Commissioning Date, Hydro Energy, Central Government Approval, Cross-Border Hydro

**TEXT:**

(k) "Hydro Renewable Obligation (HPO)" means the hydro renewable energy component to be met only by energy produced from Hydro Power Projects [including Pump Storage Projects (PSPs) and Small Hydro Projects (SHPs)], commissioned after the 31st March, 2024.

Provided that the hydro renewable energy component may also be met out of the free power being provided to the State/DISCOM from the Hydro Power Projects commissioned after the 31st March, 2024;

Provided further that the hydro renewable energy component may also be met from Hydro Power Projects located outside India as approved by the Central Government on a case-to-case basis.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Electricity Act 2003; Regulation 4 (RPPO Targets including HPO); Table-1 (HPO percentages); Regulation 5 (Fulfilment of HPO); Ministry of Power Notifications

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Hydro energy compliance in Regulation 4 and 5; Adjustment provisions between HPO and WPO (Regulation 4(2)); HPO compliance statements (Appendix formats)

## Chunk 11

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Procurement of Energy from Renewable Sources) Regulations, 2025

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 2 / Regulation 3

**PROVISION:** Regulation 3 - Applicability of Renewable Power Purchase Obligation (RPO)

**TITLE:** Applicability of Renewable Power Purchase Obligation (RPO)

**SUMMARY:**
Regulation 3 establishes that RPO applies to all obligated entities (distribution licensees, open access consumers, and captive users) in Gujarat. It specifically includes: (a) any person consuming conventional electricity through open access or otherwise; (b) any person with a captive plant exceeding 100 kW based on conventional fuel, using it for standby power during emergencies; (c) any person with such a captive plant meeting normal requirements apart from standby. However, retail consumers of distribution licensees are exempt to the extent of their retail consumption. Designated consumers under the Energy Conservation Act must comply regardless of captive plant capacity.

**KEY TOPICS:** Applicability, Obligated Entity, Distribution Licensee, Open Access Consumer, Captive Consumer, Conventional Power, Standby Supply, Designated Consumer, Exemption, Energy Conservation Act Compliance, 100 kW Threshold

**TEXT:**

3. Applicability of Renewable Power Purchase Obligation (RPO) - The Renewable Power Purchase Obligation (RPO) specified under these Regulations shall be applicable to all Obligated Entities covering Distribution Licensees, Open Access Consumers and captive users within Gujarat, and shall also include:

(a) any person, consuming electricity procured from conventional sources (i.e. other than renewable energy sources) through open access or otherwise;

(b) any person who installs Captive Generating Plant, with an installed capacity exceeding 100 KW, based on conventional technology (i.e. other than any renewable energy technology) and consumes electricity from such Plant purely for meeting his Standby or emergency backup) requirements in the exceptional circumstances of power cuts/ breakdown etc. in his normal source of power; and/or

(c) any person who installs Captive Generating Plant, with an installed capacity exceeding 100 KW based on conventional technology (i.e. other than renewable energy technology) and consumes electricity from such plant for meeting his normal requirements, apart from his standby (or emergency back-up) requirements:

Provided that, save as provided in Clause (a), (b) and (c), a retail consumer of Distribution Licensee consuming electricity supplied by such distribution licensee shall not be considered as Obligated Entity, to the extent of its consumption in its capacity as a retail consumer of the Distribution Licensee.

Provided further that, the exemptions under clauses (b) and (c) shall not be applicable to the obligated entities who are also Designated Consumers as defined under the Energy Conservation Act, 2001. Designated Consumers shall be required to comply RPO irrespective of capacity of Captive Generating Plant.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Section 86 of Electricity Act 2003; Regulation 2 (Definitions - Obligated Entity, Captive Generating Plant, Designated Consumers); Energy Conservation Act 2001; Regulation 4 (RPPO Quantum)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Regulation 5 (Fulfilment); Regulation 8 (Registration and Reporting); Regulation 9 (Compliance and Monitoring); Regulation 10 (Consequences of Default)
