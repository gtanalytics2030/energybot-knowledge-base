## CHUNK 5: OPERATIVE PERIOD

DOCUMENT: Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy-2023

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 6 – Operative Period

PROVISION: Section 6

TITLE: Operative Period and Supersession of Previous Policies

SUMMARY:
Section 6 establishes that the policy is effective from the date of notification (04 October 2023) and remains in operation until 30 September 2028 or until a new policy is notified, whichever is earlier. The policy supersedes three previous renewable energy policies: Gujarat Solar Power Policy 2021 (notified 29 December 2020), Gujarat Wind Power Policy 2016 (notified 02 August 2016), and Gujarat Wind Solar Hybrid Power Policy 2018 (notified 20 June 2018) and their amendments. Renewable energy projects installed and commissioned during the operative period are eligible for policy benefits. Projects commissioned after 19 June 2023 under the Wind Solar Hybrid Policy 2018 and before this policy's issuance are also eligible. Benefits are applicable for 25 years from commissioning or the project's lifespan as defined by GERC/MoP/MNRE, whichever is earlier.

KEY TOPICS: Policy Effective Date, Policy Duration, Supersession, Previous Policies, Eligibility Period, Benefit Duration, Project Commissioning, Lifespan Definition

TEXT:
This policy will come into effect from the date of notification and shall remain in operation up to 30-Sept-2028 or till notification of the new policy, whichever is earlier. The 'Gujarat Solar Power Policy 2021' notified vide G.R. dated December 29, 2020, 'Gujarat Wind Power Policy 2016' notified vide G.R. dated August 02, 2016, and 'Gujarat Wind Solar Hybrid Power Policy 2018'notified vide G.R. dated June 20, 2018 and their subsequent amendments shall stand superseded. 

Renewable Energy Projects that are installed and commissioned during the operative period will be eligible for the benefits and incentives outlined in this policy. Further, the projects commissioned after 19th June-2023 under Wind Solar Hybrid Policy-2018 and before the issuance of this Policy shall be eligible for the benefits under this Policy. These benefits will be applicable for a period of 25 years from the date of commissioning or the lifespan of the RE project, as defined by GERC/MoP/MNRE, whichever is earlier.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Government Resolution dated 04/10/2023, Previous policies: Gujarat Solar Power Policy 2021, Gujarat Wind Power Policy 2016, Gujarat Wind Solar Hybrid Power Policy 2018

## CHUNK 6: POLICY SCOPE

DOCUMENT: Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy-2023

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 7 – Policy Scope

PROVISION: Section 7

TITLE: Scope and Coverage of the Policy

SUMMARY:
Section 7 defines the scope of the policy to cover all ground-mounted solar, rooftop solar, floating solar, canal-top solar, wind, rooftop wind, and wind-solar hybrid projects. The policy explicitly excludes renewable energy projects set up for supplying power to units producing green hydrogen and green ammonia, which will be covered under a separate policy to be notified by the Government of Gujarat. This distinction ensures that different technological domains (bulk renewable generation vs. green hydrogen/ammonia production) are addressed through tailored policy frameworks suited to their respective operational and economic requirements.

KEY TOPICS: Policy Coverage, Solar Technologies, Wind Projects, Hybrid Projects, Green Hydrogen, Green Ammonia, Renewable Generation, Floating Solar, Canal-top Solar

TEXT:
All ground mounted solar, roof top solar, floating solar, canal top solar, wind, rooftop wind and wind-solar hybridprojects shall be covered under this policy. The provisions of this policy shall not be applicable to RE projects set up for the purpose of supply of power to the units producing Green Hydrogen and Green Ammonia which will be covered under a separate policy for Green Hydrogen I Green Ammonia sector to be notified by Government of Gujarat.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Separate policy framework for Green Hydrogen/Green Ammonia (to be notified)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Project eligibility provisions in Section 8, Technical specifications in Sections 9-11 (Solar, Wind, Hybrid), Grid connectivity in Section 20

## CHUNK 7: ELIGIBILITY – GENERAL PROVISIONS

DOCUMENT: Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy-2023

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 8 – Eligibility

PROVISION: Section 8.1, 8.2, 8.3

TITLE: General Eligibility Criteria for RE Project Developers

SUMMARY:
Section 8 establishes broad eligibility criteria for setting up renewable energy projects under the policy. Any individual, company, body corporate, association, or body of individuals (whether incorporated or not) or artificial juridical person is eligible. Projects can be set up for captive use and/or for selling electricity to third parties (registered under REC mechanism or not) or to distribution licensees, subject to the Electricity Act 2003. There is no capacity restriction for RE projects for captive use or third-party sale with respect to the consumer's contracted demand/sanctioned load (kW/kVA/MVA) with DISCOMs. The AC capacity of the RE project is considered as the project installed capacity. These provisions ensure broad-based participation while eliminating capacity-based constraints on RE developers.

KEY TOPICS: Eligible Entities, Captive Use, Third-Party Sale, REC Mechanism, Distribution Licensees, Capacity Unrestricted, AC Capacity, Installed Capacity

TEXT:
8. Eligibility

8.1 Any individual, company, body corporate, association, or body of individuals, whether incorporated or not, or artificial juridical person, will be eligible for setting up the RE projects under the policy. 

8.2 The RE projects can be set up under this policy for captive use and / or for selling electricity to any other third party whether registered under the REC mechanism or not, or selling electricity to distribution licensees, subject to the provisions of this Policy and in accordance with the provisions of the Electricity Act 2003, as amended from time to time. 

8.3 There shall be no capacity restriction for setting up of RE projects for captive use or for selling electricity to third party consumer with respect to the consumer's contracted demand! sanctioned load (kW/kVA/MVA) with DlSCOMs. The AC capacity of the RE project shall be considered as the project installed capacity.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Electricity Act 2003, REC Mechanism (CERC Regulations), Section 8.1-8.3

CROSS-REFERENCES: Project developer definition in Section 5 (Definition 11), Energy settlement in Section 14, Grid charges and transmission provisions in Section 15, Purchase of power by DISCOMs in Section 16
