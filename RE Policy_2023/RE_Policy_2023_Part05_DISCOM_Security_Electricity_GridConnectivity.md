## CHUNK 25: PURCHASE OF POWER BY DISCOMs – COMPETITIVE BIDDING FRAMEWORK

DOCUMENT: Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy-2023

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 16 – Purchase of Power by DISCOMs

PROVISION: Section 16.1

TITLE: DISCOM Power Purchase under Competitive Bidding Process

SUMMARY:
Section 16.1 establishes that DISCOMs may purchase power from RE projects to economize overall power purchase costs for consumer benefit through competitive bidding processes per Government of India guidelines under Section 63 of the Electricity Act 2003. Supply terms and conditions are governed by Power Purchase Agreements signed between RE projects and DISCOMs. This framework leverages competitive market mechanisms to ensure cost-effective procurement while maintaining transparency and regulatory oversight through PPA terms and conditions, enabling DISCOMs to meet their procurement needs while benefiting consumers through price discovery.

KEY TOPICS: DISCOM Procurement, Competitive Bidding, Section 63 Bidding, Power Purchase Agreement, Cost Optimization, Consumer Benefit, Government of India Guidelines

TEXT:

16. Purchase of power by DISCOMs 

For RE prolects covered under competitive bidding process as per MNRE Guidelines: 

16.1 DISCOM may purchase power from RE projects from time to time to economize overall power power purchase costs for the benefit of consumers by following the competitive bidding process in accordance with the guidelines notified by the Government of India from time to time under Section 63 of the Electricity Act. The terms and conditions for the supply of power shalt be governed by the provisions of respective Power Purchase Agreement signed between the RE project and DISCOM.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Section 63 of Electricity Act 2003, Government of India Guidelines for competitive bidding, MNRE Guidelines, Power Purchase Agreements

## CHUNK 26: DISCOM POWER PURCHASE – TYPE-A HYBRID PROJECTS

DOCUMENT: Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy-2023

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 16 – Purchase of Power by DISCOMs

PROVISION: Section 16.2

TITLE: Power Procurement from Type-A Hybrid Projects

SUMMARY:
Section 16.2 specifies the procurement framework for Type-A hybrid projects (conversions of existing plants). Purchase of power from existing wind or solar capacity follows the respective PPAs with DISCOMs. Purchase of power from additional or new wind or solar capacity resulting from hybridization occurs at tariffs discovered through separate competitive bidding conducted by DISCOMs for wind and/or solar power respectively. This provision ensures that existing contractual obligations are preserved while new capacity additions are subjected to competitive discovery, balancing project certainty with market efficiency.

KEY TOPICS: Type-A Hybrid Projects, Existing Capacity, Additional Capacity, Separate Competitive Bidding, Wind and Solar PPAs, Tariff Discovery

TEXT:

16.2 In the case of Type-A hybrid projects, the purchase of power from existing wind or solar capacity as the case may be, shall be in accordance with the respective PPAs with DISCOMs, and purchase of power from additional or new wind or solar capacity shalt be at the tariff discovered through competitive bidding undertaken by DISCOMs separately for purchase of wind and / or solar power.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Type-A Hybrid project definitions in Section 11.3.1, Existing PPA terms, Competitive bidding procedures

## CHUNK 27: DISCOM POWER PURCHASE – EXEMPTED PROJECTS (SOLAR UP TO 4 MW)

DOCUMENT: Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy-2023

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 16 – Purchase of Power by DISCOMs

PROVISION: Section 16.3 and 16.3.1

TITLE: Distributed Solar Projects – Pre-Fixed Tariff Framework

SUMMARY:
Section 16.3.1 allows DISCOMs to procure power from distributed solar projects up to 4 MW capacity at pre-fixed levelized tariff, exempting such projects from competitive bidding per MNRE guidelines. The tariff mechanism is the simple average of tariffs discovered and contracted under competitive bidding by GUVNL in the preceding 6-month period (April-September or October-March) plus 20 paisa/unit, applicable for PPA signing in the subsequent 6-month period. If GERC determines a generic tariff lower than this mechanism-derived rate, the lower tariff applies and remains fixed for the entire 25-year PPA term. This framework provides tariff certainty for small distributed solar developers while leveraging market discovery mechanisms and ensuring consumer benefit through competitive pricing.

KEY TOPICS: Distributed Solar, 4 MW Capacity, Exempted Projects, Pre-Fixed Tariff, GUVNL Competitive Bidding, Tariff Formula, Generic Tariff, 25-Year PPA

TEXT:

16.3 For RE Projects exempted from competitive bidding process as per MNRE guidelines: 

16.3.1 Purchase from Solar Power Projects: 

DISCOMs may procure power from distributed solar projects up to 4 MW capacity at a pre-fixed levelized tariff as per the mechanism decided by GERC i.e. simple average of tariffs discovered and contracted under the competitive bidding process conducted by GUVNL for solar projects inthe preceding 6-month period, i.e., either April to September or October to March, as the case may be, plus 20 paisa/unit, which shall be applicable for the signing of PPAs in subsequent 6-month period and such tariff shall remain fixed for the 25 year term of the PPA. 

Further, in case generic tariff is determined by GERC for solar project and such tariff is lower than tariff as stated in above para, lower tariff will be applicable for purchase of solar power from such project and the same shall be fixed for entire term of PPA.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: MNRE Exemption Guidelines for distributed solar, GERC Generic Tariff Determinations, GUVNL Competitive Bidding Process, Section 5 (Definition 11 – RE Project Developer)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Rooftop solar provisions in Section 9.2.2, Tariff formula application in wind projects (Section 16.3.2), Security deposit in Section 17.2, Generic tariff references in Section 16.3.2

## CHUNK 30: SECURITY DEPOSIT – FRAMEWORK FOR PPA-BASED AND NON-PPA PROJECTS

DOCUMENT: Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy-2023

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 17 – Security Deposit

PROVISION: Section 17.1 and 17.2

TITLE: Bank Guarantee Requirements for RE Project Developers

SUMMARY:
Section 17.1 specifies that RE developers purchasing power through Power Purchase Agreements with DISCOMs must provide Bank Guarantees for Security Deposit as per the terms of respective bid documents, government schemes, or MNRE guidelines. Section 17.2 specifies that RE developers purchasing power from projects not covered by Section 17.1 must provide Bank Guarantee for Security Deposit at Rs. 5 lakh per MW at the time of signing the PPA with DISCOM. This tiered approach provides flexibility: projects following standardized bid processes and government schemes use specified guarantee amounts, while other projects follow a fixed Rs. 5 lakh/MW standard, ensuring adequate security while avoiding excessive financial burden on developers.

KEY TOPICS: Security Deposit, Bank Guarantee, Power Purchase Agreement, Project Developers, Bid Documents, Government Schemes, MNRE Guidelines, Financial Security, Rs. 5 Lakh Per MW

TEXT:

17. Security Deposit 

17.1 In case of purchase of power by DISCOM under Power Purchase Agreement, the RE developer shall be required to provide Bank Guarantee towards Security Deposit as per the terms and conditions of the respective bid documents, government schemes, or MNRE guidelines, as the case may be. 

17.2 In the case of purchase of power by DISCOM from RE projects not falling under Clause No. 17.1 above, the RE developers shall be required to provide Bank Guarantee towards Security Deposit @ Rs. 5 lakh per MW at the time of signing the PPA with DISCOM.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Bid documents (RFP), Government scheme terms, MNRE Guidelines, PPA execution procedures

CROSS-REFERENCES: Commercial operation achievement in Section 17.3, Captive use security deposits in Section 17.4, Evacuation facility commissioning in Section 17.5-17.6

## CHUNK 31: SECURITY DEPOSIT – REFUND, CAPTIVE USE, AND EVACUATION FACILITY REQUIREMENTS

DOCUMENT: Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy-2023

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 17 – Security Deposit

PROVISION: Section 17.3, 17.4, 17.5, 17.6

TITLE: Security Deposit Refund, Captive Use Deposits, and Evacuation Facility Timeline Requirements

SUMMARY:
Section 17.3 provides that Bank Guarantees for Security Deposit are refunded if RE projects achieve commercial operation within timeframes specified in PPAs. If projects fail to achieve commercial operation as agreed, Bank Guarantees are forfeited and PPA consequences apply. Section 17.4 specifies that for captive use or third-party sale projects, developers must submit Bank Guarantees per GERC-approved connectivity procedures to STU/DISCOM for timely evacuation facility completion. If developers fail to commission entire evacuation lines, bays, and metering systems within specified periods, STU/DISCOM encashes the guarantee. Section 17.5-17.6 establish a table of commissioning timelines for evacuation facilities based on capacity (1-100 MW: 12 months; >100-200 MW: 15 months; >200-400 MW: 18 months; >400-1000 MW: 24 months; >1000 MW: 30 months from transmission capacity allotment date). Section 17.6 exempts RE developers awarded through competitive bidding from the 10% minimum commissioning requirement in Section 17.5. These detailed provisions ensure timely infrastructure development and protect utility interests.

KEY TOPICS: Security Deposit Refund, Commercial Operation, Bank Guarantee Forfeiture, Captive Use, Third-Party Sale, Evacuation Facility, Commissioning Timeline, Capacity Categories, Transmission Capacity Allotment

TEXT:

17.3 The Bank Guarantee towards Security Deposit shall be refunded if the RE project achieve commercial operation within the time period mentioned in the power purchase agreement. In case the RE project fails to achieve commercial operation as agreed in the power purchase agreement, the Bank Guarantee shall be forfeited and consequences as per the respective PPA shall be applicable. 

17.4 In cases where RE projects are set up for captive use or third-party sale, the project developer shall submit Bank Guarantee towards security deposit in accordance with the connectivity procedure approved by GERC to STU / DISCOM, as the case may be, for ensuring timely completion of the evacuation facility for RE project. In case the RE developer fails to commission the entire evacuation line along with bays and the metering system within the time period mentioned hereunder, STU or DISCOM, as the case may be, shall encash the bank guarantee.

RE Capacity(MW) | Period of Commissioning of the entire Evacuation line along with bays and metering system
1MW to 100MW | 12 months from date of allotment of transmission capacity 
> 100MW to 200MW | 15 months from date of allotment of transmission capacity 
> 200MW to 400MW | 18 months from date of allotment of transmission capacity 
>400MW to 1000MW | 24 months from date of allotment of transmission capacity 
>1000 MW | 30 months from date of allotment of transmission capacity 

17.5 The RE Developer shall commission the project for at least 10% of the allotted capacity within one month of charging the evacuation line or as per timeframe stipulated in clause 17.4 here above, whichever is earlier, failing which, the RE Developer shall be liable to pay long-term transmission charges for 10% of the allotted capacity until such 10% of the allotted capacity is commissioned. Balance 90% capacity shall require to be commissioned within one year of charging of evacuation line or as per timeframe stipulated in clause 17.4 here above, whichever is earlier, failing which STU shall cancel the capacity allotment to the extent of capacity not commissioned and the RE developer shall have no claim on such capacity and pay relinquishment charges as determined by GERC. Further, STU shall include such cancelled capacity in the list of spare available capacity for RE integration to be published on their website for prospective consumers. 

17.6 Clause No. 17.5 shall not be applicable to RE developers who are awarded projects through a competitive bidding process.

TITLE: Electricity Duty Compliance and Green Power Supply Framework

SUMMARY:

## CHUNK 34: GRID CONNECTIVITY – EVACUATION FACILITY FRAMEWORKS (RE PARKS AND NON-PARK PROJECTS)

DOCUMENT: Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy-2023

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 20 – Grid Connectivity and Evacuation Facilities

PROVISION: Section 20.4 and 20.5

TITLE: Evacuation Infrastructure for RE Parks and Distributed Projects

SUMMARY:
Section 20.4 specifies that RE Park developers establish dedicated evacuation lines up to STU or CTU sub-stations and install RTUs at their cost. For STU/DISCOM-connected RE parks: developers lay dedicated lines to STU sub-stations per system study (where grid injection is feasible). STU grants connectivity from published lists of available sub-stations per GERC-approved connectivity procedures, regularly updated. For CTU-connected RE parks: developers lay dedicated lines to CTU sub-station or interface per CTU system study, with CTU ensuring transmission connection for interstate power export. Section 20.5 addresses non-park RE projects requiring common dedicated transmission lines for clusters of adjoining projects with metering at individual project ends and common metering at STU receiving end. SLDC/ALDC apportions energy based on common meter readings using individual project meter readings. This framework optimizes infrastructure costs while ensuring accurate energy accounting and fair cost allocation among projects.

KEY TOPICS: RE Parks, Dedicated Lines, STU Connectivity, CTU Connectivity, RTU Installation, System Study, Sub-Station Lists, Common Metering, SLDC Apportionment, Cluster Projects, Evacuation Infrastructure

TEXT:

20.4 Evacuation facility within RE Park (solar / wind /Wind-solar hybrid projects) 

20.4.1 The developer of RE Park shall establish a dedicated line for evacuation of power up to the STU orCTU sub-station and install RTUs, etc. at their own cost. 

(i) RE projects / RE park connected with STU / DISCOM network: 
Such RE project or RE park developer shall lay a dedicated line for evacuation of power up to sub-stationsof STU as per the system study undertaken by STU or DISCOM where injection of power into the state grid is feasible. From there onwards, STU / DISCOM shalt ensure transmission / distribution system and connectivity as the case may be for wheeling of power. 

The connectivity shalt be granted to RE project developers by STU in accordance with the connectivity procedure approved by GERC as amended from time to time, from a published list of sub-stations where spare capacity for connectivity is available. The list of sub-stations where spare capacity is available shall be updated by STU at a regular time intervals. 

(ii) RE project developer/RE park developer connected with CTU network: 
Such RE projects or RE park developers shall lay dedicated lines for evacuation of power up to the CTU substation or CTU interface, as per the system study undertaken by CTU. From there onwards, CTU may ensure a transmission system and connectivity with the inter-state network wherever power is to be exported out of the state. 

20.5 Evacuation facility for outside RE Park ( solar / wind / hybrid) and connected with STU / DISCOM grid: To optimize costs and resources, common dedicated transmission line shall be encouraged for clusters of adjoining RE projects with appropriate metering at their respective ends of the project, as well as a common meter at the receiving end at the STU substation. Energy injection from each RE project at the receiving end shalt be worked out by SLDC/ALDC on the basis of the meter reading of the common meter and appropriately apportioned as per the sending end meter reading of individual RE project.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: GERC Connectivity Procedures, STU/CTU System Study procedures, Electricity Act 2003, CERC Interstate Transmission procedures

CROSS-REFERENCES: Metering requirements in Section 21, Grid integration standards in Section 20.2-20.3, SLDC/ALDC operations in energy settlement (Section 14), Hybrid project interconnection in Section 11.3.1-11.3.3
