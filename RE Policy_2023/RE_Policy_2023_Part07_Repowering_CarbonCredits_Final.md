## CHUNK 46: REPOWERING OF WIND PROJECTS SELLING TO DISCOMs – GENERATION AND PROCUREMENT FRAMEWORK

DOCUMENT: Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy-2023

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 29 – Repowering of Wind Projects

PROVISION: Section 29.8

TITLE: Repowering Wind Projects Under PPA with Distribution Licensees

SUMMARY:
Section 29.8 establishes the repowering framework for wind projects selling power to DISCOMs under PPAs (preferential tariff, REC mechanism, or competitive bidding). Existing capacity continues supplying under original PPA terms and conditions. Generation from existing capacity prior to repowering equals average generation during the last 3 years prior to repowering, excluding the year in which repowering occurred. This baseline is used to establish continuity of supply obligations. Additional generation capacity from repowering may be procured by DISCOM per Clause 16 (Purchase of power by DISCOMs), considering RPO requirements and competitively discovered tariff. However, procurement of additional power is not binding on DISCOM; RE developers have the option to sell additional power or consume it themselves. This framework balances developer incentives for modernization with DISCOM flexibility in procurement and provides downside protection to existing contractual obligations.

KEY TOPICS: Repowering, PPA Wind Projects, DISCOM Procurement, Existing Capacity, Additional Capacity, Baseline Generation, Competitive Bidding, RPO Requirement, Optional Procurement, Revenue Generation

TEXT:

29.8 Repowering of Wind Projects selling power to DlSCOM: 

i. In case Wind project is selling power to DISCOM under the PPA (under a preferential tariff, REC mechanism, or competitive bidding route), then such wind power generator shall continue to supply generation from the existing capacity prior to repowering as per the terms and conditions of the existing PPA. 

ii. The generation corresponding to the existing capacity prior to repowering shall be equivalent to the average generation during the last three years prior to the repowering of the wind project, excluding the year in which repowering was undertaken. 

iii. The additional generation capacity due to repowering may be procured by DISCOM as per Clause No. 16 ofthis policy, taking into account the RPO requirement and tariff discovered through the competitive bidding process, as may be decided from time to time. However, it will not be binding for the DISCOM to purchase additional power as a result of repowering of wind project and RE developer shall have option to sell power or use for selfconsumption.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Clause 16 (Purchase of Power by DISCOMs), Section 5 (Definition 11 – RE Project Developer), RPO obligations, Competitive bidding procedures

CROSS-REFERENCES: DISCOM procurement framework in Section 16, Existing PPA terms, Baseline generation methodology, Optional procurement provisions

## CHUNK 47: REPOWERING OF WIND PROJECTS UNDER WHEELING ARRANGEMENT

DOCUMENT: Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy-2023

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 29 – Repowering of Wind Projects

PROVISION: Section 29.9

TITLE: Repowering Wind Projects Under Wheeling and Third-Party Sale Arrangements

SUMMARY:
Section 29.9 establishes repowering framework for wind projects under wheeling arrangement for third-party or captive consumption. Existing generation quantum consumption follows existing wheeling and transmission agreement terms. Existing generation quantum is determined from average generation in 3 financial years prior to repowering, excluding the repowering year. Wheeling of additional generation above existing quantum follows policy provisions (Clause 14-15). Wheeling agreements are modified or amended to reflect the new configuration. If incremental post-repowering capacity is offered to DISCOMs, procurement follows Clause 16 (Purchase of power by DISCOMs). This framework maintains existing contractual certainty while enabling modernization benefits and providing flexibility in deploying new capacity.

KEY TOPICS: Repowering, Wheeling Arrangement, Third-Party Sale, Captive Consumption, Existing Generation, Additional Generation, Wheeling Agreement Amendment, Incremental Capacity, DISCOM Procurement

TEXT:

29.9 Repowering of Wind Projects setup under wheeling arrangement: 

i. The consumption of existing wind generation quantum shall be governed by the existing wheeling andtransmission agreement. 

ii. Existing generation quantum shall be determined based on average generation in the previous 3 financial years prior to repowering, excluding the year in which repowering was undertaken. The wheeling of additional generation over and above the existing generation quantum will be governed as per the provisions of this policy. 

iii. The Wheeling Agreement shall have to be modified or amended to give effect of the same. 

iv. If the incremental capacity post-repowering is offered to concerned DISCOM, the same may beprocured by DISCOM in accordance with Clause No. 16 of this policy.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Clause 14 (Energy Settlement and Banking), Clause 15 (Grid Charges), Clause 16 (Purchase of Power by DISCOMs), Wheeling agreement amendment procedures

CROSS-REFERENCES: Wheeling provisions in Sections 9.1.3, 10.4, 11.4, Grid charges in Section 15, Energy settlement in Section 14

## CHUNK 48: REPOWERING WIND PROJECTS – CONNECTIVITY EXTENSIONS, EXEMPTIONS, AND DECOMMISSIONING

DOCUMENT: Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy-2023

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 29 – Repowering of Wind Projects

PROVISION: Section 29.10, 29.11, 29.12, 29.13

TITLE: Connectivity and Land Extensions, Exemptions from PPA Obligations, and Superseded Policy

SUMMARY:
Section 29.10 establishes that for existing wind projects completing 25 years of life from Date of Commercial Operation, extensions in connectivity by STU and land lease extensions by GEDA are granted only upon repowering of existing wind turbine generators. Additional wind capacity from repowering is governed per Clauses 29.6-29.7. Section 29.11 specifies that dismantling and decommissioning of existing RE projects, land acquisition, transmission system augmentation to GETCO/STU sub-station, lease renewals, and consent renewals are at the cost, risk, and responsibility of RE developers. Section 29.12 exempts wind projects undergoing repowering from existing PPA non-availability of generation obligations for maximum 4 months during repowering execution. For wheeling-based repowering, projects may purchase grid power during execution at tariffs applicable to their consumer category. Section 29.13 specifies that the 'Gujarat Repowering of Wind Projects Policy 2018' (G.R. dated 21 May 2018) is superseded. These provisions incentivize modernization while ensuring resource accountability and timeline clarity.

KEY TOPICS: Connectivity Extension, Land Extension, Commercial Operation Date, Repowering Obligation, Exemption from PPA, Grid Power Purchase, Repowering Duration, Decommissioning Costs, Policy Supersession, 4-Month Exemption

TEXT:

29.10 For existing wind projects completing 25 years of life from the date of CoD, the extensions in connectivity by STU and land lease extensions by GEDA shall be granted only upon repowering of the existing wind turbine generators. Additional wind capacity due to repowering will be governed by Clause No. 29.6 and 29.7, as applicable. 

29.11 The dismantling and de-commissioning of existing RE projects, land acquisition, augmentation of the transmission system up to the GETCO I STU sub-station, renewal of leases, renewal of consents, etc. shall be at the cost, risk, and responsibility of the RE developer. 

29.12 The wind projects undergoing repowering shall be exempted from the obligations under the existing PPA for non-availability of generation during the period of execution of such repowering, subject to a maximum period of four months. Similarly, in the case of repowering by wind power project set up under wheeling arrangement, the wind project shall be allowed to purchase power from the grid during the period of execution of repowering upon payment of tariff to concerned DISCOM as applicable to respective consumer category. 

29.13 The 'Gujarat Repowering of Wind Projects Policy 2018' notifiedvide G.R. dated May 21, 2018 stand superseded.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Clause 29.6-29.7 (Repowering notification and certification), Date of Commercial Operation (CoD), STU Connectivity procedures, GEDA Land Extension procedures, PPA terms, DISCOM tariff schedules

## CHUNK 49: SHARING OF CARBON CREDIT BENEFITS

DOCUMENT: Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy-2023

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 30 – Sharing of Carbon Credit Benefits

PROVISION: Section 30.1, 30.2, 30.3

TITLE: Carbon Credit Eligibility and Benefit Sharing Framework

SUMMARY:
Section 30 establishes that RE projects are eligible for carbon credits including CERs (Certified Emission Reductions), VERs (Verified Emission Reductions), Gold Standard, or other standards adopted at national or international level for renewable energy projects. Section 30.2 provides that for projects installed through competitive bidding, carbon credits are availed of and retained by the RE developer. For projects with GERC-determined tariffs, carbon credit benefit sharing follows GERC tariff order provisions. Section 30.3 specifies that rooftop solar/wind projects implemented under central or state government schemes must pass carbon credit benefits to DISCOMs. This framework incentivizes large-scale renewable development through private carbon credit monetization while ensuring scheme-based distributed projects contribute to utility finance, balancing market mechanisms with policy objectives.

KEY TOPICS: Carbon Credits, CER, VER, Gold Standard, International Standards, Competitive Bidding, RE Developer Retention, GERC Tariff Orders, Rooftop Solar, Government Schemes, DISCOM Benefits

TEXT:

30. Sharing of Carbon Credit benefits 

30.1 RE projects are eligible for Carbon credits which include CERs, VERs, Gold Standards, or any other standards adopted at the national or international level for the issuance of carbon credits for renewable energy projects. 

30.2 For all the projects installed through the competitive bidding process, carbon credits can be availed of and retained by the RE developer. Further, for the projects for which tariff is determined by GERC, the sharing of carbon credit benefits shall be as per tariff order of GERC. 

30.3 Rooftop solar/wind projects that are implemented under the scheme of the central or state government, as the case may be, shall have to pass the benefit of carbon credits to the DISCOM.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: CER/VER Standards (UNFCCC), Gold Standard for Renewable Energy, GERC Tariff Orders, Government Scheme Terms and Conditions

CROSS-REFERENCES: Competitive bidding framework in Section 16, GERC tariff determinations in Sections 9.2.2, 10.2, 16.3.1-16.3.2, Government schemes referenced in Section 16.3.4

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 34 – Wind Projects Registered Under Gujarat Wind Power Policy 2016

PROVISION: Section 34

TITLE: Grandfathering Provisions for Wind Projects Under Previous Policy

SUMMARY:
Section 34 establishes transitional provisions for wind projects registered with GEDA per Gujarat Wind Power Policy 2016 (as amended and extended) before this policy's notification but not yet commissioned as of this policy's effective date. Such registered projects are eligible to avail benefits under Wind Power Policy 2016 if they are commissioned by 31 December 2023. All wind projects commissioned after 31 December 2023 are governed by this Renewable Energy Policy-2023. This provision creates clarity: projects in advanced development stages under the previous framework can complete commissioning per original terms, while projects not meeting the 31 December 2023 deadline transition to new policy provisions. The cutoff date provides certainty for both projects and regulators.

TEXT:

34. Wind Projects registered under Gujarat Wind Policy 2016: 

Wind power projects which are registered with GEDA as per provisions of Gujarat Wind Policy 2016 as amended and extended from time to time, before notification of this policy, but not commissioned until effective date of this policy shall be eligible to avail the benefits under the Wind Power Policy-2016 if the registered projects are commissioned by 31/12/2023. All the projects commissioned after 31/12/2023 will be governed as per the Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy-2023.

## CHUNK 54: POWER TO REMOVE DIFFICULTIES AND POWER TO INTERPRET

DOCUMENT: Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy-2023

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy

HIERARCHY: Section 35 and 36 – Power to Remove Difficulties and Power to Interpret

PROVISION: Section 35 and Section 36

TITLE: Administrative Flexibility and Authoritative Interpretation

SUMMARY:
Section 35 establishes that if difficulties arise in implementing the policy, the state government may issue clarifications or interpretations to remove such difficulties, either on its own motion or based on stakeholder representations. The Energy and Petrochemicals Department is authorized to amend, review, relax, or interpret policy provisions as required. If difficulties arise with specific provisions, the department issues clarifications and interpretations as necessary and expedient, either on its own or after hearing affected parties. Section 36 establishes that if confusion or disputes arise about the meaning, intent, or purpose of any policy provision, the Energy and Petrochemicals Department's interpretation is final and binding on all concerned. Together, these provisions create administrative flexibility while centralizing authority, enabling responsive governance without requiring formal policy amendment while ensuring interpretive clarity and consistency.

KEY TOPICS: Power to Remove Difficulties, Administrative Flexibility, Clarifications, Interpretations, Stakeholder Consultation, Policy Amendment Authority, Final Binding Interpretation, Department Authority, Expedient Resolution

TEXT:

35. Power to remove difficulties: 

35.1 If any difficulty arises in giving effect to this policy, the state government may issue clarification or interpretation to remove such difficulties, either on its own motion or based on representations from stakeholders. 

35.2 The Energy and Petrochemicals Department shall amend, review, relax, or interpret any of the provisions under this policy as and when required. If any difficulty arises in giving effect to any provision of this policy, the Energy and Petrochemicals Department shall issue clarifications and interpretations to such provisions of the policy as may appear to be necessary and expedient for removing the difficulties, either on its own or after hearing those parties who have represented a change in any provision. 

36. Power to interpret 

If there is any confusion or dispute about the meaning, intent, or purpose of any provision of this policy, the interpretations given by the Energy and Petrochemicals Department, Government of Gujarat, shall be final and binding on all concerned.
