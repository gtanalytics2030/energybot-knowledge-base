**PROVISION:** Section 3 - Title and Operative Period

**TITLE:** Policy Title, Operative Period, and Project Eligibility Timeline

**SUMMARY:** This section establishes that the policy is known as the "Gujarat Integrated Renewable Energy Policy - 2025". The policy comes into effect from the date of notification and remains operative until 31st December 2030 or until notification of a new policy, whichever is earlier. RE projects that are registered and commissioned during the operative period are eligible for benefits and incentives outlined in the policy for a period of 25 years from commissioning or the lifespan of the RE project as defined by GERC/Ministry of Power/MNRE, whichever is earlier. The previous Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy 2023 and its amendments are superseded. Existing projects under the 2023 Policy may complete implementation within the time specified in their agreement or six months from this policy's notification, whichever is later, after which they are governed by the 2025 Policy.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Policy name; Policy title
- Operative period; Duration (until 31 Dec 2030)
- Notification date; Policy commencement
- Project registration and commissioning eligibility
- Benefit period (25 years)
- Lifespan definition (GERC/MoP/MNRE)
- Supersession of 2023 Policy
- Transition provisions for existing projects
- Implementation completion deadlines

**TEXT:**

This Policy shall be known as the "Gujarat Integrated Renewable Energy Policy - 2025".

This Policy will come into effect from the date of notification and shall remain in operation up to 31st December 2030 or till notification of the new Policy, whichever is earlier.

Renewable Energy Projects that are registered and commissioned during the operative period will be eligible for the benefits and incentives outlined in this Policy. These benefits will be applicable for a period of 25 years from the date of commissioning or the lifespan of the RE project, as defined by GERC/MoP/MNRE, whichever is earlier.

The Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy 2023, notified vide G.R. dated October 04, 2023, and its subsequent amendments shall stand superseded.

RE projects which are under implementation under the Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy 2023 may be allowed to complete the project within the time period mentioned in the agreement/sanction/allotment letter or six months from the notification of this Policy, whichever is later. Thereafter, the RE projects shall be governed as per this Policy.

**TITLE:** Scope of the Gujarat Integrated Renewable Energy Policy - 2025

**SUMMARY:** The policy scope encompasses six key areas establishing the technologies and approaches covered: (a) All ground-mounted solar, rooftop solar, floating solar, wind, rooftop wind, rooftop wind-solar hybrid, and wind-solar hybrid projects with or without Battery Energy Storage Systems, including independent grid-connected BESS, are covered; (b) Off-grid RE projects with or without BESS are eligible subject to State Nodal Agency's Standard Operation Procedure; (c) Research, development, and deployment of emerging renewable technologies including ocean energy, geothermal energy, floating solar, Concentrated Solar Thermal (CST), Building-Integrated Photovoltaics (BIPV), Rail/Road-Integrated Photovoltaics (RIPV), Agriphotovoltaics (AgriPV), and vertical-axis wind turbines with other RE advancements; (d) Repowering and life extension of wind projects; (e) Policy provisions do not apply to RE projects for Green Hydrogen and Green Ammonia supply or Pumped Storage Hydro (PSP) projects, which will be covered under separate policies; (f) Skill development initiatives to generate green employment opportunities.

**TEXT:**

Scope:

a) All ground-mounted solar, rooftop solar, floating solar, wind, rooftop wind, rooftop wind-solar hybrid, and wind-solar hybrid projects, with or without Battery Energy Storage Systems, shall be covered under this Policy. This also includes an independent grid-connected Battery Energy Storage System (BESS).

b) Off-grid RE projects with or without BESS will be eligible under this Policy, subject to adherence to the State Nodal Agency's Standard Operation Procedure prescribed by the State Nodal Agency.

f) Skill development initiatives to generate green employment opportunities.

**SUMMARY:** Section 7.2 establishes the permissible uses for RE projects developed under the policy. ## CHUNK 029

**DOCUMENT:** Gujarat Integrated Renewable Energy Policy – 2025

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Policy Document

**HIERARCHY:** Section 7 (Eligibility)

**PROVISION:** Section 7.3 - No Capacity Restriction for RE Projects

**TITLE:** Eligibility: No Capacity Ceiling for RE Project Development

**SUMMARY:** Section 7.3 provides that there is no capacity restriction for setting up RE projects for captive use or for selling electricity to third-party consumers with respect to the consumer's contracted demand or sanctioned load (measured in kW/kVA/MVA) with Distribution Companies. The AC (Alternating Current) capacity of the RE project is considered as the project's installed capacity, meaning developers have full flexibility in project sizing relative to consumer demand. This provision eliminates the traditional constraint where RE project capacity had to be limited to contracted demand, enabling optimal resource utilization and greater renewable energy deployment.

**KEY TOPICS:**
- No capacity restriction; Capacity ceiling removal
- Captive use projects; Third-party sale projects
- Contracted demand; Sanctioned load
- kW/kVA/MVA measurements; DISCOM capacity
- AC capacity; Installed capacity definition
- Project sizing flexibility; Resource optimization
- Capacity unlimited authorization

**TEXT:**

There shall be no capacity restriction for setting up of RE projects for captive use or for selling electricity to any third-party consumer with respect to the consumer's contracted demand/ sanctioned load (kW/kVA/MVA) with DISCOMs. The AC capacity of the RE project shall be considered as the project's installed capacity.
