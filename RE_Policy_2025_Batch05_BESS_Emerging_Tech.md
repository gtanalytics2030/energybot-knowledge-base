**HIERARCHY:** Section 11 (Battery Energy Storage System)

**PROVISION:** Section 11.1 - BESS Integration Scope and Authorization

**TITLE:** Battery Energy Storage System: Co-located, Distant, and Grid-Connected Integration Framework

**SUMMARY:** Section 11.1 establishes that integration of co-located and/or distant located and/or grid-connected independent Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) is authorized for existing or proposed Renewable Energy (RE) projects. Such integration must comply with prevailing regulations, guidelines, and orders issued by relevant authorities (GERC, CERC, MoP, MNRE) as amended from time to time. This flexible framework enables three deployment models: (1) Co-located BESS—storage at the same site as RE generation for immediate flexibility; (2) Distant/remote BESS—storage away from generation sites for grid-level optimization; (3) Independent grid-connected BESS—standalone storage without RE project coupling. All models operate within evolving regulatory frameworks ensuring compatibility with national standards.

**TEXT:**
