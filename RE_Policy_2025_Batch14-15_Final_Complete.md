**PROVISION:** Section 28.1 - Sustainable Procurement and Low-Carbon Supply Chain Standards

**TITLE:** Supply Chain: Low-Carbon Equipment Procurement and Sustainable Manufacturing

**SUMMARY:** Section 28.1 establishes sustainable procurement standards ensuring RE equipment manufacturing and supply chains minimize embodied carbon and environmental impact. Supply chain greening framework: (1) **Low-Carbon Equipment Standards:** (a) **Solar Panels:** Eligible panels must demonstrate: (i) Manufacturing carbon intensity ≤50 kg CO2/kW installed capacity (via lifecycle assessment per ISO 14040), (ii) Example: 400 W panel, 50 kg CO2/kW = 20 kg CO2 per module (typical for modern high-efficiency panels), (iii) High-carbon panels (>100 kg CO2/kW, typical for older thick-crystal designs) may be deselected. GEDA maintains approved panel list (low-carbon only); developers restricted to GEDA-approved manufacturers. (b) **Wind Turbines:** Eligible turbines must demonstrate: (i) Manufacturing carbon intensity ≤3-4 kg CO2/kW installed (blade + tower + nacelle manufacturing), (ii) Lifecycle GHG emissions (manufacture → transport → installation → operation → decommissioning) ≤10 g CO2/kWh generated (benchmark: thermal 800-1000 g CO2/kWh, offshore wind 10-15 g CO2/kWh typical, onshore 6-8 g CO2/kWh). (c) **BESS (Battery Energy Storage):** Eligible batteries must: (i) Demonstrate low-carbon manufacturing (≤80 kg CO2/kWh energy capacity for lithium-ion), (ii) Recyclability documentation (≥80% material recovery post-end-of-life), (iii) Safety certification (no hazardous substance prohibition). (2) **Recycled Content Requirements:** (a) **Steel Components:** Turbine towers, BESS frames, equipment racks must contain ≥80% recycled steel (vs. virgin steel). Recycled steel saves ~70% embodied carbon vs. virgin. (b) **Copper & Aluminum:** Electrical cables, transformers, inverter enclosures must use ≥70% recycled copper/aluminum content (saves ~95% carbon vs. virgin). (c) **Validation:** Suppliers certify recycled content via (i) Material documentation (supplier mill certifications for recycled origin), (ii) Audits: GEDA conducts 10% annual audit sample verifying recycled content claims (prevents greenwashing). (3) **Green Logistics & Transportation:** (a) **Carbon-Neutral Transport:** Equipment transport from manufacturer to site must utilize low-carbon methods: (i) Shipping by sea (lowest carbon per tonne-km): <10 g CO2/tonne-km (marine fuel-efficient vessels), (ii) Rail transport: <30 g CO2/tonne-km (electric rail preferred), (iii) Road transport: minimize (only final-mile from rail/port to site; if necessary, use electric trucks or LNG trucks ~200 g CO2/tonne-km, vs. diesel 400+ g CO2/tonne-km). (b) **Supply Chain Mapping:** Large projects (≥25 MW) must map supply chain (manufacturer → shipping → port → rail → site) and document carbon footprint—submitted to GEDA for review (non-binding, informational). (c) **Offset Option:** If transport carbon >10 tonnes CO2 equivalent, developer may purchase carbon offsets (₹1000/tonne CO2) to neutralize transport emissions. (4) **Circular Design Requirements:** (a) **Design-for-Disassembly:** Equipment must be designed for easy disassembly post-end-of-life—components segregated into material streams (glass, metal, plastic, silicon, etc.) enabling efficient recycling. (b) **Hazardous Substance Minimization:** Equipment must minimize hazardous substances (lead-free, cadmium-free per RoHS directive compatibility standards), reducing recycling cost and environmental risk. (c) **Durability Specification:** Equipment must achieve 25-year design life minimum (matching project life per policy), reducing per-year environmental burden. (5) **Supplier Selection & Compliance:** (a) **Procurement Process:** Developers issue RFQ (Request for Quote) to equipment suppliers specifying: (i) ALMM compliance, (ii) Low-carbon manufacturing (<50 kg CO2/kW), (iii) Recycled content (80% steel, 70% copper/aluminum), (iv) Carbon-neutral transport (sea/rail preference), (v) Circular design (disassembly, hazardous-substance minimization, 25-year life). (b) **Supplier Sustainability Certification:** Preferred suppliers: ISO 14001 (Environmental Management), ISO 50001 (Energy Management), or Carbon Trust certified. (c) **Audit & Compliance:** GEDA conducts annual audits (10% project sample) verifying supplier claims—spot-check manufacturer facilities for low-carbon practices. Non-compliance triggers: product rejection, supplier blacklist (1-2 year ban from ALMM list). (6) **Policy Incentive:** (a) **Cost vs. Benefit:** Low-carbon equipment typically costs 5-10% more than conventional (higher manufacturing cost), but: (i) Grid charges reduction (0.5-1% wheeling discount for certified low-carbon projects), (ii) Carbon credit premium (Gold Standard credits ₹2000-5000/credit vs. standard ₹1500, ₹0.50/kWh revenue uplift), (iii) Net NPV impact: Positive over 25-year project life (₹50-100 lakh additional benefit). (b) **Target:** 50% of RE projects using low-carbon equipment by 2030 (mandatory 100% by 2035 per 5-year review recommendation). Supply chain greening quantifies "production footprint" enabling holistic carbon accounting (not just operation, but manufacture + transport + decommissioning).

**KEY TOPICS:**
- Low-carbon equipment standards
- Solar panels: ≤50 kg CO2/kW manufacturing intensity
- Wind turbines: ≤3-4 kg CO2/kW manufacturing, ≤10 g CO2/kWh lifecycle
- BESS batteries: ≤80 kg CO2/kWh, ≥80% recyclability
- Recycled content: 80% steel, 70% copper/aluminum
- Green logistics: sea/rail (low carbon), minimize road transport
- Carbon-neutral transport option (offset purchase ₹1000/tonne CO2)
- Design-for-disassembly (post-end-of-life segregation)
- Hazardous substance minimization (lead-free, RoHS compliance)
- 25-year design life durability
- Supplier sustainability certification (ISO 14001, 50001, Carbon Trust)
- Annual GEDA audits (10% project sample)
- Product rejection and supplier blacklist (non-compliance)
- Cost premium (5-10% higher equipment cost)
- Revenue benefits (grid charge reduction, carbon credit premium)
- 50% adoption target 2030, 100% by 2035

**TEXT:**

Supply chain greening standards: Solar ≤50 kg CO2/kW manufacturing, wind ≤3-4 kg CO2/kW manufacturing, BESS ≤80 kg CO2/kWh. Recycled content: 80% steel, 70% copper/aluminum. Green logistics: prioritize sea/rail (≤10-30 g CO2/tonne-km), minimize road; offset option ₹1000/tonne CO2. Design-for-disassembly, hazardous-substance-free, 25-year durability. Supplier sustainability certification (ISO 14001, 50001, Carbon Trust). GEDA audits 10% sample annually; non-compliance → product rejection, blacklist. Equipment cost premium 5-10%, offset by grid charge reduction (0.5-1%) and carbon credit premium (₹0.50/kWh). Target: 50% adoption 2030, 100% by 2035.

**PROVISION:** Section 29.1 - Green Jobs Target and Employment Creation Goal

**TITLE:** Green Jobs: Target of 100,000 Employment by 2030 and Skills Development

**SUMMARY:** Section 29.1 establishes green jobs target—direct and indirect employment in renewable energy sector. Green jobs framework: (1) **Employment Target:** (a) **2030 Target:** 100,000 cumulative jobs in RE sector by 2030 (direct + indirect), (b) **Breakdown:** (i) Direct jobs (~60,000): Manufacturing (panels, turbines, inverters), Installation (construction, electrical), O&M (operation, maintenance), (ii) Indirect jobs (~40,000): Supply chain (logistics, warehousing), Engineering (design, consulting), Administration (project management, finance). (c) **Current Baseline (2025):** Estimated 30,000-40,000 jobs existing—target requires 60,000-70,000 net new jobs over 5 years (12,000-14,000 jobs/year growth). (d) **Achievability:** With 100 GW deployment by 2030, employment multiplier ~1 job per MW installed (1 job/MW is standard for solar/wind industry globally), supporting 100,000 job target. (2) **Gender Equity Target:** (a) **Women Participation:** Minimum 40-50% women employees in RE sector by 2030 (vs. current 15-20% women in manufacturing/construction). (b) **Targeted Roles:** (i) Manufacturing: Women in panel assembly, testing, quality control roles (30-40% women typical), (ii) Electrical installation: Women electricians, cable pulling teams (20-30% target, overcoming male-dominated culture), (iii) O&M: Women technicians, supervisors (40-50% target for customer-facing maintenance roles). (c) **Support Mechanism:** GSDM (Gujarat State Development and Mission) subsidizes women training programs (₹50,000-100,000 per trainee, 6-12 month course) enabling women entry into technical roles. (d) **Incentive:** Companies hiring 50%+ women receive: (i) Tax credit (₹2 lakh per woman employee/year), (ii) Training subsidy (GSDM covers 50% of training cost), (iii) Public recognition (awards, media coverage). (3) **Job Types & Skill Requirements:** (a) **Manufacturing Jobs:** (i) Solar panel assembly: Semi-skilled laborers, 2-3 month training, ₹15,000-25,000/month wage, (ii) Turbine blade production: Skilled composite workers, 6-12 month training, ₹25,000-40,000/month. (b) **Installation Jobs:** (i) Solar installation crews: Roofers, electricians, 3-6 month training, ₹20,000-35,000/month, (ii) Wind turbine erection: High-altitude workers, rope access certified, 6-12 month training, ₹40,000-60,000/month (higher risk, premium wage). (c) **O&M Jobs:** (i) Site operators: Monitor generation, troubleshoot, ₹20,000-30,000/month, 6-month training, (ii) Technicians: Repair inverters, transformers, ₹25,000-40,000/month, 12-month apprenticeship, (iii) Supervisors: Manage team, safety compliance, ₹40,000-60,000/month, experience-based. (d) **Engineering/Design:** (i) Project engineers, energy auditors, solar consultants, ₹50,000-100,000/month, Bachelor's degree + 2-3 month RE specialization, (ii) Supply chain managers, procurement officers, ₹40,000-80,000/month, management experience. (4) **Job Creation Pathway:** (a) **Year 0-1 (2025-26):** 100 GW deployment ramp-up → 10,000-12,000 jobs created (manufacturing and installation jobs). (b) **Year 1-3 (2026-28):** 50 GW cumulative operation → 30,000-40,000 jobs (O&M jobs become significant, 25% of total). (c) **Year 3-5 (2028-30):** 100 GW cumulative operation, new 50 GW installation → 80,000-100,000 jobs (O&M + new installation both significant). (d) **Distribution:** By 2030: 40,000 manufacturing/installation, 40,000 O&M, 20,000 engineering/support = 100,000 total. (5) **Geographic Distribution:** (a) **High-Potential RE Zones:** Kutch, Jamnagar, Morbi (wind), Mehsana, Banaskantha (solar) → Concentration of 40-50% of jobs in these regions. (b) **Industrial Corridors:** Dedicated RE manufacturing clusters in Vadodara, Surat → 20,000 manufacturing jobs concentration. (c) **Distributed O&M:** Across all installation sites → 40,000 O&M jobs distributed geographically (local employment benefit). (6) **Economic Impact:** (a) **Average Wage:** ₹25,000-30,000/month across all roles = ₹3-3.6 lakh/year, (b) **Annual Wage Bill:** 100,000 jobs × ₹3.3 lakh/year = ₹330 crore/year economic impact (consumer spending, tax revenue). (c) **Multiplier Effect:** Wage spending generates 1.5-2x indirect economic activity (supply chain, services) = ₹500-660 crore total economic impact/year. (7) **Policy Support & Facilitation:** (a) **RE Skilling Hub Program** (see 29.2) provides training infrastructure enabling job creation. (b) **GSDM Support:** ₹50-100 crore fund for women training, marginalized community programs. (c) **Industrial Policy Alignment:** RE jobs qualify under Gujarat Industrial Policy 2020—eligible for capital subsidy (₹5-10 lakh per manufacturing facility) and interest subsidy (1-2% on bank loans). Green jobs target transforms RE from clean energy policy to economic development tool—provides livelihoods while decarbonizing grid.
