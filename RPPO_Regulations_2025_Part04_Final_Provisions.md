## Chunk 32

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Procurement of Energy from Renewable Sources) Regulations, 2025

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Regulation 12

**PROVISION:** Regulation 12 - Overriding Effect

**TITLE:** Regulation 12: Overriding Effect of RPPO Regulations

**SUMMARY:**
Regulation 12 establishes that the provisions of these RPPO Regulations 2025 and all orders/directions issued by the Commission under these regulations shall have overriding effect notwithstanding anything contained in any other GERC regulations relating to tariff determination and open access provisions under the Electricity Act 2003. This provision ensures that RPPO compliance takes precedence over conflicting tariff and open access requirements, establishing the primacy of renewable energy procurement objectives.

**KEY TOPICS:** Overriding Effect, Regulatory Supremacy, RPPO Precedence, Tariff Regulations Subordination, Open Access Regulations Subordination, Electricity Act 2003, Regulatory Hierarchy, Conflict Resolution

**TEXT:**

12. Overriding effect. - The provisions of these Regulations, including the orders or directions issued by the Commission from time to time under these Regulations, shall have effect notwithstanding anything contained in any other Regulations, framed by the Commission, relating to the determination of tariff, and/or making provisions for Open Access under the Act.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Electricity Act 2003 (Section 181, 61, 86); GERC Tariff Regulations; GERC Open Access Regulations; Regulation 14 (Issue of orders and directions)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Regulatory hierarchy; Conflict resolution between RPPO and tariff/open access requirements; Commission's regulatory powers under Electricity Act 2003

## Chunk 33

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Procurement of Energy from Renewable Sources) Regulations, 2025

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Regulation 13

**PROVISION:** Regulation 13 - Power to Remove Difficulties

**TITLE:** Regulation 13: Commission's Authority to Resolve Implementation Difficulties

**SUMMARY:**
Regulation 13 grants the Commission authority to remove any difficulties arising from implementing these regulations through general or special orders, either suo-motu or on application by the licensee, generator, or other person. The Commission may direct appropriate action not inconsistent with the Electricity Act 2003. This provision provides flexibility to address unanticipated implementation challenges while maintaining consistency with the parent legislation.

**KEY TOPICS:** Difficulty Resolution, Commission Discretion, Suo-Motu Order, Implementation Challenge, Regulatory Flexibility, Application-Based Relief, Electricity Act Consistency, Administrative Authority, General Order, Special Order

**TEXT:**

13. Power to remove difficulties. If any difficulty arises in giving effect to any of the provisions of these Regulations, the Commission may, either Suo- Motu or on an application made to it, by general or special order, direct the licensee/generator or any other person to take suitable action, not being inconsistent with the Act, which appears to the Commission to be necessary or expedient for the purpose of removing the difficulty.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Electricity Act 2003 (Section 181, general regulatory powers); Regulation 15 (Saving of inherent powers); Regulation 14 (Issue of orders and directions)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Commission's exercise of discretion; Extraordinary circumstances; Implementation relief mechanisms; Public interest considerations

Regulation 14 empowers the Commission to issue orders and practice directions regarding implementation of these regulations, procedures for such implementation, and incidental/ancillary matters. ## Chunk 35

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Procurement of Energy from Renewable Sources) Regulations, 2025

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Regulation 15

**PROVISION:** Regulation 15 - Saving of Inherent Powers of the Commission

**TITLE:** Regulation 15: Preservation of Commission's Discretionary Authority

**SUMMARY:**
Regulation 15 preserves the Commission's inherent powers to adopt procedures at variance with the regulation provisions if the Commission deems it necessary or expedient due to special circumstances. The Commission must record reasons in writing for departing from specified procedures. This provision ensures the Commission retains discretionary flexibility for exceptional cases while maintaining procedural transparency through written justifications.

**KEY TOPICS:** Inherent Powers, Procedural Discretion, Special Circumstances, Exceptional Cases, Written Reasoning, Regulatory Flexibility, Deviation Authority, Transparency Requirement, Reasoned Departure

**TEXT:**

15. Saving of Inherent Powers of the Commission. Nothing contained in these Regulations shall limit or otherwise affect the inherent powers of the Commission to adopt a procedure, which is at variance with any of the provisions of these Regulations, if the Commission, in view of the special circumstances of the matter or class of matters and for reasons to be recorded in writing, deems it necessary or expedient to depart from the procedure specified in these Regulations.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Electricity Act 2003 (Commission's inherent authority); Regulation 13 (Power to remove difficulties); Regulation 14 (Issue of orders and directions)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Special circumstances; Extraordinary circumstances; Reasoned decision-making; Regulatory deviation authority; Transparency in decision-making

**PROVISION:** Regulation 16(1) - Repeal of Earlier RPPO Regulations

**TITLE:** Regulation 16(1): Repeal of RPPO Regulations 2005 and 2010

**SUMMARY:**
Regulation 16(1) repeals the GERC (Power Procurement from Renewable Sources) Regulations 2005, the GERC (Power Procurement from Renewable Sources) Regulations 2010, and all amendments thereto, effective from the date these 2025 Regulations come into force. This provision completes the transition from the earlier regulatory framework to the new comprehensive RPPO regime.

**KEY TOPICS:** Repeal, Earlier Regulations, Regulatory Succession, 2005 Regulations, 2010 Regulations, Effective Date, Regulatory Transition, Framework Update

**TEXT:**

16. Repeal and Savings. -

(1) Save as otherwise provided in these Regulations, the GERC (Power Procurement from Renewable Sources) Regulations, 2005 and GERC (Power Procurement from Renewable Sources) Regulations, 2010 and all subsequent amendments thereto shall stand repealed from the date of coming into force of these Regulations.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** GERC RPPO Regulations 2005; GERC RPPO Regulations 2010; Regulation 1 (Commencement); Regulation 16(2) (Savings clause)

## Chunk 37

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Procurement of Energy from Renewable Sources) Regulations, 2025

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Regulation 16(2)

**PROVISION:** Regulation 16(2) - Savings Clause for Earlier Actions

**TITLE:** Regulation 16(2): Validation of Actions Under Repealed Regulations

**SUMMARY:**
Regulation 16(2) provides continuity for actions taken under the repealed 2005 and 2010 regulations. Any action taken, permission granted, document executed, or direction given under the repealed regulations shall be deemed valid under the corresponding provisions of these 2025 Regulations, provided such action is not inconsistent with the new regulations. Additionally, renewable energy certificates issued under the repealed CERC REC Regulations 2010 shall be governed by the CERC REC Regulations 2022. This savings clause ensures a smooth transition while preventing conflicts between old and new regulatory requirements.

**KEY TOPICS:** Savings Clause, Earlier Actions Validation, Regulatory Continuity, Deemed Compliance, Certificate Treatment, CERC REC Regulations, Transition Safeguard, Inconsistency Resolution

**TEXT:**

(2) Notwithstanding such repeal:

(a) anything done or any action taken or required to be taken or purported to have been done or taken or permission granted, or any document or instrument executed or any direction given under the repealed Regulations shall, in so far as it is not inconsistent with the provisions of these Regulation, be deemed to have been done or taken under the corresponding provisions of these Regulations;

(b) as far as treatment of Certificates issued under the provisions of repealed CERC (REC) Regulations, 2010 shall be governed as per the provisions of CERC (REC) Regulations, 2022.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Repealed regulations (GERC RPPO Regulations 2005 and 2010); CERC REC Regulations 2010; CERC REC Regulations 2022; Regulation 16(1) (Repeal clause); Regulation 6 (Certificates)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Earlier regulatory framework; Certificate grandfathering; Regulatory transition; Inconsistency management; Earlier obligations and permissions
