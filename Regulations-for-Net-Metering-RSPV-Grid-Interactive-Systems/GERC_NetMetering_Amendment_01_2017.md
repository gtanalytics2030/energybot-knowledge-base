**TITLE:** Short Title, Extent and Commencement of First Amendment Regulations 2017

**SUMMARY:**
The First Amendment Regulations establish their short title, territorial scope, and effective date. These regulations shall be called the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) (First Amendment) Regulations, 2017. They extend to the whole state of Gujarat and come into force from the date of their publication in the Official Gazette (6 October 2017).

(i) These Regulations shall be called the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) (First Amendment) Regulations, 2017.

(ii) These Regulations shall extend to the whole of the State of Gujarat.

2) These Regulations shall come into force with effect from the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

## CHUNK 002

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) (First Amendment) Regulations, 2017

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Notification

**HIERARCHY:** Notification No. 2 of 2017 / Amendment in Regulation 6.2

**PROVISION:** Amendment in Regulation 6.2 of Principal Regulations

**TITLE:** Amendment to Regulation 6.2 - Maximum Rooftop Solar PV System Capacity for Eligible Consumers

**SUMMARY:**
The First Amendment modifies Regulation 6.2 of the Principal Regulations to establish capacity limits for rooftop solar PV systems. For non-residential consumers, the maximum capacity shall not exceed 50% of the consumer's sanctioned load or contract demand. For residential consumers, the rooftop solar PV system capacity shall be permitted irrespective of sanctioned load or contract demand. This distinction recognizes different consumption patterns between residential and non-residential premises.

**KEY TOPICS:** Rooftop Solar PV, Capacity Limit, Sanctioned Load, Contract Demand, Residential Consumers, Non-Residential Consumers, System Capacity

**TEXT:**
3) Amendment in Regulation 6.2 of the Principal Regulations:

The first para of Regulation 6.2 shall stand amended as under:

The maximum Rooftop Solar PV System capacity to be installed at any Eligible Consumer's premises except Residential Consumers shall be upto a maximum of 50% of consumer's sanctioned load/contract demand; whereas in case of Residential Consumers, the Rooftop Solar PV System capacity shall be irrespective of their sanctioned load/contract demand.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Principal Regulation 6.2; Regulation 6.1 (Eligible Consumer definition); Regulation 8 (Connectivity Levels)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Related to Technical Feasibility Report requirements in Regulation 7; Interconnection Agreement Clause 1.3; Modified further in Second Amendment (Regulation 6.2) and Third Amendment (Regulation 6.2)

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Notification

**HIERARCHY:** Notification No. 2 of 2017 / Amendment in Regulation 7

**PROVISION:** Amendment in Regulation 7 of Principal Regulations

**TITLE:** Amendment to Regulation 7 - Capacity of Solar Rooftop in Application to Distribution Licensee

**SUMMARY:**
The First Amendment modifies the activity table under Regulation 7 (Application to Distribution Licensee) to clarify that the capacity of solar rooftop to be installed shall be subject to Regulation 6.2. This administrative change ensures that capacity specifications in applications are aligned with the mandatory capacity limits established by Regulation 6.2, preventing non-compliant applications at the application stage.

## CHUNK 004

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) (First Amendment) Regulations, 2017

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Notification

**HIERARCHY:** Notification No. 2 of 2017 / Amendment in Clause 1.3 of Annexure IV

**PROVISION:** Amendment in Clause 1.3 of Annexure IV - Interconnection Agreement

**TITLE:** Amendment to Interconnection Agreement (Clause 1.3) - Capacity Compliance for Non-Residential Consumers

**SUMMARY:**
The First Amendment introduces a capacity compliance clause into the Interconnection Agreement between Distribution Licensee and Solar Rooftop PV project owner. Non-residential consumers must ensure that rooftop solar capacity does not exceed 50% of contract load or sanctioned load during the initial 2 years of connectivity agreement. Residential consumers face no such capacity restriction. This creates a binding contractual obligation that mirrors Regulation 6.2 requirements and enforces compliance during the critical early operational period.

**KEY TOPICS:** Interconnection Agreement, Net Metering, Capacity Restriction, Contract Demand, Sanctioned Load, Residential Consumers, Non-Residential Consumers, Compliance Period

**TEXT:**
5) Amendment in Clause 1.3 of Annexure – IV, Inter connection Agreement between Distribution Licensee and Solar Rooftop PV project owner, Net Metering Inter Connection Agreement of the Principal Regulations:

Clause 1.3 of Annexure – IV, Inter connection Agreement between Distribution Licensee and Solar Roof top PV project owner, Net Metering Inter Connection Agreement of the principal Regulations shall be amended as under:

Consumer other than Residential Consumer shall ensure capacity of Rooftop Solar not to exceed 50% of his contract load/demand/sanctioned load during initial 2 years of the connectivity agreement; whereas in case of Residential Consumers, the Rooftop Solar PV System capacity shall be irrespective of their sanctioned load/contract demand.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Annexure IV (Principal Regulations) - Interconnection Agreement; Regulation 6.2 (as amended); Regulation 1.2 (Eligibility in Interconnection Agreement); Definition of Consumer per Regulation 2.1

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Enforces Regulation 6.2 capacity limits; Interconnection process under Regulation 7; Modified substantially in Third Amendment (Regulation 6.1 and new Clause 1.3 provisions for prosumers and third-party sale)

**PROVISION:** Administrative Header and Authority

**TITLE:** Regulatory Authority and Powers for First Amendment

**SUMMARY:**
The notification establishes the legal authority and powers under which the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission made the First Amendment Regulations. Authority is derived from Sections 61, 86, and 181 of the Electricity Act, 2003 (Act No. 36 of 2003), and all other enabling powers. The amendment was made after previous publication as required by law. The Secretary of GERC (Roopwant Singh, IAS) signed the notification on 6 October 2017 in Gandhinagar, Gujarat.
