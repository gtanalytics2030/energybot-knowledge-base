## CHUNK 1

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) Regulations, 2016

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation (Notification)

**HIERARCHY:** Preliminary / Section 1

**PROVISION:** Section 1 (Short title, and commencement)

**TITLE:** Short Title and Commencement

**SUMMARY:**
This Regulation is officially titled "Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) Regulations, 2016" (Notification No. 5/2016, dated 18 June 2016). The Regulations establish the framework for grid-connected rooftop solar photovoltaic systems with net metering arrangements in Gujarat. The Regulations came into force on the date of publication in the Official Gazette. These Regulations are issued under the powers conferred by Sections 61, 66, 86(1)(e), and 181 of the Electricity Act, 2003.

**KEY TOPICS:** Net Metering, Rooftop Solar PV, Grid Interactive Systems, Regulatory Framework, GERC Notification 5/2016, Commencement Date

**TEXT:**
These Regulations shall be called the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) Regulations, 2016.

These Regulations shall come into force from the date of its notification in the Official Gazette.

Date: 18/06/2016
Notification No. 5/2016

In exercise of powers conferred under Sections 61, 66, 86(1)(e) and 181 of the Electricity Act, 2003 (Act 36 of 2003) and all other powers enabling it in this behalf, the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission hereby makes the following Regulations for the grid connected Solar Rooftop Photovoltaic System.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Electricity Act, 2003 (Sections 61, 66, 86(1)(e), 181)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Applicable to all distribution licensees and consumers in the State of Gujarat (per Regulation 3.1); Supersedes or amends any prior net metering arrangements; Referenced in subsequent regulations on eligibility, capacity targets, and settlement procedures.

## CHUNK 2

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) Regulations, 2016

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Preliminary / Section 2 (Definitions and Interpretations)

**PROVISION:** Definition – "Net Metering"

**TITLE:** Definition of Net Metering

**SUMMARY:**
Net metering is an arrangement under which a rooftop solar PV system installed at an eligible consumer's premises delivers surplus electricity to the distribution licensee after offsetting the electricity supplied by the distribution licensee to the consumer during the applicable billing period. This definition emphasizes that the consumer uses the net metering mechanism to balance generation and consumption within each billing cycle, with surplus being fed back to the grid for compensation.

**KEY TOPICS:** Net Metering, Surplus Electricity, Billing Period, Grid Injection, Offset, Consumer Load

**TEXT:**
"Net Metering" means an arrangement under which Rooftop Solar PV System installed at Eligible Consumer's premises delivers surplus electricity, if any, to the Distribution Licensee after off-setting the electricity supplied by the distribution licensee to such Eligible Consumer during the applicable billing period.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Regulation 9 (Energy Accounting and Settlement), Section 2.1 (Definitions)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Related to billing cycle definition (Regulation 2.1(e)), Energy Accounting procedures (Regulation 9), Net meter definition (Regulation 2.1(q)), Banking arrangements

## CHUNK 3

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) Regulations, 2016

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Preliminary / Section 2 (Definitions and Interpretations)

**PROVISION:** Definition – "Eligible Consumer"

**TITLE:** Definition of Eligible Consumer

**SUMMARY:**
An eligible consumer is a consumer of electricity in the area of supply of the distribution licensee who intends to use a rooftop solar PV system for their own use, provided the system is self-owned. The consumer must own or be in legal possession of the premises (including rooftop, terrace, building, infrastructure, or open land) on which the system is installed. This definition establishes that eligibility is based on consumer status with the local distribution licensee and legal ownership/possession of the installation location.

**KEY TOPICS:** Eligible Consumer, Self-Owned System, Legal Possession, Rooftop Installation, Consumer Status

**TEXT:**
"Eligible Consumer" means a consumer of electricity in the area of supply of the distribution licensee, who intends to use a Rooftop Solar PV System, given that such system is self owned, to offset part or all of the consumer's own electrical requirements.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Regulation 6.1 (detailed eligibility criteria for Rooftop Solar PV System with net metering), Section 2.1 (Definitions)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Connected to Regulation 6 (Eligible Consumer and individual project capacity), which expands on eligibility requirements including consumer status with local licensee and legal possession; related to premises definition (Regulation 2.1(s))

## CHUNK 4

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) Regulations, 2016

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Preliminary / Section 2 (Definitions and Interpretations)

**PROVISION:** Definition – "Rooftop Solar PV Power Plant" / "Rooftop Solar PV System"

**TITLE:** Definition of Rooftop Solar PV System

**SUMMARY:**
A rooftop solar PV system is a solar photovoltaic power plant installed on rooftops, ground-mounted surfaces, or open land at consumer premises that converts sunlight directly into electricity through photovoltaic technology. The system may be self-owned and located on the consumer's legal property. This definition encompasses both rooftop-mounted and ground-mounted configurations at consumer premises, distinguishing small distributed systems from utility-scale solar installations.

**KEY TOPICS:** Rooftop Solar PV, Photovoltaic Technology, Ground-Mounted Solar, Distributed Generation, Solar Panel Installation

**TEXT:**
"Rooftop Solar PV Power Plant" or "Rooftop Solar PV System" or "Solar Generating Plant" means the Solar Photo Voltaic Power Plant including small solar systems installed on the rooftops/ground mounted or open land of consumer premises that uses sunlight for direct conversion into electricity through photovoltaic technology.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Regulation 2.1 (Definitions), Regulation 3.2 (Scope - system must be located in consumer's premises)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Related to renewable energy system definition (Regulation 2.1(v)), interconnection point definition (Regulation 2.1(n)), premises definition (Regulation 2.1(s)); technical requirements in Regulation 8

## CHUNK 5

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) Regulations, 2016

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Preliminary / Section 2 (Definitions and Interpretations)

**PROVISION:** Definition – "Net meter"

**TITLE:** Definition of Net Meter

**SUMMARY:**
A net meter is an appropriate energy meter capable of recording both import and export of electricity, or alternatively a pair of meters with one meter recording import and another recording export. This two-directional metering capability enables the distribution licensee to accurately measure both the consumer's consumption from the grid and the surplus solar generation fed back to the distribution system.

**KEY TOPICS:** Net Meter, Bidirectional Meter, Import-Export Recording, Metering Equipment, Energy Accounting

**TEXT:**
"Net meter" means an appropriate energy meter capable of recording both import & export of electricity or a pair of meters one each for recording the import and export of electricity as the case may be.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Regulation 13 (Metering arrangement), Central Electricity Authority (Installation & Operation of Meters) Regulations, 2006

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Related to utility meter definition, solar meter definition (for generation measurement), ABT meter for industrial consumers; metering specifications detailed in Annexure-VI

## CHUNK 6

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) Regulations, 2016

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Preliminary / Section 2 (Definitions and Interpretations)

**PROVISION:** Definitions – "Distribution Licensee", "Interconnection Point", "Connected Load", "Contracted Load"

**TITLE:** Key Definitions: Distribution Licensee, Interconnection Point, and Load Parameters

**SUMMARY:**
The regulation defines distribution licensee as a person licensed under Section 14 of the Electricity Act to operate and maintain the distribution system and supply electricity in their area of supply (including UGVCL, DGVCL, PGVCL, MGVCL, and Torrent Power in Gujarat). The interconnection point is the interface where the rooftop solar PV system connects to the load side of the meter or distribution licensee cutout/switchgear at the consumer's premises. Connected load is the aggregate rated capacity of all energy-consuming devices at the premises that can operate simultaneously, while contracted/sanctioned load is the maximum demand agreed to be supplied by the licensee in the consumer agreement.

**KEY TOPICS:** Distribution Licensee, Interconnection Point, Connected Load, Contracted Load, Supply Area, Grid Connection Point

**TEXT:**
"Distribution licensee" or "licensee" or "supply licensee" means a person who is granted a licence under Section 14 of the Act authorizing him to operate and maintain a distribution system and supplying electricity to the consumers in his area of supply.

"Interconnection Point" means the interface of Rooftop Solar PV System connected with the load side of meter/Distribution licensee cut out/switch gear fixed in the premises of the consumer.

"Connected load" expressed in kW, kVA or HP, refers to aggregate of the manufacturer's rated capacities of all the energy consuming devices or apparatus connected with the Distribution licensee's service line on the consumer's premises which can be operated simultaneously. For the purpose of levy of any charges and for deciding the supply voltage, the connected load shall be determined as per the method prescribed in the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Electricity Supply Code and Related Matters) Regulations, 2015 and subsequent amendments thereto.

"Contracted load" or "Sanctioned load" or "Contracted demand" means the maximum demand in kW, kVA or HP, agreed to be supplied by the licensee and indicated in the agreement executed between the licensee and the consumer.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Electricity Act 2003 (Section 14), GERC Electricity Supply Code and Related Matters Regulations 2015, Regulation 5 (Capacity Targets), Regulation 6.2 (Maximum capacity as percentage of sanctioned load)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Distribution licensee categories in Annexure-I; interconnection standards in Regulation 8; load determination procedures in GERC Supply Code 2015; connected load used to determine net metering capacity limits (Regulation 6.2 specifies max 50% of sanctioned load)

## CHUNK 7

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) Regulations, 2016

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Preliminary / Section 2 (Definitions and Interpretations)

**PROVISION:** Definitions – "Average Power Purchase Cost (APPC)", "Billing Cycle", "Consumer"

**TITLE:** Key Definitions: APPC, Billing Cycle, and Consumer

**SUMMARY:**
Average Power Purchase Cost (APPC) is the weighted average pooled price at which the distribution licensee has purchased electricity in the previous year from all energy suppliers (long-term, medium-term, short-term basis) but excluding renewable energy purchases. The APPC determined for the year in which the rooftop solar PV system is commissioned applies for the entire 25-year project life. Billing cycle/period is the regular interval at which electricity bills are prepared for different consumer categories by the distribution licensee. Consumer is any person supplied electricity by a distribution licensee or government or other authorized entity, including any person whose premises is connected to receive electricity.

**KEY TOPICS:** APPC (Average Power Purchase Cost), Pooled Price, Billing Cycle, Consumer Definition, Power Purchase

**TEXT:**
"Average Power Purchase Cost" (APPC) means the Weighted Average Pooled Price at which the distribution licensee has purchased the electricity including cost of self generation, if any, in the previous year from all the energy suppliers on long-term, medium-term and short-term basis, but excluding energy purchased from renewable energy sources.

"Billing Cycle or Billing Period" means the period for which regular electricity bills are prepared for different categories of consumers by the Distribution licensee, as specified by the Commission.

"Consumer" means any person who is supplied with electricity for his own use by a Distribution licensee or the Government or by any other person engaged in the business of supplying electricity to the public under the Act or any other law for the time being in force and includes any person whose premises, for the time being, is connected for the purpose of receiving electricity with the works of a distribution licensee, the Government or such other person, as the case may be.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Regulation 9 (Energy Accounting and Settlement - specifies APPC applicability for surplus energy purchase), Regulation 2.1 (Definitions)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** APPC application in energy settlement provisions (Regulation 9.2, 9.3, 9.4); banking provisions within billing cycle (Regulation 9.2); tariff determination process

## CHUNK 9

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) Regulations, 2016

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Section 3 (Scope and Application)

**PROVISION:** Regulation 3 (Scope and Application)

**TITLE:** Scope and Application of Net Metering Regulations

**SUMMARY:**
These Regulations apply to all distribution licensees in Gujarat and their consumers receiving electricity supply within their licensed area, as well as consumers receiving electricity from their own generating source located in the distribution licensee's area. Eligible consumers may install rooftop solar PV systems under net metering arrangements subject to: (1) system capacity within permissible limits, (2) system located on consumer premises, and (3) safe parallel operation with the distribution licensee network in compliance with CEA Technical Standards 2007 and 2013, and GERC Open Access Regulations 2011. The Regulations do not preclude the distribution licensee or State Government from undertaking rooftop solar projects exceeding 1 MW capacity through alternative mechanisms.

**KEY TOPICS:** Scope, Application, Distribution Licensee Area, Eligible Consumer, System Capacity, Grid Interconnection, CEA Standards, Open Access

**TEXT:**
These Regulations shall apply to the distribution licensee and consumers of electricity of distribution licensee availing supply from it in its area of supply in the State of Gujarat, or consumers who are receiving electricity from its own generating source but situated in the distribution licensee area.

The Eligible Consumer may install the Rooftop Solar PV System under net metering arrangement which,
a) shall be within the permissible rated capacity as defined under these Regulations.
b) shall be located in the consumer's premises.
c) shall interconnect and operate safely in parallel with the distribution licensee network.

These Regulations do not preclude the right of relevant Distribution licensee or State Government Department/authorities to undertake Rooftop Solar PV projects above 1 MW capacity through alternative mechanisms.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Regulation 5 (Capacity Targets), Regulation 6 (Eligible Consumer and individual project capacity), Regulation 8 (Interconnection with the Grid), CEA Technical Standards Regulations 2007 and 2013, GERC Open Access Regulations 2011, Electricity Act 2003

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Capacity constraints in Regulation 5 (65% of transformer peak capacity) and Regulation 6.2 (50% of sanctioned load); interconnection standards in Regulation 8; Central Electricity Authority regulations provide technical framework

## CHUNK 10

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) Regulations, 2016

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Section 4 (General Principles)

**PROVISION:** Regulation 4 (General Principles)

**TITLE:** General Principles for Net Metering Provision

**SUMMARY:**
The distribution licensee shall provide net metering arrangements to eligible consumers on a non-discriminatory and first-come-first-served basis. This principle ensures equal access to net metering for all qualifying consumers without preferential treatment. Provision is conditional on: (1) consumer meeting specified eligibility criteria, (2) consumer demonstrating eligibility to install grid-connected rooftop solar PV systems of the rated capacity specified in the Regulations, and (3) interconnection compliance with CEA Technical Standards 2007, CEA Technical Standards for Distributed Generation 2013, and GERC Open Access Regulations 2011. These provisos ensure consumer-side compliance and system safety.

**KEY TOPICS:** Non-Discriminatory Access, First-Come-First-Served, Net Metering Provision, Eligibility Compliance, Technical Standards, Open Access

**TEXT:**
The distribution licensee shall provide the net metering arrangement to the eligible consumer, who intends to install grid connected Rooftop Solar PV System, in its area of supply on non-discriminatory and first come first served basis.

Provided that the consumer is eligible to install the grid connected Rooftop Solar PV System of the rated capacity as specified under these Regulations;

Provided further that the interconnection of such system with the grid is undertaken as specified under the Central Electricity Authority (Technical Standards for Connectivity to the Grid) Regulations, 2007 read with Central Electricity Authority (Technical Standards for Connectivity of Distributed Generated Resources) Regulations, 2013 and provisions of GERC (Terms and Condition of Intra-State Open Access) Regulations, 2011 as amended from time to time.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Regulation 5 (Capacity Targets), Regulation 6 (Eligible Consumer and individual project capacity), Central Electricity Authority (Technical Standards for Connectivity to the Grid) Regulations 2007, Central Electricity Authority (Technical Standards for Connectivity of Distributed Generation Resources) Regulations 2013, GERC (Terms and Conditions of Intra-State Open Access) Regulations 2011

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Non-discriminatory principle mirrors utility industry best practices; capacity specifications in Regulation 5 and 6 operationalize the eligibility proviso; technical standards are mandatory requirements codified in CEA regulations; open access framework in GERC 2011 Regulations governs interconnection
