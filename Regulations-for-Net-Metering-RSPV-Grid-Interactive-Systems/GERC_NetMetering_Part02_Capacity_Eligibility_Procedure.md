## CHUNK 11

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) Regulations, 2016

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Section 5 (Capacity Targets for Distribution Licensee)

**PROVISION:** Regulation 5 (Capacity Targets for Distribution Licensee)

**TITLE:** Capacity Targets and Transformer Capacity Constraints

**SUMMARY:**
Distribution licensees shall provide net metering arrangements to eligible consumers. The cumulative capacity of rooftop solar PV systems connected to any particular distribution transformer shall not exceed 65% of the peak capacity of that distribution transformer. This constraint prevents overloading the transformer with reverse power flow. Licensees must update information on available transformer capacity for net metering on a yearly basis and report to the Commission. If transformer or distribution network augmentation is required to accommodate a consumer's solar system, the cost of augmentation is borne by the consumer. The minimum rooftop solar PV system capacity is 1 kW. These provisions balance grid stability with consumer accessibility.

**KEY TOPICS:** Capacity Targets, Distribution Transformer, Peak Capacity, 65% Limit, Network Augmentation, Consumer Cost, Minimum Capacity (1 kW)

**TEXT:**
The Distribution Licensee shall provide net metering arrangement to Eligible Consumers.

Provided that the cumulative capacity to be allowed at a particular distribution transformer shall not exceed 65% of the peak capacity of the distribution transformer;

The distribution licensee shall update distribution transformer capacity available for connecting Rooftop Solar PV Systems under net metering arrangement on yearly basis and shall provide the information to the Commission.

Provided that if augmentation of transformer/distribution network is required, the cost of such augmentation shall be borne by the consumer. 

The capacity of Rooftop Solar PV System to be installed at the premises of any consumer shall not be less than one Kilo Watt (1kW).

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Regulation 6.2 (Individual project capacity not to exceed 50% of sanctioned load and 1 MW), Regulation 7 (Procedure for Application includes TFR assessment of transformer capacity), Regulation 8 (Interconnection technical requirements)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Transformer capacity assessment is performed during Technical Feasibility Report (TFR) in Regulation 7; system strengthening responsibility shared between licensee (for augmentation cost recovery) and consumer (for payment); impacts interconnection feasibility determination in Regulation 8

## CHUNK 12

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) Regulations, 2016

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Section 6 (Eligible Consumer and individual project capacity)

**PROVISION:** Regulation 6.1 (Eligible Consumer Criteria)

**TITLE:** Detailed Eligibility Criteria for Net Metering Consumers

**SUMMARY:**
In addition to the general definition of eligible consumer, specific detailed criteria apply: (1) Must be a current consumer of the local distribution licensee, (2) Must own or have legal possession of the premises including rooftop, terrace, building, infrastructure, or open land on which the solar PV system is installed, (3) Must connect the proposed rooftop solar PV system to the distribution licensee's distribution system, (4) Must consume all electricity generated from the rooftop solar PV system at the same premises; if unable to do so, must follow Regulation 9 provisions (banking and settlement). These criteria ensure the net metering mechanism serves consumer self-consumption while preventing unauthorized third-party sales or remote consumption arrangements.

**KEY TOPICS:** Eligible Consumer, Local Distribution Licensee, Legal Possession, Premises Ownership, Rooftop Installation, Self-Consumption, Same-Premises Requirement

**TEXT:**
In addition to the general eligibility defined in Regulation 2.1(l) of these Regulations, the Eligible Consumer for the Rooftop Solar PV System with net metering shall:

i. be a consumer of the local distribution licensee;

ii. own or be in legal possession of the premises including the rooftop or terrace or building or infrastructure or open areas of the land or part or combination thereof on which the Solar PV System is proposed to be installed;

iii. connect the proposed Rooftop Solar PV System to the Distribution System of the Licensee;

iv. consume all of the electricity generated from the Rooftop Solar PV System at the same premises. If the consumer is not able to consume all of generated electricity in the same premises, he shall be governed by Regulation 9 of these Regulations.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Regulation 2.1(l) (General definition of Eligible Consumer), Regulation 9 (Energy Accounting and Settlement for different consumer types and off-premises arrangements)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Legal possession requirement connects to application documentation (Regulation 7 requires NOC from co-owners); same-premises requirement relates to net metering definition (Regulation 2.1(p)) and settlement procedures (Regulation 9); consumer status determined via consumer number and connection records

## CHUNK 13

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) Regulations, 2016

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Section 6 (Eligible Consumer and individual project capacity)

**PROVISION:** Regulation 6.2 (Individual Project Capacity Limits)

**TITLE:** Individual Project Capacity Constraints and Limitations

**SUMMARY:**
The maximum rooftop solar PV system capacity at any eligible consumer's premises shall not exceed 50% of the consumer's sanctioned load/contract demand, with absolute limits of minimum 1 kW and maximum 1 MW. These capacity limits prevent over-generation relative to consumer load and grid stability concerns. The installed capacity must also comply with provisions for permitting consumer connections stated in GERC Electricity Supply Code 2015 and GERC Intra-State Open Access Regulations 2011. These cross-references ensure that solar installations align with broader consumer connection approval standards and open access framework for potential surplus energy transactions.

**KEY TOPICS:** Individual Project Capacity, 50% of Sanctioned Load, 1 kW Minimum, 1 MW Maximum, Capacity Limit, Consumer Load Matching

**TEXT:**
The maximum Rooftop Solar PV System capacity to be installed at any Eligible Consumer's premises shall be upto a maximum of 50% of consumer's sanctioned load/contract demand;

Provided that the installed capacity shall not be less than 1 kW and shall not exceed 1 MW;

Provided also that the installed capacity is aligned with the provisions for permitting consumer connections as stated in the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Electricity Supply Code and Related Matters) Regulations, 2015 as amended from time to time, read with the provisions of GERC (Terms and Conditions of the Intra-State Open Access) Regulations, 2011 as amended from time to time.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Regulation 5 (Capacity Targets specifying 65% limit at distribution transformer level), Regulation 7 (Application procedure includes TFR to verify sanctioned load), GERC Electricity Supply Code and Related Matters Regulations 2015, GERC Terms and Conditions of Intra-State Open Access Regulations 2011

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Capacity verification occurs during TFR stage (Regulation 7) when distribution licensee confirms consumer's sanctioned load/contract demand; 50% limit represents a balance between consumer self-consumption and grid stability; 1 MW cap aligns with regulatory definition of rooftop/distributed systems versus utility-scale projects; open access regulations framework enables surplus energy sale if applicable

## CHUNK 14

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) Regulations, 2016

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Section 7 (Procedure for Application) - Table: Stage 1-2

**PROVISION:** Regulation 7 (Procedure for Application) - Registration and Approval Activities

**TITLE:** Application Procedure Stage 1-2: GEDA Registration and Chief Electrical Inspector Approval

**SUMMARY:**
Net metering applicants must follow a time-bound procedural pathway. Stage 1: Applicant registers with GEDA (Gujarat Energy Development Agency) and receives a registration certificate within 5 days of submitting a duly completed application. Stage 2: Applicant submits documents to Chief Electrical Inspector (CEI) for approval of Single Line Diagram, Earthing Diagram, and Wiring Diagram; CEI provides approval within 10 days of receiving completed application. These early-stage approvals ensure that the solar system design complies with safety and electrical standards before technical feasibility assessment by the distribution licensee.

**KEY TOPICS:** GEDA Registration, Chief Electrical Inspector Approval, Single Line Diagram, Earthing Diagram, Wiring Diagram, Time Frame (5-10 days), Design Approval

**TEXT:**
Sr. No. | Activity | Sub Activity | Duration in day(s)
---|---|---|---
1. | Registration at GEDA | GEDA shall issue Registration Certificate | 5 days from receipt of duty completed application
2. | Approval from Chief Electrical Inspector | CEI shall approve Single Line Diagram, Earthing Diagram and Wiring Diagram | 10 days from receipt of duly completed application

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Regulation 7 (full procedural table), GEDA responsibilities under Government of Gujarat Solar Policy 2015, CEI authority under Electricity Act 2003 and Central Electricity Authority regulations

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** GEDA registration is prerequisite for Distribution Licensee application (Regulation 7, Stage 3); CEI approval documents are required submission with application (Regulation 7, Stage 3); CEI performs commissioning inspection and issues commissioning certificate (Regulation 7, Stage 15); establishes design compliance pathway before grid interconnection

**HIERARCHY:** Section 7 (Procedure for Application) - Table: Stage 3-5

**PROVISION:** Regulation 7 (Procedure for Application) - Application to Licensee and Technical Feasibility Report

**TITLE:** Application Procedure Stage 3-5: Licensee Application and Technical Feasibility Assessment

**SUMMARY:**
Stage 3: Applicant submits application to distribution licensee in prescribed format within 5 days of TFR notice, along with: capacity to be installed (max 50% of sanctioned load), GEDA registration copy, legal possession documents/co-owner NOCs, and CEI approval of electrical diagrams. Stage 4: Licensee's concerned Circle/Division is notified to prepare Technical Feasibility Report (TFR), providing applicant specifications for CTPT and meter. Stage 5: TFR (within 10 days) must include: consumer load details, 11 kV feeder name, transformer capacity, solar capacity already connected/approved on that transformer, assessment whether total solar capacity stays within transformer rated capacity, maximum demand in last year, no-dues certificate, no-legal-disputes certificate, and detailed estimate for system strengthening (recovered from applicant if required). TFR is the critical gate for determining technical feasibility and required network upgrades.

**KEY TOPICS:** Technical Feasibility Report (TFR), Licensee Application, Capacity Verification, Transformer Capacity Assessment, No-Dues Certificate, Legal Disputes, System Strengthening Estimate, Load Details

---|---|---|---
3. | Application to Distribution Licensee | Applicant shall submit application in prescribed format along with following compliance and documents to Distribution Licensee: • Capacity of Solar Rooftop to be installed (Maximum shall be 50% of the Consumer's sanctioned load) • Copy of registration at GEDA • Documents related to legal possession of roof-top /NOC of co-owners, in case of joint ownership. • Approval of Chief Electrical Inspector (CEI) for Single Line Diagram, Earthing Diagram and Wiring Diagram. | [Not separately specified]

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Section 7 (Procedure for Application) - Table: Stage 6-7

**PROVISION:** Regulation 7 (Procedure for Application) - In-Principle Consent and Connectivity Agreement

**TITLE:** Application Procedure Stage 6-7: Consent, Estimate, and Connectivity Agreement Execution

**SUMMARY:**
Stage 6 (within 5 days of receiving TFR): Distribution licensee Head Office issues one of two outcomes: (A) if no system strengthening required, letter granting in-principle consent for connectivity, specifying connectivity charges, and requiring execution of connectivity agreement within 15 days; or (B) if system strengthening is required, issuing detailed estimate for strengthening (payable within 30 days), connectivity charges, and requiring connectivity agreement execution. Stage 7 (within 5 days of agreement execution): Licensee issues letter authorizing project work completion within 6 months (Case 1: no strengthening) or within 6 months after payment of strengthening estimate and connectivity charges (Case 2: with strengthening). This stage operationalizes the technical feasibility decision and formally commits both parties.

**KEY TOPICS:** In-Principle Consent, Connectivity Charges, Connectivity Agreement, System Strengthening Estimate, Project Completion Timeline (6 months), Payment Terms

**TEXT:**

OR Issuing estimate to applicant for system strengthening (if required) to be paid within 30 days, payment of connectivity charges and execution of connectivity agreement. | 5 days from receipt of TFR from field office
7. | Signing of connectivity agreement and issuance of letter to applicant for completion of project work | Case 1 (No system strengthening required): On payment of Connectivity Charges and execution of Connectivity Agreement within 15 days of consent. **CROSS-REFERENCES:** Connectivity charges specified in Annexure-III are standardized rates; connectivity agreement template provided in Annexure-IV includes consumer obligations and licensee responsibilities; 6-month project completion deadline triggers notice to applicant if not met (Regulation 7, Stage 9)

## CHUNK 17

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) Regulations, 2016

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Section 7 (Procedure for Application) - Table: Stage 8-10

**PROVISION:** Regulation 7 (Procedure for Application) - System Strengthening and Non-Completion Procedures

**TITLE:** Application Procedure Stage 8-10: Licensee System Strengthening and Non-Completion Penalties

**SUMMARY:**
Stage 8: Distribution licensee completes required system strengthening work on receipt of payment from consumer, typically within 45 days in parallel with applicant's project installation. Stage 9: Upon expiry of 6-month project completion period, licensee issues two-month notice to applicant for commissioning within 5 days. Stage 10: If applicant provides no intimation of completion within the 2-month notice period (8 months total from authorization), the application is cancelled within 30 days and all charges paid for the solar rooftop project are forfeited. This time-bound approach with penalty ensures applicants proceed with installation and prevents indefinite reservation of network capacity.

**KEY TOPICS:** System Strengthening Completion (45 days), Project Completion Timeline (6 months + 2 months notice), Non-Completion Penalty, Application Cancellation, Charge Forfeiture

**TEXT:**
Sr. No. | Activity | Sub Activity | Duration in day(s)
---|---|---|---
8. | System strengthening by Distribution Licensee | Distribution Licensee to complete the work of system strengthening on payment of estimate. | 45 days in parallel to project installation
9. | Notice to applicant for commissioning | Issuance of two months notice to applicant for commissioning of the project on expiry of 6 months project completion period. | Within 5 days on expiry of 6 months
10. | In case of non-completion of work by applicant | If no intimation received from applicant on expiry of 2 months notice period, application shall be cancelled informing the applicant within 30 days forfeiting all charges paid for Solar Rooftop Project. | Within 5 days on expiry of 2 months

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Regulation 7 (full procedural timeline), Connectivity Agreement (Annexure-IV) specifies consumer project completion obligations

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Non-completion penalty (charge forfeiture) enforces timeline discipline; 45-day strengthening window enables consumer to undertake simultaneous installation work; cancellation right protects licensee from indefinite network capacity reservation; applicant may reapply as new consumer after cancellation

## CHUNK 18

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) Regulations, 2016

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Section 7 (Procedure for Application) - Table: Stage 11-12

**PROVISION:** Regulation 7 (Procedure for Application) - Document Submission and Meter Installation Notification

**TITLE:** Application Procedure Stage 11-12: Project Completion Documentation and Meter Installation

**SUMMARY:**
Stage 11: Upon applicant's work completion intimation, licensee requests the following documents within 5 days (if not already submitted): (1) Solar PV system ownership proof, (2) CEI installation charging approval, (3) Meter/CTPT testing certificate from high-tech lab and ERDA, (4) IEC standard compliance certificates/test reports for all equipment (modules, inverters, cables, junction box, transformer, RMU, CTPT, meter), (5) Proper protection system documentation (inverter anti-islanding feature, second-line protection like no-volt relay), and connectivity agreement. If applicant fails to submit within 5 days, application is cancelled with charge forfeiture. Stage 12: Upon receipt of all documents, licensee notifies field office/sub-division to install meters (solar meter for total generation recording and bidirectional/ABT meter for net metering) within 5 days. This stage ensures equipment compliance verification and establishes metering infrastructure.

**KEY TOPICS:** Equipment Documentation, CEI Installation Approval, Meter Testing Certificate, IEC Compliance, Anti-Islanding Protection, Meter Installation, ERDA Testing

---|---|---|---

Ownership of Solar PV system 2. Installation charging approval of Chief Electrical Inspector (CEI) 3. Meter/CTPT testing certificate from High-tech lab and ERDA. 4. All equipment should comply with IEC standards. ## CHUNK 19

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Net Metering Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems) Regulations, 2016

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Section 7 (Procedure for Application) - Table: Stage 13-15

**PROVISION:** Regulation 7 (Procedure for Application) - Meter Installation and Commissioning Certificate

**TITLE:** Application Procedure Stage 13-15: Meter Installation and GEDA Commissioning Certificate

**SUMMARY:**
Stage 13 (within 7 days of receiving installation notification): Field office/sub-division installs solar meter and bidirectional/ABT meter for net metering, and reports completion to Head Office. Stage 14 (within 5 days of field report): Distribution Licensee Head Office notifies GEDA to issue commissioning certificate. Stage 15 (within 5 days of GEDA notification): GEDA visits the site in consultation with distribution licensee and applicant, conducts final inspection, and issues commissioning certificate. Date of commissioning is recognized from the date of successful commissioning test. The commissioning certificate must contain: (1) Solar PV panel details (manufacturer, type, size, capacity), (2) Inverter details (type, size), (3) Total solar plant capacity, (4) Meter details (type, accuracy, serial number). This stage finalizes the regulatory approval pathway and establishes the official commissioning date for tariff, APPC, and settlement purposes.

**KEY TOPICS:** Meter Installation, Commissioning Certificate, GEDA Commissioning, Site Inspection, Commissioning Date, Equipment Specifications

**TEXT:**
Sr. No. | Activity | Sub Activity | Duration in day(s)
---|---|---|---
13. | Report from field/sub-division | Installation of meter (Solar meter to record total generation and bidirectional/ABT meter for net metering) and intimate to applicant and report to HO | 7 days from receipt of letter from HO
14. | Intimation to GEDA | Distribution Licensee shall intimate to GEDA for issuing commissioning certificate | 5 days from receipt of letter from Distribution Licensee
15. | Issuance of Commissioning Certificate from GEDA | GEDA shall visit the site in consultation with Distribution Licensee and applicant and issue Commissioning Certificate. Commissioning certificate shall be signed by all the above named parties. Date of commissioning shall be recognised from the date on which successful commissioning test is conducted. The Commissioning certificate must contain of the following details: I. Details of Solar PV panels including name of the manufacturer, type, size/capacity of the panels, etc.; II. Details of meter installed along with the types of meter accuracy, serial number, etc. | 5 days from the receipt of intimation from applicant

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Regulation 8 (Interconnection testing requirements), Regulation 9 (commissioning date determines APPC rate applicability), Regulation 13 (Meter types and specifications)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Commissioning date is critical for tariff determination - APPC rate is locked at the rate determined in the year of commissioning and applies for the entire 25-year project life (Regulation 9.2-9.5); meter specifications verified per Annexure-VI; commissioning certificate triggers energy accounting in Regulation 9
