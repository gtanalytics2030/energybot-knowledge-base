TITLE: Security Deposit Review, Revision, and Adjustment Procedures

SUMMARY:
Regulations 4.5-4.9 establish comprehensive procedures for reviewing and adjusting security deposits. Additional demand requires fresh deposit calculation as new service. Licensees must review deposit adequacy annually (preferably post-tariff revision) based on 12-month average consumption. Consumers may request reviews based on actual consumption; licensees must review demand reduction requests. Review outcomes determine deposit adjustments. Shortfalls up to 10% require no additional payment; excesses above 10% must be refunded through bill adjustment or direct payment demand within 30 days.

**4.5 Additional Demand - Fresh Deposit Calculation:**

In the case of consumers whose additional demand is to be sanctioned, the amount of Security Deposit shall be calculated as if it is a new service, for the entire load at prevailing applicable tariff rate as specified in Clause 4.4

**Application:**
- Applies when consumer requests increase in contract demand or contracted load
- Entire load (original + additional) is treated as new service
- Calculation uses Regulation 4.4 methodology for the consumer's category
- Uses prevailing applicable tariff rates at the time of request
- Fresh calculation required; no incremental calculation basis

**Impact:**
- Deposit amount may increase substantially when additional demand is sanctioned
- Treated as if all load is newly applied, not just the incremental portion
- Full Regulation 4.4 calculation table applies

**4.6 Annual Review of Security Deposit Adequacy:**

Subject to the restrictions of the periods of three months or two months or one and half months as specified in Clauses 4.1 and 4.2, the adequacy of the amount of Security Deposit calculation in respect of consumers may be reviewed by the licensee usually once in every year (preferably after revision of tariff for the respective year) based on the average unit consumption for the period representing 12 (twelve) months from April to March of the previous year.

**Review Parameters:**
- **Frequency:** Once annually (minimum)
- **Timing:** Preferably after annual tariff revision
- **Measurement period:** 12 months from April to March (financial year)
- **Basis:** Average unit consumption during 12-month period

**Minimum Deposit Periods (Not to be reduced below):**
- **LT consumers (bi-monthly billing):** 3 months' Consumption Charges
- **LT consumers (monthly billing):** 2 months' Consumption Charges
- **HT consumers:** 1.5 months' Consumption Charges

**Process:**
- Licensee initiates review (no consumer action required)
- Review examines whether deposit is adequate based on actual consumption patterns
- Deposit may be increased or decreased based on findings

**4.7 Consumer-Requested Review - Refund of Excess:**

In case, a consumer requests, based on his average consumption in the last twelve (12) months, to review his Security Deposit amount, then the licensee shall review the same. Based on such review, refund of excess will be made by the licensee.

**Consumer Rights:**
- Consumer may request review at any time based on 12-month average consumption
- Request must be based on actual average consumption data
- Licensee shall conduct review upon receipt of request
- Refund of excess is mandatory upon review completion

**Refund Mechanism:**
- Refund executed by licensee after review
- No interest mentioned on refunded amounts (distinct from Regulation 4.11)
- Likely through bill adjustment or direct payment

**12-Month Consumption Average Calculation:**
- Actual consumption data for preceding 12-month period
- Used to recalculate required deposit per Regulation 4.4 methodology
- Reduced deposit requirement justified where consumption shows downward trend

**4.8 Demand Reduction - Revised Deposit Calculation:**

In case, a consumer seeks reduction in contract demand/contracted load, then the licensee shall review the request. Upon such review, if licensee reduces contract demand/contracted load, then the Security Deposit shall be calculated at the prevailing applicable tariff rate for the reduced demand as if it is a new service, as specified in Clause 4.4. Based on such review, refund of excess will be made by the licensee.

**Trigger:**
- Consumer makes explicit request for reduction in contract demand/contracted load
- Applies to both HT and LT consumers with demand-based billing

**Licensee Review Process:**
- Licensee examines the request
- Determines technical feasibility of demand reduction
- May approve or reject based on system considerations

**Upon Approval of Demand Reduction:**
- Revised deposit calculated using reduced demand/load
- Calculation treats as new service using Regulation 4.4 methodology
- Prevailing tariff rates applied
- Excess deposit (original minus reduced deposit) refunded

**Example:**
- Original contract demand: 50 kW
- New requested demand: 30 kW
- New deposit calculated based on 30 kW as if new service
- Excess between two deposits refunded

**4.9 Deposit Adjustment - Increase, Decrease, and Tolerance Threshold:**

Based on such review, the licensee may either seek increase in amount of Security Deposit or reduce its amount as the case may be. Provided that if the Security Deposit payable by the consumer is short by not more than 10% of the existing Security Deposit, no demand will be made for payment of further Security Deposit. If the existing Security Deposit of a consumer is found to be in excess by more than 10% of the required Security Deposit, refund of the excess Security Deposit shall be made by adjustment in future bills. Where the consumer is required to pay additional amount by way of Security Deposit, a notice for payment within 30 days shall be issued.

**DEPOSIT ADJUSTMENT OUTCOMES:**

**Scenario 1: Shortfall (but ≤ 10%)**
- If required deposit exceeds existing by ≤ 10%:
  - **Action:** No demand notice issued
  - **Reason:** Shortfall deemed immaterial
  - **Result:** Consumer retains existing deposit; no payment required

**Example:**
- Existing deposit: Rs. 1,000
- Required deposit: Rs. 1,050 (5% higher)
- Action: No additional payment required

**Scenario 2: Shortfall (> 10%)**
- If required deposit exceeds existing by > 10%:
  - **Action:** Demand notice issued for additional payment
  - **Timeline:** Payment due within 30 days of notice
  - **Non-payment consequence:** See Regulation 4.13 (surcharge applies)

**Example:**
- Existing deposit: Rs. 1,000
- Required deposit: Rs. 1,200 (20% higher)
- Shortfall: Rs. 200
- Action: Notice demanding Rs. 200 within 30 days

**Scenario 3: Excess (> 10%)**
- If existing deposit exceeds required by > 10%:
  - **Action:** Mandatory refund
  - **Mechanism:** Adjustment in future electricity bills
  - **Timeline:** Refund adjustment begins in subsequent bills

**Example:**
- Existing deposit: Rs. 1,000
- Required deposit: Rs. 800 (20% lower than existing)
- Excess: Rs. 200
- Action: Refund via bill adjustments

**Scenario 4: Exact or near-exact match**
- If existing deposit matches required deposit (±10%):
  - **Action:** No adjustment required
  - **Status:** Deposit maintained as-is

**Notice Requirements:**
- Written notice required when additional payment demanded
- Notice must specify:
  - Required deposit amount
  - Payment deadline (30 days)
  - Payment method (cash or DD, per Regulation 4.3)
- Notice must be served by licensee

PROCEDURAL TIMELINE:

1. **Review Trigger** (Regulation 4.6, 4.7, or 4.8)
2. **Licensee Examination** (determines new required deposit)
3. **Comparison with Existing** (calculates variance percentage)
4. **Determination:**
   - If shortfall ≤ 10%: No action
   - If shortfall > 10%: Issue notice (Regulation 4.9, 4.13)
   - If excess > 10%: Refund adjustment in bills (Regulation 4.9)
5. **Consumer Action (if shortfall > 10%):** Pay within 30 days or face surcharge
6. **Refund Processing (if excess > 10%):** Adjustment in subsequent bills
