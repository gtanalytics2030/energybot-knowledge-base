SUMMARY:
The Third Amendment Regulations 2023 formally amend the Principal Security Deposit Regulations 2005 (Notification No. 8 of 2005, as amended by the First Amendment 2010 and Second Amendment 2015). The Third Amendment Regulations are titled GERC (Security Deposit) (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2023, extend to the entire State of Gujarat, and became effective upon publication in the Gujarat Government Gazette on 6 December 2023 (Notification No. 04 of 2023). This amendment is issued under Section 47 and Section 181(2)(v) and (w) of the Electricity Act 2003 and Clause 5.3 of the Principal Regulations, utilizing the Commission's regulatory power to amend provisions as circumstances require. The Third Amendment makes three targeted amendments affecting temporary service definition, interest calculation exclusion for temporary consumers, and expedited refund timelines.

**SCOPE OF AMENDMENTS - THIRD AMENDMENT 2023:**

The Third Amendment modifies **THREE** existing provisions:

1. **Amendment to Clause 2.1(m)** (Chapter 2) - Definition of Temporary Service
   - **Change:** Period extended from "one month" to "twelve months"
   - **Effect:** Longer initial supply period for temporary connections

2. **Amendment to Clause 4.11** (Chapter 4) - Interest on Deposits
   - **Change:** Added exclusion for temporary consumers
   - **Effect:** No interest payable on deposits from temporary consumers

3. **Amendment to Clause 4.14** (Chapter 4) - Refund Timeline
   - **Change:** Reduced from 30 days to 7 days
   - **Effect:** Faster refund upon supply termination

---

**POLICY RATIONALE - THIRD AMENDMENT:**

**Temporary Service Extension (Clause 2.1(m)):**
- Rationale: Temporary connections require 12 months for typical non-permanent needs
- Benefit: Alignment with actual temporary connection usage patterns
- Impact: Classification criteria more realistic for temporary service period

**Temporary Consumer Interest Exclusion (Clause 4.11):**
- Rationale: Temporary consumers receive short-term supply; interest not warranted
- Benefit: Reduced administrative burden for temporary deposits
- Impact: Distinction between permanent and temporary consumer protections

**Accelerated Refund Timeline (Clause 4.14):**
- Rationale: 30 days deemed excessive; consumer protection enhancement
- Benefit: Faster return of deposits to consumers; improved cash flow
- Impact: Licensee must have efficient deposit tracking systems

**EFFECTIVE APPLICATION:**

**For Temporary Consumers:**
- Temporary Service period now extends to 12 months (from 1 month)
- No interest accrual on temporary deposits
- Faster refund upon termination (7 days instead of 30)
- Overall benefit: More favorable terms for temporary connections

**For Permanent Consumers:**
- Deposit provisions unchanged
- Interest continues to accrue per Clause 4.11 (as amended)
- Faster refund upon termination (7 days - accelerated benefit)
- Overall benefit: Significantly faster deposit refunds

**For Distribution Licensees:**
- Must reclassify existing temporary connections if exceeding original 1-month period
- Must exclude temporary deposits from interest calculations
- Must implement 7-day refund timeline (from 30 days)
- System updates required for faster refund processing

**For GERC (Regulatory Body):**
- Monitors licensee compliance with amended provisions
- Receives consumer grievances regarding refund timelines
- May issue orders/guidelines for implementation (Regulation 5.2)
- Authority to amend further if required (Regulation 5.3)
