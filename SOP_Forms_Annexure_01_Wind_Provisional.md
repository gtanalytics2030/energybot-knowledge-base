TITLE: Annexure-1: Required Details for Wind Power Project Provisional Registration Application

SUMMARY:
This form captures all details required for provisional registration of wind power projects (with or without BESS) under GIREP-2025. It collects applicant information, entity details, grid connectivity preferences, wind turbine specifications, project site location, BESS details (if applicable), and fee payment information. Provisional registration is the first step before applying for development permission.

KEY TOPICS: Wind Power, Provisional Registration, Application Form, WTG Details, BESS, Grid Connectivity, GEDA Registration, Applicant Information

TEXT:
ANNEXURE-1: WIND POWER PROJECT - PROVISIONAL REGISTRATION APPLICATION
REQUIRED DETAILS & INFORMATION CHECKLIST

SECTION A: APPLICANT & ENTITY INFORMATION

What Information You MUST Provide:

1. Applicant Name
   - Full legal name (individual or business entity)
   - Exactly as registered with government authorities

2. Contact Address
   - Street/House number
   - Village name and Taluka
   - City name
   - State
   - PIN code
   - Phone number (landline)
   - Mobile number
   - WhatsApp number
   - Email address (active, official)
   - Company website URL (if applicable)

3. Tax Information
   - PAN (Permanent Account Number)
   - GST Registration Number

4. Entity Type Classification (Select One)
   - Individual
   - Proprietary Firm
   - Partnership
   - Private Limited Company
   - Public Limited Company
   - MSME
   - Group of Company
   - Other (specify)

5. Entity Registration Documents (Provide Self-Certified Copy)
   - MOA (Memorandum of Association) - if company
   - ROC Registration Certificate - if company
   - ROF (Register of Firms) - if partnership
   - AOA (Articles of Association) - if company
   - COI (Certificate of Incorporation) - if company
   - Partnership Deed - if partnership firm
   - LLP Agreement - if Limited Liability Partnership
   - Sole Proprietorship Proof - if individual
   - MSME Certificate - if MSME registered

6. Managing Director / Chief Executive Details (if Company)
   - Full name
   - Designation/Title
   - Phone number
   - Mobile number
   - Email address
   - Enclose certified copy of Board Resolution authorizing this person

7. Authorized Signatory Details
   - Full name
   - Designation
   - Phone number
   - Mobile number
   - Email address
   - MUST provide certified Board Resolution authorizing signatory

SECTION B: GRID CONNECTIVITY PREFERENCES

What Details You MUST Specify:

1. Transmission Connectivity Level (Select One)
   - State Transmission Utility (STU) [for GETCO connected]
   - Central Transmission Utility (CTU) [for PGCIL connected]

2. Project Category (Select One)
   - RE Developer (independent power producer)
   - RE Park Developer (developing multi-developer park)
   - RE Generator (large capacity developer)
   - Any Other (specify nature)

3. Substation Connection Details
   - Name(s) of proposed GETCO/PGCIL substation(s)
   - Single Substation OR Multiple Substations (select)
   
   If Multiple Substations:
   - Specify EACH substation name
   - Wind capacity planned at each substation (in MW)
   - Total capacity across all substations (in MW)

4. Power Injection Voltage Level (Select One)
   - 11 kV (distribution level)
   - 66 kV (sub-transmission)
   - Above 66 kV (transmission)

5. End Use Purpose (Select One)
   - DISCOMs Bid (sale to distribution companies)
   - REIA Bid (state renewable auction)
   - Captive Use (self-consumption)
   - Third Party (third party sale)
   - Open Access (third party purchase with wheeling)
   - Bid Winner (auction winner)
   - Prototype (technology demonstration)

6. If Sale to DISCOMs:
   - MUST attach LOI (Letter of Intent) or PPA (Power Purchase Agreement)
   - Attachment from GUVNL or respective DISCOM

SECTION C: WIND TURBINE GENERATOR (WTG) SPECIFICATIONS

What Technical Details You MUST Provide:

1. Number of WTGs
   - Total count of wind turbines in project

2. Capacity Information
   - Capacity of EACH WTG in MW (e.g., 2.75 MW)
   - Total Project Capacity in MW (sum of all WTGs)
   - Examples: 2 × 2.75 MW = 5.5 MW total

3. WTG Make & Model
   - Manufacturer name (e.g., Siemens, Vestas, GE)
   - Exact model number (e.g., Siemens Gamesa SG 2.7-92)

4. Wind Turbine Physical Specifications
   - Rotor Diameter (in meters) - e.g., 92m, 120m
   - Hub Height (in meters) - e.g., 80m, 100m, 120m

5. Equipment Certification Status
   - Is proposed WTG approved in ALMM-Wind list?
     * YES: Provide ALMM approval validity date (DD/MM/YY)
     * NO: Provide undertaking committing to submit ALMM approval before transfer or commissioning

6. Equipment Standards Compliance
   - Ensure equipment meets IEC 61400-1 (Wind Turbine Safety Standard)
   - Ensure equipment meets applicable Indian Standards
   - Have manufacturer's technical datasheet ready

SECTION D: PROJECT SITE LOCATION

What Location Information You MUST Provide:

1. Village Name
   - Exact official name of village where project is located

2. Taluka (Administrative Division)
   - Name of taluka/tehsil within district

3. District Name
   - Name of district in Gujarat

NOTE: Detailed coordinates, UTM, latitude-longitude provided in later Development Permission stage

SECTION E: BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEM (BESS) - IF APPLICABLE

What Information Required ONLY if Adding BESS:

1. BESS Inclusion Decision
   - No BESS
   - Yes, BESS included (skip to details below)

2. If BESS Included - Specify Type
   - BESS with Existing Wind Power Project
     * If YES: Also provide existing wind project registration details
   - BESS with New Wind Power Project

3. BESS Capacity Specifications
   - DC Capacity (in MWh) - actual energy storage
   - AC Capacity (in MW) - power conversion rating
   - Example: 10 MWh DC, 5 MW AC

4. Battery Pack Details
   - Battery Make (manufacturer name)
   - Battery Chemistry Type (Li-ion, LFP, Lead-acid, etc.)
   - Number of BESS Racks/Modules
   - Rating per rack (in kW or MW)

5. Power Conversion System (PCS) Invertor Details
   - PCS Type (Central, String, Distributed)
   - PCS Make (manufacturer)
   - Number of PCS Invertors
   - Rating per PCS Invertor (in MW)

NOTE: Detailed BESS specifications required in Development Permission stage

SECTION F: PROJECT PERFORMANCE DATA

What Estimates You MUST Provide:

1. Expected Annual Energy Output
   - Total estimated generation per year (in kWh)
   - Based on capacity and wind resource assessment
   - Example: 5 MW project might generate 12,000,000 kWh/year

2. Tentative Commissioning Date
   - Target date for project commissioning
   - Format: DD/MM/YYYY
   - Timeline: Typically 18-24 months from approval

3. Approximate Project Cost
   - Total estimated project investment (in Rs. Crores or Rs. Lakhs)
   - This is approximate; detailed cost in development permission
   - Include capex for WTGs, infrastructure, grid connectivity

4. Employment Generation Expected
   - Number of jobs (direct and indirect) expected from project
   - Include construction and operational jobs

SECTION G: PROCESSING FEE & PAYMENT

What Fee Information You MUST Know:

1. Provisional Registration Fee
   - Amount: Rs. 10,000 + GST
   - GST Rate: 18% (currently)
   - Total Payable: Rs. 10,000 + Rs. 1,800 (GST) = Rs. 11,800
   - Current GST Registration No: 24AAATG1858Q1ZA

2. Payment Method
   - Online payment through RE Portal
   - Non-refundable fee

3. Payment Proof
   - Keep online payment receipt/confirmation
   - Submit payment confirmation with application

SECTION H: MANDATORY SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS TO ATTACH

What Documents You MUST Upload (PDF/Image Format):

1. Entity Registration Proof
   - Self-certified copy of MOA/ROC/ROF/AOA/COI/Partnership Deed/LLP Agreement
   - Notarized or self-certified (as per form instructions)

2. Board Authorization
   - Certified Board Resolution authorizing the signatory to submit application
   - Extract from Board minutes
   - Company seal and date required

3. PAN Certificate
   - Copy of PAN card (front and back)
   - Self-certified is acceptable

4. GST Certificate
   - Copy of GSTIN certificate
   - Optional for individual applicants

5. Additional Documents if Applicable
   - LOI/PPA from GUVNL or DISCOM (if DISCOMs bid purpose)
   - Any permission/approvals from landowner (if land secured)
   - Technical datasheet of proposed WTG model
   - ALMM approval letter (if equipment already ALMM-approved)

HOW TO SUBMIT:

- Application submission: Online through RE Portal (http://srtgeda.gujarat.gov.in/)
- Document upload: PDF, JPG, PNG formats
- File size limits: Per portal specifications (typically 5 MB per file)
- Validity check: After fee payment, application is considered submitted

PROCESSING TIMELINE:

- After complete submission: Provisional registration issued within 3-5 working days
- Incomplete applications: Rejected with correction notice within 2 days
- Re-submission after corrections: 5 days to resubmit

IMPORTANT NOTES:

✓ All measurements in meters (rotor diameter, hub height)
✓ All capacities in MW (for WTG) or MW/MWh (for BESS)
✓ All rupee amounts in Indian currency
✓ PAN must be valid and registered with income tax authorities
✓ GST registration must be active at time of registration
✓ Board Resolution must be certified by company authority
✓ ALMM approval undertaking required if equipment not yet certified
