TITLE: Detailed Requirements for Grid-Connected Rooftop Solar Registration (Residential, Commercial, Institutional)
SUMMARY:
This simplified online registration form is for rooftop/distributed solar PV installations on residential, commercial, or institutional buildings. It requires building details, load information, solar system specifications, grid connectivity from DISCOM, CEI electrical approval, and net metering agreement. Processing is faster (60-110 days) with exempted fees for residential category. Key documents: electricity bill, building ownership proof, site photos, equipment quotations.
KEY TOPICS: Rooftop Solar, Distributed Solar, Net Metering, Building Details, Load Profile, Residential Solar, Simplified Registration

3. CEI Inspection & Energization Permission (After Installation)

Project Schedule:

1. Estimated Timeline Phases
   - GEDA registration: 1-5 days
   - DISCOM TFR issuance: 5-10 days
   - CEI drawing approval: 5-10 days

   - CEI inspection & testing: 2-5 days

SECTION M: DECLARATION & UNDERTAKING

Applicant Certification:
"I certify that all information is true and accurate. The solar system will be installed at the declared location on a safe, structurally sound building. I undertake to follow all GERC net metering regulations, comply with DISCOM terms, maintain the system as per manual, and report generation data as required. I accept all terms for net metering and grid interaction."

KEY REQUIREMENTS & RESTRICTIONS:

✓ System capacity should NOT exceed DISCOM's maximum permissible limit (typically 50-100 kW)
✓ For residential: Typically 3-10 kW systems (common)
✓ Structural stability MANDATORY before installation
✓ CEI approval MANDATORY before energization
✓ DISCOM meter installation MANDATORY before grid synchronization
✓ Annual generation reporting: Mandatory for net metering claims
✓ 25-year minimum agreement term with DISCOM recommended
✓ Export tariff as per GERC regulations (typically Rs. 2-4/kWh)
✓ No generation guarantee offered (generation depends on weather)

TIMELINE & VALIDITY:

- Registration validity: No expiry once commissioned
- Can operate indefinitely with proper maintenance
- Annual reporting: Required for net metering settlements
- Deregistration: Possible if system not operational for 1 year
