TITLE: Annexure-5: Required Details for Standalone Battery Energy Storage System Provisional Registration
SUMMARY:
This form is for provisional registration of standalone battery energy storage systems (not coupled with existing RE projects). BESS projects can be registered independently for grid services, ancillary services, time-shifting arbitrage, or peak shaving. The form captures applicant details, BESS capacity specifications, grid connectivity, technology specifications, and processing fees. Standalone BESS is critical infrastructure for grid stability and renewable energy integration.

IMPORTANT NOTES FOR STANDALONE BESS PROJECTS:

✓ Provisional registration is first step (like wind/solar)
✓ Development permission required before construction
✓ Grid operator feasibility assessment mandatory
✓ Safety certifications (fire, electrical, thermal) MANDATORY
✓ 25-year minimum asset life expected
✓ Battery degradation expected (~20% over 10 years typical)
✓ Recycling plan required (battery end-of-life)
✓ Insurance requirements (fire, theft, grid liability)

KEY ADVANTAGES OF STANDALONE BESS:

1. Grid Stability: Provide frequency regulation and voltage support
2. Peak Shaving: Reduce peak demand charges
3. Renewable Integration: Smooth intermittent wind/solar
4. Time-Shifting: Arbitrage charging during low-price hours
5. Black Start: Assist in grid restoration after blackouts
6. Transmission Support: Defer expensive transmission upgrades

CRITICAL REQUIREMENTS:

⚠️ Safety systems are NON-NEGOTIABLE (fire detection/suppression MANDATORY)
⚠️ BMS (Battery Management System) MANDATORY for all configurations
⚠️ Thermal management CRITICAL (temperature monitoring required)
⚠️ Cyber security REQUIRED (grid operator approval needed)
⚠️ Grid coordination MANDATORY (GETCO/DISCOM approval required)
⚠️ Insurance MANDATORY (liability for grid impact)
⚠️ 24/7 monitoring REQUIRED (remote supervision capability)

PROCESSING TIMELINE:

- Application submission with complete documents: Day 1
- Initial verification: 5 days
- Grid operator pre-feasibility assessment: 5-10 days
- Safety review: 5 days
- Total approval time: 15-25 working days (faster than wind/solar due to smaller scale)

NEXT STEPS AFTER PROVISIONAL REGISTRATION:

1. Development Permission application (detailed design)
2. Grid operator (GETCO/DISCOM) formal feasibility study
3. Environmental approval (if required)
4. Safety certification & testing
5. Grid connection agreement signing
6. Commissioning & grid synchronization
