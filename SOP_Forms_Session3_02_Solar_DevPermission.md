TITLE: Annexure-8: Comprehensive Requirements for Solar Power Development Permission Application (Ground-Mounted, Detailed Technical Design)
SUMMARY:
This form is for detailed development permission of ground-mounted solar power projects (with or without BESS). Unlike provisional registration, development permission requires comprehensive technical design, site assessment, PV equipment final specifications, electrical design, environmental clearance, and O&M planning. Form captures array layout design, PV module final specifications, inverter design, grid protection, environmental impact, land finalization, and processing fees. Development permission is required before civil works commence.
KEY TOPICS: Solar Development Permission, Ground-Mounted Solar, PV Array Design, Inverter Specifications, Grid Protection, Environmental Assessment, GEDA

SECTION M: PROCESSING FEES & CHARGES

Development Permission Fee Structure:

1. Fee Calculation
   - Based on project capacity:
     * < 1 MW: Rs. 8,000
     * 1-10 MW: Rs. 12,000 per MW
     * > 10 MW: Rs. 18,000 per MW
   - + 18% GST

   Examples:
   - 5 MW: 5 × 12,000 = Rs. 60,000 + GST = Rs. 70,800
   - 15 MW: 15 × 18,000 = Rs. 2,70,000 + GST = Rs. 3,18,600

2. Payment Terms
   - Online through RE Portal
   - Non-refundable
   - Receipt required

IMPORTANT REQUIREMENTS FOR SOLAR DEVELOPMENT PERMISSION:

✓ All designs by licensed professionals (sealed drawings required)
✓ PV module and inverter models must be ALMM-approved
✓ Solar irradiance data must be site-specific or professionally extrapolated
✓ Geotechnical survey mandatory for foundation design
✓ DISCOM TFR and connection approval MANDATORY
✓ Environmental clearance required if applicable
✓ 24-month validity from approval date
✓ Commissioning within validity period

TIMELINE FOR APPROVAL:

- Complete application submission: Day 1
- Document verification: 5-7 days
- DISCOM TFR review: 10-15 days
- Technical design scrutiny: 5-10 days
- Environmental review: 5-10 days (if required)
- TOTAL: 30-45 working days

KEY DIFFERENCES FROM PROVISIONAL REGISTRATION:

Provisional Registration:
- Basic project information
- Quick approval (3-5 days)
- Rs. 8,000-12,000 fee
- Next step: Development Permission

Development Permission:
- Complete technical design
- 30-45 day review
- Rs. 12,000-18,000/MW fee
- Can commence civil works
- 24-month validity for project execution
