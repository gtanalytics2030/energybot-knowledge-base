PROVISION: Section 4.1

TITLE: Access to the RE Portal by RE Project Developer

SUMMARY:
Section 4.1 establishes that all RE project developers must register and access the RE (Renewable Energy) Portal hosted by GEDA (Gujarat Energy Development Agency) to initiate the process of provisional registration for their renewable energy projects. The portal serves as the single-window system for project registration, application submission, and approval tracking. Access to the portal is mandatory for all project developers regardless of project type or capacity.

KEY TOPICS: RE Portal, GEDA, Registration, Project Developer, Access, Single-window System, Online Portal

TEXT:
Section 4.1 - Access to the RE Portal by RE Project Developer

All RE project developers intending to set up renewable energy projects (Grid-connected or Off-grid) with or without Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) in Gujarat must register themselves on the RE Portal hosted by GEDA.

The RE Portal shall serve as a single-window integrated system for:
(a) Online registration of renewable energy projects
(b) Submission of development applications
(c) Payment of processing fees
(d) Tracking of application status
(e) Approval of grid connectivity and project registration
(f) Allocation of renewable energy projects

Registration on the RE Portal is mandatory and is the first step in the provisional registration process.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: GIREP-2025, Section 4 (General Provisions)

PROVISION: Section 4.2

TITLE: Documents Required for Provisional Registration

SUMMARY:
Section 4.2 lists all mandatory documents required to be submitted by RE project developers for provisional registration of their renewable energy projects. The documents include proof of legal possession of land, financial statements, identity proof of authorized signatory, board resolutions, technology certifications, and project-specific technical details. All documents must be uploaded on the RE Portal in the prescribed format.

KEY TOPICS: Provisional Registration, Required Documents, Land Proof, Financial Statements, Certifications, Board Resolution, Technical Details

TEXT:
Section 4.2 - Documents Required for Provisional Registration

RE project developers shall submit the following documents for provisional registration on the RE Portal:

1. Covering letter indicating the type and capacity of the proposed RE project
2. Duly filled application form signed by authorized signatory
3. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from relevant authorities (DISCOM/GETCO)
4. Copy of Certificate of Incorporation / MOA / Partnership Deed
5. Copy of Permanent Account Number (PAN) certificate
6. Copy of Goods and Services Tax (GST) certificate
7. Board Resolution / Letter of Authority authorizing the signatory
8. Proof of legal possession of land (Registered Sale Deed / Lease Deed)
9. For grid-connected projects: No Objection from DISCOM/GETCO for grid connectivity
10. For private land: Permission under Non-Agricultural (NA) use or Bonafide Industrial Use
11. Declaration regarding newness of equipment and compliance with guidelines
12. Undertaking on Rs. 300/- stamp paper regarding ownership and project details

All documents must be uploaded on the RE Portal in the prescribed formats and must be complete and legible. Incomplete applications shall not be considered for processing.

PROVISION: Section 4.3

TITLE: Provisional Registration Fee Payable by RE Projects

SUMMARY:
Section 4.3 specifies the provisional registration fees applicable for renewable energy projects based on their installed capacity. The fee is non-refundable and must be paid online at the time of provisional registration through the RE Portal. The fee structure differentiates between projects below 1 MW and projects of 1 MW or above, with a per-MW charge for larger projects.

KEY TOPICS: Provisional Registration Fee, Payment, Non-refundable, Online Payment, Project Capacity, GST

TEXT:
Section 4.3 - Provisional Registration Fee Payable by RE Projects

The following provisional registration fees shall be payable by RE project developers at the time of provisional registration:

| Project Capacity | Fee | GST (18%) | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Less than 1 MW | Rs. 5,000 | Rs. 900 | Rs. 5,900 |
| 1 MW and above | Rs. 10,000 per MW | As applicable | As applicable |

Payment shall be made online through the RE Portal only. Payment receipts shall be generated automatically upon successful payment. The provisional registration fee is non-refundable and shall be applicable only once per project site and capacity.

In case of amendment or modification to project capacity after provisional registration, the difference in fee (if any) shall be levied as applicable.

PROVISION: Section 4.4

TITLE: Application Processing Fee Structure for Development Permission / Final Registration

SUMMARY:
Section 4.4 establishes the application processing fee structure for development permission and final registration of renewable energy projects. Fees are differentiated based on project type (wind, solar, hybrid, BESS, rooftop) and installed capacity, with specific charges for development permission and transfer permission. All fees are exclusive of 18% GST.

KEY TOPICS: Application Processing Fee, Development Permission, Final Registration, Transfer Permission, Fee Structure, GST, Project Type

TEXT:
Section 4.4 - Application Processing Fee Structure for Development Permission / Final Registration

The following application processing fees shall be applicable for Development Permission and Final Registration:

A. WIND POWER PROJECTS (Grid-connected and Rooftop)
- Less than 1 MW: Rs. 10,000/- plus GST
- 1 MW to 10 MW: Rs. 15,000/- per MW plus GST
- Above 10 MW: Rs. 20,000/- per MW plus GST

B. SOLAR POWER PROJECTS (Ground-mounted and Rooftop)
- Less than 1 MW: Rs. 8,000/- plus GST
- 1 MW to 10 MW: Rs. 12,000/- per MW plus GST
- Above 10 MW: Rs. 18,000/- per MW plus GST

C. WIND-SOLAR HYBRID PROJECTS
- Less than 1 MW: Rs. 12,000/- plus GST
- 1 MW to 10 MW: Rs. 18,000/- per MW plus GST
- Above 10 MW: Rs. 25,000/- per MW plus GST

D. STANDALONE BESS PROJECTS
- Flat fee: Rs. 25,000/- per MWh plus GST

E. TRANSFER PERMISSION (for all project types)
- 50% of the applicable Development Permission fee

All fees are payable online through the RE Portal. GST as applicable by law shall be charged additionally at 18% (or as amended by the Government).

PROVISION: Section 4.5

TITLE: Grid Connectivity

SUMMARY:
Section 4.5 establishes the framework for grid connectivity of renewable energy projects. It mandates that grid-connected projects must obtain approval from the relevant transmission utility (GETCO for state level, PGCIL for central level) and distribution licensee, obtain NOC from DISCOM, follow technical standards prescribed by CEI and CEA, and comply with forecasting and scheduling regulations. The section also addresses cost sharing and timeline for connectivity.

KEY TOPICS: Grid Connectivity, GETCO, PGCIL, DISCOM, NOC, Technical Standards, CEI, CEA, Transmission, Distribution

TEXT:
Section 4.5 - Grid Connectivity

A. APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS

All grid-connected renewable energy projects (wind, solar, hybrid) shall require:
(1) Approval from the competent grid operator:
    - State Transmission Utility (GETCO/STU) for state-level connectivity
    - Power Grid Corporation of India Limited (PGCIL) for central-level connectivity
(2) No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the distribution licensee (DISCOM) within whose area the project is located
(3) Technical feasibility report from DISCOM confirming feasibility of grid connection
(4) Compliance with technical standards prescribed by CEI (Chief Electrical Inspector) and CEA (Central Electricity Authority)

B. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS FOR GRID CONNECTIVITY

(1) All projects must comply with:
    - CERC Grid Code, 2023
    - GERC Grid Code, as amended
    - IEEE and IEC standards for renewable energy equipment
    - Safety standards as prescribed by CEI
    
(2) Projects must meet:
    - Voltage stability requirements
    - Frequency deviation limits
    - Harmonic distortion limits
    - Power quality standards
    - Fault ride-through capability (as per CERC/GERC specifications)

(3) All projects must have:
    - SCADA (Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition) system
    - Remote terminal unit (RTU) for grid operator interface
    - Automatic generation control (AGC) capability (if required)
    - Ramp rate limiting device (for solar and wind projects)

C. METERING AND INSTRUMENTATION

(1) All grid-connected projects shall have:
    - Net Energy Meter at the point of interconnection
    - Revenue meter complying with Indian Standards
    - Instrument Transformers (CT/PT) as per technical specifications

(2) Meter readings shall be recorded:
    - Daily for monitoring
    - Monthly for billing
    - Real-time data shall be available on the RE Portal

D. TIMELINES FOR GRID CONNECTIVITY

(1) DISCOM shall issue NOC within 15 days of receipt of complete application
(2) Technical feasibility assessment shall be completed within 30 days
(3) Final approval for grid connectivity shall be provided within 60 days of commissioning of project
(4) In case of refusal, written reason shall be provided within the above timeline

E. COST OF GRID CONNECTIVITY

(1) Cost of grid connectivity up to the point of interconnection shall be borne by the project developer
(2) DISCOM/GETCO shall provide necessary infrastructure for evacuation of power
(3) Cost sharing arrangement shall be as per CERC/GERC regulations and applicable guidelines

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: GIREP-2025, CERC Grid Code 2023, GERC Grid Code, CEI Standards, CEA Guidelines

CROSS-REFERENCES: Land Responsibilities (Section 4.6), Technical Standards (Section 4.7), Metering (Section 4.5), Wind Projects (Section 5), Solar Projects (Section 6), Hybrid Projects (Section 7)

PROVISION: Section 4.6

TITLE: Land Related Responsibilities

SUMMARY:
Section 4.6 clarifies that RE project developers are solely responsible for acquiring, securing legal possession of, and maintaining all land required for their projects. GEDA assumes no liability for land-related disputes or issues. Developers must provide proof of land ownership (sale deed) or lease rights, obtain necessary permissions from government authorities, and resolve any third-party claims before project commissioning.

KEY TOPICS: Land Acquisition, Land Ownership, Lease, Legal Possession, Land Dispute, Government Permission, Third-party Rights, Liabilities

TEXT:
Section 4.6 - Land Related Responsibilities

A. RESPONSIBILITY OF RE PROJECT DEVELOPER

(1) The RE project developer shall be solely responsible for:
    - Acquiring land for the proposed renewable energy project
    - Obtaining legal possession of the land through registered sale deed or lease agreement
    - Obtaining all necessary government permissions and approvals
    - Resolving any land-related disputes or third-party claims
    - Maintaining the land throughout the project lifecycle
    - Decommissioning and land restoration after project life (as applicable)

(2) The developer must provide:
    - Registered Sale Deed (for owned land) or Lease Deed (for leased land)
    - Land ownership certificate from revenue authorities
    - Non-Agricultural (NA) permission or Bonafide Industrial Use permission (for private agricultural land)
    - NOC from gram panchayat (if applicable)
    - NOC from forest department (if project site is in forest area)
    - Environmental clearance (if required as per MoEF guidelines)

B. RESPONSIBILITY OF GEDA

(1) GEDA shall not:
    - Be responsible for land acquisition
    - Be liable for any land-related disputes or legal issues
    - Be responsible for third-party claims on the land
    - Intervene in land disputes between the developer and land owner
    - Be held responsible for cancellation of lease or sale agreements

(2) GEDA shall only:
    - Verify that the developer has provided proof of legal possession
    - Ensure that all necessary government permissions are in place
    - Monitor compliance with land use regulations

C. VERIFICATION OF LAND DETAILS

(1) At the time of provisional registration, developer shall provide:
    - Plot/Survey numbers
    - Latitude and longitude (UTM coordinates)
    - AMSL (Above Mean Sea Level) height
    - Area of land (in hectares)
    - Copy of revenue receipts (for tax payment)

(2) At the time of development permission, developer shall provide:
    - Micro-siting survey report
    - Detailed land layout with equipment positions
    - Updated latitude/longitude coordinates (if changed)

D. LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

(1) The RE project developer shall indemnify GEDA against:
    - Any claims by land owner
    - Any disputes with third parties
    - Any breach of land use regulations
    - Any environmental violations related to land use
    - Loss of lease agreement or sale deed validity

(2) In case of land-related issues, GEDA may:
    - Suspend or cancel project registration
    - Forfeit processing fees already paid
    - Debar the developer from future projects (in case of fraudulent claims)

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: GIREP-2025, Land Acquisition, Legal Possession, Government Permissions, Land Use Regulations

CROSS-REFERENCES: Provisional Registration (Section 4.2), Development Permission (Wind Section 5.2.2), Technical Standards (Section 4.7)

PROVISION: Section 4.7

TITLE: Technical Standards for Development of RE Project

SUMMARY:
Section 4.7 requires that all renewable energy projects comply with technical standards prescribed by the Chief Electrical Inspector (CEI), Central Electricity Authority (CEA), and GERC. Projects must meet requirements for equipment certification, safety standards, interconnection specifications, and operational parameters. Developers must obtain CEI approval for electrical drawings and installation before project commissioning.

KEY TOPICS: Technical Standards, CEI, CEA, GERC, Equipment Certification, Safety Standards, Electrical Drawings, Interconnection, Quality Assurance

TEXT:
Section 4.7 - Technical Standards for Development of RE Project

A. APPLICABLE TECHNICAL STANDARDS

All RE projects (grid-connected and off-grid) shall comply with:

(1) Indian Standards (IS):
    - IS 1166 (Electrical safety in low voltage installations)
    - IS 800 (Code of practice for general construction in steel)
    - IS 1893 (Criteria for earthquake resistant design of structures)
    - IS 9512 (Code of practice for installation of solar photovoltaic systems)
    - IS 61400 series (Wind turbine standards)

(2) International Standards:
    - IEC 61724 (Photovoltaic system performance monitoring)
    - IEC 61400-1 (Wind turbines - Design requirements)
    - IEEE 1547 (Interconnection of distributed resources with electric power systems)
    - IEEE 1003 (Utility interconnection of distributed energy resources)

(3) GERC/CERC Standards:
    - GERC Grid Code and amendments
    - CERC Grid Code, 2023
    - CERC Frequency Response Support Standards
    - GERC Forecasting and Scheduling Regulations

B. EQUIPMENT CERTIFICATION REQUIREMENTS

(1) All major equipment shall carry:
    - CE certification (for European equipment)
    - Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) certification (for Indian equipment)
    - National Accreditation Board for Certification Bodies (NABCB) certification

(2) For Wind Turbine Generators (WTGs):
    - Equipment must be from approved Revised List of Machines and Models (RLMM)
    - Type approval certificate from accredited test authority
    - Warranty certificate from manufacturer
    - Micro-siting study report (for large projects)

(3) For Solar PV Modules:
    - Equipment must be from approved Approved List of Models and Manufacturers (ALMM)
    - IEC certification
    - Performance guarantee certificate
    - Fire rating and UV resistance testing

(4) For BESS:
    - Battery management system (BMS) certification
    - Power Conversion System (PCS) certification
    - Safety data sheets for battery technology
    - Fire suppression system approval

C. ELECTRICAL INSTALLATION STANDARDS

(1) All installations shall have:
    - Single-line diagram prepared by qualified engineer
    - CEI-approved electrical drawings and designs
    - Equipment layout drawing with safety clearances
    - Earthing design and protection system
    - Fire safety provisions

(2) Inspection and Testing:
    - High-voltage testing as per IS standards
    - Insulation resistance testing
    - Earthing resistance measurement
    - Load testing before commissioning
    - Thermal imaging of electrical connections

D. SAFETY REQUIREMENTS

(1) General Safety:
    - All equipment shall be properly grounded
    - Safety signage and warning labels as per standards
    - Fencing around project area (for ground-mounted projects)
    - Access restrictions to high-voltage areas
    - Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) for workers

(2) Fire Safety:
    - Fire detection and alarm systems
    - Fire suppression equipment
    - Emergency shutdown provisions
    - Regular fire safety audits

(3) Personal Safety:
    - Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures
    - Fall protection systems
    - Electrical safety training for personnel
    - First aid and emergency response facilities

E. PERFORMANCE AND MONITORING STANDARDS

(1) All projects shall have:
    - Energy monitoring system
    - Real-time data acquisition
    - Daily performance reporting
    - Annual efficiency testing

(2) Minimum performance standards:
    - Solar projects: Minimum 90% of expected annual output
    - Wind projects: Minimum 85% of expected annual output
    - Hybrid projects: Combined performance as applicable

(3) Reactive Power Requirements:
    - Reactive power range as per GERC specifications
    - Power factor maintenance (0.97 leading to 0.97 lagging)
    - Reactive power capability of inverters

PROVISION: Section 4.8

TITLE: Adherence to Forecasting and Scheduling Regulations

SUMMARY:
Section 4.8 requires all RE generators to comply with GERC Forecasting and Scheduling Regulations. Generators must provide accurate day-ahead forecasts of generation, real-time scheduling information, and follow deviation settlement procedures. Non-compliance with forecasting and scheduling requirements may result in penalty charges and potential disconnection from the grid.

KEY TOPICS: Forecasting, Scheduling, Day-ahead Forecast, Real-time Scheduling, Deviation Settlement, Grid Code Compliance, GERC Regulations

TEXT:
Section 4.8 - Adherence to Forecasting and Scheduling Regulations

A. REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

(1) All grid-connected RE generators shall comply with:
    - GERC Forecasting and Scheduling Regulations (as amended)
    - CERC Grid Code provisions on forecasting and scheduling
    - State and central grid operator guidelines
    - SLDC (State Load Dispatch Centre) instructions

(2) The purpose of forecasting and scheduling:
    - Enable efficient grid operation and load balancing
    - Maintain grid frequency and voltage stability
    - Facilitate power exchange and settlement
    - Support ancillary services procurement

B. FORECASTING REQUIREMENTS

(1) Day-ahead Forecast (required for solar and wind projects):
    - Forecast generation for next 24 hours (hourly or 15-minute intervals)
    - Submission deadline: Before 12:00 noon for next day generation
    - Accuracy requirement: ±20% of actual generation (as per GERC standards)
    - Monthly reconciliation of forecast accuracy

(2) Real-time Forecast (for large projects >10 MW):
    - Intra-day forecast update (every 2-4 hours)
    - Day-ahead revision as required
    - Wind ramp rate forecast (for wind projects)

(3) Forecast Submission:
    - Online submission through SLDC/STU portal
    - SCADA interface for real-time data
    - Backup manual submission system
    - Confirmation of receipt

C. SCHEDULING REQUIREMENTS

(1) Energy Scheduling:
    - Generator shall schedule energy injection as per day-ahead forecast
    - Schedule shall be submitted to SLDC
    - Schedule binding for ±20% of scheduled quantity (tolerance band)
    - In case of deviation >20%, penalty charges shall apply

(2) Ramp Rate Limiting:
    - Solar projects: Maximum ramp rate 10% per minute
    - Wind projects: As per wind speed conditions (monitored)
    - Hybrid projects: Combined ramp rate as applicable

(3) Minimum/Maximum Generation:
    - Solar projects: Actual generation follows irradiance variation
    - Wind projects: Actual generation follows wind availability
    - No artificial curtailment without prior approval
    - Planned downtime shall be communicated 48 hours in advance

D. DEVIATION SETTLEMENT

(1) Deviation Definition:
    - Difference between scheduled and actual generation
    - Within tolerance band: No settlement
    - Beyond tolerance band: Penalty charge or incentive payment

(2) Deviation Settlement Mechanism:
    - Positive deviation (over generation): Penalty charge
    - Negative deviation (under generation): Compensation payment
    - Rate determined by GERC Energy Market
    - Monthly settlement and payment

(3) Deviation Charges:
    - Current rates as per GERC Energy Market regulations
    - Rates subject to quarterly review
    - Payment due within 15 days of invoice

E. MONITORING AND COMPLIANCE

(1) SLDC shall monitor:
    - Forecast accuracy
    - Scheduling compliance
    - Ramp rates
    - Frequency response capability

(2) Non-compliance Consequences:
    - First deviation: Written warning
    - Repeated deviation: Financial penalty
    - Persistent deviation: Grid disconnection notice
    - Disconnection may result in loss of registration

(3) Data Requirements:
    - Real-time generation data (every 5 minutes minimum)
    - Daily performance report
    - Monthly compliance certificate
    - Annual audit of forecasting accuracy

F. FORECASTING CAPABILITY REQUIREMENTS

(1) Generators shall ensure:
    - Forecasting capability with accuracy ±15% (target)
    - Collaboration with meteorological agencies
    - Weather station data integration
    - Machine learning models (for large projects)
    - Regular calibration and improvement

(2) For new projects:
    - Commissioning period (3 months) to establish baseline accuracy
    - Transition period with relaxed tolerance
    - Final compliance after 6 months of operation
