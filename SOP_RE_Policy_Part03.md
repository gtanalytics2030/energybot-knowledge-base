PROVISION: Section 5.1

TITLE: Standalone Wind Projects

SUMMARY:
Section 5.1 covers the procedural framework for development of standalone wind power projects without any hybrid component. This section specifies the registration requirements, development permission process, transfer permission procedures, commissioning requirements, and post-commissioning operational compliance for wind turbine generators (WTGs). Both single-machine projects and aggregated multi-machine wind farms are covered under this framework.

KEY TOPICS: Wind Power, Standalone Wind Projects, Wind Turbine Generators, WTG, Registration, Grid-connected Wind, Development Permission, Commissioning

TEXT:
Section 5.1 - Standalone Wind Projects

Standalone wind projects are those using wind energy resource exclusively for electricity generation without integration with other renewable energy sources. This section covers all procedures for registration, grid connectivity, development permission, transfer permission, commissioning, and operational requirements for standalone grid-connected wind power projects.

The framework applies to:
(a) Single wind turbine generator installations
(b) Wind farms with multiple WTGs at single or multiple sites
(c) Projects of any capacity
(d) Both individual developers and aggregated projects

DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy / Standard Operating Procedure

HIERARCHY: Section 5 / Wind Power Projects

PROVISION: Section 5.2

TITLE: Registration and Grid Connectivity for Wind Projects

SUMMARY:
Section 5.2 establishes the complete procedure for registration and grid connectivity of wind power projects. It covers coordinates verification, development permission process including application forms, processing fees, validity periods, site suitability assessment, land requirements, micro-siting of WTGs, metering arrangements, and progress tracking. Each step specifies documentation requirements, timelines, and approval criteria that project developers must follow.

KEY TOPICS: Wind Registration, Grid Connectivity, Development Permission, Coordinates Verification, Micro-siting, Metering, Work Progress, Application Form

TEXT:
Section 5.2 - Registration and Grid Connectivity

A. COORDINATES VERIFICATION (5.2.1)

(1) Project developers shall submit verified coordinates (latitude and longitude in UTM format) of the proposed wind project site
(2) GEDA shall verify coordinates using satellite imagery and ground surveys
(3) Verification shall confirm:
    - Site location matches application details
    - Site is within Gujarat state boundary
    - No overlap with protected areas or forest zones
    - Compliance with land use regulations

(4) Verification timeline: 10-15 days from submission

B. DEVELOPMENT PERMISSION (5.2.2)

(1) Application Submission:
    - Online application through RE Portal
    - Attachment of detailed Micro-siting Report
    - Technology specifications and equipment list
    - Project layout drawings with WTG positions
    - Environmental and social impact assessment

(2) Development Permission Validity:
    - Valid for 24 months from date of issue
    - Extendable for additional 12 months on request
    - Automatic cancellation if project not commissioned within validity period

(3) Development Permission Revision:
    - Changes in WTG specifications allowed
    - Changes in number of WTGs subject to capacity limits
    - Change of developer requires fresh application
    - Revision requests processed within 20 days

(4) Filing Deadlines:
    - Development permission application: Within 90 days of provisional registration
    - Transfer permission application: At any time before commissioning
    - Final registration application: Within 10 days of project commissioning

C. TRANSFER PERMISSION (5.2.3)

(1) Transfer Process:
    - Original developer (Transferor) and new developer (Transferee) submit joint application
    - Transferee must meet all eligibility criteria as original developer
    - Board Resolution from both entities required
    - Financial capability of Transferee shall be assessed

(2) Transfer Permission Validity:
    - Valid for 24 months from date of issue
    - Same extension provisions as Development Permission

D. COMMISSIONING REQUIREMENTS (5.2.3.9)

(1) Pre-commissioning:
    - All development activities completed
    - CEI inspection and approval of electrical installations
    - Grid connectivity approval from DISCOM/GETCO
    - Completion certificate from developer
    - Insurance policy valid for project life

(2) Commissioning Process:
    - Synchronization with grid by DISCOM/GETCO
    - Energy meter installation and testing
    - Data acquisition system (SCADA) integration
    - Initial generation test run

(3) Post-commissioning:
    - Final registration by GEDA within 10 days of commissioning
    - Communication of commissioning details to SLDC/GETCO
    - Energy accounting setup
    - Monthly reporting to GEDA

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: GIREP-2025, Section 5 (Wind Projects), Development Permission Process, Registration

CROSS-REFERENCES: Coordinates Verification (5.2.1), Application Forms, Land Responsibilities (Section 4.6), Technical Standards (Section 4.7), Grid Connectivity (Section 4.5)

HIERARCHY: Section 6 / Solar Projects

PROVISION: Section 6.1

TITLE: Ground-Mounted Solar Projects

SUMMARY:
Section 6.1 covers the procedural framework for development of ground-mounted solar photovoltaic (PV) power projects. It specifies the registration and development permission process, transfer permission requirements, final registration procedures, and commissioning process. The section covers both individual projects and aggregated solar farms, with specific requirements for site selection, land use, metering arrangements, and work progress monitoring.

KEY TOPICS: Solar Power, Ground-mounted Solar, Solar PV, Solar Registration, Development Permission, Commissioning, Metering, Work Progress

TEXT:
Section 6.1 - Ground-Mounted Solar Projects

Ground-mounted solar projects are those installing solar PV modules on ground-level structures (non-rooftop) for grid-connected electricity generation. This section covers all procedures for registration, grid connectivity, development permission, final registration, and operational requirements.

Types of Ground-Mounted Solar Projects covered:
(a) Utility-scale solar farms (>10 MW)
(b) Medium-scale solar projects (1-10 MW)
(c) Small-scale solar projects (<1 MW)
(d) Solar plants with BESS storage
(e) Aggregated solar projects across multiple sites

A. REGISTRATION AND GRID CONNECTIVITY (6.1.1)

Registration Process:
(1) Provisional Registration:
    - Online application on RE Portal
    - Submission of basic project details and land documents
    - Payment of provisional registration fee
    - Receipt of provisional registration certificate

(2) Development Permission Application (6.1.2):
    - Detailed project report
    - Technical specifications of PV modules and inverters
    - Site layout plan with equipment positioning
    - Single-line diagram
    - Environmental and social impact assessment

(3) Processing Timeline:
    - Complete application to approval: 30-45 days
    - Incomplete applications: 60 days maximum after submission

B. DEVELOPMENT APPLICATION PROCESSING FEES (6.1.2.2)

Fee Structure (exclusive of 18% GST):
- Less than 1 MW: Rs. 8,000/-
- 1 MW to 10 MW: Rs. 12,000/- per MW
- Above 10 MW: Rs. 18,000/- per MW

C. VALIDITY AND REPROCESSING (6.1.2.3, 6.1.2.4)

Development Permission Validity:
(1) Valid for 24 months from date of issue
(2) Extendable for 12 months on application
(3) Reprocessing allowed for:
    - Change in PV module specifications (same or better capacity)
    - Change in inverter capacity (within 10% of original)
    - Change in project layout (if not affecting adjacent properties)
(4) Reprocessing fee: 50% of original development permission fee

D. SITE SUITABILITY AND LAND (6.1.2.6, 6.1.2.7)

Site Suitability Assessment:
(1) Minimum solar irradiance: 5 kWh/m²/day
(2) Site accessibility and road connectivity
(3) Soil bearing capacity for mounting structures
(4) No interference with water bodies, wetlands, or protected areas
(5) Distance from residential areas: Minimum 500 meters

Land Requirements:
(1) Proof of legal possession (registered sale deed or lease)
(2) Minimum lease period: 25 years (for leased land)
(3) Land area requirement: Approximately 2-2.5 hectares per MW
(4) Non-agricultural use permission (if on agricultural land)
(5) No encroachment on common lands or water bodies

E. METERING AND WORK PROGRESS (6.1.2.8, 6.1.2.9)

Metering Arrangements:
(1) AC side energy meter at point of interconnection
(2) Revenue meter complying with IS standards
(3) Real-time monitoring through SCADA/DAS system
(4) Net metering for projects with BESS or export restrictions

Work Progress Reporting:
(1) Quarterly progress reports mandatory
(2) Photographic documentation of:
    - Land preparation
    - Civil works
    - PV module installation
    - Inverter installation
    - Grid connectivity infrastructure
(3) Milestone-based payment release (if in line with any government subsidy)

F. FINAL REGISTRATION (6.1.3)

(1) Application submitted within 10 days of commissioning
(2) Documentation:
    - Completion certificate from developer
    - CEI inspection and approval
    - Grid operator commissioning certificate
    - Meter installation certificate
(3) Final registration issued within 5 days of complete application

G. COMMISSIONING REQUIREMENTS (6.1.4)

Pre-Commissioning Checklist:
(1) Civil works completed and certified
(2) All electrical installations inspected and approved by CEI
(3) PV modules installed and tested
(4) Inverters commissioned and synchronized
(5) Cables and earthing system tested and verified
(6) SCADA system operational
(7) Insurance policy issued and valid
(8) Grid connectivity approved by DISCOM

Commissioning Process:
(1) Grid synchronization by DISCOM representatives
(2) Initial generation test run (minimum 24 hours)
(3) Energy meter testing and sealing
(4) Feed-in approval from grid operator
(5) Documentation of time and date of commissioning

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: GIREP-2025, Section 6 (Solar Projects), Ground-mounted Solar, Development Permission, Registration Process

CROSS-REFERENCES: Rooftop Solar (6.2), Development Permission Fees (6.1.2.2), Technical Standards (Section 4.7), Land Responsibilities (Section 4.6), Grid Connectivity (Section 4.5)

HIERARCHY: Section 6 / Solar Projects

PROVISION: Section 6.2

TITLE: Rooftop Solar Projects

SUMMARY:
Section 6.2 covers the procedure for grid-connected rooftop solar photovoltaic projects. These are typically smaller-scale installations on residential, commercial, or institutional building rooftops. The section specifies eligibility criteria, online registration process, technical feasibility assessment by distribution licensee, grid connectivity requirements, CEI approval, DISCOM agreement, and commissioning process with simplified documentation.

KEY TOPICS: Rooftop Solar, Solar Rooftop, Distributed Solar, Net Metering, DISCOM Agreement, CEI Approval, TFR, Residential Solar, Commercial Solar

TEXT:
Section 6.2 - Rooftop Solar Projects

Rooftop solar projects are grid-connected solar PV installations on building rooftops for self-consumption with provision for grid export (subject to net metering). These are typically smaller installations for residential, commercial, or institutional use.

A. ELIGIBILITY AND SCOPE (6.2.1)

Eligibility:
(1) Building owner with legal ownership or authorization from owner
(2) Rooftop structurally suitable for solar PV installation
(3) Minimum solar insolation: 5 kWh/m²/day
(4) Grid connectivity available through DISCOM
(5) Subject to GERC Net Metering Regulations

Types of Rooftop Solar:
(1) Pure net metering (generation exported to grid)
(2) Self-consumption with surplus export
(3) Rooftop solar with BESS (battery storage)

B. ONLINE REGISTRATION (6.2.2.1)

Registration Process:
(1) Project owner registers on RE Portal
(2) Submission of:
    - Building ownership documents (sale deed or lease)
    - Building plan and rooftop area details
    - Authorization letter (if not owner)
    - Quotation from authorized installer
    - Building load and consumption details

(3) Registration number issued within 5 days

C. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS (6.2.2.2)

Documents to be uploaded on RE Portal:
(1) Covering letter with project details
(2) Building ownership proof
(3) Site photographs (rooftop, building elevation)
(4) Building architectural plan
(5) Load calculation and consumption details
(6) Equipment list (PV module make/model, inverter rating)
(7) Single-line diagram of proposed installation
(8) Quotation and technical proposal from installer
(9) Authorization letter (if not building owner)

D. GRID CONNECTIVITY (6.2.2.3)

Process:
(1) Distribution licensee (DISCOM) issues NOC within 10 days
(2) DISCOM prepares Technical Feasibility Report (TFR)
(3) TFR specifies:
    - Maximum permissible capacity
    - Grid stability assessment
    - Required protection devices
    - SCADA/monitoring requirements
(4) If capacity exceeds TFR limit, project is not feasible

E. CEI ELECTRICAL DRAWING APPROVAL (6.2.2.4)

Process:
(1) Project owner submits electrical drawings to CEI
(2) Drawings should include:
    - Single-line diagram
    - Panel layout plan
    - Protection and safety devices
    - Earthing system design
    - Cable sizing and routing
(3) CEI approval issued within 10 days
(4) Approval valid for 12 months

F. DISCOM AGREEMENT (6.2.2.5)

Agreement Terms:
(1) DISCOM and project owner sign Net Metering Agreement
(2) Agreement specifies:
    - Permitted installation capacity
    - Metering arrangement (gross/net metering)
    - Export tariff and charges
    - Liability and insurance
    - Dispute resolution mechanism
(3) Agreement term: Minimum 25 years

G. WORK PROGRESS (6.2.2.6)

Requirements:
(1) Project owner maintains project progress photo documentation
(2) Quarterly updates on RE Portal (if requested)
(3) Milestone-based documentation:
    - Foundation and structure completion
    - PV module installation
    - Electrical system installation
    - Meter installation

H. METERING ARRANGEMENT (6.2.2.7)

Metering Setup:
(1) Net metering: Export and import separately measured
(2) Meter specifications: Bi-directional, net meter complying with IS standards
(3) DISCOM bears cost of meter installation
(4) Meter reading monthly or quarterly

I. FINAL INSPECTION AND APPROVAL (6.2.2.8)

Process:
(1) Upon completion, project owner requests final inspection
(2) DISCOM inspection checklist:
    - All equipment installed per CEI drawings
    - Earthing and protection devices functional
    - Meter installed and tested
    - Safety labels and warnings in place
(3) CEI conducts electrical safety inspection
(4) Upon approval, DISCOM energizes the system

J. VALIDITY AND REGISTRATION (6.2.2.9, 6.2.2.10)

Validity:
(1) Registration valid from date of provisional registration
(2) No validity period (indefinite once commissioned)
(3) De-registration possible on owner request or non-operation for 1 year

Processing Fees:
(1) Registration fee: Rs. 2,000/- plus GST
(2) Processing fee: Nil (covered under DISCOM net metering agreement)

K. STEP-BY-STEP PROCEDURE (6.2.2.11)

Timeline and Steps:
(1) Registration with GEDA (1-5 days)
(2) TFR from DISCOM (5-10 days)
(3) CEI Approval (5-10 days)
(4) Equipment procurement and installation (30-60 days)
(5) CEI Inspection and Energization Approval (5 days)
(6) DISCOM Agreement signing and meter installation (5-10 days)
(7) Project commissioning (1-2 days)
(8) GEDA portal commissioning update (1-2 days)

Total Timeline: 60-110 days

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: GIREP-2025, Section 6 (Solar Projects), Rooftop Solar, Net Metering, DISCOM, CEI, Grid-connected

CROSS-REFERENCES: Ground-mounted Solar (6.1), GERC Net Metering Regulations, Distribution Code, Technical Standards (Section 4.7), Grid Connectivity (Section 4.5)
