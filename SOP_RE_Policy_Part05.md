DOCUMENT TYPE: Policy / Standard Operating Procedure

HIERARCHY: Section 5.3 / Wind Power - Repowering & Refurbishment

PROVISION: Section 5.3

TITLE: Repowering, Life Extension and Refurbishment of Wind Projects

SUMMARY:
Section 5.3 establishes the comprehensive framework for repowering (replacement with larger, modern turbines), life extension (extending project life beyond 25 years), and refurbishment (component repairs/upgrades) of wind power projects. This section aligns with MNRE's National Repowering & Life Extension Policy 2023 and enables developers to revitalize aging wind farms by replacing them with efficient technology. Repowered projects must achieve 1.5x higher annual energy generation and follow strict eligibility, implementation, and grid connectivity procedures.

KEY TOPICS: Wind Repowering, Life Extension, Refurbishment, WTG Replacement, Repowering Project, Aggregation Project, MNRE Policy, Technology Upgrade, Decommissioning, Waste Management

TEXT:
Section 5.3 - Repowering, Life Extension and Refurbishment of Wind Project

OBJECTIVE AND POLICY FRAMEWORK:

The repowering initiative aims to re-energize old, small-sized and inefficient wind turbines and replace them with larger and more efficient WTGs incorporating improved technology (increased rotor diameters, larger blades, taller towers, higher hub heights) to optimally utilize existing land and infrastructure.

Repowering shall be undertaken in alignment with MNRE's "National Repowering & Life Extension Policy for Wind Power Projects – 2023" and its amendments, ensuring compliance with national standards and certification requirements for new WTGs.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR REPOWERING (Clause 5.3 (e)):

Wind turbines eligible for repowering include:
(1) All wind turbines not in compliance with the quality control order issued by MNRE/GoI
(2) Turbines that have completed their design life as certified under the Type Test Certificate in accordance with applicable standards
(3) Wind turbines with a rated capacity below 2 MW
(4) Turbines based on commercial/voluntary consideration after 15 years of installation
(5) Any other wind turbines qualified for repowering as per the National Repowering & Life Extension Policy for Wind Power Projects – 2023, as amended

Note: Wind turbines required to be replaced within their design life due to malfunctioning, workmanship issues, or safety concerns shall also be repowered or refurbished.

MANDATORY REQUIREMENTS:

1. Registration Timeline (Clause 5.3 (c)):
   - Wind projects completed 25 years from commissioning before GIREP-2025 issuance must register with GEDA within 6 months from policy issuance date

2. Post-Life Obligations (Clause 5.3 (d)):
   - If developers fail to repower WTGs at project life expiry or agreement term end, they must:
     * Decommission the wind power project
     * Surrender grid connectivity
     * Surrender leasehold rights (if on Government-leased land)

3. Repowered Project Life (Clause 5.3 (h)):
   - Life of repowered project: 25 years or actual turbine life, whichever is earlier

4. Energy Generation Requirement (Clause 5.3 (i)):
   - Annual energy generation must be enhanced by at least 1.5x compared to average actual generation in last 3 years prior to repowering/refurbishment

5. Extension and Connectivity (Clause 5.3 (j)):
   - For projects completing 25 years of life, STU connectivity extensions and GEDA land lease extensions granted only upon WTG repowering
   - Additional wind capacity governed by GIREP-2025 Clauses 15.10 and 15.11

6. Exemptions During Execution (Clause 5.3 (k)):
   - Repowering projects exempted from existing PPA obligations for non-availability during execution (maximum 24 months)
   - For wheeling arrangement projects: allowed to purchase grid power during repowering execution
   - Developer permission granted without requiring dismantling of old WTGs (reduces changeover period)

7. Waste Management (Clause 5.3 (l)):
   - All waste from repowering managed per GPCB (Gujarat Pollution Control Board) guidance
   - Developer must hand over waste to authorized recyclers

8. Hybrid Conversion Option (Clause 5.3 (m)):
   - Repowered projects may be converted to hybrid with BESS by installing solar capacity
   - Subject to GETCO connectivity conversion procedures (GETCO Connectivity Procedure 2025)

9. Incentives (Clause 5.3 (g)):
   - Developers eligible for incentives as per GIREP-2025 Clause 15.18

TYPES OF REPOWERING PROJECTS (Section 5.3.1):

(1) Standalone Project: Single or group of WTGs owned by single entity

(2) Aggregation Project: Group of WTGs owned by multiple owners with shared common infrastructure

APPLICATION PROCESS (Section 5.3.2):

Required Documents:
- Prescribed application form (signed and sealed)
- Detailed Project Report (DPR) for repowering, including:
  * Details of old project and proposed new WTGs
  * New WTG capacity, make, rotor diameter, hub height, count
  * Final project capacity
  * Proposed UTM coordinates
  * Technical particulars
- Consent letters from all WTG owners (aggregation projects only)
- Copy of existing Developer Permission / Transfer Permission
- Certificate of Commissioning from GEDA
- Existing land lease agreements and ownership documents
- Existing LTOA / BPTA with GETCO
- Existing PPA / Wheeling Agreement
- Type Test Approval documentation of new WTGs (RLMM-Wind)
- Application processing fees per Section 4.4

Upon submission, GEDA shall issue consent for repowering. Developers obtain grid connectivity from GETCO and sign PPA/wheeling agreement with DISCOM.

IMPLEMENTATION ARRANGEMENTS (Section 5.3.3):

5.3.3.1 Standalone Project:
- Developer submits application in prescribed format with requisite documents
- Refurbishment (life extension) allowed with GEDA consent
- Turbine assessment and certification per UL 4143 (Wind Turbine Generator Lifetime Extension) or BIS standards
- Separate type certificate or extension of existing type certificate issued by certification body
- Total operational life of refurbished turbines shall not exceed 40 years from original commissioning

5.3.3.2 Aggregation Project:
- Private developer or existing WTG owner may act as Wind Repowering Project Aggregator (WRPA)
- WRPA submits repowering proposal to GEDA on behalf of multiple owners
- Requires duly executed consent letters from all identified WTG owners and requisite documents
- If WRPA has acquired WTGs from existing owners, may transfer repowered WTGs to prospective clients
- WRPA assumes all responsibilities per Clause 7(ii) of National Repowering & Life Extension Policy 2023

GRID CONNECTIVITY (Section 5.3.4):

- Developers/owners obtain grid connectivity from GETCO for augmented repowered capacity
- Must comply with GETCO norms, procedures, timelines
- Subsequently sign PPA/wheeling agreement with DISCOM
- Commissioning procedure per Section 5.2.3.9 of guidelines

PPA CONDITIONS AND MORATORIUM (Section 5.3.5):

(1) PPA Existing Generation Quantum:
   - Developers continue supply equivalent to existing capacity generation prior to repowering
   - Determined based on average generation in prior 3 financial years (excluding repowering year)
   - Must comply with existing PPA terms and conditions

(2) Additional Capacity:
   - Additional generation beyond existing quantum may be procured by DISCOMs per GIREP-2025 Clause 19
   - Not obligatory on DISCOMs
   - Developer may sell to third party or self-consume

(3) Wheeling Projects:
   - Consumption of existing generation governed by existing wheeling/transmission agreement
   - Wheeling of additional generation per GIREP-2025 provisions
   - Wheeling Agreement modified/amended accordingly

(4) Post-Repowering Options:
   - Developer may sell incremental capacity to DISCOM or use for self-consumption
   - DISCOM may procure per GIREP-2025 Clause 19

DISMANTLING AND DECOMMISSIONING (Section 5.3.6):

- All dismantling, de-commissioning, land acquisition, evacuation system augmentation
- Renewal of leases and approvals at developer's cost, risk, and responsibility
- GEDA or Government of Gujarat not accountable

REPOWERED STATUS CERTIFICATION (Section 5.3.7):

- Completion and certification checked per MNRE/CEA guidelines
- Developer informs GEDA upon repowering completion
- Date certified by GEDA considered as date of repowering

REFURBISHMENT PROVISIONS (Section 5.3.8):

- Allowed per National Repowering & Life Extension Policy 2023 (MNRE)
- May include gearbox, blade, generator, controller modifications
- Enhancement of rotor diameter, hub height allowed
- Subject to safety and performance assessment per BIS and regulatory bodies
- Total refurbished turbine/project life shall not exceed 40 years from original commissioning (as certified by GEDA)

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: GIREP-2025, MNRE Repowering Policy, Wind Repowering, Life Extension, Refurbishment, WTG Replacement, Decommissioning, Aggregation

CROSS-REFERENCES: National Repowering & Life Extension Policy 2023 (MNRE), GIREP-2025 (Clauses 15.3, 15.5, 15.10, 15.11, 15.18, 19), Development Permission (Section 5.2.2), Grid Connectivity (Section 5.3.4), PPA Conditions

HIERARCHY: Section 5.4-5.5 / Wind Power - Rooftop Wind Turbines

PROVISION: Section 5.4-5.5

TITLE: Grid-Connected Small-Scale Rooftop Wind Turbine Generators (with or without BESS)

SUMMARY:
Section 5.4-5.5 establishes the procedure for installing mini and micro-scale wind turbines (capacity <50 kW) on rooftops or within consumer premises under net/gross metering arrangements. Rooftop wind enables small-scale distributed generation, offsetting consumption or enabling grid export. This section covers online registration, technical feasibility assessment, CEI approval, DISCOM agreement, metering, inspection, commissioning, and validation requirements specific to small-scale wind turbines. Technology selection includes vertical axis (SSVAWT) and horizontal axis (SSHAWT) turbines with specific compliance requirements.

KEY TOPICS: Rooftop Wind, Small-Scale Wind, SSVAWT, SSHAWT, Mini Wind, Micro Wind, Distributed Generation, Net Metering, Rooftop Installation, Grid-Connected

TEXT:
Section 5.4-5.5 - Grid-Connected Small-Scale Rooftop Wind Turbine Generators with or without BESS

POLICY FRAMEWORK:

Per Clause 9.2 of GIREP-2025, mini and micro-scale wind turbines (capacity <50 kW) may be installed by consumers on rooftops or within premises under net metering or gross metering arrangements to offset consumption or sell power to DISCOMs. Projects may participate in Virtual Net Metering (VNM) and Group Net Metering (GNM) schemes as notified by GERC.

Eligible RE developers may avail incentives/benefits under central or State Government schemes when available. Distribution Licensees may procure power per GIREP-2025 Clause 19.

ONLINE REGISTRATION (Section 5.5.1):

Process:
(1) Wind Turbine Manufacturer, Supplier, or EPC Contractor submits application on RE Portal on behalf of consumer
(2) Application submitted to GEDA/RE Portal with required documents for project registration
(3) Consumer applies online through GEDA's Renewable Energy Portal
(4) Applicant enters Consumer Number (auto-fetched), selects Net/Gross metering and Captive/Third Party options
(5) Upon verification, GEDA issues Registration Letter online

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS (Section 5.5.2):

For Individual Consumers:
- Copy of Aadhar Card
- Last receipt of property tax
- Index copy of owned property

For Companies/Organizations:
- Company Registration Certificate (self-certified copy of MOA/ROC/Articles/CoI/Partnership Deed/LLP as applicable)
- Board Resolution authorizing signatory

For All Applicants:
- GST Certificate (if applicable)
- Project Details (capacity, specifications of Small-Scale Rooftop Wind Turbine Generators)
- Structure Stability Certificate from registered/licensed Structural Engineer (building must be under approved building plan)
- Other necessary technical details per GERC net metering regulations and portal specifications

GRID CONNECTIVITY (Section 5.5.3):

(1) Consumer obtains Technical Feasibility Report (TFR) from concerned DISCOM
(2) TFR issued per DISCOM procedures and norms
(3) Consumer uploads TFR on GEDA's RE Portal

CEI ELECTRICAL DRAWING APPROVAL (Section 5.5.4):

(1) For installations not under Central Government jurisdiction, applicant submits technical details with electrical layout and single-line diagram to CEI Gujarat
(2) CEI reviews and approves design per CEA/Electrical safety norms
(3) Rotor swept area must be within consumer premises for safe distance maintenance from adjoining properties

DISCOM AGREEMENT (Section 5.5.5):

(1) After obtaining TFR and CEI approval, consumer signs Net/Gross Metering Agreement with DISCOM
(2) Agreement complies with GERC Net Metering Regulations

WORK PROGRESS (Section 5.5.6):

(1) Consumer installs Rooftop Wind Turbine per BIS/IEC Standards
(2) DISCOM conducts Pre-Commissioning Inspection
(3) Upon satisfaction, DISCOM issues Synchronization Approval

METERING ARRANGEMENT (Section 5.5.7):

(1) Consumer procures bidirectional meters per GERC regulations
(2) Individual wind energy metering installed before termination point/per Supply Code regulations/general DISCOM norms
(3) Ensures accurate energy accounting and regulatory compliance

FINAL INSPECTION AND APPROVAL (Section 5.5.8):

(1) Upon completion, consumer submits detailed test report to CEI Gujarat or CEA per Central Electricity Authority (Measures relating to Safety and Electric Supply) Regulations 2023
(2) CEI/CEA conducts inspection and issues energization permission
(3) DISCOM seals bidirectional meter (deemed commissioning)

VALIDITY OF REGISTRATION (Section 5.5.9):

- Registration valid up to One Year from permission grant date
- Or until GIREP-2025 amendments in force, whichever is earlier
- Within validity, project must be commissioned

TECHNOLOGY SELECTION (Section 5.5.10):

5.5.10.1 Small Scale Vertical Axis Wind Turbines (SSVAWTs):
- Permissible in any region of Gujarat including rooftop applications
- Subject to safety and structural stability norms/GoI Guidelines
- Must minimize noise and vibration per CPCB standards
- Must adhere to wind safety guidelines

5.5.10.2 Small Scale Horizontal Axis Wind Turbines (SSHAWTs):
- Require extensive justification due to noise, shadow flicker, environmental effects
- Must be at substantial buffer zones from habitat areas
- Subject to safety and structural stability norms/GoI Guidelines
- Must demonstrate noise and vibration significantly below CPCB permissible limits

5.5.10.3 Compliance & Certification (Section 5.5.10.3):
- All small-scale wind turbines must comply with certification requirements
- Must meet safety and technical standards ensuring operational efficiency and grid compatibility
- Must be certified by NIWE/MNRE/other International accredited government-recognized agencies
- Applicant must ascertain:
  * Site feasibility and power evacuation capability
  * Expected energy output from SSWT Generator
  * Environmental clearances and Airport Authority clearance (if applicable)
  * Restrictions/regulations under any law or executive order upon area/site
- Government of Gujarat/GEDA neither guarantees electricity generation nor responsible for sub-optimal SSWT performance
- Government absolved from liabilities arising from non-compliance of clearances/approvals

OTHER NECESSARY PROVISIONS (Section 5.5.11):

(1) Wind Turbine Standards:
   - SSWTs must comply with BIS/IEC standards
   - Approved by National Institute of Wind Energy (NIWE) or other International accredited government-recognized agency

(2) Land and Structural Safety:
   - Consumer/developer ensures roof/installation structure withstands all static and dynamic loads
   - Must account for environmental actions and interactions

(3) Safety Compliance:
   - Consistent compliance per CEA (Measures relating to Safety and Electric Supply) Regulations 2023
   - Per manufacturer guidelines

(4) Operation:
   - Consumer/developer monitors safe operation
   - Restricts unauthorized access

(5) Periodical Maintenance:
   - Consumer/developer ensures maintenance per manufacturer recommendations

REGISTRATION APPLICATION PROCESSING FEES (Section 5.5.12):

Fee Structure for Non-Residential Category:
- As per Section 4.4 of guidelines

Fee Structure for Residential Category:
- Residential category exempted from application processing fees
- No charges levied for rooftop solar/wind systems at residential level

STEP-BY-STEP PROCEDURE (Section 5.5.13):

I. Registration with GEDA (1-5 days)
   - Applicant submits online application through GEDA RE Portal
   - Upon verification, GEDA issues Registration letter

II. Technical Feasibility Report from Distribution Licensee (5-10 days)
   - Applicant submits application to Distribution Licensee with GEDA registration
   - DISCOM conducts field assessment (transformer capacity, connected load, feasibility)
   - DISCOM issues feasibility approval or rejection

III. Approval from Chief Electrical Inspector (5-10 days)
   - For installations not under Central Government jurisdiction
   - Consumer submits technical details with electrical layout and single-line diagram to CEI Gujarat
   - CEI issues necessary approvals if satisfactory

Total estimated timeline: 60-110 days from registration to full commissioning and portal update
