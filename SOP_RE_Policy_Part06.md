HIERARCHY: Section 7.2 / Hybrid Projects - Rooftop Wind-Solar Hybrid

PROVISION: Section 7.2

TITLE: Hybridization of Small-Scale Wind-Solar Rooftop Projects (with or without BESS)

SUMMARY:
Section 7.2 establishes the framework for combining wind and solar generation on rooftops or within consumer premises for small-scale applications. Rooftop wind-solar hybrid projects leverage complementary generation patterns (wind typically stronger at night/cloudy days, solar during day) for improved capacity utilization. The section covers two project types: Type-A (hybridization of existing RE projects) and Type-B (new hybrid projects). Procedures include online registration, fee structure, grid connectivity, CEI approval, DISCOM agreement, metering, inspection, and commissioning.

KEY TOPICS: Rooftop Hybrid, Wind-Solar Hybrid, Distributed Hybrid Generation, Small-Scale Hybrid, Rooftop Installation, Type-A Hybridization, Type-B Hybrid, Net Metering

TEXT:
Section 7.2 - Hybridization of Small-Scale Wind-Solar Rooftop Projects

POLICY FRAMEWORK:

Procedures for Rooftop Wind-Solar Hybrid projects follow provisions outlined under Clause 10.3.4 of GIREP-2025.

TWO PROJECT TYPES:

Type-A Projects (Hybridization of Existing Renewable Energy Projects):
- Existing Small-Scale Wind Power or Rooftop Solar Power developers willing to install Solar PV systems or Small-Scale Wind Turbine Generators (SSWTGs) respectively at existing locations
- Aim to avail benefits under GIREP-2025
- Must comply with technical and safety regulations specified by CEA, GERC, and CERC

Type-B Projects (New Wind-Solar Hybrid Power Generation Projects):
- New small-scale rooftop wind-solar hybrid projects permitted under GIREP-2025

ONLINE REGISTRATION (Section 7.2.1):

Process:
(1) EPC player, Supplier, Developer, or Aggregator submits application on RE Portal on behalf of consumer
(2) Application submitted to GEDA/RE Portal with required documents for project registration
(3) Consumer applies online through GEDA's Renewable Energy Portal
(4) Applicant enters Consumer Number (auto-fetched)
(5) Selects relevant details: Net/Gross metering and Captive/Third Party options
(6) Upon submission and verification, GEDA issues Registration Letter online

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS (Section 7.2.1):

For Individual Consumers:
- Copy of Aadhar Card
- Last receipt of property tax
- Index copy of owned property

For Companies/Organizations:
- Company Registration Certificate (self-certified copy of MOA/ROC/Articles/CoI/Partnership Deed/LLP as applicable)
- Board Resolution authorizing signatory

For All Applicants:
- GST Certificate (if applicable)
- Project Details: Capacity and specifications of small-scale rooftop wind turbines and solar PV systems
- Other necessary technical details per GERC net metering regulations and portal specifications

REGISTRATION APPLICATION PROCESSING FEES (Section 7.2.2):

Fee Structure for Non-Residential Category:
- As per Section 4.4 of guidelines

Fee Structure for Residential Category:
- Residential category exempted from application processing fees
- No charges levied for rooftop small-scale wind turbine generators or solar systems at residential level

GRID CONNECTIVITY (Section 7.2.3):

(1) Consumer must obtain Technical Feasibility Report (TFR) from concerned DISCOM
(2) TFR issued per DISCOM procedures and norms
(3) Consumer must upload TFR on GEDA's RE Portal

CEI ELECTRICAL DRAWING APPROVAL (Section 7.2.4):

(1) For installations not under Central Government jurisdiction
(2) Applicant submits technical details with electrical layout and single-line diagram to CEI Gujarat
(3) CEI grants necessary approvals if found satisfactory
(4) CEI reviews and approves design per CEA/Electrical safety norms

SIGNING OF AGREEMENT WITH DISCOM (Section 7.2.5):

(1) After obtaining TFR and CEI approval, consumer signs Net/Gross Metering Agreement with DISCOM
(2) Agreement complies with GERC Net Metering Regulations as issued

WORK PROGRESS (Section 7.2.6):

(1) Consumer installs rooftop wind turbine per BIS/IEC Standards
(2) DISCOM conducts Pre-Commissioning Inspection
(3) If satisfactory, DISCOM issues Synchronization Approval

METERING ARRANGEMENT (Section 7.2.7):

(1) Consumer procures bidirectional meters per GERC regulations and approved procedure of RE connectivity 2025
(2) Individual metering for solar energy generation installed before termination point
(3) Per Supply Code regulations/general DISCOM norms
(4) Ensures accurate energy accounting and regulatory compliance

FINAL INSPECTION AND APPROVAL (Section 7.2.8):

(1) Upon completion, consumer submits detailed test report to CEI Gujarat or CEA
(2) Per Central Electricity Authority (Measures relating to Safety and Electric Supply) Regulations 2023
(3) CEI or CEA conducts inspection and issues energization permission
(4) DISCOM seals bidirectional meter (deemed commissioning for Rooftop solar system)

VALIDITY OF REGISTRATION (Section 7.2.9):

- Registration valid up to One Year from permission grant date
- Or until GIREP-2025 or amendments in force, whichever is earlier
- Within validity, project must be commissioned

HYBRID SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE:

Combined Generation:
- Wind and solar components produce complementary generation profiles
- Wind generation typically peaks during night, cloudy, and winter periods
- Solar generation peaks during daytime and summer periods
- Combined system achieves higher capacity utilization factor (CUF) than standalone systems

Storage Integration:
- Hybrid systems may include Battery Energy Storage (BESS) for:
  * Load shifting and arbitrage
  * Grid support and frequency regulation
  * Improved renewable integration
  * Backup power during low generation periods

OPERATIONAL BENEFITS:

(1) Land Optimization:
   - Dual technology uses same land resource
   - Reduces total land requirement vs. separate facilities

(2) Infrastructure Sharing:
   - Shared grid connection point
   - Single metering arrangement
   - Unified DISCOM agreement

(3) Improved Reliability:
   - Complementary generation patterns reduce output variability
   - Enhanced capacity factor vs. single technology

(4) Consumer Benefits:
   - Higher total generation offsetting consumption
   - Reduced grid import
   - Lower electricity costs over project life

(5) Grid Benefits:
   - Distributed generation reduces transmission losses
   - Smoother renewable integration
   - Reduced grid peak demand

COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS:

All rooftop wind-solar hybrid installations must comply with:
- CEA (Measures relating to Safety and Electric Supply) Regulations 2023
- BIS/IEC standards for electrical safety
- GERC Net Metering Regulations
- CPCB noise and environmental standards
- Local building bylaws and structural safety codes
- DISCOM connectivity and operational requirements

STEP-BY-STEP PROCEDURE (Estimated Timeline: 60-120 days):

1. Registration with GEDA (1-5 days)
2. Technical Feasibility Report (5-10 days)
3. CEI Electrical Approval (5-10 days)
4. Equipment Procurement and Installation (30-60 days)
5. CEI Inspection and Energization (5 days)
6. DISCOM Agreement and Meter Installation (5-10 days)
7. Project Commissioning (1-2 days)
8. GEDA Portal Commissioning Update (1-2 days)

POST-COMMISSIONING REQUIREMENTS:

(1) Monthly Energy Monitoring:
   - Real-time generation data tracking
   - Component performance monitoring
   - Grid export/import tracking

(2) Maintenance:
   - Quarterly wind turbine maintenance (if applicable)
   - Monthly/quarterly solar module cleaning
   - Annual system performance audit

(3) Regulatory Compliance:
   - Annual compliance report to GEDA
   - Participation in net metering settlement
   - Adherence to forecasting requirements (if applicable)

(4) Safety Operations:
   - Qualified personnel trained in hybrid system operation
   - Emergency shutdown capabilities
   - Regular safety inspections
