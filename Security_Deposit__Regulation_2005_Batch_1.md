SUMMARY:
Regulation 1 establishes the formal basis and scope of the Security Deposit Regulations 2005. The Regulations are designated as GERC (Security Deposit) Regulations, 2005, apply state-wide to all Distribution Licensees in their licensed areas, and came into force upon publication in the Gujarat Government Gazette (Notification No. 8 of 2005, dated 31st March 2005).

KEY TOPICS:
- Regulation name and title
- Territorial extent and applicability
- Effective date and commencement
- Distribution Licensees
- Licensed areas

TEXT:

**CHAPTER-1**

**1. Short Title, Extent and Commencement**

(a) These Regulations may be called the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Security Deposit) Regulations, 2005.

(b) These Regulations extend to the whole of the State of Gujarat and shall apply to all Distribution Licensees within their respective licensed areas.

(c) These Regulations shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Gazette.

REGULATORY BASIS:
These Regulations are made in exercise of powers conferred under:
- Section 47 of the Electricity Act, 2003 (Act 36 of 2003)
- Subsection (2)(v) and (w) of Section 181 of the Electricity Act, 2003
- All powers enabling the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission in that behalf

REFERENCED PROVISIONS:
- Section 47 of the Electricity Act, 2003
- Section 181 of the Electricity Act, 2003
- Regulation 3 (Power to require security)
- Regulation 4 (Security Deposit provisions)

CROSS-REFERENCES:
- Applies to all subsequent Regulations and chapters in this notification
- All Distribution Licensees in Gujarat must comply with these Regulations
- Subsequent regulations interpret and implement the scope established here

APPLICABILITY:
This Regulation applies uniformly across:
- All Distribution Licensees in Gujarat
- All licensed areas within respective DISCOM jurisdictions
- All consumer categories unless specifically exempted elsewhere
