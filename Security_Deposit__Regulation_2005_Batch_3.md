TITLE: Interpretation Rules and Power to Require Security Deposit

SUMMARY:
Regulation 2.2 establishes interpretive rules for construing the Regulations, ensuring consistent application of singular/plural terms, inclusive language, evolving regulatory references, and proper use of headings. Regulation 3 establishes the Distribution Licensee's authority under Section 47 of the Electricity Act 2003 to require consumers to deposit sufficient security against estimated payments for electricity supply or associated infrastructure (lines, plants, meters). Failure to provide security allows the licensee to refuse supply.

**2.2 Interpretation:**

In the interpretation of these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

(a) words in the singular or plural term, as the case may be, shall also be deemed to include the plural or the singular term, respectively;

(b) the terms "include" or "including" shall be deemed to be followed by "without limitation" or "but not limited to" regardless of whether such terms are followed by such phrases or words of like import;

(c) references herein to the "Regulation" shall be construed as a reference to these Regulations as amended or modified by the Commission from time to time in accordance with the applicable laws in force.

(d) the headings are inserted for convenience and may not be taken into account for the purpose of interpretation of these Regulations.

(e) references to the statutes, regulations or guidelines shall be construed as including all provisions consolidating, amending or replacing such statutes, regulations or guidelines, as the case may be, referred to.

**CHAPTER-3**

**3. Power to require security**

**3.1** As provided in section 47 of the Act, the Distribution Licensee may require from any person, who requires a supply of electricity to his premises in pursuance of section 43 of the Act, to deposit sufficient security against the estimated payment which may become due to him –

a) in respect of electricity supplied to such person (including Energy Charges, Fixed / Demand Charges, Fuel Price and Power Purchase Adjustment (FPPPA) charges, Electricity Duty and any other charges as may be levied from time to time), or

b) where any electric line or electric plant or electric meter is to be provided for supplying electricity to such person, in respect of the provision of such line or plant or meter.

**3.2** If such person fails to give such security under Regulation 3.1, the distribution licensee may refuse to give the supply of electricity for the period during which the failure continues.

REGULATORY BASIS:
- Section 43 of the Electricity Act, 2003 (Right to supply)
- Section 47 of the Electricity Act, 2003 (Power to require security deposit)

SCOPE OF SECURITY DEPOSIT:

The security deposit covers:
1. **Electricity Supply Charges:**
   - Energy Charges (consumption-based)
   - Fixed/Demand Charges
   - Fuel Price and Power Purchase Adjustment (FPPPA) charges
   - Electricity Duty
   - Any other charges levied from time to time

2. **Infrastructure Provision:**
   - Electric lines (transmission/distribution lines to premises)
   - Electric plants (substations, distribution equipment)
   - Electric meters (metering equipment)

AUTHORITY AND CONDITIONS:

The Distribution Licensee has discretionary authority to require security:
- May require security (not mandatory in all cases)
- Requirement is contingent on person requiring electricity supply
- Security must be "sufficient" against estimated payments
- Applies to any person requiring supply to their premises

CONSEQUENCES OF NON-COMPLIANCE:

Under Regulation 3.2:
- Failure to provide required security allows refusal of supply
- Refusal continues for the period during which failure persists
- No supply rights accrue until security is deposited
- This is a condition precedent to electricity supply

RELATIONSHIP TO ELECTRICITY ACT 2003:

Section 47 of the Electricity Act 2003 empowers the Distribution Licensee to:
- Require security deposit
- Refuse supply if security not given
- The amount and form are subject to regulatory prescription (covered in Regulation 4)

Section 43 of the Electricity Act 2003 establishes:
- Right to demand supply
- This right is exercised subject to security deposit requirements

INTERPRETIVE GUIDANCE:

1. **"Sufficient security"** - Amount is specified in Regulation 4 based on consumer category
2. **"Estimated payment"** - Based on consumption charges calculation methodology in Regulation 4.4
3. **"Any person"** - Applies to all consumer categories (residential, commercial, industrial, agricultural, temporary)
4. **"Refuse to give supply"** - Complete non-supply until security deposited (see Regulation 4.10 for consequences)
