TITLE: Security Deposit for Electricity Supplied/To Be Supplied - Amount and Calculation

SUMMARY:
Regulation 4.1-4.4 establishes binding security deposit requirements for LT and HT consumers. LT consumers must maintain deposits equal to three months' consumption charges (bi-monthly billing) or two months' (monthly billing). HT consumers must maintain one-and-half months' charges. New applicants must pay calculated deposits based on consumer category. Deposits may be tendered as cash or demand draft (DD). The regulation provides comprehensive deposit calculation tables covering residential, commercial, industrial, agricultural, and temporary supply categories with specific unit-consumption multipliers and minimum amounts.

**CHAPTER-4**

**4. Security Deposit for the electricity supplied / to be supplied**

**4.1 LT Consumers - Ongoing Deposit Maintenance:**

The LT consumers shall at all times maintain with the licensee an amount equivalent to Consumption Charges of three months where bi-monthly billing cycle is applicable or to such charges of two months in the case of monthly billing cycle, as the case may be, as security against any default in payment towards the electricity supplied / to be supplied to him during the period, the agreement for supply of energy is in force:

Provided that as and when the bi-monthly cycle is replaced with monthly billing cycle, the licensee shall refund the excess amount if any, over the two months charges estimated at prevailing tariff rate by adjustment against the existing dues or those becoming due immediately thereafter.

**Billing Cycle Requirements:**
- **Bi-monthly billing cycle:** Maintain 3 months' Consumption Charges
- **Monthly billing cycle:** Maintain 2 months' Consumption Charges
- **Transition provision:** When billing cycle changes from bi-monthly to monthly, excess deposits are refunded through adjustment against bills

---

**4.2 HT Consumers - Ongoing Deposit Maintenance:**

The HT consumers shall at all times maintain with the licensee an amount equivalent to Consumption Charges of one and half months as security towards the electricity supplied / to be supplied, against any default in payment during the period the agreement for supply of electricity is in force.

**HT Deposit Requirement:**
- **Fixed requirement:** One-and-half (1.5) months' Consumption Charges
- **Applicable to:** All HT consumers continuously throughout the supply agreement period
- **Basis:** Consumption Charges as defined in Regulation 2.1(d)

---

**4.3 Form of Security Deposit:**

The amount payable towards security shall be in the form of cash or demand draft (DD) drawn in favour of the licensee.

**Accepted Forms:**
1. **Cash** - Direct payment to licensee
2. **Demand Draft (DD)** - Made payable to the Distribution Licensee

**Note:** No other form of security (cheques, bonds, guarantees, etc.) is prescribed.

---

**4.4 Initial Security Deposit for New Applicants:**

The applicants for electricity shall pay in the beginning a Security Deposit towards the electricity to be supplied to them as under:

**SECURITY DEPOSIT CALCULATION TABLE FOR NEW APPLICANTS:**

| **Consumer Category** | **Security Deposit Calculation** | **Minimum Amount** |
|---|---|---|
| **(i) Residential premises** | Consumption Charges for consumption of 50 Units for every 0.5 kW of applied load at prevailing tariff | Rs. 200/- |
| **(ii) Non-residential premises (other than residential)** | Consumption Charges for consumption of 75 Units for every 0.5 kW of applied load at prevailing tariff | Rs. 500/- |
| **(iii) LT Motive Power (governed by connected load)** | **For different HP ranges:** Up to 20 HP: 75 Units/HP; 21-50 HP: 150 Units/HP; 51 HP and above: 300 Units/HP | Rs. 800/- |
| **(iv) LT Supply to Agricultural consumer** | Consumption Charges equivalent to 25% of the prevailing annual tariff rate (based on H.P. tariff) per B.H.P. of contracted load | — |
| **(v) LT Temporary supply** | Consumption Charges for consumption of 10 units/kW of applied load at the prevailing tariff for a minimum period of seven days | — |
| | **For demand-based supply (items vi-x):** Calculation basis is 1.5 times the monthly Consumption Charges with specified demand and load factors | — |
| **(vi) Continuous Process Industry** | 1.5 × Monthly Consumption Charges: Demand charges based on 85% of contract demand + Energy charges based on 80% of load factor + all other levies and taxes (Power factor = 0.9) | — |
| **(vii) Non-Continuous Process Industry** | 1.5 × Monthly Consumption Charges: Demand charges based on 85% of contract demand + Energy charges based on 60% of load factor + all other levies and taxes (Power factor = 0.9) | — |
| **(viii) Central Government, semi-Government and local authority establishments** | 1.5 × Monthly Consumption Charges: Demand charges based on 85% of contract demand + Energy charges based on 40% of load factor + all other levies and taxes (Power factor = 0.9) | — |
| **(ix) Temporary Supply (demand-based)** | 2.0 × Monthly Consumption Charges: Demand charges based on 85% of contract demand + Energy charges based on 60% of load factor + all other levies and taxes (Power factor = 0.9) | — |
| **(x) Other purposes** | 1.5 × Monthly Consumption Charges: Demand charges based on 85% of contract demand + Energy charges based on 50% of load factor + all other levies and taxes (Power factor = 0.9) | — |

**CALCULATION METHODOLOGY:**

**For Energy-Based Categories (i-v):**
- Applied load = Sanctioned load or contract demand
- Unit consumption = Specified units per unit of load capacity
- Consumption Charges = Units × Prevailing tariff rate for relevant consumer category

**For Demand-Based Categories (vi-x):**
- Multiplier = 1.5 or 2.0 times monthly consumption charges (as specified)
- Demand charges component = Specified percentage (85%) × Contract demand × Demand rate
- Energy charges component = Specified load factor percentage × Contract demand × Energy rate
- Power factor = 0.9 (fixed for calculation purposes)
- All levies and taxes = As applicable in tariff schedule
