DOCUMENT: GERC (Security Deposit) (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2023
DOCUMENT TYPE: GERC Amendment Regulation - Substantive Changes
NOTIFICATION: Notification No. 04 of 2023
AMENDS: GERC (Security Deposit) Regulations, 2005 (Principal Regulations, Notification No. 8 of 2005)
HIERARCHY: Amendment / Sections 2 and 3
PROVISION: Amendments to Clauses 2.1(m), 4.11, 4.14
TITLE: Substantive Amendments - Temporary Service, Interest Exclusion, and Accelerated Refund

SUMMARY:
The Third Amendment introduces three focused changes to the Principal Regulations: (1) Clause 2.1(m) extends temporary service period from "one month" to "twelve months," accommodating longer-term temporary electricity supply needs; (2) Clause 4.11 excludes temporary consumers from interest accrual on deposits, reducing administrative burden and distinguishing temporary from permanent consumer protection regimes; (3) Clause 4.14 accelerates security deposit refund timeline from "30 days" to "7 days," significantly enhancing consumer protection and cash flow efficiency. These amendments reflect consumer-centric policy refinement while maintaining regulatory clarity and operational feasibility.

KEY TOPICS:
- Temporary service definition extension
- Temporary service period (1 month to 12 months)
- Interest calculation mechanism
- Temporary consumer exclusion from interest
- Security deposit refund procedures
- Refund timeline acceleration (30 days to 7 days)
- Consumer cash flow protection
- Operational efficiency improvements

---

**SECTION 2: AMENDMENT IN CHAPTER 2 OF THE PRINCIPAL REGULATIONS**

**Amendment to Clause 2.1(m) - Definition of "Temporary Service"**

**Original Clause 2.1(m) (Principal Regulations 2005):**

"Temporary Service" means supply of electricity will be given initially for a period not exceeding one month subject to review for further extension in accordance with the classifications of installation for purpose of permanent supply.

**Amended Clause 2.1(m) (Third Amendment 2023):**

"Temporary Service" means supply of electricity will be given initially for a period not exceeding twelve months' subject to review for further extension in accordance with the classifications of installation for purpose of permanent supply.

---

**COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS - CLAUSE 2.1(m):**

| Aspect | Original (2005) | Amended (6 Dec 2023) |
|--------|---|---|
| **Temporary Service Period** | "Not exceeding one month" | "Not exceeding twelve months" |
| **Duration Extension** | 1 month | 12 months (12x increase) |
| **Subject to Review** | Yes (for extension) | Yes (for extension) |
| **Purpose** | Purpose of permanent supply | Purpose of permanent supply [UNCHANGED] |
| **Rationale** | Short-term temporary need | Accommodates extended temporary needs |

---

**KEY CHANGES - CLAUSE 2.1(m) AMENDMENT:**

**Change 1: Period Extended from 1 Month to 12 Months**

**Original:** "not exceeding one month"
**Amended:** "not exceeding twelve months"

**Magnitude:** 1200% increase in initial supply period

**Effect:**
- Temporary connections can now operate for up to 12 months initially
- Much closer to actual temporary usage patterns
- More practical for seasonal operations, project-based industries, events
- Reduces need for frequent reclassification or new connections

**Context:**
The 1-month limit in 2005 regulations was likely based on very short-term supply needs. Modern temporary applications (construction sites, seasonal industries, project installations, entertainment events) often require 3-12 months of supply.

**Consumer Categories Affected:**
- Construction/demolition sites (typically 3-12 months)
- Seasonal agricultural/horticultural operations (6-12 months)
- Entertainment/event venues (days to months)
- Temporary industrial operations (3-6 months)
- Film production and tourism facilities (1-3 months)
- Disaster relief installations (weeks to months)

**Example Applications:**

**Before Amendment (2005 rules):**
- Construction site needs 6-month supply
- Must apply for temporary connection (1 month max)
- After 1 month, either: convert to permanent OR apply for new temporary (again limited to 1 month)
- Result: Multiple applications, compliance burden

**After Amendment (2023):**
- Construction site needs 6-month supply
- Applies for temporary connection
- Gets 12-month initial supply authorization
- Supply automatically terminates at project end or can be converted to permanent
- Result: Single application, cleaner compliance

---

**Implications for Deposit Calculation:**

**For Temporary Service Deposits (Regulation 4.4(v)):**

Original Rule:
- Deposit: "Consumption Charges for consumption of 10 units/kW of applied load at the prevailing tariff for a minimum period of seven days"
- Basis: 7-day minimum (very short term)

With 12-month authorization:
- Same deposit rule applies, but now covers 12-month period (not 1 month)
- Deposit amount calculated based on 10 units/kW multiplier
- Covers entire 12-month risk period (from 7 days to 12 months)
- No deposit increase required (same multiplier applies to extended period)

**SECTION 3: AMENDMENT IN CHAPTER 4 OF THE PRINCIPAL REGULATIONS**

**Amendment to Clause 4.11 - Interest on Deposits (PART I)**

**Original Clause 4.11 (Principal Regulations 2005, as may have been amended):**

"The licensee shall pay interest on Security Deposit of consumer for the electricity supplied, at the Bank Rate (as on 1st April of every year) notified by Reserve Bank of India or such higher rate as may be fixed by the Commission from time to time. The interest accrued to the credit of the consumer during the previous year shall be adjusted in electricity bills of May of every year for consumers covered under the monthly billing cycle and in electricity bill of May or June for those covered under two monthly billing cycles."

**Amended Clause 4.11 (Third Amendment 2023):**

"The licensee shall pay interest on Security Deposit of consumer other than Security Deposit received from Temporary Consumer for the electricity supplied, at the Bank Rate (as on 1st April of every year) notified by Reserve Bank of India or such higher rate as may be fixed by the Commission from time to time. The interest accrued to the credit of the consumer during the previous year shall be adjusted in electricity bills of May of every year for consumers covered under the monthly billing cycle and in electricity bill of May or June for those covered under bi monthly billing cycles."

---

**COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS - CLAUSE 4.11:**

| Aspect | Original (2005/2015) | Amended (6 Dec 2023) |
|---|---|---|
| **Interest Scope** | "Security Deposit of consumer" (all) | "Security Deposit of consumer other than Temporary Consumer" |
| **Temporary Consumer Interest** | Entitled to interest | NOT entitled to interest |
| **Bank Rate Application** | All consumers | Permanent consumers only |
| **Interest Rate** | RBI Bank Rate or higher (GERC) | RBI Bank Rate or higher (GERC) [UNCHANGED FOR PERMANENT] |
| **Crediting Mechanism** | May (May/June) billing cycle | May (May/June) billing cycle [UNCHANGED FOR PERMANENT] |

---

**KEY CHANGE - CLAUSE 4.11 AMENDMENT:**

**Change 1: Exclusion of Temporary Consumers from Interest**

**Original:** "Security Deposit of consumer" (includes all consumers, including temporary)
**Amended:** "Security Deposit of consumer other than Security Deposit received from Temporary Consumer"

**Effect:**
- Temporary consumers NO LONGER accrue interest on deposits
- Only permanent consumers accrue interest
- Simplifies deposit accounting for short-term temporary connections
- Reduces administrative burden

**Rationale:**
Temporary consumers hold deposits for short periods (now up to 12 months). Interest accrual on short-term deposits is:
- Administratively complex
- Yields minimal interest benefit (12 months × 6-7% = small amount)
- Creates tracking complexity
- Not material for temporary supply relationship

---

**Interest Distinction - Permanent vs. Temporary:**

**PERMANENT CONSUMERS (Residential, Commercial, Industrial):**
- Deposit held for: Years (often 5-10+ years)
- Interest accrual: YES (at Bank Rate or higher)
- Crediting: Annually in May (or May/June) bills
- Example: Rs. 5,000 deposit × 6% = Rs. 300 annual interest
- Accumulation: Compounds over years (significant)

**TEMPORARY CONSUMERS (Construction, Events, Projects):**
- Deposit held for: Months (now up to 12 months initially)
- Interest accrual: NO (as of 6 December 2023)
- Crediting: N/A
- Example: Rs. 5,000 deposit × 6% × (3-12 months) = Minimal benefit
- Result: Zero administrative complexity, zero interest tracking

---

**Implementation Impact:**

**For Licensees:**
- Segregate temporary vs. permanent deposits in accounting systems
- Exclude temporary deposits from annual interest calculations
- No interest entries for temporary consumer deposits
- Reduced monthly/annual interest calculation complexity

**For Permanent Consumers:**
- NO CHANGE - Continue to receive interest accrual
- Interest mechanism unchanged
- Crediting still in May (or May/June) bills

**For Temporary Consumers:**
- Deposits NO LONGER earn interest
- Refund upon termination will not include accumulated interest
- Simple, transactional deposit relationship
- Faster processing (no interest reconciliation)

---

**EXAMPLE SCENARIO:**

**Permanent Consumer (LT Residential):**
- Deposit: Rs. 3,000
- Connected date: January 2021
- Interest rate: 6% p.a. (Bank Rate)
- Till December 2023: 3 years of interest accrual
- Interest accrued: Rs. 3,000 × 6% × 3 = Rs. 540
- Upon termination (Dec 2023): Refund = Rs. 3,540 (deposit + interest)

**Temporary Consumer (Construction Site):**
- Deposit: Rs. 5,000
- Connection date: January 2023 (under amended rules)
- Temporary service period: 12 months (Jan-Dec 2023)
- Interest accrual (amended 6 Dec 2023): NONE (after amendment date)
- Interest accrued: Rs. 0 (excluded from amended 4.11)
- Upon termination (Dec 2023): Refund = Rs. 5,000 (deposit only, no interest)

**Comparison:**
- Permanent consumer gets interest benefit (Rs. 540)
- Temporary consumer gets no interest (deposit only)
- Reflects distinction in consumer relationship (permanent vs. transitory)

**SECTION 3: AMENDMENT IN CHAPTER 4 (CONTINUED)**

**Amendment to Clause 4.14 - Refund of Security Deposit (PART II)**

**Original Clause 4.14 (Principal Regulations 2005):**

"The Licensee, on termination of the agreement by either party shall refund Security Deposit after adjustment of dues, if any within 30 days."

**Amended Clause 4.14 (Third Amendment 2023):**

"The Licensee, on termination of the agreement by either party shall refund Security Deposit after adjustment of dues, if any within 7 days."

---

**COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS - CLAUSE 4.14:**

| Aspect | Original (2005) | Amended (6 Dec 2023) |
|---|---|---|
| **Refund Timeline** | Within 30 days | Within 7 days |
| **Period Reduction** | 30 days | 7 days (23-day reduction) |
| **Acceleration** | Baseline | 77% faster (30 days → 7 days) |
| **Trigger** | Termination by either party | Termination by either party [UNCHANGED] |
| **Condition** | After adjustment of dues | After adjustment of dues [UNCHANGED] |

---

**KEY CHANGE - CLAUSE 4.14 AMENDMENT:**

**Change 1: Refund Timeline Reduced from 30 Days to 7 Days**

**Original:** "within 30 days"
**Amended:** "within 7 days"

**Acceleration:** 23-day reduction (77% faster)

**Effect:**
- Dramatically faster consumer access to refunded deposits
- Significant liquidity improvement for consumers
- Refund must be issued within one week of termination
- Much tighter compliance deadline for licensees

---

**IMPACT OF ACCELERATION:**

**Consumer Impact - Benefit:**

**Before Amendment (30 days):**
- Consumer terminates supply on 1 January
- Calculation period: 1-30 January (full month)
- Refund could arrive: By 31 January (or even February if delayed)
- Total deposit locked: ~40-50 days (if some delay)
- Example: Rs. 5,000 × ~45 days = ~Rs. 8.20 lost liquidity per day

**After Amendment (7 days):**
- Consumer terminates supply on 1 January
- Calculation period: 1-7 January (one week)
- Refund must arrive: By 8 January (strict deadline)
- Total deposit locked: ~8-10 days (minimal delay possible)
- Example: Rs. 5,000 × ~9 days = ~Rs. 1.23 lost liquidity per day
- Improvement: ~82% reduction in liquidity lock period

---

**Practical Scenarios:**

**Scenario 1: Residential LT Consumer Moving Away**

*Before Amendment:*
- Termination notice given: 1 June
- Dues adjusted: By 10 June
- Refund deadline: 30 June
- Reality: Check received 1-15 July (if minor delays)
- Deposit amount: Rs. 3,000
- Waiting period: ~30-45 days

*After Amendment:*
- Termination notice given: 1 June
- Dues adjusted: By 3 June
- Refund deadline: 7 June
- Reality: Check/transfer by 8 June (strict compliance)
- Deposit amount: Rs. 3,000
- Waiting period: ~5-7 days (5-6 times faster)

**Scenario 2: Large Industrial Consumer Relocating Facility**

*Before Amendment:*
- Termination notice: 1 September
- Final billing: By 15 September
- Refund deadline: 15 October (30 days)
- Reality: Wire transfer received: ~20 October (if processed)
- Deposit amount: Rs. 5,00,000
- Waiting period: ~40-50 days
- Opportunity cost: ~40 days of no deposit proceeds

*After Amendment:*
- Termination notice: 1 September
- Final billing: By 3 September
- Refund deadline: 9 September (7 days)
- Reality: Wire transfer received: 10 September (strict deadline)
- Deposit amount: Rs. 5,00,000
- Waiting period: ~8-9 days
- Opportunity cost: Minimal (~1/5th of before)

---

**Licensee Operational Impact:**

**Systems Changes Required:**

1. **Accelerated Billing Cycle:**
   - Must complete final billing within 2-3 days (not 15-20)
   - Requires real-time meter reading
   - Digital billing integration essential

2. **Faster Due Adjustment:**
   - Dues must be calculated immediately
   - Cannot batch process
   - Integration with accounting system

3. **Quick Refund Issuance:**
   - Cheque printing/signing within 5-6 days
   - Or digital fund transfer setup
   - Process automation necessary

4. **Documentation:**
   - Refund statement prepared quickly
   - Reconciliation completed within 7 days
   - No room for delays or batch processing

**Implementation Timeline Example:**

```
Day 1: Consumer gives termination notice
Day 2: Meter reading scheduled and completed
Day 3: Final bill calculated and issued; dues determined
Day 4: Due adjustment completed; refund amount finalized
Day 5-6: Refund issuance processed (cheque/DD prepared or fund transfer initiated)
Day 7: Refund deadline (must be delivered or credited)
```

**For Distribution Licensees:**

**AMENDMENT SUMMARY - CONSUMER PERSPECTIVE:**

**Temporary Consumer (e.g., Construction Site):**
- **Benefit 1:** Can use temporary supply for full 12 months (vs. 1 month)
- **Benefit 2:** No interest accrual (simpler calculation, lower deposit in some contexts)
- **Benefit 3:** Refund within 7 days (vs. 30 days) - much faster cash recovery

**Permanent Consumer (e.g., Residential Household):**
- **Benefit 1:** No change to deposit requirements
- **Benefit 2:** Interest continues to accrue at Bank Rate
- **Benefit 3:** Refund within 7 days (vs. 30 days) - 4x faster cash recovery

**REGULATORY PHILOSOPHY EVOLUTION:**

**2005 Regulations:**
- Conservative approach (1-month temp, 30-day refund)
- Maximum protection for licensees
- Simple, rigid framework

**2015 Amendment (Second):**
- Consumer protection enhancements
- Bank guarantee options, bill disclosure, faster refunds (30 to next-bill adjustment)
- Recognition of large-deposit consumers

**2023 Amendment (Third):**
- Further consumer-centric refinements
- 12-month temporary authorization (practical reality)
- Simplified temporary treatment (no interest)
- Accelerated refunds (7-day hard deadline)
- Modern operational approach

**Trend:** Progressive shift toward consumer protection while maintaining operational clarity

---

**EFFECTIVE DATE & APPLICABILITY:**

All three amendments effective: **6 December 2023**

**Prospective Application:**
- All new temporary connections: 12-month authorization
- Existing temporary connections: Subject to reclassification (review to 12 months if ongoing)
- All deposits: Refund within 7-day deadline
- Temporary deposits (from 6 Dec 2023): No interest accrual

**Transition Issues:**
- Existing temporary connections on 6 Dec 2023: May be reclassified
- Deposits held on 6 Dec 2023: Existing rules apply until refund
- New connections from 7 Dec 2023: All amended rules apply
