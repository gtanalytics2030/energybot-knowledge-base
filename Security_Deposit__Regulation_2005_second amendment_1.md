SUMMARY:
The Second Amendment Regulations 2015 formally amend the Principal Security Deposit Regulations 2005 (Notification No. 8 of 2005). The Amendment Regulations are titled GERC (Security Deposit) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2015, extend to the entire State of Gujarat, became effective upon publication in the Gujarat Government Gazette on 15 July 2015, and supersede the earlier First Amendment Regulations 2010 (effective from 21 August 2010). This amendment is issued under Section 47 and Section 181(2)(v) and (w) of the Electricity Act 2003 and Clause 5.3 of the Principal Regulations, utilizing the Commission's regulatory power to amend provisions as circumstances require.

TEXT:

**SECTION 1: SHORT TITLE, EXTENT AND COMMENCEMENT**

**(i) Short Title:**

These Regulations may be called the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Security Deposit) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2015.

**(ii) Extent:**

These Regulations extend to whole of the State of Gujarat.

**(iii) Commencement:**

These Regulations shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Gujarat Government Gazette.

**(iv) Supersedence:**

These Regulations shall supersede the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (Security Deposit) (First Amendment) Regulations, 2010, which came into effect from 21-8-2010.

**SCOPE OF AMENDMENTS (Overview):**

The Second Amendment modifies **FOUR** existing provisions and adds **ONE** new provision:

1. **Amendment to Clause 4.3** (Payment Form) - Adds bank guarantee option
2. **Amendment to Clause 4.6** (Review Frequency) - Changes review timelines for HT and LT consumers
3. **Amendment to Clause 4.9** (Deposit Adjustment) - Modifies refund procedure and timelines
4. **Amendment to Clause 4.15** (New) - Adds new requirement for deposit disclosure on bills

3. Improve refund efficiency and consumer protection
4. Enhance transparency through deposit disclosure on bills

**EFFECTIVE APPLICATION:**

**For Consumers:**
- All consumers must comply with amended provisions from 15 July 2015
- Existing deposits: May be subject to bank guarantee conversion if exceeding Rs. 25 Lacs
- New consumers: Apply amended calculation and payment form rules

**For Distribution Licensees:**
- Must implement amended procedures immediately
- Must maintain compliance with all amended clauses
- Must disclose existing deposits on bills (new requirement Clause 4.15)
- Must adjust review frequency per Clause 4.6 amendments

**For Regulatory Body (GERC):**
- Monitors licensee compliance with amended provisions
- Receives consumer grievances regarding amendments
- May issue orders/guidelines for implementation (Regulation 5.2)
