SUMMARY:
Exhibit A presents the comprehensive 25-year tariff computation model for biomass power projects utilizing water-cooled condenser technology. The exhibit displays annual cost and tariff calculations spanning years 1–25 of the plant's useful life (01 April 2023 to 31 March 2048 for projects with SCOD on 01 April 2023). Key parameters: Net energy sold ranges from 55.19 lakh kWh (Year 1, 70% PLF) to 63.07 lakh kWh (Years 2–25, 80% PLF). Cost components include O&M (5% of capital cost, 5.72% annual escalation), depreciation (4.67% p.a. Years 1–15; 2% Years 16–25, SLM method up to 90% of Rs. 575 Cr/MW capital cost), term loan interest (9.65%, 15-year tenure), working capital interest (10.15%), RoE (15.5%), taxes (MAT 17.47% Yr 1–10; CT 34.94% Yr 11–25), and fuel cost (Rs. 5044/MT base, 5% annual escalation, 4423 kCal/kg GCV, 3800 kCal/kWh SHR, 10% auxiliary consumption). Fixed tariff levelized at 9.52% WACC discount rate yields Rs. 2.16/kWh (without AD benefit) or Rs. 2.00/kWh (with accelerated depreciation benefit applied). Variable tariff escalates annually: FY 2023-24 Rs. 4.82/kWh, FY 2024-25 Rs. 5.06/kWh, FY 2025-26 Rs. 5.31/kWh, FY 2026-27 Rs. 5.57/kWh (applied year-wise per tariff order methodology).

TITLE: 25-Year Tariff Levelization Model – Bagasse-based Cogeneration Technology

SUMMARY:
Exhibit C presents the comprehensive 25-year tariff computation model for bagasse-based cogeneration power plants. The exhibit displays annual cost and tariff calculations spanning years 1–25 of plant useful life. Key parameters: Net energy sold is constant at 48.09 lakh kWh annually (60% PLF assumption for cogeneration plants, lower than biomass due to process steam integration constraints). Capital cost: Rs. 5.22 Cr/MW. Cost components include O&M (3% of capital cost—lower than biomass due to simpler fuel handling, 5.72% annual escalation), depreciation (4.67% p.a. Years 1–15; 2% Years 16–25, SLM method up to 90% of capital cost), term loan interest (9.65%, 15-year tenure), working capital interest (10.15%), RoE (15.5%), taxes (MAT 17.47% Yr 1–10; CT 34.94% Yr 11–25), and fuel cost (Rs. 2780/MT base, 5% annual escalation, 2250 kCal/kg GCV—lower than biomass due to bagasse's lower energy content, 3600 kCal/kWh SHR, 8.5% auxiliary consumption—lower than biomass systems). Fixed tariff levelized at 9.52% WACC discount rate yields Rs. 2.15/kWh (without AD benefit) or Rs. 1.99/kWh (with accelerated depreciation). Variable tariff escalates annually: FY 2023-24 Rs. 4.86/kWh, FY 2024-25 Rs. 5.10/kWh, FY 2025-26 Rs. 5.36/kWh, FY 2026-27 Rs. 5.63/kWh. Cogeneration fixed tariff is lower than biomass-only systems despite smaller scale, reflecting operational synergies with sugar factory heat/steam integration and lower O&M burden.
