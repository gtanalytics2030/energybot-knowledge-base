**PROVISION:** Annexure III

**TITLE:** Tariff Schedule for MSW-based Power Projects Using Mass Incineration Technology

**SUMMARY:**
Annexure III presents the year-by-year tariff schedule for MSW-to-Energy projects using mass incineration technology over 25-year project life. The schedule includes normative operational parameters: 1st year PLF 65%, 2nd year onwards PLF 75%, net energy sold 48.11 lakh kWh in year 1 increasing to 55.52 lakh kWh from year 2 onwards, auxiliary consumption 15.5%. Financial costs tracked include: O&M costs escalating at 3.84% annually; straight-line depreciation at 4.67% for first 15 years then 2%; interest on 70:30 debt-equity ratio financed over 15-year loan tenure at 10.15% interest; working capital at 10.65% interest; return on equity at 14%; and taxation (17.47% MAT first 10 years, 34.94% corporate tax years 11-25). Year-by-year tariff declines from Rs. 8.54/kWh in year 1 to Rs. 6.07/kWh by year 16, with levelized tariff of Rs. 7.07/kWh for projects not availing accelerated depreciation (AD) benefit and Rs. 6.50/kWh for projects availing AD benefit.

RDF technology achieves higher PLF and lower auxiliary consumption than mass incineration, resulting in different tariff profile.
