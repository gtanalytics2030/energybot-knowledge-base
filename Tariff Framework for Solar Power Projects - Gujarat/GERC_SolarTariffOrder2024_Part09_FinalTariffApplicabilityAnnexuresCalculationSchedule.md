TITLE: Final Determined Tariff — Levelized Rs. 2.76/kWh, Net Tariff Rs. 2.48/kWh (After AD Benefit) for Solar Projects Below 5 MW

SUMMARY:
This is the operative tariff-determination clause for Solar Power Projects below the threshold limit of eligibility for competitive bidding (below 5 MW), commissioned during the New Control Period, decided under Section 62 read with Section 86(1)(a),(e) of the Electricity Act, 2003 and the National Tariff Policy 2016/GERC (MYT) Regulations. The clause sets out the complete Benchmark Operational and Financial Parameters table (consolidating all values fixed in Sections 4.1-4.10: Capital Cost Rs. 305 Lakhs/MW, 70:30 Debt-Equity, 10.15% Interest on Term Loan, 15-year repayment, depreciation 4.67%/2%, 9.77% Discount Rate, O&M Rs. 3 Lakh/MW with 3.84% escalation, 15.5% RoE, 17.47%/34.94% MAT/Corporate Tax, 10.65% interest on working capital, 19% CUF, 16.64 Lakh kWh Annual Generation, 25-year project life) and states the final tariff decided by the Commission: Levelized tariff of Rs. 2.76/kWh, AD (Accelerated Depreciation) benefit of Rs. 0.28/kWh, and Net tariff of Rs. 2.48/kWh. The clause clarifies that developers eligible for Central or State Government financial assistance, incentives, subsidies, or other benefits must factor such benefits into the tariff computation, and the Commission shall re-determine project-specific tariff accordingly where such benefits are availed. All Solar Power Projects below 5 MW commissioned during the Control Period of this Order are eligible to receive this determined tariff.

For each of the 25 years it sets out Net Energy sold (constant at 16.64 lakh kWh, derived from the 19% CUF fixed in Section 4.3), and the cost components: O&M (escalating from Rs. 3.00 lakh at 3.84% p.a. per Section 4.2), Insurance (nil throughout), Depreciation under Straight Line Method (Rs. 14.23 lakh for Years 1-15, stepping down to Rs. 6.10 lakh for Years 16-25 per the 4.67%/2% rates fixed in Section 4.6), Interest on term loan (declining from Rs. 20.95 lakh in Year 1 to nil by Year 16 as the 15-year term loan fixed in Section 4.5 is repaid), Interest on working capital (per Section 4.7), Return on Equity (constant at Rs. 14.18 lakh throughout, per the 15.5% RoE fixed in Section 4.8), and Tax on equity (Rs. 2.48 lakh for Years 1-10 under MAT, stepping up to Rs. 4.96 lakh for Years 11-25 under Corporate Tax, per Section 4.8). These sum to a year-by-year Total Cost (Rs. lakh) ranging from Rs. 55.69 lakh in Year 1 down to Rs. 31.52 lakh in Year 16 and rising gradually to Rs. 34.06 lakh by Year 25, and a corresponding annual Tariff (Rs./kWh) ranging from Rs. 3.35 in Year 1 down to Rs. 1.89 in Year 16 and rising to Rs. 2.05 by Year 25. Applying the Discount Rate of 9.77% (fixed in Section 4.9) to this 25-year cash flow yields the final Levelized Tariff of Rs. 2.76/kWh, which is the figure carried into Section 4.11's final tariff determination (before subtracting the AD benefit to arrive at the Net Tariff of Rs. 2.48/kWh).
