## CHUNK 7: Clause 3.3 – Wheeling of Power for Third-Party Sale (Injection at 66 KV or above / Drawl at 66 KV or above)

---
DOCUMENT: GERC Order No. 05 of 2024 - Tariff Framework for Procurement of Power by Distribution Licensees and Others from Wind Power Projects for the State of Gujarat

DOCUMENT TYPE: Tariff Order

HIERARCHY: Section 2 / Clause 2.6 / Sub-Clause 2.6.3

PROVISION: Clause 3.3(a) – Wheeling of Power for Third-Party Sale (High Voltage Level)

TITLE: Transmission Charges and Losses for Third-Party Wind Power Sale at 66 KV and Above

SUMMARY:
For wind power projects engaged in third-party sale where electricity is injected at 66 KV level or above and drawn at 66 KV level or above, transmission of energy from injection point to drawl place shall be allowed by paying applicable transmission charges and losses on energy feed basis. Charges are determined by the Commission from time to time, as applicable to green energy open access transactions under GERC (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations, 2024, and shall be consistent with tariff regulations of the Commission. The Commission does not allow concessional transmission/wheeling chargers and losses, as such concessions would burden other consumers. This provision applies when both injection and drawl voltages are at the same level (66 KV or above).

KEY TOPICS: Third-party sale, Wheeling, Transmission Charges, Transmission Losses, Green Energy Open Access, GETCO (transmission), Voltage level (66 KV), Energy feed basis, Commission Tariff Regulations, Distribution Licensee

TEXT:
**2.6.3 Analysis and Commission's Ruling**

The Commission has gone through the suggestion received from various stakeholders. The Commission also notes that State Government has notified Renewable Energy Policy, 2023. Further, the Commission also notes that Ministry of Power has notified Green Energy Open Access Rules, 2022. The Commission has notified Green Energy Open Access Regulations, 2024. The Commission considers that for the promotion of Green Energy projects, the benefits be granted in such a manner that it should not result in burden to the end consumer. The Commission has to strike balance between the captive / third party generator supplying/ using electricity in the license area of DISCOM and its impact on the business of the distribution licensees.

In view of this, the Commission do not find any merit for allowing concessional transmission / wheeling chargers and losses as it will burden other consumers. However, the Commission decides to allow concessional Cross Subsidy Surcharge and Additional Surcharge, if any, for a specific Open Access Transaction which result in benefit of both the RE generator and the distribution utility. Accordingly, the Commission decides to modify the relevant clause of this order as under:

**Wheeling of power for Third Party Sale**

a) In case of injection of the electricity at 66 KV level or above and drawl of electricity at 66 KV level or above, the transmission of energy from the injection point to drawl place shall be allowed by paying applicable transmission charges and losses on energy feed basis as determined by the Commission from time to time, as applicable to Green Energy Open Access Transaction as per GERC (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations, 2024 and amendments in it read with tariff Regulations of the Commission as applicable from time to time.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: GERC (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations, 2024, GERC Tariff Regulations (from time to time), MoP Green Energy Open Access Rules 2022, Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy 2023, Distribution Code provisions on open access, Clause 3.3(d) on Cross Subsidy Surcharge concessions

CROSS-REFERENCES: Clause 3.3(b) for injection/drawl at different voltage levels, Clause 3.3(d) on Cross Subsidy Surcharge and Additional Surcharge concessions (25% concession for renewable attributes not claimed), Clause 3.2(a) on control period applicability, GERC Grid Code Section 4 on transmission and wheeling, GETCO Tariff Regulations

## CHUNK 8: Clause 3.3 – Wheeling of Power for Third-Party Sale (Injection at 66 KV / Drawl Below 66 KV)

---
DOCUMENT: GERC Order No. 05 of 2024 - Tariff Framework for Procurement of Power by Distribution Licensees and Others from Wind Power Projects for the State of Gujarat

DOCUMENT TYPE: Tariff Order

HIERARCHY: Section 2 / Clause 2.6 / Sub-Clause 2.6.3

PROVISION: Clause 3.3(b) – Wheeling of Power for Third-Party Sale (Mixed Voltage Levels)

TITLE: Transmission and Wheeling Charges for Third-Party Wind Power Sale (66 KV Injection / Below 66 KV Drawl)

SUMMARY:
For third-party wind power sale where electricity is injected at 66 KV level or above but drawn at voltage levels below 66 KV, wheeling is allowed upon payment of: (1) applicable transmission charges and transmission losses on energy feed basis; (2) wheeling charges and wheeling losses on the energy fed into grid as measured at receiving Sub-Station of GETCO. All charges are determined by the Commission from time to time and applied as per GERC Green Energy Open Access Regulations 2024, amendments thereto, and tariff regulations of the Commission. This provision addresses the scenario where power from a high-voltage injection point is distributed through lower-voltage distribution networks to multiple consumer locations.

KEY TOPICS: Third-party sale, Wheeling, Transmission Charges, Transmission Losses, Wheeling Charges, Wheeling Losses, Voltage levels (66 KV / below 66 KV), GETCO, Distribution network, Energy feed basis, Green Energy Open Access, Multiple locations

TEXT:
b) In case of injection of electricity at 66 KV or above and drawl of electricity below 66 KV voltage level, wheeling of electricity generated from the Wind power Project to desired location(s) within the State shall he allowed on payment of applicable transmission charges & transmission losses on energy feed basis and wheeling charges & wheeling losses on the energy fed into grid as measured at receiving Sub-Station of GETCO, as determined by the Commission from time to time and applicable to green energy open access transaction as per GERC (Green Energy Open Access) Regulations 2024 and amendments in it read with Tariff Regulations of the Commission as applicable from time to time.

## CHUNK 9: Clause 3.3 – Wheeling of Power for Third-Party Sale (Intra-DISCOM Below 66 KV)

---
DOCUMENT: GERC Order No. 05 of 2024 - Tariff Framework for Procurement of Power by Distribution Licensees and Others from Wind Power Projects for the State of Gujarat

DOCUMENT TYPE: Tariff Order

HIERARCHY: Section 2 / Clause 2.6 / Sub-Clause 2.6.3

PROVISION: Clause 3.3(c) – Wheeling of Power for Third-Party Sale (Below 66 KV, Within Same DISCOM Area)

TITLE: Wheeling Charges and Losses for Intra-DISCOM Third-Party Wind Power Sale

SUMMARY:
For third-party wind power sale where both injection and drawl points are at voltage levels below 66 KV and lie within the area of the same DISCOM, wheeling of energy from the wind project is allowed upon payment of wheeling charges and wheeling losses of energy fed to grid. Charges apply as per GERC (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations, 2024, and amendments thereto, as applicable to green energy open access transactions. This provision simplifies the charging mechanism for local third-party transactions within a single distribution licensee's supply area, bypassing transmission-level charges.

## CHUNK 10: Clause 3.3(d) – Cross Subsidy Surcharge and Additional Surcharge (Third-Party Sale)

---
DOCUMENT: GERC Order No. 05 of 2024 - Tariff Framework for Procurement of Power by Distribution Licensees and Others from Wind Power Projects for the State of Gujarat

DOCUMENT TYPE: Tariff Order

HIERARCHY: Section 2 / Clause 2.6 / Sub-Clause 2.6.3

PROVISION: Clause 3.3(d) – Cross Subsidy Surcharge and Additional Surcharge for Wind Energy Third-Party Sale

TITLE: Cross Subsidy Surcharge (CSS) and Additional Surcharge Concessions for Third-Party Wind Power Sale

SUMMARY:
The Commission allows 25% concession in Cross Subsidy Surcharge (CSS) and Additional Surcharge for third-party wind power sale when: (1) the renewable energy (RE) generator and consumer do not claim RE attribute; and (2) the distribution licensee is allowed to avail the same for Renewable Purchase Obligation (RPO) compliance. In contrast, wind energy generators selling power under third-party sale while utilizing RE attribute for RPO compliance of the consumer, or projects registered under REC mechanism, are NOT eligible for CSS concession and must pay 100% Cross Subsidy Surcharge and Additional Surcharge as determined by the Commission. These provisions are governed by MoP's Rules and GERC Green Energy Open Access Regulations, 2024, as amended from time to time. The concession benefits both the RE generator (through lower charges) and the distribution utility (through RPO compliance with unclaimed attributes).

KEY TOPICS: Cross Subsidy Surcharge (CSS), Additional Surcharge (AS), Third-party sale, RE attribute, RPO (Renewable Purchase Obligation), REC mechanism, Green Energy, 25% concession, Distribution Licensee benefit, Electricity Act 2003 Section 42

TEXT:
d) The Commission decides to promote the third-party sale /consumption of Wind Energy by allowing 25% concession in the Cross Subsidy Surcharge and Additional Surcharge wherein the RE generator and consumer does not claim RE attribute and allow distribution licensee to avail the same for RPO compliance.

No concession in the Cross Subsidy Surcharge shall be allowed to the Wind generator who are selling power under third party sale and utilizing RE attribute for RPO compliance of the consumer or the projects are registered under REC mechanism. They shall be liable to pay 100% Cross Subsidy Surcharge and Additional Surcharge, if any, as determined by the Commission read with the provisions of GERC Green Energy Open Access Regulations.

e) The provisions related to Cross Subsidy Surcharge and Additional Surcharge shall be governed as per the MOP Rules and GERC (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations, 2024 as amended from time to time.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Electricity Act 2003 Section 42 (cross subsidy surcharge and additional surcharge), MoP Green Energy Open Access Rules 2022, GERC (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations 2024, CERC REC Mechanism Regulations, Renewable Purchase Obligation (RPO) guidelines

CROSS-REFERENCES: Clause 3.3(a)-(c) on transmission and wheeling charges (where CSS/AS are applied), Clause 3.9 on exemptions from demand cut (removed during this control period), Clause 3.10 on CDM benefit sharing, GERC Tariff Regulations (CSS and AS components), Distribution Code provisions on open access conditions

PROVISION: Clause 3.3 – Wheeling of Power for Captive Use (High Voltage Level)

TITLE: Transmission Charges and Losses for Captive Wind Power Consumption at 66 KV and Above

SUMMARY:
For wind power projects where consumers use the generated electricity for captive consumption (own consumption by a captive consumer), and electricity is injected at 66 KV level or above with drawl also at 66 KV level or above, transmission of energy from injection point to drawl place is allowed by paying applicable transmission charges and losses on energy feed basis. HIERARCHY: Section 2 / Clause 2.6 / Sub-Clause 2.6.3

PROVISION: Clause 3.3 – Wheeling of Power for Captive Use (Mixed Voltage and Intra-DISCOM Scenarios)

TITLE: Wheeling Charges for Captive Wind Power Consumption at Lower Voltage Levels

SUMMARY:
For captive consumption of wind power: (1) where injection is at 66 KV or above but drawl is below 66 KV, applicable transmission charges, transmission losses on energy feed basis, wheeling charges, and wheeling losses on the energy fed into grid (as measured at GETCO receiving Sub-Station) apply; (2) where both injection and drawl are below 66 KV within the same DISCOM area, only wheeling charges and wheeling losses on grid-fed energy apply. All charges are determined by the Commission from time to time as per GERC Green Energy Open Access Regulations 2024 and amendments. The person claiming captive consumption must provide ownership details of the captive generating plant and consumption details annually to the distribution licensee in whose supply area the captive consumer is situated, in accordance with GERC Green Energy Open Access Regulations, 2024, and Electricity Rules, 2005, to ensure captive status compliance.

KEY TOPICS: Captive use, Captive Generating Plant (CGP), Wheeling Charges, Wheeling Losses, Transmission Charges, Voltage levels (66 KV / below 66 KV), Intra-DISCOM, Ownership verification, Annual reporting, Captive consumer, Electricity Rules 2005, Green Energy Open Access

## CHUNK 13: Clause 3.3 – Wheeling to More Than One Location

---
DOCUMENT: GERC Order No. 05 of 2024 - Tariff Framework for Procurement of Power by Distribution Licensees and Others from Wind Power Projects for the State of Gujarat

DOCUMENT TYPE: Tariff Order

HIERARCHY: Section 2 / Clause 2.6 / Sub-Clause 2.6.3

PROVISION: Clause 3.3 – Wheeling of Power to More Than One Location

TITLE: Additional Wheeling Charges for Multiple Consumer Locations (Captive or Third-Party)

SUMMARY:
Wind power project developers who desire to wheel electricity to more than one location for captive use or third-party sale shall pay an additional charge of 5 paise per unit (5 P/kWh) on energy fed into the grid, as measured at the receiving end sub-station of GETCO, to the concerned DISCOM in whose area power is consumed. This additional charge is in addition to the transmission charges, transmission losses, wheeling charges, and wheeling losses as applicable under the relevant provision (whether third-party sale or captive use scenario). The 5 paise per unit charge reflects the additional complexity of managing multiple draw-off points and coordination across multiple DISCOM supply areas.

KEY TOPICS: Wheeling, Multiple locations, Additional charge (5 paise per unit), Captive use, Third-party sale, GETCO, DISCOM, Energy feed basis, Grid coordination

TEXT:
**Wheeling of power to more than one locations:**

Wind power Project Developers, who desire to wheel electricity to more than one location for captive use/third-party sale, shall pay 5 paise per unit on energy fed in the grid as measured at receiving end sub-station of GETCO, to the concerned DISCOM in whose area power is consumed in addition to above mentioned transmission charges & losses and Wheeling charges & losses, as applicable.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: GERC Tariff Regulations (from time to time), GERC (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations 2024, GETCO Tariff Regulations, Clause 3.3(a)-(c) for primary wheeling charges applicable scenarios

CROSS-REFERENCES: Clause 3.3(a)-(c) on main wheeling charge provisions, Clause 3.4 on metering and registration at each location, GERC Distribution Code Section 4 on open access charges, GERC Green Energy Open Access Regulations 2024 (multiple consumer scenarios)
