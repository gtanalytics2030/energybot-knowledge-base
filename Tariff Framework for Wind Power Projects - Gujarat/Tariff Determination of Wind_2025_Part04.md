## CHUNK 14: Clause 3.4 – Metering Point and Interconnection Point – Commission's Decision

---
DOCUMENT: GERC Order No. 05 of 2024 - Tariff Framework for Procurement of Power by Distribution Licensees and Others from Wind Power Projects for the State of Gujarat

DOCUMENT TYPE: Tariff Order

HIERARCHY: Section 2 / Clause 2.7 / Sub-Clause 2.7.3

PROVISION: Clause 3.4 – Metering Point and Interconnection Point for Wind Power Projects

TITLE: Metering and Interconnection Requirements for Wind Power Projects

SUMMARY:
The Metering Point and Interconnection Point for wind power projects shall be at the point of connection at the GETCO substation where feasibility and connectivity is granted by GETCO. Wind Project Developers shall provide energy metering and communication facility in accordance with Central Electricity Authority (Installation and Operation of meters) Regulations, 2014, Gujarat Electricity Grid Code 2013, GERC Intra-State Open Access Regulations 2011, GERC Distribution Code 2004, and GERC (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations, 2024. Wind projects shall install four-quadrant ABT (Availability Based Tariff) compliant meters at both interface points (receiving and sending end) that conform to CEA Regulations 2014, compatible with SIDC (State Integrated Dispatch Centre) AMR (Automatic Meter Reading) facility. Meter data shall be available every Tuesday for the previous week to SLDC through AMR-compatible meters. For projects connected to ISTS through state network, metering provisions follow intra-state generator rules; for projects directly connected to ISTS, CERC Regulations apply. Remote Terminal Units (RTU) must be installed at pooling stations and individual WTGs for real-time data transfer to SLDC.

KEY TOPICS: Metering Point, Interconnection Point, GETCO, Four-quadrant ABT meter, CEA Regulations 2014, SIDC AMR facility, SLDC, Remote Terminal Unit (RTU), Energy metering, Communication facility, State grid, ISTS connection, Captive Generating Plant

TEXT:
**2.7.3 Analysis and Commission's Ruling**

As regard to the suggestions of IWEA, the Commission notes that the installation of RTU on each WTG is necessary for monitoring of injection of energy by the SLDC on a real time basis. The Commission finds merit in the submission of SLDC and hence accepts the suggestion received from SLDC for benefit of all stakeholders and decide to modify the provisions as under:

• Metering Point and Interconnection Point for the Wind Power Project shall be the point of connection at the GETCO substation where feasibility and connectivity is granted by GETCO. The Wind Project Developers shall provide energy metering and communication facility in accordance with the (a) CEA (Installation and Operation of meters) (Amendment) Regulations 2014 and its subsequent amendments, (b) Gujarat Electricity Grid Code 2013 and its subsequent amendments (c) GERC (Terms and Conditions of Intra-State Open Access) Regulations, 2011 and its subsequent amendments, (d) GERC Distribution Code 2004 and its subsequent amendments and (e) GERC (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations, 2024 and its subsequent amendments.

• Wind Power Projects shall have to provide four quadrant ABT compliant meters at both interface points (i.e. receiving and sending end) which shall conform to the Central Electricity Authority (Installation and Operation of Meters) Regulations, 2014, as amended from time to time. GETCO/DISCOM to stipulate necessary specifications in this regard. The ABT meter shall be compatible with SIDC AMR facility.

Further, wind generators/GEDA/DISCOM/GETCO as case may be shall ensure that the Generator end and GETCO substation end data shall be available on every Tuesday for the previous week to SLDC through such AMR compatible meters. In case of non-availability of AMR, it will be responsibility of Wind Generators/GEDA/DISCOM/GETCO, as the case may be, to send ABT meter data in encrypted mode to SLDC with other mode of communication as per SLDC procedure and requirement.

• The Wind Project Developers shall also install Remote Terminal Unit (RTU) at the pooling station as well at individual WTG at their own cost for transferring the real time data to SLDC for its monitoring purpose, and in accordance with the GERC orders from time to time.

• State Load Dispatch Centre shall certify actual injected energy and energy drawn (if any) from local DISCOM on monthly basis. Energy metering and communication facility shall be provided by the developer of Wind Power Projects in accordance with the following Regulations/Codes/Orders and their subsequent amendments:

i. Central Electricity Authority (Installation and Operation of meters) Regulations 2014 and its subsequent amendments
ii. Gujarat Electricity Grid Code 2013 and its subsequent amendments
iii. GERC (Terms and Conditions of Intra-State Open Access) Regulations, 2011 and its subsequent amendments
iv. GERC distribution code 2004 and its subsequent amendment
v. GERC terms and conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations 2024

For the purpose of energy accounting, all Wind Power Project Developer shall have to provide ABT compliant (four quadrant) meters and if the power is to be wheeled to consumers' premises, then ABT cum Tariff compatible meter is to be installed at the consumers' premises also. While in case of consumer seeking open access below 1 MW, installation of Special Energy Meter capable of energy recording on 15 Minute Time Block basis at consumption end shall be allowed. GEDA, GETCO and DISCOMs shall ensure the energy accounting of Active and Reactive energy from Wind project for each consumer/customer. Energy Accounting shall be done by SLDC.

Provided that in case of Wind project connected with Inter-State Transmission System (ISTS) network through state network, the provisions related to metering point and interconnection point shall be governed by the provisions of Intra-State generator provided in this Order. While in case of the Wind Projects directly connected to ISTS network shall be governed by the provisions of CERC Regulations.

## CHUNK 15: Clause 3.5 – Pricing of Reactive Power – Commission's Decision

---
DOCUMENT: GERC Order No. 05 of 2024 - Tariff Framework for Procurement of Power by Distribution Licensees and Others from Wind Power Projects for the State of Gujarat

DOCUMENT TYPE: Tariff Order

HIERARCHY: Section 2 / Clause 2.8 / Sub-Clause 2.8.3

PROVISION: Clause 3.5 – Pricing of Reactive Power

TITLE: Reactive Power Charges and Grid Support for Wind Turbine Generators

SUMMARY:
Reactive power pricing for all prospective wind power projects shall follow the same methodology as decided by the Commission in the GETCO Tariff Order, read with provisions of the GERC Grid Code, from time to time. Reactive power is required by wind energy generators during initial start-up and by station transformers for continuous operation. To maintain grid stability and limit reactive power consumption from the grid or injection into the grid, wind projects are required to install suitable compensation devices. Reactive energy charges are applicable and leviable as per GERC Grid Code and Tariff Orders. When Wind Turbine Generators support the grid as per Grid Code provisions (supplying or absorbing reactive power per SLDC instruction), no reactive energy charges are applicable. The Commission did not modify reactive power provisions, as they are adequately covered in existing Grid Code and Tariff Orders and address stakeholder concerns regarding fair treatment of projects that provide grid-stabilization services.

KEY TOPICS: Reactive Power, Reactive Energy Charges, Wind Turbine Generators (WTG), Grid stability, Compensation devices, Grid support, GERC Grid Code, GETCO Tariff Order, Net basis (import less export), WTG capability specifications, SLDC instruction, Grid Code compliance

TEXT:
**2.8.3 Analysis and Commission's Ruling**

The Commission notes that the Reactive Power is required by Wind Energy Generators during initial start-up and station transformers also continuously require reactive power from the grid. Hence, in order to maintain grid stability, it is necessary to limit such reactive power consumption from the grid or injection into the grid by installation of suitable compensation devices.

In order to restrain the wind power projects from consuming more reactive power from the grid and to encourage them to install suitable compensation devices to limit such reactive power consumption, the levy of reactive charges is necessary.

The Commission is of view that the provisions related to pricing of Reactive Energy Charges as proposed in the discussion paper adequately covers the concerns of the Stakeholders and therefore there is no need to make any changes in this provision.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: GERC Grid Code (reactive power requirements and specifications), GETCO Tariff Order (reactive power pricing), GERC Tariff Regulations (reactive energy charge determination), Electricity Act 2003 Section 21 (grid code and technical standards), Indian Electricity Grid Code 2023 (grid support requirements)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Clause 3.4 on metering (ABT meters measure reactive energy), Distribution Code provisions on technical standards for generators, GERC Grid Code Regulations on reactive power limits, SLDC Procedure and operation manuals, CERC Grid Code (for ISTS-connected projects)
