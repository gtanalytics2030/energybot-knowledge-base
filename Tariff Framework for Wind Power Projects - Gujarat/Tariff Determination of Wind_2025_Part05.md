## CHUNK 16: Clause 3.7 – Banking Facility and Energy Accounting – Commission's Decision (Overview)

---
DOCUMENT: GERC Order No. 05 of 2024 - Tariff Framework for Procurement of Power by Distribution Licensees and Others from Wind Power Projects for the State of Gujarat

DOCUMENT TYPE: Tariff Order

HIERARCHY: Section 2 / Clause 2.9 / Sub-Clause 2.9.3

PROVISION: Clause 3.7 – Banking Facility and Energy Accounting (Overview and Framework)

TITLE: Banking Facility, Energy Accounting, and Surplus Energy Treatment for Wind Power Projects

SUMMARY:
Banking facility and energy accounting provisions for wind power projects are governed by MoP's Green Energy Open Access Rules, 2022, and GERC (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations, 2024, and subsequent amendments. Banking is an optional facility for consumers availing open access from wind projects. Consumers may choose not to avail banking by furnishing an undertaking as specified in GERC Open Access Regulations 2024. For projects directly connected to ISTS and supplying power to state consumers, banking provisions follow applicable CERC Regulations read with MoP Rules. Applicable charges (transmission, wheeling, CSS, Additional Surcharge) apply to consumers using state grid for open access with or without ISTS utilization. Energy accounting provisions differ based on whether banking facility is availed: Case-1 applies banking (30% minimum, evaluated on 15/5-minute time block basis); Case-2 applies 15-minute time block basis without banking; Case-3 applies REC mechanism per CERC Regulations. The Commission kept banking and energy accounting provisions unchanged from the discussion paper, as they align with MoP Rules and GERC Open Access Regulations 2024.

KEY TOPICS: Banking Facility, Energy Accounting, Banked energy, Billing cycle, 15-minute time block, 5-minute time block, RPO (Renewable Purchase Obligation), REC mechanism, Distribution Licensee, Captive consumer, Third-party sale, MoP Rules, CERC Regulations, Surplus energy

TEXT:
**2.9.3 Analysis and Commission's Ruling**

The Commission would like to clarify that the banking and energy accounting related provisions proposed in the discussion paper are in line with the MoP Green Energy Open Access Rules and GERC (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations 2024 and quite clearly dealt in the GERC (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations 2024.

In view of above, the Commission decided to keep the banking and energy accounting related provisions for new control period as proposed in the discussion paper without any modifications.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: MoP Green Energy Open Access Rules 2022, GERC (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations 2024, CERC REC Mechanism Regulations, Electricity Act 2003 Section 42 (open access and conditions), Electricity Rules 2005 (captive plant definition)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Clause 3.7 Case-1, Case-2, and Case-3 (detailed energy accounting provisions), Clause 3.3 (applicable charges and CSS concessions), Clause 3.4 (metering points for energy accounting), Clause 3.2(e) on forecasting and scheduling, GERC Green Energy Open Access Regulations 2024 (banking facility detailed rules)

## CHUNK 17: Clause 3.8 – Security Deposit and Commissioning Timeline – Commission's Decision

---
DOCUMENT: GERC Order No. 05 of 2024 - Tariff Framework for Procurement of Power by Distribution Licensees and Others from Wind Power Projects for the State of Gujarat

DOCUMENT TYPE: Tariff Order

HIERARCHY: Section 2 / Clause 2.10 / Sub-Clause 2.10.3

PROVISION: Clause 3.8 – Security Deposit and Consequences for Delay in Commissioning of Wind Power Projects

TITLE: Bank Guarantee Requirements and Commissioning Timeline for Wind Power Capacity Evacuation Systems

SUMMARY:
Wind power developers setting up projects shall provide a Bank Guarantee (BG) of Rs. 10 lakhs per MW to GETCO based on allotment of transmission capacity. If the developer fails to commission the wind capacity within the specified time period, GETCO shall encash the Bank Guarantee. Commissioning timelines for the entire evacuation line (including bays and metering system) vary by capacity: 1-100 MW (12 months), >100-200 MW (15 months), >200-400 MW (18 months), >400-1000 MW (24 months) from the date of transmission capacity allotment. Developers must ensure evacuation system readiness prior to SCOD or the stipulated timeframe, whichever is earlier; failure does not entitle waiver of liquidated damages per Agreement/PPA. The developer must commission at least 10% of allotted capacity within one month of evacuation line charging or per timeframe, whichever is earlier, or pay long-term transmission charges for that 10% until commissioned. The balance 90% must be commissioned within one year of line charging or per timeframe, whichever is earlier; failure results in STU cancellation of connectivity/open access for uncommissioned capacity with no claim on such capacity and requirement to pay relinquishment charges. For wind projects under competitive bidding route (DISCOM PPAs), these provisions are governed by approved bid documents/PPA. Projects facing unforeseen circumstances may approach the Commission for time extension.

KEY TOPICS: Security Deposit, Bank Guarantee (Rs. 10 lakhs/MW), GETCO, Transmission Capacity Allotment, Commissioning Timeline, 10% Capacity, 90% Capacity, Scheduled Commissioning Date (SCOD), Evacuation System, DISCOM PPA, Connectivity, Open Access, Relinquishment Charges, Liquidated Damages, Force Majeure

TEXT:
**2.10.3 Analysis and Commission's Ruling**

The Commission clarify that in case of Wind Power project selling power to DISCOM under PPA, the delay in commissioning of the project shall be considered as per the provisions of the respective PPA / Agreement with concerned DISCOM. However, in respect of Wind Power Plant set up for third party sale / captive use, the consequences for delay in commissioning of projects shall be governed as provided in this Order.

The Commission decides that the Wind Power Developer setting up project shall be required to provide Bank Guarantee @ 10 lakhs per MW to GETCO based on allotment of transmission capacity.

**Table 24: Capacity and Commissioning Period for the Wind Projects**

| Sr. No. | Wind Capacity in MW | Period for commissioning the entire evacuation line along with bays and metering system |
|---------|-------|-----------------|
| 01. | 1MW to 100 MW | 12 months from the date of allotment of transmission capacity |
| 02. | >100 MW to 200 MW | 15 months from the date of allotment of transmission capacity |
| 03. | >200 MW to 400 MW | 18 months from the date of allotment of transmission capacity |
| 04. | >400 MW to 1000 MW | 24 months from the date of allotment of transmission capacity |

The Wind Project Developer shall ensure and prove that the Evacuation System consist of Transmission and /or Distribution System shall be ready prior to SCOD or aforesaid timeframe, whichever is earlier. Failure to it, the project developer is not eligible to get any waiver in Liquidated Damages payable by it, in terms of Agreement/ PPA.

The Wind Power Project Developer shall commission the project for at least 10% of the allotted capacity within one month of charging the evacuation line or as per timeframe stipulated table above, whichever is earlier, failing which, the Developer shall be liable to pay long-term transmission charges for 10% of the allotted capacity until such 10% of the allotted capacity is commissioned.

The balance 90% capacity shall require to be commissioned within one year of charging of evacuation line or as per timeframe stipulated above, whichever is earlier, failing which STU shall cancel the connectivity and Open Access granted, to the extent of capacity not commissioned and the RE developer shall have no claim on such capacity and pay relinquishment charges as determined by the Commission. Further, STU shall include such cancelled capacity in the list of spare available capacity for RE integration to be published on their website for prospective consumers.

If the Wind Project Developer (as Generator / Consumer/ Licensee) fails to Commission the entire allocated evacuation system along with bays and metering System within stipulated time-period due to unforeseen reasons, they may approach to the Commission seeking for extension of time period.

In case of Wind Project set up under competitive bidding route, in that case the aforesaid provision shall be governed by the provisions of approved bid documents /PPA.

## CHUNK 18: Clause 3.9 – Exemptions from Demand Cut – Commission's Decision (Removal)

---
DOCUMENT: GERC Order No. 05 of 2024 - Tariff Framework for Procurement of Power by Distribution Licensees and Others from Wind Power Projects for the State of Gujarat

DOCUMENT TYPE: Tariff Order

HIERARCHY: Section 2 / Clause 2.11 / Sub-Clause 2.11.3

PROVISION: Clause 3.9 – Exemptions from Demand Cut (Removed in New Control Period)

TITLE: Removal of Demand Cut Exemption for Wind Power Projects (Third-Party Sale and Captive Use)

SUMMARY:
The Commission agreed to remove the provision that would have exempted 50% of installed capacity of wind power projects engaged in captive consumption and third-party sale from demand cut obligations. The Commission noted that this exemption was not provided by the State Government in the Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy 2023, and GERC (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations 2024 also do not provide for such exemption from demand cut. Therefore, for the new control period (06.06.2022 to 31.03.2027), wind power projects are subject to the standard demand cut provisions applicable to all consumers during peak demand periods, with no special exemptions based on renewable energy status or project type.

KEY TOPICS: Demand Cut, Wind Power Projects, Captive consumption, Third-party sale, Exemption (removed), Green Energy Open Access, Distribution Licensee, Demand management, Load shedding, Peak demand

TEXT:
**2.11.3 Analysis and Commission's Ruling**

The Commission agree to the suggestion made by GUVNL and decided to remove the provision of exemption from demand cut to the extent of 50% of installed capacity of Wind Power Project in case of captive consumption and third-party sale within the State during new control period.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Gujarat Renewable Energy Policy 2023, GERC (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations 2024, GERC Distribution Code (demand management provisions), Electricity Act 2003 Section 31 (load management during contingencies)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Clause 3.2(a) on control period applicability, GERC Green Energy Open Access Regulations 2024 (no exemptions from demand cut), Distribution Code Section on consumer obligations, GERC Tariff Orders (demand cut procedures)

## CHUNK 19: Clause 3.10 – Sharing of CDM and Carbon Credit Benefits – Commission's Decision

---
DOCUMENT: GERC Order No. 05 of 2024 - Tariff Framework for Procurement of Power by Distribution Licensees and Others from Wind Power Projects for the State of Gujarat

DOCUMENT TYPE: Tariff Order

HIERARCHY: Section 2 / Clause 2.12 / Sub-Clause 2.12.3

PROVISION: Clause 3.10 – Sharing of Clean Development Mechanism (CDM) Benefits and Carbon Credits

TITLE: Benefit-Sharing Framework for CDM Credits and Carbon Offsets from Wind Power Projects

SUMMARY:
Wind power projects may qualify for benefits under Clean Development Mechanism (CDM) or other carbon credit mechanisms (e.g., voluntary carbon markets) based on energy generation from wind-based power generation projects. Such projects shall apply to the concerned authority to avail/receive these benefits. Benefits receivable or received shall be shared with the procurer of power and/or the distribution licensee as follows: (1) Year 1: Project developer retains 100% of gross proceeds from CDM or other clean energy mechanism benefits; (2) Year 2 onwards: Beneficiary share (power procurer/licensee) increases by 10% annually until reaching 50% of proceeds, thereafter shared equally between the generating company and beneficiaries; (3) Competitive Bidding Route: Benefit-sharing governed by terms and conditions of bid documents read with PPA; (4) Open Access Route: Benefit-sharing governed by agreement between power producer and consumer. The Commission found these provisions adequately address stakeholder concerns and determined no changes were necessary in the CDM benefit-sharing framework.

KEY TOPICS: CDM (Clean Development Mechanism), Carbon Credits, CERs (Certified Emission Reductions), Carbon Offset, Renewable Energy Benefits, Wind Power Projects, Benefit-sharing, Power Procurer, Distribution Licensee, Generating Company, Competitive Bidding, Open Access, PPA

TEXT:
**2.12.3 Analysis and Commission's Ruling**

The Commission notes that the provisions related sharing of CDM benefits adequately addresses the concerns of the Stakeholder and therefore no need to make any changes in the provisions related to sharing CDM benefits proposed in the discussion paper.

**Key Elements of CDM Benefit-Sharing Framework:**

a) 100% of the gross proceeds on account of such CDM benefit or any other benefit under Clean Energy Mechanism from any source to be retained by the project Developer in the first year after the date of commercial operation of the generating station.

b) In the second year, the share of the Beneficiaries like power procurer/licensee shall be 10% which shall be progressively increased by 10% every year till it reaches 50%, thereafter the proceeds shall be shared in equal proportion, by the Generating Company and the Beneficiaries like power procurer/licensee.

c) In case of PPA signed under competitive bidding process, the sharing of benefits received by the wind power project shall be as per terms and conditions of bid documents read with the PPA.

d) In case of Wind Power Project set up under Open Access regime, the sharing of above benefits shall be in accordance with the Agreement between Power Producer and Consumer.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (CDM eligibility), Ministry of Environment guidelines on CDM and carbon credits, Electricity Act 2003 Section 61 (tariff terms and conditions), Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) terms, CERC REC Mechanism Regulations

CROSS-REFERENCES: Clause 3.1 on tariff framework (tariff discovery in competitive bidding where CDM benefits are typically factored), Clause 3.3 on transmission and wheeling charges (applicable alongside benefit-sharing), GERC Green Energy Open Access Regulations 2024 (for open access scenario), PPA provisions in Distribution Code

## CHUNK 20: Clause 3.12 – Procedure for Integration of Wind Power Project with Grid – Commission's Decision

---
DOCUMENT: GERC Order No. 05 of 2024 - Tariff Framework for Procurement of Power by Distribution Licensees and Others from Wind Power Projects for the State of Gujarat

DOCUMENT TYPE: Tariff Order

HIERARCHY: Section 2 / Clause 2.13 / Sub-Clause 2.13.3

PROVISION: Clause 3.12 – Pre-Commissioning Integration Procedure for Wind Power Projects

TITLE: Compliance Certificate and Documentation Requirements for Wind Power Project Grid Integration

SUMMARY:
Prior to commissioning and integration of wind projects with the grid, wind generators/developers must submit a compliance certificate signed by an authorized signatory (CMD, CEO, MD, or Full-Time Director) to SLDC, GEDA, and concerned distribution licensees before declaration of Scheduled Commissioning Date (SCOD). The certificate must confirm that the generating station, including main plant equipment (wind turbines, auxiliary systems), has complied with all relevant provisions of: (1) CEA Technical Standards for Connectivity; (2) CEA Technical Standards for Communications; (3) CEA (Measures relating to Safety and Electricity Supply) Regulations, 2010; (4) Gujarat Grid Code; and (5) RLMM (Renewable Energy Generating Station Registration and Monitoring Mechanism) Certificate number issued by the competent authority. Absence of such certificate prevents grant of commissioning certificate. GEDA, STU, and transmission/distribution licensees shall verify compliance with prudent check and may request additional requirements from the generating station. GEDA shall ensure relevant compliance before conducting testing and commissioning activities; commissioning certificates issued without such verification lack legal validity.

KEY TOPICS: Commissioning, Integration, Grid connection, Compliance Certificate, CEA Technical Standards, RLMM Certificate, Wind Turbine Generators, Auxiliary Systems, Gujarat Grid Code, SLDC, GEDA, Distribution Licensee, Testing, SCOD (Scheduled Commissioning Date)

TEXT:
**2.13.3 Analysis and Commission's Ruling**

The Commission has noted the submission of the Stakeholders. The Commission agree to the modification proposed by SLDC to the extent of procedure for integration of Wind project to the grid and decides to make necessary modification in the Order.

As regard to other submission of Stakeholders in regard to submission of documents for allowing integration of Wind Power Projects with grid, the Commission is of the view that the Wind Power Project developers need to submit the documents to GEDA as well as SLDC and DISCOM/ STU/ GETCO for better coordination and record purpose.

**Compliance Requirements for Grid Integration:**

The Wind Generator/Developer shall submit a certificate signed by the authorised signatory not below the rank of CMD or CEO or MD or Full Time Director, to the SLDC, GEDA and concerned Distribution Licensees before declaration of SCOD, that the said Generating Station including main plant equipment's such as Wind Turbines, Auxiliary Systems, as case may, has complied with all relevant provisions of CEA Technical Standards for Connectivity, CEA Technical Standards for Communications, CEA (Measures relating to Safety and Electricity Supply) Regulations, 2010 and Gujarat Grid Code and also mention RLMM Certificate No. issued by the Competent Authority. In absence of such Certificate, no commissioning certificate shall be granted.

The GEDA, STU, transmission/ distribution licensees may verify the same with prudent check and ask to generating station for additional requirement, if any.

GEDA shall ensure following compliance from the developer of wind pooling station/ generator, before carrying out testing, commissioning activities and issuing the Commissioning Certificate to the Wind Project and failure to it, the commissioning certificate, if any, issued, shall not be qualified as legal and valid.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: CEA Technical Standards for Connectivity, CEA Technical Standards for Communications, CEA (Measures relating to Safety and Electricity Supply) Regulations 2010, Gujarat Electricity Grid Code 2013, RLMM Guidelines (Renewable Energy Generating Station Registration and Monitoring Mechanism), Electricity Act 2003 Section 68 (grid operator procedures)

CROSS-REFERENCES: Clause 3.13 on commissioning of wind power project (detailed commissioning procedures following this integration step), Clause 3.4 on metering and interconnection point, GERC Grid Code Regulations, GEDA Procedure Manual, CERC Grid Code (for ISTS-connected projects)

## CHUNK 21: Clause 3.13 – Commissioning of Wind Power Project – Commission's Decision

---
DOCUMENT: GERC Order No. 05 of 2024 - Tariff Framework for Procurement of Power by Distribution Licensees and Others from Wind Power Projects for the State of Gujarat

DOCUMENT TYPE: Tariff Order

HIERARCHY: Section 2 / Clause 2.14 / Sub-Clause 2.14.3

PROVISION: Clause 3.13 – Commissioning of Wind Power Project (Certification and Verification)

TITLE: Commissioning Certification Process for Wind Power Projects and Real-Time Data Transfer Requirements

SUMMARY:
After following the grid integration procedure (Clause 3.12), commissioning of wind projects shall be certified by GEDA in the presence of GETCO and/or distribution licensee representatives. GEDA shall ensure: (1) all equipment conforms to MNRE-approved list of WTG manufacturers of rated capacity; (2) ALMM (Amended List of Models and Manufacturers) Certificate has been issued for WTGs by concerned authority, if applicable; (3) equipment complies with Indian Standards on Renewable Energy notified by Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS); (4) energy has flown into the grid and been recorded in energy meters installed at project site; (5) generation of electricity has been witnessed by authorized representatives of DISCOM/GETCO. GEDA shall coordinate with DISCOMs/GETCO for fixing commissioning date. Representatives of GEDA, GETCO, and/or distribution licensees shall sign the commissioning documents recording necessary details; non-signing by any representative disqualifies the commissioning as legally valid. The commissioning certificate shall contain details of wind turbines, ALMM Certificates (if applicable), and other relevant data. Generation data from the wind project must be transferred in real-time to SLDC via RTU. The Commission noted that requests to align commissioning provisions with wind-solar hybrid tariff order provisions are subject to a pending GETCO petition under Commission consideration.

KEY TOPICS: Commissioning, GEDA, GETCO, Distribution Licensee, MNRE-approved WTGs, ALMM Certificate, BIS standards, Energy metering, RTU (Real-Time Data), SLDC, Commissioning Certificate, Project commissioning date, Generation data

TEXT:
**2.14.3 Analysis and Commission's Ruling**

The Commission has noted that the suggestion of the Stakeholders is also subject matter of a Petition filed by GETCO for adjudication, which is under consideration of the Commission.

**Commissioning Procedure:**

The discussion paper proposes that after following the procedure of integration of Wind Power Project with grid, the commissioning of Wind Project be followed. Commissioning with respect to the Wind Project shall be certified by the GEDA in presence of GETCO and /or Distribution Licensee representative. The GEDA should ensure that all equipment as per MNRE approved list of WTG manufacturers of rated capacity, ALMM Certificate issued for WTG by concerned Authority, if applicable, and as per Indian Standards on Renewable Energy notified by BIS has been installed and energy has flown into the grid and recorded in the energy meters installed at project site and witnessing of such generation of electricity by representative authorised by DISCOM and /or GETCO. The GEDA shall co-ordinate with DISCOMs and/or GETCO for fixing date of commissioning of plant. The representatives of GEDA, GETCO and/or Distribution Licensees shall sign on the commissioning of project by recording necessary details. Non-signing of documents of commissioning of Wind Projects by any of the representative of above entity, shall not qualify as valid 'commissioning' of the project. The commissioning certificate consists of the details of the Wind Turbine, ALMM Certificate, if applicable etc. Further, it shall also ensure that generation data from the Wind Project is also transferred in the real time basis through RTU to SLDC.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: MNRE WTG Manufacturer List, ALMM (Amended List of Models and Manufacturers), Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) Renewable Energy Standards, Electricity Act 2003 Section 68 (grid operator procedures), CERC Technical Standards, GERC Distribution Code

CROSS-REFERENCES: Clause 3.12 on integration procedure preceding commissioning, Clause 3.4 on metering requirements, Clause 3.8 on commissioning timelines and security deposit, GEDA Procedure Manual, Electricity Rules 2005 (energy generating station operation), SLDC Operating Procedures
