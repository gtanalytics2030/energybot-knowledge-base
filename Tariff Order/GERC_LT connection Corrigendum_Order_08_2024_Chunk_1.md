TITLE: Petition Registration, Truing Up FY 2022-23, ARR Approval, and Tariff Determination for FY 2024-25 – Original Tariff Order Context
SUMMARY: The Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (GERC) received tariff petitions from ten Distribution Licensees in Gujarat for truing up FY 2022-23 accounts and determining tariff for FY 2024-25. The petitions were registered as separate cases for each licensee (DGVCL, MGVCL, PGVCL, UGVCL, TPL-D Ahmedabad, TPL-D Surat, TPL-D Dahej, MUL, GIFT PCL, AIVPL) in accordance with the Electricity Act, 2003 and relevant GERC Regulations. The Commission issued original Tariff Orders on 1st June 2024, approving electricity tariff rates for retail consumers effective 1st June 2024. This Corrigendum amends those original orders.

1. The State-Owned utilities and other Distribution Licensees of the State filed the Tariff Petitions for truing up of FY 2022-23, Approval of ARR and Determination of Tariff for FY 2024-25. All these utilities filed their petitions along with Annual Accounts on the principles and methodology prescribed under the GERC Multi-Year Tariff (MYT) Regulations, 2016 as directed by the Commission.

2. These petitions were registered as Dakshin Gujarat Vij Company Ltd. (Petition No. 2318/2024), Madhya Gujarat Vij Company Ltd. (Petition No. 2319/2024), Paschim Gujarat Vij Company Ltd. (Petition No. 2320/2022), Uttar Gujarat Vij Company Ltd. (Petition No. 2321/2024), Torrent Power Ltd. – Generation (APP) (Petition No. 2322/2024), Torrent Power Limited - Distribution (Ahmedabad) (Petition No. 2323/2024), Torrent Power Limited - Distribution (Surat) (Petition No. 2324/2024) and Torrent Power Limited - Distribution (Dahej) (Petition No. 2325/2024), MPSEZ Utilities Ltd. (Petition No. 2326/2024), GIFT Power Company Limited (Petition No. 2327/2024) and Aspenpark Infra Vadodara Pvt. Ltd. (Petition No. 2328/2024) in accordance with the Electricity Act, 2003 and the relevant Regulations and the Commission proceeded with the Tariff Orders.

3. The Commission determined the tariff and issued all the afore-said Tariff orders on these petitions on 1st June, 2024. The Commission has also approved Electricity Tariff Rates for retail consumers, which has been come into force on consumption effective from 1st June 2024.
