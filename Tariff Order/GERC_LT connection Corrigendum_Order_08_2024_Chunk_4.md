SUMMARY: The LT (Low Tension) Tariff Category definition in the FY 2024-25 Tariff Schedules is amended to expand the consumer eligibility threshold from a fixed "up to 100 kVA/kW of Contracted Demand" to a flexible range of "up to 100 kVA/kW of Contracted Demand / up to 150 kVA/kW of Contracted Demand if opted by the applicant/consumer." This amendment aligns the tariff applicability with the amended Clause 3.2 of the GERC Supply Code Regulations, which now permits consumers to optionally request up to 150 kVA/kW contracted demand while remaining within the LT voltage classification (400V Three Phase). The revised definition applies uniformly across all ten distribution licensees for FY 2024-25.

DETAILED PROVISION ANALYSIS:

**Original Definition:**
The LT Tariff Category in the Tariff Orders dated 1st June 2024 had a fixed upper threshold of 100 kVA/kW (or 100 kW) of Contracted Demand for all installations.

**Revised Definition:**
The LT Tariff Category is expanded to allow two sub-categories:

1. **Standard LT Category (up to 100 kVA/kW):** All installations with contracted demand up to 100 kVA/kW remain automatically eligible for LT tariffs. This maintains continuity with the original definition.

2. **Enhanced LT Category (100–150 kVA/kW on opt-in basis):** Installations with contracted demand exceeding 100 kVA/kW but not exceeding 150 kVA/kW may optionally request (and consumer may opt for) LT tariff applicability, provided the consumer/applicant specifically requests this option at the time of connection or during a tariff migration request.

**Rationale for Revision:**
The expansion aligns with the GERC (Electricity Supply Code and Related Matters) (Fourth Amendment) Regulations, 2024 (Notification No. 14 of 2024), which amended Clause 3.2 to permit consumers in the 100–150 kVA/kW range to retain 400V Three Phase supply (LT supply) on optional basis, instead of being mandatorily moved to 11 kV Three Phase (HT supply).

**Applicability:**
This revised definition applies uniformly to the Tariff Schedules of all the following distribution licensees for FY 2024-25:
- Dakshin Gujarat Vij Company Ltd. (DGVCL)
- Madhya Gujarat Vij Company Ltd. (MGVCL)
- Paschim Gujarat Vij Company Ltd. (PGVCL)
- Uttar Gujarat Vij Company Ltd. (UGVCL)
- Torrent Power Limited - Distribution (Ahmedabad) (TPL-D(A))
- Torrent Power Limited - Distribution (Surat) (TPL-D(S))
- Torrent Power Limited - Distribution (Dahej) (TPL-D(Dahej))
- MPSEZ Utilities Ltd. (MUL)
- GIFT Power Company Limited (GIFT PCL)
- Aspenpark Infra Vadodara Pvt. Ltd. (AIVPL)

LEGAL_IMPLICATION: The expanded LT Tariff Category definition now permits consumer flexibility in voltage selection. A consumer with 100–150 kVA/kW contracted demand may choose to remain on 400V Three Phase LT supply and pay LT tariffs, rather than being mandatorily moved to HT supply and paying higher HT tariffs. This is a consumer-friendly amendment.
