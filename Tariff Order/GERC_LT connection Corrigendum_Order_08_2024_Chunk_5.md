**Paragraph 7. Unchanged Provisions**

Other terms and conditions of the Tariff Orders dtd. 01.06.2024 for DGVCL, MGVCL, PGVCL, UGVCL, TPL-D(A), TPL-D(S), TPL-D(Dahej), MUL, GIFT PCL & AIVPL remain unchanged.

INTERPRETATION AND SCOPE:

**Scope of Corrigendum:**
This Corrigendum Order No. 08 of 2024 is limited exclusively to amending the definition of the LT Tariff Category in the Tariff Schedules attached to the original Tariff Orders dated 1st June 2024. The amendment expands the contracted demand threshold from 100 kVA/kW to 150 kVA/kW (on optional basis).

**Provisions Remaining in Full Effect:**

All of the following components of the original Tariff Orders dated 1st June 2024 remain unchanged:

1. **Tariff Rates and Charges:**
   - Fixed charges / Demand charges
   - Energy charges (per unit rates)
   - Fuel adjustment charges (FAC)
   - Additional surcharges
   - Cross-subsidy surcharges

2. **Tariff Supplements and Credits:**
   - Voltage rebates
   - Power factor incentives / penalties
   - Time-of-Day (ToU) tariff multipliers
   - Industrial promotional rates (if applicable)
   - Agricultural tariff rates
   - Street lighting tariff rates

3. **Applicability Conditions:**
   - Consumer category definitions (other than the expanded LT category)
   - Voltage supply categories (except alignment with amended Clause 3.2)
   - Consumption slabs and brackets
   - Connected load classifications

4. **Billing and Payment Provisions:**
   - Billing methodology and frequency
   - Payment due dates
   - Late payment surcharges
   - Meter reading procedures
   - Deviation settlement charges

5. **Compliance and Procedural Requirements:**
   - Connection application procedures
   - Technical standards for installations
   - Safety compliance requirements
   - Reporting and audit obligations
   - Dispute resolution mechanisms (CGRF, Ombudsman)

6. **Other Policy Matters:**
   - Renewable Purchase Obligation (RPO) provisions
   - Energy Efficiency Charges
   - Transmission charges and their applicability
   - Special consumer programs (if notified in original orders)
