EFFECTIVE_DATE_DETERMINATION:

**Operative Date:**
The Corrigendum Order becomes effective from the date of publication/notification of the GERC (Electricity Supply Code and Related Matters) (Fourth Amendment) Regulations, 2024, as notified under Notification No. 14 of 2024. The exact date of this notification is referenced in Paragraph 4 of this Order and establishes the trigger date for the Corrigendum's entry into force.

**Alignment with Supply Code Amendment:**
The Corrigendum is explicitly timed to align with and operationalize the Supply Code amendment. The expanded LT Tariff Category definition (100–150 kVA/kW) can only be applied to tariffs once the Supply Code amendment permits the corresponding voltage classification (400V Three Phase for 100–150 kVA/kW consumers). This timing ensures regulatory consistency and legal validity.

**Retroactive Application:**
While the Corrigendum is issued on 25/09/2024, its effective date is pegged to the notification date of Notification No. 14 of 2024, which may be an earlier date. This construction ensures that tariff applicability for consumers who have opted into the 100–150 kVA/kW LT category applies from the moment the underlying Supply Code amendment took effect.

**Prospective Applicability for New Consumers:**
For consumers seeking new connections or modification of existing connections on or after the effective date of Notification No. 14 of 2024, the revised LT Tariff Category definition (allowing optional uprating to 150 kVA/kW) applies.

**Existing Consumers:**
For consumers already supplied under the original 100 kVA/kW LT Tariff Category definition, the amendment is optional. Such consumers may request modification of their contracted demand to up to 150 kVA/kW and request tariff category adjustment if permitted by the distribution licensee's procedures and subject to any applicable conditions.
