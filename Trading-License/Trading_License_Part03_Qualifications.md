# GERC Licensing of Electricity Trading Regulations 2005 - Batch 03

## CHUNK 021

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Licensing of Electricity Trading) Regulations, 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 4 / Qualifications for obtaining a Trading Licence

**PROVISION:** Regulation 18 — Professional Requirements of Personals

**TITLE:** Minimum Professional Staffing Requirements for Trading Licensees

**SUMMARY:**
Regulation 18 establishes mandatory professional staffing requirements for trading licensees. An applicant must employ at least one full-time professional from each of two critical disciplines in decision-making positions within the organization: (1) Power system operations and technical & commercial aspects of power transfer; and (2) Finance, commerce and accounts. Before undertaking intra-state trading, the applicant must provide the Commission with detailed information about all full-time professionals and support staff engaged for trading operations. This ensures that trading licensees possess adequate in-house expertise in both grid/technical matters and financial/accounting functions.

**KEY TOPICS:** Professional Requirements, Full-time Staff, Power System Operations, Technical and Commercial, Finance and Accounts, Decision-Making Positions, Staffing Details, Support Staff, Qualified Personnel

**TEXT:**

**Chapter-4**

**Qualifications for obtaining a Trading Licence**

**18. Professional Requirements of personals:**

a. An Applicant shall have at least one full-time professional from each of the following disciplines, holding a decision making position within its organisation, namely:

i. Power system operations and Technical & commercial aspects of power transfer; and

ii. Finance, commerce and accounts

b. The Applicant shall furnish to the Commission the details of the professionals and the support staff, engaged by him on full-time basis before undertaking intra-state trading.

## CHUNK 022

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Licensing of Electricity Trading) Regulations, 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 4 / Qualifications for obtaining a Trading Licence

**PROVISION:** Regulation 19 — Net Worth Criteria

**TITLE:** Minimum Net Worth Requirements Based on Trading Volume Category

**SUMMARY:**
Regulation 19 establishes tiered minimum net worth requirements for trading licensees based on proposed annual electricity trading volume in kilowatt-hours (KWH). The table specifies seven trading volume categories and corresponding minimum net worth requirements: (A) up to 50 million KWH requires Rs. 1.00 Crore; (B) 50–100 million KWH requires Rs. 1.50 Crores; (C) 100–200 million KWH requires Rs. 3.00 Crores; (D) 200–500 million KWH requires Rs. 7.50 Crores; (E) 500–700 million KWH requires Rs. 10.00 Crores; (F) 700–1000 million KWH requires Rs. 15.00 Crores; (G) above 1000 million KWH requires Rs. 20.00 Crores. During operations, the net worth must not drop below 75% of the minimum requirement for the applicable category and must be replenished to full statutory levels at the end of each quarter (March, June, September, December). Licensees must submit audited certificates of compliance to the Commission within 30 days of each quarter-end. This ensures ongoing financial stability of trading operations and protects market participants.

**KEY TOPICS:** Net Worth, Financial Requirement, Trading Volume, Minimum Capital, Crore, Category, Quarterly Maintenance, 75% Threshold, Audited Certificate, Financial Stability, Market Protection

**TEXT:**

**19. Net worth Criteria**

Considering the volume of intra state trading proposed to be undertaken, the net worth of the Applicant at the time of application shall not be less than the amounts specified hereunder:

| S. No. | Category | Volume of electricity proposed to be traded (in KwH) per annum | Minimum Net worth (in Rs. Crores) |
|--------|----------|---------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| 1 | A | Upto 50 million | 1.00 |
| 2 | B | 50 – 100 million | 1.50 |
| 3 | C | 100 – 200 million | 3.00 |
| 4 | D | 200 – 500 million | 7.50 |
| 5 | E | 500-700 million | 10.00 |
| 6 | F | 700-1000 million | 15.00 |
| 7 | G | Above 1000 million | 20.00 |

The net worth at any point of time during the operation of trading should not drop below 75% of the minimum requirements specified for the respective category of trader and shall be replenished at the end of every quarter ending March, June, September & December every year to the above specified levels. An audited certificate of compliance would need to be submitted to the Commission for every quarter within a period of 30 days from the end of that quarter.

## CHUNK 023

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Licensing of Electricity Trading) Regulations, 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 4 / Qualifications for obtaining a Trading Licence

**PROVISION:** Regulation 20 — Creditworthiness

**TITLE:** Credit and Default Eligibility Criteria

**SUMMARY:**
Regulation 20 establishes creditworthiness eligibility standards that applicants and their related parties must meet. Specifically: (1) The applicant and, if the applicant is a company, the promoters of the company, and if the applicant is a partnership firm, the partners of the firm, shall at no point in time have been classified as a "wilful defaulter" by the Reserve Bank of India or other relevant authority. This exclusion ensures applicants have not demonstrated intentional non-payment of financial obligations. (2) If an applicant maintains one or more borrowing accounts with banks, all such borrowing accounts must be classified as "Standard" by each respective banker at the time of application. This ensures current credit ratings reflect acceptable payment performance. These provisions protect the integrity of the trading market by excluding financially irresponsible parties.

**KEY TOPICS:** Creditworthiness, Wilful Defaulter, Reserve Bank of India, RBI, Promoters, Partners, Standard Classification, Borrowing Accounts, Credit Rating, Bank Classification, Financial Responsibility

**TEXT:**

**20. Creditworthiness**

a. The Applicant, and if the Applicant is a company, the promoters of the company, and if the Applicant is a partnership firm, the partners of the firm, shall at no point of time have been classified as "wilful defaulter" by the Reserve Bank of India or other relevant authority

b. Where an Applicant has one or more borrowal accounts with a bank/s, all such accounts shall be classified as 'Standard' by each of its bankers.
