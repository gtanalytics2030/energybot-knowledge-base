## CHUNK 035

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Licensing of Electricity Trading) Regulations, 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Schedule

**HIERARCHY:** Schedules / Schedule-1

**PROVISION:** Schedule-1 — Application Form for Grant of Intra-State Trading Licence

**TITLE:** Application Form Template for Trading License

**SUMMARY:**
Schedule-1 provides the standardized application form for seeking a trading license under these Regulations. The form captures comprehensive information about the applicant in 22 sections covering: (1) applicant name, incorporation form, office/registered address, contact person details, phone/fax/email, place and year of incorporation; (2) geographical trading area; (3) supporting documentation (registration certificate, business commencement certificate, M&A, power of attorney, income tax registration, balance sheet); (4) net worth for past 5 financial years in home currency and Indian rupees with exchange rates; (5) annual turnover for past 5 years with conversion details; (6) credit rating certificate; (7) "Standard" borrowal account certificate; (8) RBI non-willful-defaulter certificate; (9) list of supporting documents; (10-13) financing details covering self-financing (amount/percentage), third-party equity participation (name, contact, equity amount/percentage, tie-up nature), and debt proposal (lender details, loan amounts, lender letters); and (14) organizational/managerial capability (organizational structure, CVs, office/communication facilities). Finally, section 22 requires description of approach/methodology including trading volume first year and 5-year trading plans. The form is signed and dated by the applicant. This form structure ensures systematic collection of all information required to assess applicant qualifications.

**KEY TOPICS:** Application Form, Applicant Information, Incorporation, Address, Supporting Documentation, Financial Data, Net Worth, Turnover, Credit Rating, Borrowal Accounts, Financing, Equity, Debt, Organizational Structure, Trading Methodology, 5-Year Plan

## CHUNK 036

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Licensing of Electricity Trading) Regulations, 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Schedule

**HIERARCHY:** Schedules / Schedule-2

**PROVISION:** Schedule-2 — GUJARAT ELECTRICITY REGULATORY COMMISSION LICENSE TO TRADE IN ELECTRICITY AS AN ELECTRICITY TRADER

**TITLE:** Standard Trading License Certificate

**SUMMARY:**
Schedule-2 provides the standardized form of the trading license certificate issued by GERC to successful applicants. The license is granted under Section 14 of Electricity Act 2003 to a named licensee in a specified trading category (A through G) and geographic area to trade electricity as an electricity trader. The license is issued subject to: (1) Electricity Act 2003 provisions (Sections 17–22), (2) general conditions specified in GERC (Licensing of Electricity Trading) Regulations 2005, and (3) other Commission regulations issued from time to time (including statutory amendments, alterations, modifications, re-enactments), all of which are incorporated as part of the license. The license is explicitly declared non-transferable except as provided in the Act, Rules, and Regulations. Prohibited transactions requiring prior Commission approval include: acquisition/purchase/takeover/otherwise acquiring another licensee's utility; merging licensee's utility with another's utility; and assigning/transferring the license or utility or any part thereof by sale, lease, exchange or otherwise. Any such unauthorized transaction is void. The license does not grant exclusivity—the Commission may issue licenses to other traders in the same area. The licensee may engage in any business for optimum asset utilization (except transmission) with prior Commission intimation. The licensee shall pay license fees as specified by Commission. License duration is 25 years from commencement date unless earlier revoked. Provisions of Sections 19–22 of Electricity Act 2003 apply regarding license revocation and utility sale. The license is executed and sealed by the GERC Secretary.

**KEY TOPICS:** Trading License Certificate, Category, Geographic Area, Electricity Act 2003, General Conditions, Non-Transferable, Restricted Transactions, Exclusivity Waived, License Duration, 25 Years, License Fees, Revocation, Sections 19-22, GERC Secretary

**PROVISION:** Schedule-3 — PROFORMA FOR SUBMISSION OF INFORMATION FOR THE QUARTER

**TITLE:** Quarterly Trading Information Reporting Proforma

**SUMMARY:**
Schedule-3 provides the standardized proforma for quarterly information submission by trading licensees to the State Load Despatch Centre and Commission regarding all trading transactions. The proforma captures trader name, license details, and transaction-level data across 13 fields per transaction: (1) serial number; (2) volume of trading in million kWH; (3) purchased from (with category indication: generator, captive power plant, distribution licensee, government, consumer); (4) sold to (with category indication); (5) point of purchase; (6) purchase price; (7) point of sale; (8) sale price; (9) transmission/wheeling charge borne by buyer/seller/trader; (10) transmission loss borne by buyer/seller/trader; (11) UI charge borne by buyer/seller; (12) trading margin; (13) remarks. Critically, all transactions must be reported transaction-wise without aggregation. All buyer/seller categories must be specified (generator, captive power plant, distribution licensee, government, consumer where applicable). Transmission/wheeling charges, transmission losses, and UI charges must clearly indicate responsibility allocation (buyer/seller/trader). Quarterly reports are submitted to the State Load Despatch Centre with copy to Commission by deadlines specified in Regulation 24.

## CHUNK 038

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Licensing of Electricity Trading) Regulations, 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation / Schedule

**HIERARCHY:** Schedules / Schedule-4

**PROVISION:** Schedule-4 — PROFORMA FOR SUBMISSION OF PERFORMANCE DETAILS OF ELECTRICITY TRADER

**TITLE:** Quarterly Performance Details Reporting Proforma

**SUMMARY:**
Schedule-4 provides the standardized proforma for quarterly performance reporting by electricity traders to the Commission and State Load Despatch Centre. The proforma requires submission of the following information for each reporting period: (1) serial number; (2) volume of trading during the quarter in KWH; (3) cumulative trading up to the current quarter in KWH; (4) statement of whether any category change occurred during the quarter; (5) statement of whether net worth increased due to category change; (6) certification whether applicable license fees were deposited with Commission; (7) identification of any violations to license conditions pointed out by any agency or observed by the electricity trader; (8) remarks. The proforma is submitted to both the Commission directly and to the State Load Despatch Centre. The SLDC verifies the volume of trading data and forwards the verified Schedule-4 to the Commission. Submission deadlines align with quarterly calendars as specified in Regulation 28. This proforma enables the Commission to monitor licensee compliance with category-based financial requirements and fee payment obligations while tracking operational performance.
