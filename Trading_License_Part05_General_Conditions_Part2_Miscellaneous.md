## CHUNK 029

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Licensing of Electricity Trading) Regulations, 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 5 / General Conditions of the Trading Licence

**PROVISION:** Regulation 26 — Duties of Electricity Trader in relation to Consumers

**TITLE:** Application of Distribution Licensee Standards to Trading Licensees Supplying Consumers

**SUMMARY:**
Regulation 26 extends consumer protection standards to trading licensees. When a trading licensee undertakes supply of electricity to a consumer, the licensee becomes subject to all provisions of various codes relating to: electricity supply to consumers, maintenance of grid discipline, and technical/safety standards that apply to distribution licensees. Additionally, the obligations imposed on distribution licensees by GERC Standards of Performance regulations apply to trading licensees supplying electricity to consumers, to the extent applicable. This regulatory approach ensures that consumers supplied by traders receive equivalent protections and service standards as consumers supplied by traditional distribution licensees, preventing race-to-the-bottom in service quality.

**KEY TOPICS:** Consumer Supply, Distribution Licensee Standards, Standards of Performance, Grid Discipline, Technical Standards, Safety Standards, Service Quality, Consumer Protection, Supply of Electricity

**TEXT:**

**26. Duties of Electricity Trader in relation to Consumers**

a. The provisions of the various codes relating to supply of electricity to consumers, maintenance of grid discipline and technical and safety standards, which apply to a Distribution Licensee, shall be applicable to a Trading Licensee as and when such licensee undertakes the supply of electricity to a consumer;

b. The obligations imposed upon Distribution Licensees by the GERC Standards of Performance shall, as far as may be, be applicable to the supply of electricity by a Trading Licensee to one or more consumers as and when such licensee undertakes the supply of electricity to a consumer.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** GERC Standards of Performance, Distribution Code, Supply Code, Grid Code

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Regulation 21 (Obligations), Regulation 22 (Prohibited Activities), Distribution Licensee Regulations

## CHUNK 030

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Licensing of Electricity Trading) Regulations, 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 5 / General Conditions of the Trading Licence

**PROVISION:** Regulation 27 — Investigation of certain matters by the Commission

**TITLE:** Commission Investigation Powers and Actions Against Non-Compliant Licensees

**SUMMARY:**
Regulation 27 grants the Commission investigative authority and enforcement powers over trading licensees. When satisfied that a trading licensee has failed to comply with license conditions or with Electricity Act 2003 provisions, rules, or regulations, the Commission may at any time direct by written order any designated person (referred to as "Investigating Authority") to investigate the licensee's affairs and report findings to the Commission. The Investigating Authority conducts investigations in accordance with Electricity Act 2003 procedures. Upon receiving the investigation report, and after giving the licensee reasonable opportunity to make written representations regarding the report, the Commission may by written order either: (1) require the licensee to take specified corrective action as the Commission deems fit regarding matters arising from the report, or (2) suspend or cancel the trading license. The Commission may additionally take any other action authorized by the Electricity Act 2003. This mechanism provides enforcement authority for regulatory breaches without requiring formal adjudication proceedings.

**KEY TOPICS:** Investigation Authority, Non-Compliance, License Conditions, Electricity Act, Investigation, Report, Representation, Corrective Action, Suspension, Cancellation, Enforcement, Written Order

**TEXT:**

**27. Investigation of certain matters by the Commission**

a. The Commission may, on being satisfied that a Trading Licensee has failed to comply with any of the conditions of Trading Licence or has failed to comply with any of the provisions of the Act or rules or regulations made thereunder, at any time, by order in writing, direct any person (referred to as "Investigating Authority") specified in the order to investigate the affairs of any Trading Licensee and to report to the Commission on any investigation made by such Investigating Authority.

b. The Investigating Authority shall conduct the investigation in accordance with the provisions of the Act.

c. On receipt of investigation report from the Investigating Authority, the Commission may, in accordance with the provisions of the Act and after giving such opportunity to the Trading Licensee to make a representation in connection with the report as in the opinion of the Commission seems reasonable, by order in writing

i. require the Trading Licensee to take such action in respect of any matter arising out of the report as the Commission may think fit; or

ii. suspend or cancel the Trading Licence.

The Commission may also take any other action in accordance with the provisions of the Act.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Electricity Act 2003, Section 142 of Electricity Act 2003, Section 143 of Electricity Act 2003

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Regulation 16 (Revocation), Regulation 21 (Obligations), Regulation 25 (Prudential Reporting), Section 19 of Electricity Act 2003

## CHUNK 031

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Licensing of Electricity Trading) Regulations, 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 5 / General Conditions of the Trading Licence

**PROVISION:** Regulation 28 — Standards of performance

**TITLE:** Performance Standards and Quarterly Performance Reporting

**SUMMARY:**
Regulation 28 addresses performance standards for trading licensees. The Commission may, after consultation with electricity traders, specify performance standards applicable to licensees or classes of licensees. Electricity traders must furnish quarterly performance details to the Commission in the format prescribed in Schedule-4, submitted by the 15th day of the first month following each quarter (i.e., by 15th April, July, October, January for quarters ending March 31, June 30, September 30, December 31 respectively). Additionally, the performance standards of distribution and transmission licensees issued by the Commission are applicable to relevant trading licensees. This framework ensures ongoing monitoring of trading market performance and consistency with standards applied across the broader electricity industry.

**KEY TOPICS:** Performance Standards, Quarterly Reporting, Schedule-4, Performance Details, Commission Consultation, Distribution Licensee Standards, Transmission Licensee Standards, Market Performance, Monitoring

**TEXT:**

**28. Standards of performance**

a. The Commission may, after consultation with the Electricity Trader specify the standards of performance of a licensee or a class of licensees.

b. The Electricity Trader shall furnish the performance details for each year to the Commission on quarterly basis for the quarters ending 31st March, 30th June, 30th September and 31st December in the format prescribed in Schedule - 4 appended to these Regulations by 15th day of the first month of the following quarter.

c. Standard of performance of Distribution Licensee and Transmission Licensee, as may be issued by the Commission from time to time, shall be applicable to the relevant the Trading licensees.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Schedule-4 (Performance Reporting Proforma), Electricity Act 2003

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Regulation 21 (Obligations), Regulation 24 (Information Provision), Distribution Licensee Performance Standards, Transmission Licensee Performance Standards

## CHUNK 032

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Licensing of Electricity Trading) Regulations, 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 5 / General Conditions of the Trading Licence

**PROVISION:** Regulation 29 — Provisions applicable to Deemed Licensees

**TITLE:** Extension of General Conditions to Deemed Licensees

**SUMMARY:**
Regulation 29 provides that the general conditions of trading license specified in Chapter 5 of these Regulations (Regulations 21–28) shall apply to deemed licensees under the fifth proviso to Section 14 of the Electricity Act 2003. This ensures that entities that are deemed to have been granted trading licenses by operation of law are subject to the same compliance obligations and performance standards as formally granted trading licensees, maintaining uniform regulatory standards across the trading sector.

**KEY TOPICS:** Deemed Licensee, Fifth Proviso, Section 14, Electricity Act 2003, General Conditions, Regulatory Standards, Compliance

**TEXT:**

**29. Provisions applicable to Deemed Licensees**

The general conditions of Trading Licence specified in these Regulations shall be applicable to a deemed licensee under the fifth proviso to Section 14 of the Act.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Section 14 of Electricity Act 2003 (Fifth Proviso)

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Regulations 21–28 (General Conditions), Chapter 5

## CHUNK 033

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Licensing of Electricity Trading) Regulations, 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 6 / Miscellaneous

**PROVISION:** Regulation 30 — Savings

**TITLE:** Preservation of Commission Authority and Justice Powers

**SUMMARY:**
Regulation 30 contains three savings clauses that preserve Commission authority and flexibility. First, nothing in these Regulations shall limit the Commission's power to make such orders as necessary to meet the ends of justice or prevent abuse of the Commission's process. Second, nothing bars the Commission from adopting procedures at variance with any Regulation provision if, in the Commission's view and for recorded reasons, special circumstances or classes of matters warrant such variance to deal with the matter or matters appropriately. Third, nothing bars the Commission from exercising any power or function under the Electricity Act 2003 in such manner as the Commission deems fit. These savings preserve regulatory flexibility and prevent rigid application of procedural rules where justice or specific circumstances require deviation.

**KEY TOPICS:** Savings Clause, Commission Authority, Ends of Justice, Abuse of Process, Special Circumstances, Procedural Variance, Recorded Reasons, Commission Discretion, Electricity Act 2003

**TEXT:**

**Chapter 6**

**Miscellaneous**

**30 Savings**

a. Nothing in these Regulations shall be deemed to limit or otherwise affect the power of the Commission to make such orders as may be necessary to meet the ends of justice or to prevent abuse of process of the Commission.

b. Nothing in these Regulations shall bar the Commission from adopting a procedure at variance with any of the provisions of these Regulations, if the Commission, in view of the special circumstances of a matter or class of matters and for reasons to be recorded in writing, deems it necessary or expedient in order to deal with such a matter or class of matters.

c. Nothing in these Regulations shall, expressly or impliedly, bar the Commission from dealing with any matter or exercising any power under the Act, and the Commission may deal with such matters, and exercise such powers and functions in such manner as it thinks fit.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Electricity Act 2003

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** All Regulations (general applicability), Section 66 of Electricity Act 2003 (Commission's power to modify regulations)

## CHUNK 034

**DOCUMENT:** GERC (Licensing of Electricity Trading) Regulations, 2005

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Regulation

**HIERARCHY:** Chapter 6 / Miscellaneous

**PROVISION:** Regulation 31 — Power to remove difficulties

**TITLE:** Commission Authority to Resolve Implementation Difficulties

**SUMMARY:**
Regulation 31 grants the Commission authority to address difficulties arising from implementing these Regulation provisions. If any difficulty arises in giving effect to any Regulation provision, the Commission may by general or special order undertake any action not inconsistent with the Electricity Act 2003, rules, or regulations in force, which in the Commission's opinion is necessary or expedient for removing the difficulty. This provides administrative flexibility for resolving unanticipated implementation issues that may arise as the regulations are applied in practice.

**KEY TOPICS:** Difficulty Removal, Implementation, Commission Authority, General Order, Special Order, Expedient Action, Consistency with Act, Administrative Flexibility

**TEXT:**

**31 Power to remove difficulties**

If any difficulty arises in giving effect to any of the provisions of these Regulations, the Commission may, by general or special order, do anything, not inconsistent with the provisions of the Act, rules or regulations in force, which, in its opinion is necessary or expedient for the purpose of removing the difficulty.

**REFERENCED PROVISIONS:** Electricity Act 2003

**CROSS-REFERENCES:** Regulation 30 (Savings), Section 66 of Electricity Act 2003
