## Chunk 5.1

DOCUMENT: GERC Licensing of Transmission Regulations 2005

DOCUMENT TYPE: Regulation

HIERARCHY: Chapter 5 / Miscellaneous / Regulation 34

PROVISION: Regulation 34

TITLE: Savings

SUMMARY:
Regulation 34 provides three savings provisions protecting Commission authority and regulatory flexibility. First, nothing in these Regulations limits or affects the Commission's power to make orders necessary to meet the ends of justice or to prevent abuse of the Commission's process. Second, nothing prevents the Commission from adopting procedures at variance with any Regulation provision, if the Commission deems it necessary or expedient for special circumstances affecting a matter or class of matters, with reasons to be recorded in writing. Third, nothing limits the Commission's authority to deal with matters or exercise powers under the Acts, and the Commission may do so in such manner as it thinks fit. These savings provisions ensure the Commission retains flexibility to respond to unusual circumstances and to exercise its full statutory authority while operating generally within the framework established by these Regulations.

KEY TOPICS: Savings, Commission Authority, Procedural Flexibility, Justice and Equity, Special Circumstances, Written Reasons, Statutory Powers

TEXT:

Chapter 5
Miscellaneous

34. Savings

(a) Nothing in these Regulations shall be deemed to limit or otherwise affect the power of the Commission to make such orders as may be necessary to meet the ends of justice or to prevent abuse of process of the Commission.

(b) Nothing in these Regulations shall bar the Commission from adopting a procedure at variance with any of the provisions of these Regulations, if the Commission, in view of the special circumstances of a matter or class of matters and for reasons to be recorded in writing, deems it necessary or expedient in order to deal with such a matter or class of matters.

(c) Nothing in these Regulations shall, expressly or impliedly, bar the Commission from dealing with any matter or exercising any power under the Acts, and the Commission may deal with such matters, and exercise such powers and functions in such manner as it thinks fit.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Electricity Act 2003, Gujarat Electricity Industry (Reorganisation and Regulation) Act 2003

CROSS-REFERENCES: Related to Commission's Conduct of Business Regulations 2004; preserves Commission flexibility under applicable Acts

PROVISION: Regulation 35

TITLE: Power to Remove Difficulties

SUMMARY:
Regulation 35 grants the Commission authority to remove difficulties that arise in giving effect to the Regulations' provisions. If any difficulty arises, the Commission may, by general or special order, do anything not inconsistent with the Electricity Act or State Act that the Commission deems necessary or expedient for removing the difficulty. This provision provides a residual mechanism for addressing unforeseen implementation challenges while ensuring all Commission actions remain within the legal boundaries established by the Acts. By this provision, the Commission can issue interim guidance, clarifications, or remedial orders to facilitate effective implementation of these Regulations.

KEY TOPICS: Power to Remove Difficulties, General or Special Order, Implementation Challenges, Consistency with Acts, Commission Authority

TEXT:

35. Power to remove difficulties

If any difficulty arises in giving effect to any of the provisions of these Regulations, the Commission may, by general or special order, do anything, not inconsistent with the provisions of the Acts, which is in its opinion necessary or expedient for the purpose of removing the difficulty.

REFERENCED PROVISIONS: Electricity Act 2003, Gujarat Electricity Industry (Reorganisation and Regulation) Act 2003

CROSS-REFERENCES: Similar "removal of difficulties" provisions in other GERC regulations and in the Acts themselves
